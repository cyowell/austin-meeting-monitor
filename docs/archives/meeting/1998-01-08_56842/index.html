Austin's Watershed, Housing & Street Names

Thursday, January 8, 1998 regular

Here's a summary of the January 8, 1998 Austin City Council meeting:

  • Environmental Policy Shift:

    A public hearing was set to officially rename the city's "Drainage Utility" to the "Watershed Protection Department," reflecting a stronger focus on environmental management.

  • Affordable Housing & Community Planning:

    The Council initiated a feasibility study for reasonably priced housing in the Montopolis area and requested a land-use survey for Far South Central Austin.

  • Public Safety & Accessibility Upgrades:

    Funds were approved for 14 new police motorcycles and significant ADA (Americans with Disabilities Act) improvements, including new sidewalks, curb ramps, and modifications to health clinics.

  • Civic and Development Updates:

    A public hearing was scheduled to rename Washington Avenue to S.L. Davis Avenue, development fees were waived for recently annexed properties, and updates to the city's mechanical and plumbing codes were set for future discussion.
