Thursday, December 16, 1909 regular
Transcript

• Property Encroachment Resolution: The council declared that property owner Joseph Schapiro illegally fenced off a 4,905-square-foot portion of public Garden Street, and ordered city officials to remove the fence and restore public access to the street.

• Budget Approvals: Council passed ordinances to fund street department salaries ($80), water and light department salaries ($1,500), and a major bond payment of $30,590 due in January 1910.

• Pound Fee Petition Denied: The council rejected a request from resident S.H. Weaver to refund a pound fee he had paid to the city.

• New Services Approved: The council granted the Bonner-Tarr agency permission to install and maintain trash cans along Austin streets within the fire limits.

• Administrative Changes: City Health Officer Dr. W.J. Matthews resigned and was accepted by the council.
