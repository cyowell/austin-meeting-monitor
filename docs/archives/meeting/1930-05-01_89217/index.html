  • Infrastructure Upgrades:

    Plans were finalized to pave North Guadalupe Street, new high-pressure gas mains were approved for Oldham Street, and several new sanitary sewer extensions were authorized, partially funded by project savings.

  • Utility & Financial Management:

    The council considered public petitions for one-hour parking on Colorado Street and for paving Neches Street, also approving a burial plot for a public school benefactor.
