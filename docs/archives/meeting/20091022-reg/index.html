Austin's New Driving Rules & Future Transport

Thursday, October 22, 2009 Austin City Council Regular Meeting

Here's a summary of the Austin City Council's agenda from October 22, 2009:

  • New Driving Rules Adopted:

    The city passed a ban on texting while driving, with enforcement delayed until January 1st, 2010, to allow for a public education campaign. This decision followed debate about the ordinance's broad scope and enforcement.

  • Strategic Mobility Plan Underway:

    Council received a briefing on a new plan designed to identify and address Austin's transportation gaps across roads, bike lanes, and public transit, aiming for efficient and sustainable solutions.

  • Comprehensive City Plan Consultant Approved:

    A major consultant contract was approved for the city's overall Comprehensive Plan, despite discussions regarding proposed modifications to the scope of work and concerns about potential project delays.
