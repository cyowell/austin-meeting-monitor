Austin Urban Agenda: Furnace, Fuel, Parks

Thursday, August 15, 1912 regular

  • Blast Furnace Blocked:

    City leaders rejected a permit request to erect a blast furnace at Colorado and 5th streets, signaling early considerations for urban planning and industrial development.

  • Future Energy Secured:

    A new contract for lignite coal was approved for the city's power plant, with provisions anticipating a future transition to power generated from the new Colorado River dam.

  • Park & City Hall Upgrades:

    Funds were reallocated to support improvements at several city parks, including East Avenue, Pease, and Wooldridge Parks, and for general City Hall operations.

  • Citizen Tax & Fee Relief:

    The council remitted impound fees for citizens' cows, with special consideration given to a widow and cases involving new pound limits, and corrected mistaken property and automobile tax assessments.

  • Delinquent Taxes Pursued:

    Legal action was authorized against numerous property owners to collect unpaid taxes, demonstrating the city's enforcement of financial obligations.

The Council was called to order by the Mayor with all members present: Councilman Bartholomew offered the following resolution : Whereas the Rockdale Lignite Co ,Lignite mine at or near Rockdale,Texas, has taken fire and it has been found necessary to close the mine to extinguish the fire,which closing may continue for an indefinite time,and, Contract for Lignite Aug 15th 1912- to Aug 15th 1913 Awarded Ryan & Perry Whereas, said Rockdale Lignite Co has asked to be relieved from its contract to furnish the City of Austin with Lignite for the Water,Light and Power Plant for the balance of the year 1912, and Messrs Ryan and Perry of Rockdale ,Texas, have signified their willingness to carry out the contract with certain qualifications; Now therefore, Resolved that the Supt of Parks and Public Property be authorized to contract with Messrs Ryan and Perry for Lignite for the City of Austin, Texas, for the year commencing August 15th 1912, and ending August 15th 1913, at $1.35 per ton f o b Austin,Texas, under similar contract to that with the Rockdale Lignite Co; with the proviso that when the City of Austin can receive power from the dam now being erected across the Colorado River above the City of Austin ,it shall not be obligated to take only such lignite as shall be required for additional power ; and provided also that the City of Austin shall have the option of extending the contract for Lignite with Messrs Ryan & Perry until Jan 1st 1914, upon the same terms by giving the said Ryan & Perry notice of such desired extension 30 days before the expiration of the proposed contract.
