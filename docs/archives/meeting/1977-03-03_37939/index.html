Austin's Cable, Parks, Ethics, & Civic Rights

Thursday, March 3, 1977 regular

  • Cable TV's Future Ignites Debate:

    A proposal to create a task force to study cable television expansion, services, and potential municipal ownership failed after extensive discussion about costs, technical feasibility, and the company's existing contract.

  • New Utility Oversight and Ethics Standards:

    Plans progressed for a permanent Citizen's Electric Utility Commission, and the council approved clearer ethics and financial disclosure rules for city officials.

  • Givens Park Recreation Center Funding Secured:

    The city approved moving forward with the Givens Park Recreation Center project by reallocating funds from parkland acquisition, with a commitment to secure additional grants for its full completion.

  • Volunteer Political Rights Questioned:

    A controversial legal opinion restricting the political activities of volunteer members on city boards and commissions sparked heated debate, with several council members expressing strong disagreement over limiting civic participation.
