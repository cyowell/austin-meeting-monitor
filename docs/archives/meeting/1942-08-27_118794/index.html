Wartime Austin: Airbase, Market, and Equity

Thursday, August 27, 1942 regular

  • New City Market Regulations:

    Approved an ordinance establishing detailed rules for the City Market, creating a "Market Master" position, and outlining penalties for violations.

  • Wartime Airport Expansion:

    Appropriated $17,000 for the construction of a new hangar at the Municipal Airport, having secured necessary materials approval from the War Production Board.

  • Support for Military Operations:

    Released the city's rights to electric infrastructure within the Del Valle Air Support Command Base to the U.S. Government for the duration of World War II to aid military efforts.

  • Racial Equity Discussion:

    Addressed a request from the Negro Community Welfare Association for a Black police woman, ultimately deciding to prioritize planning a new park for Black residents with a director who would hold police powers.
