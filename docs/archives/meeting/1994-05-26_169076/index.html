MINUTES OF THE AUSTIN HOUSING FINANCE CORPORATION BOARD OF DIRECTOR'S MEETING May 26, 1994 The meeting was called to order at 5:00 p.m. Present: Bruce Todd, Gus Garcia, Jackie Goodman, Michael "Max" Nofziger Ronney Reynolds Absent: Brigid Shea Agenda Item No. 40 (1) Approve Minutes of April 7, 1994 meeting.
