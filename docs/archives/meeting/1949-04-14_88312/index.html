Austin: Sewers, Coliseum, Officer Uniforms

Thursday, April 14, 1949 regular

  • City Coliseum Overhaul:

    The Council discussed the future operations of the City Coliseum, setting new rates, advertising for concession bids, and considering the purchase of 1,000 additional chairs.

  • Sewer Project Budget Concerns:

    A major sanitary sewer construction project faced a hurdle as the City Manager delayed a recommendation after all bids came in substantially higher than the city's estimate.

  • Utility Franchise & Employee Benefits:

    A new franchise was granted to Southwestern Bell Telephone, and a clothing allowance was approved for uniformed officers in the Police and Fire Departments.

  • Zoning Changes & Unique Permits:

    Public hearings were scheduled for several property zoning change requests, and specific permits were granted for items like an air conditioning pipe under an alley and a private gasoline plant for a taxi company.
