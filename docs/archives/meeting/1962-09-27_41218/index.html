Sidewalk Safety, Park Expansion, New Taxes

Thursday, September 27, 1962 regular

  • Public Safety and Sidewalks:

    Residents intensely debated the need for sidewalks, particularly near schools, prompting the City Manager to study revising existing construction ordinances to address costs and public opposition.

  • New Community Facilities:

    Plans advanced for an Austin Area Garden Center in Zilker Park and authorization was given to proceed with a golf course on airport clear zone land, expanding city recreational options.

  • Growth and Infrastructure Investment:

    The city approved the 1962 municipal property tax levy, annexed several tracts of land, and awarded contracts for essential sewer systems, storm drains, and street paving projects.

  • Utility and Parking Policy:

    Permits were granted for extending gas mains across multiple streets, and reviews were initiated for apartment garbage collection procedures and parking restrictions on congested thoroughfares.
