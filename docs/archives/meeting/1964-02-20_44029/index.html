Thursday, February 20, 1964 regular
Transcript

  • Major Public Investments:

    Approved a $2 million bond sale to finance hospital improvements, street construction, and right-of-way acquisitions. Significant contracts were also awarded for the Holly Street Power Station expansion, with a contract modification saving over $270,000, and partial funding committed for a pedestrian overpass on State Highway 71 for school safety.

  • Urban Renewal and Flood Mitigation:

    Advanced plans for the Glen Oaks Urban Renewal Project and initiated a broader General Neighborhood Renewal Plan, focusing on combating urban blight and developing flood control solutions for the Boggy Creek area.

  • Building Code and Developer Fees Debate:

    Held a public discussion with home builders regarding proposed increases in building inspection fees and explored streamlining permit processes, agreeing to a future study of departmental inspection costs.

  • Water Policy Review:

    Extended existing water refund contracts to newly acquired service districts and mandated a study to evaluate and potentially revise the city's current, considered "liberal," refund policy for developers.
