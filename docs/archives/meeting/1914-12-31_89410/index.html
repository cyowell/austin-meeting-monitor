New Hospital, Lower Water Bills, Dam Payments

Thursday, December 31, 1914 regular

  • Addressing Dam Impacts:

    Payments were approved for landowners whose properties were perpetually flooded by the city's recently rebuilt dam.

  • Equipping a New City Hospital:

    Contracts were awarded to furnish the New City Hospital with furniture and essential surgical and sanitary equipment.

  • Reduced Water Rates:

    A significant policy change lowered the minimum water rate for citizens, effective January 1915.

  • Major Infrastructure Investments:

    Substantial funds were allocated for expanding water, light, and power utilities, alongside improvements for city streets and bridges.
