Austin's Future: Hotel Tax, Housing, Waste

Tuesday, August 15, 2017 Austin City Council Work Session

  • Future of Austin's Convention Center & Tourism Funds:

    A task force recommended expanding the Convention Center by increasing hotel taxes. This plan aims to generate millions for arts, music, historic preservation, and park projects, while *not* using local property taxes.

  • Tackling Displacement and Affordable Housing:

    Plans were discussed for a task force to combat gentrification and displacement. Separately, efforts were made to ensure new developments maximize income-restricted affordable units and to explore legal action against housing discrimination.

  • Overhauling Waste Management & Dillo Dirt:

    The City reviewed new policies for waste contracts, including a critical need to re-evaluate the Dillo Dirt program and address significant, fire-risk compost piles at Hornsby Bend.

  • Challenging State Laws:

    The City is looking into potential legal challenges against state legislation, specifically a "small cell" tower bill that could cost taxpayers millions, and another on source-of-income housing discrimination.
