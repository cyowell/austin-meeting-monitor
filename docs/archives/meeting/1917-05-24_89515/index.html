Austin Policy: Health Funds, Street Upgrades, New Valuations

Thursday, May 24, 1917 regular

Here's a summary of the Austin City Council's agenda:

  • Public Health & Infrastructure Funding:

    The city allocated $2,500 to Public Health and approved significant fund transfers for various street and sewer improvements, including new paving, storm sewers, and a bridge.

  • New Real Estate Valuation System:

    A major $6,600 contract was approved to implement a "unit system of equalization of real estate values" for property assessment, requiring the contractor to post a $5,000 indemnity bond.

  • "Sand Beach" Lease & Damage Settlement:

    The council accepted terms for leasing the "Sand Beach" reserve area from the State of Texas and authorized a $2,000 settlement payment for damages.

  • Citizen Requests:

    Three appeals contesting property tax assessments were denied, while a permit was granted for an individual to construct a modern concrete and iron workshop.
