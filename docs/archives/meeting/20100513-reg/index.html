Thursday, May 13, 2010 Austin City Council Regular Meeting
Official City Record Agenda Watch Video Transcript

Here's what was on Austin City Council's agenda:

  • Taking a Stand on Immigration:

    The city council voted to examine direct investments with the state of Arizona, signaling strong disagreement with its new immigration law.

  • Boosting Water Conservation Efforts:

    A new strategic marketing plan was presented to increase public awareness and participation in year-round water conservation, aiming for a more efficient water future.

  • Advancing Mental Health & Housing Solutions:

    An annual report detailed ongoing efforts to address mental health challenges, including new strategies to reduce jail incarceration for the mentally ill and expand permanent supportive housing for people experiencing homelessness.

  • Honoring Community & History:

    Discussions included renaming a bridge to honor civil rights leader J.J. Seabrook, symbolizing unity, and advancing plans for affordable senior housing redevelopment at the RBJ tract.
