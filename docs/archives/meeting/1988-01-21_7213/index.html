Austin Infrastructure & Community Issues

Thursday, January 21, 1988 regular

  • Major Infrastructure Push:

    The council advanced several significant road projects by authorizing land acquisition (eminent domain) for improvements along routes like US 183, US 290/SH 71, North Lamar Boulevard, and Burleson Road. They also approved substantial funding and agreements for expanding water and wastewater systems, notably in the North Travis County MUDs.

  • Community Engagement & Future Policy:

    Citizens communicated concerns on various topics, including converting solid waste to compost, the South Texas Nuclear Project, and establishing task forces for electric utility planning. A public hearing was also scheduled for a new Private Sewage Facilities Ordinance.

  • Local Development & Health Services:

    Key approvals included the annexation of 64 acres to the Block House Creek Municipal Utility District and consenting to revenue bonds to help finance a health facility located within Austin.

  • Challenger Commemoration:

    The meeting began with a poignant commemoration of the second anniversary of the Space Shuttle Challenger disaster, featuring a video and art print presentations.
