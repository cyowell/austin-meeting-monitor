Austin's New Cemetery Rules & Urban Works

Thursday, December 16, 1915 regular

  • Cemetery Expansion and Regulations:

    The official map for the Oakwood Cemetery Annex was adopted. New policies set grave plot prices ($7.50 for adults, $5.00 for children) and limited families to purchasing two grave spaces. Sales proceeds will fund future cemetery land acquisition.

  • Infrastructure Upgrades:

    Projects were approved for new sanitary sewer line extensions on 8th, 12th, and 14th Streets. Additionally, a storm drain on Wichita Street and concrete paving for an alley near 23rd Street were funded.

  • City Finances & Citizen Appeals:

    Financial transfers and appropriations were made for various city operations, including interest payments and sewer project reallocations. A request for fine remission from one citizen was refused, while fines for two other individuals were remitted.
