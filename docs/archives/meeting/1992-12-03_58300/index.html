Austin: Dell, Mopac, City Growth & Key Services

Thursday, December 3, 1992 regular

  • Economic Boost & Future Growth:

    Authorized negotiations for a major strategic partnership with Dell Computer Corporation and approved annexations for several new areas, expanding the city's limits.

  • Transportation & Development:

    Adjusted Mopac frontage road speed limits (up to 55 mph) and approved updates to development impact fees, influencing city growth and infrastructure.

  • Public Health & Social Services:

    Funded critical HIV early intervention services with multiple agencies and approved expanded facilities for homeless support through the HOBO resource center.

  • Major Infrastructure Projects:

    Advanced plans for a multi-million dollar South Austin Outfall wastewater project, including an easement through city parkland, and approved significant upgrades for the airport.
