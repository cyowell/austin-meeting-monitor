Jitneys, Coal, and Compassion in Austin

Thursday, June 10, 1915 regular

  • New Transportation Regulation:

    The city began drafting an ordinance to define, license, and regulate "Jitneys" — early forms of shared ride services — operating on Austin streets.

  • Fueling City Utilities:

    A contract was approved to secure lignite coal at 85 cents per ton for the city's Water, Light, and Power Plant.

  • Compassionate Justice:

    A $48 fine was suspended for M.A. McWhirter, acknowledging his family's situation and his struggle with alcohol, conditional on good behavior.
