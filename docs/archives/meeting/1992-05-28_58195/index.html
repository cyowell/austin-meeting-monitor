Thursday, May 28, 1992 regular
Transcript

  • Major Financial and Infrastructure Decisions:

    Authorized over $351 million in utility bond refinancing, held a public hearing on a future city bond election, and began discussions for creating a Downtown Public Improvement District.

  • Pioneering Environmental Policies:

    Established new fees and a permit program for ozone-depleting chemicals, and approved the purchase of cleaner-burning Compressed Natural Gas (CNG) vehicles for city operations.

  • Key Media and Community Updates:

    Settled a lawsuit with Austin Cablevision for $315,000 while also discussing the potential transfer of the cable franchise to Time Warner Entertainment. Additionally, created an Animal Advisory Commission and planned to preserve the static aircraft display at Recce Park following Bergstrom Air Force Base's upcoming closure.
