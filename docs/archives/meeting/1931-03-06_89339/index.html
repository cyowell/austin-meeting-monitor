Austin's New Commercial Zones & Street Rules

Friday, March 6, 1931 special called

  • Street Use Regulations Amended:

    Rules governing street usage for the Scarbrough Building contractor were updated, incorporating recommendations from the city's Building Inspector and Traffic Department.

  • Commercial Zoning Approved (Waller Blvd/26th St):

    A property at the intersection of the proposed Waller Boulevard and 26th Street was rezoned for Commercial "C" use.

  • Commercial Zoning with Condition (East 15th/East Ave):

    Another property at East 15th Street and East Avenue was also rezoned to Commercial "C," with the specific requirement that any new building built there must front East 15th Street.
