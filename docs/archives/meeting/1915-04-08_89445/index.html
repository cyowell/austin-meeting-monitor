Austin OKs Construction, Shows, Fuel

Thursday, April 8, 1915 regular

  • Confirmed Leadership:

    Mayor A.P. Wooldridge and four councilmen were confirmed for their new two-year terms following the recent city election.

  • Fueling Local Commerce:

    Approved a permit for the Lake Austin Navigation Company to install a temporary gasoline tank and pump at the city dam.

  • Public Health & Sanitation:

    Granted a temporary permit for a downtown business to erect a water closet connected to the city's sanitary sewer, and allocated additional funds for the new City Hospital's supplies and budget corrections.

  • Urban Development & Advertising:

    Authorized temporary use of city space for a business to suspend construction timber and a dirt bin over an alley, and permitted advertising banners for local shows.
