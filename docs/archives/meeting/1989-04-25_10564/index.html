Tuesday, April 25, 1989 special called
Transcript

  • Convention Center Development Moves Forward:

    Over $100,000 was approved to secure legal and appraisal services for land acquisition, preparing the way for the new Convention Center project.

  • Market Study Funding Debated:

    A proposal for an additional $25,000 to update the Convention Center's economic impact analysis faced opposition, passing its initial vote with one "no" due to cost concerns.

  • New Committee Formed for Convention Center:

    A special committee, chaired by the Mayor, was established to accelerate the project's timeline and provide further reports by mid-May.

CONVENTION CENTER Council had before them for consideration an ordinance transferring $174,500.00 from the Convention Center Reserve Fund to the Convention Center CIP Fund for the services of David Armbrust with the law firm of Armbrust & Brown for legal/real estate services, W.F. After discussion the following motions were made: Motion Councilmember Carl-Mitchell made a motion, seconded by Mayor Cooke to retain W.F. Smith Co. to do the appraisal footprint for $73,000.00; retain David Armbrust to do the legal and real estate work on each one of the individual parcels, representing the City, not to exceed $30,000; and proceed with Coopers and Lybrand with their proposal and ask the City Manager to continue to work with them to see if we can negotiate any parts of the price of the total package as it deals with trying to be very prudent and what numbers are needed prior to the bond election. Councilmember Humphrey asked what the hourly rate is for Coopers & Lybrand and was told it is $180.00 an hour. City Manager Barnett said she wanted to clarify that all amounts are not to exceed those specified "if we can bring it in lower we certainly will". Councilmember Carl-Mitchell suggested they divide the question because both Councilmembers Humphrey and Barnstone had expressed reservations about spending the money for an updated market analysis. Mayor Cooke said, "We have the motion and we will divide all three parts". Results are as follows. Motion The Council, on Councilmember Carl-Mitchell's motion, Mayor Cooke's second, waived the requirement for three readings and finally passed an ordinance approving W.F. Smith for appraisal services and transferral of $73,000 from the Convention Center Reserve Fund to the Convention Center CIP Fund to do the appraisal footprint. (5-0 Vote, Councilmembers Urdy and Barnstone absent.) Council Memo 2 Motion 4/25/89 The Council, on Councilmember Carl-Mitchell's motion, Mayor Cooke's second, waived the requirement for three readings and finally passed an ordinance transferring $30,000.00 from the Convention Center Reserve Fund to the Convention Center CIP Fund to retain David Armbrust to do the legal and real estate work on each one of the individual parcels. (4-1-0 Vote, Councilmember Barnstone voted No, Councilmembers Urdy and Nofziger absent.)
