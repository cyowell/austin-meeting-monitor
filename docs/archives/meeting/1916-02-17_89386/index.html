Austin's Budget, Jitneys, and City Life

Thursday, February 17, 1916 regular

  • Transportation Regulation:

    A citizen petition to regulate "jitneys" (early ride-share motor vehicles) was rejected due to insufficient signatures, though petitioners were advised they could refile.

  • City Budget Adoption:

    The 1916 annual budget, totaling over $858,000 for city operations, was officially adopted.

  • Modernizing City Services:

    Funds were allocated for new motor vehicles, including a motorcycle for the Police Department and an automobile for the Engineering Department.

  • Urban Improvements & Culture:

    The Driskill Hotel received a temporary permit to plant trees on a sidewalk, and an appropriation was made for the Municipal Chorus.

  • Judicial Remissions:

    The Council remitted several fines for intoxication and one for unlawfully riding a train, often upon recommendation from city legal counsel.
