Austin's Future: Valet, Bag Ban, Water, Wayfinding

Thursday, November 8, 2012 Austin City Council Regular Meeting

  • Downtown Makeover Planned:

    A new Wayfinding Master Plan was presented, aiming to enhance the visitor experience with improved signage, digital tools, and better connections to public transit and bike paths.

  • Bag Ban Rules Finalized:

    New administrative rules for the city's single-use carryout bag ordinance were approved, following discussions on restaurant exemptions and the ordinance's overall implementation.

  • Valet Parking Fees Increase:

    The city approved a new fee structure for downtown valet services, intended to recoup lost revenue from metered parking, with ongoing debate among council members about the pace of future increases.

  • Water Plant Cost Concerns:

    A citizen raised alarms about significant cost overruns, estimated at $35 million, for Water Treatment Plant 4, urging accountability from city utility management.

  • Veteran Business Study:

    A disparity study was authorized to assess opportunities for veteran-owned businesses and explore the potential for new support programs within the city's contracting initiatives.
