Rates, Refunds, and Smart Meter Upgrades

Monday, September 19, 2016 Austin Energy Utility Oversight Committee Regular Meeting

  • Electric Rate Controversy:

    Austin Energy was challenged on whether recent rate cuts truly benefited the lowest-usage residential customers, leading to a commitment for further analysis.

  • Metro Tax Refund:

    The utility announced plans to refund approximately 6,000 Williamson County residents for years of improperly collected metro transit taxes and initiate an audit.

  • Smart Meter Overhaul:

    A $29.1 million project was approved to replace 245,000 outdated residential smart meters, aiming for improved customer communication, energy management, and operational efficiency.

  • Financial & Transparency Boost:

    Austin Energy plans to refinance bonds for over $2 million in savings and launched a new public performance dashboard to track financial health and service goals.
