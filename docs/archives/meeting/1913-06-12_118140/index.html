Electric Signs, Fund Transfers, Tax Corrections

Thursday, June 12, 1913 regular

Here's a summary of the Austin City agenda from June 12, 1913:

  • New Financial Policy:

    The city adopted a significant policy to transfer all interest earned from major funds (like those for sanitary sewers, street improvements, hospitals, and cemeteries) directly into its Interest and Sinking Fund, with over $1,000 transferred from May’s earnings.

  • Public Space for Businesses:

    Multiple permits were issued for electric signs extending over sidewalks on Congress Avenue for businesses, as well as a private staircase on San Jacinto Street, all subject to city revocation.

  • Correcting Old Tax Records:

    The council approved the correction or cancellation of several historical tax assessments for citizens, some dating back to 1895, due to errors in original property or personal levies.

  • Key City Appointments:

    Important city roles were filled, including the confirmation of a new Chief and officers for the Fire Department, and the appointment of two Assistant Engineers.
