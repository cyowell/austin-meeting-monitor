Austin: Taxi Permit Returns, Special Officer

Saturday, August 31, 1918 special called

  • Taxi Permit Reinstated:

    Taxi operator Sid Look successfully had his permit for "Public Service Car No. 38" reinstated after it was previously canceled. However, he was prohibited from obtaining any additional permits for the remainder of 1918.

  • Unpaid Special Policeman Appointed:

    A. P. Barnes was confirmed as a special policeman, notably serving in the role without pay.

It is, however, expressly stipulated that no additional permit or permits will be granted to the said Sid Look to drive any additional public service car, over and above Public Service Car No. 38, during the balance of the calendar year 1918. The resolution was adopted by a vote of 4 yeas, no nays; Councilman Anthony not being present when the vote was taken. APPROVED, August 29, 1918; A. P. Wooldridge, Mayor. The Council then adjourned.
