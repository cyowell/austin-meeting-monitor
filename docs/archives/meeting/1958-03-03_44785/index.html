Austin's Utility Future & Sewer System Overhaul

Monday, March 3, 1958 special called

  • Major Utility Review:

    City officials reviewed a comprehensive engineering and financial study by Ebasco detailing Austin's municipal utility operations.

  • Utility Rate Discussions:

    The report prompted discussions about conducting a rate study and considering future changes to utility rates.

  • Proposed Sewer System Shift:

    A significant proposal was considered to separate the sewer system from the utility department, placing it under Public Works and potentially financing it through general obligation bonds.

  • New Utility Promotion Strategies:

    Ideas were explored for promoting utility services through educational programs aimed at architects, contractors, and distributors.
