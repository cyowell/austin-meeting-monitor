Defense Council, Airport Expansion, Zoning

Thursday, August 21, 1941 regular

  • Mobilizing for National Defense:

    City leaders established a new Municipal Defense Council and initiated efforts to acquire additional land (potentially through eminent domain) to expand the Municipal Airport for U.S. Army and Navy aircraft.

  • Urban Growth & Zoning:

    A public hearing was scheduled to consider a significant zoning change for properties on West 24th and Leon Streets, moving them from single-family to multi-family residential.

  • Infrastructure & Business Permits:

    The city approved extensive plans for new gas main construction across multiple streets and granted a permit for a private company to operate its own gasoline plant for fleet vehicles.

  • Community Engagement:

    The council acknowledged and appreciated an offer of service from the Women's Motor Corps of Texas to assist with any local defense programs.
