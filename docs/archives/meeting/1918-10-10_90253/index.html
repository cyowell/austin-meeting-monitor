REGULAR MEETING OF THE CITY COUNCIL: Austin, Texas, Oct. 10, 1918. The Council was called to order by the Mayor, all members being present. The minutes of October 3 and subsequent special meetings were read and approved by a vote of 5 yeas, no nays. A delegation composed of John L. Peeler, Wr. F. L. Griffith, A. J. Eilers, H. E. Long, A. C. Goeth and a committee from the Police Department came before the Council and presented a petition for increased sal- aries for the police. Col. Peeler and others addressed the Council in this behalf. The Mayor replied at length, saying that the financial condition of the city made an increase this year out of the question, but that he hoped to grant increases to all city employees beginning Jan. 1, 1919.
