Thursday, March 7, 1991 regular
Transcript

  • Major Financial & Infrastructure Boost:

    The city approved $143.74 million in bonds for its combined utility systems and moved forward with acquiring land for the U.S. Highway 183 improvement project.

  • Critical Health & Environmental Initiatives:

    Funding was restored for AIDS services to support HIV prevention and education, and master planning began for three significant creek watersheds.

  • Public Safety & Urban Development:

    Officials directed the immediate start of a Police Cadet class, approved a new charter bus franchise, and accepted conceptual plans for the Sixth Street Historic District Streetscape.
