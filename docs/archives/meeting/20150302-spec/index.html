Austin: Making Waste Work, Creating Jobs

Monday, March 2, 2015 Austin City Council Special Called Meeting
  • Austin aims for aggressive zero waste goals, targeting 90% diversion of materials from landfills by 2040, up from the current 40%.
  • Key policy discussions included streamlining universal recycling for all businesses, expanding city-wide food waste composting, and implementing new construction waste recycling rules.
  • The city is developing an Austin Remanufacturing Hub at a former landfill to create local markets and manufacturing jobs from recycled materials.
  • Challenges highlighted include low compliance with existing business recycling mandates, the need for improved public education, and balancing program costs with city affordability.
  • Non-profit Keep Austin Beautiful partners with the city, engaging thousands of volunteers in cleanups, fighting illegal dumping, and promoting community beautification.
