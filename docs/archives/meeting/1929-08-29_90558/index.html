Thursday, August 29, 1929 regular
Transcript

  • Railroad Noise Investigation:

    Residents protested "unbearable noises" from train switching near West Sixth Street, prompting a city survey into the railway's legal right to occupy streets.

  • Controversial Gas Station Permit:

    A proposed gasoline filling station at 1010 Lavaca Street sparked significant debate over traffic hazards, with the permit decision deferred due to a tie vote.

  • Utility & Infrastructure Expansion:

    The city approved numerous new gas mains for the Texas Public Service Company and telephone pole installations for Southwestern Bell, alongside funding for Water and Light Plant construction.

  • Street Improvement & Land Acquisitions:

    Contracts were finalized for street paving projects (like Lavaca Street) with associated property assessments, and the city approved purchasing land for a hospital expansion and other municipal uses.
