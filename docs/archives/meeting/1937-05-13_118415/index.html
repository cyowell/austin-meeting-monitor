Austin: New Gas Mains, Parks, Streets & Thanks

Thursday, May 13, 1937 regular
  • The Texas House of Representatives formally thanked Austin citizens for hosting a barbecue and entertainment event at Barton Springs.
  • Approved the construction of new gas mains on Niles Road and Newfield Lane, expanding the city's utility infrastructure.
  • Made significant land use decisions, including a property exchange to reconfigure public alleys and accepting a land donation for extending West Live Oak and Hodges Streets.
  • Authorized the purchase of land for future park development, allocating $800 from a bond fund for the acquisition.
