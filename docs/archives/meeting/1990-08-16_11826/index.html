Landfill Fight, Street Funding, & City Growth

Thursday, August 16, 1990 regular

  • Landfill Debate:

    A motion to repeal endorsement of a new landfill permit near Creedmore failed, but the city directed staff to investigate options for future city landfills, highlighting ongoing environmental concerns.

  • Major Infrastructure & Development:

    Approved significant contracts for the Convention Center foundation and a large portion of the US Highway 183 improvement project, alongside efforts to secure additional funding for street repair and maintenance.

  • Public Safety & Environmental Protection:

    Enacted a ban on public alcohol consumption in designated areas and prohibited throwing objects from Mt. Bonnell. Also initiated a plan to protect the water quality of Town Lake and Lake Austin.

  • Community Investment:

    Allocated over $4.6 million in Community Development Block Grant funds for various activities and began negotiations for leasing the Arthur B. DeWitty Building for community use.
