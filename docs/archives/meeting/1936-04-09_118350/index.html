Austin Debates Parking, Approves New Gas Lines

Thursday, April 9, 1936 regular

  • Parking Meter Debate Heats Up:

    Amid petitions from local merchants, the council postponed installing parking meters, opting instead to send a committee to Dallas to study their effectiveness before making a decision.

  • Centennial Prep & Special Train:

    Officials discussed beautifying Zilker Park for upcoming Centennial visitors. Separately, a special agreement was reached to temporarily block downtown streets for the Rexall Stream-lined Convention Special Train.

  • Good Friday Observance:

    City Hall was approved to close for a half-day on Good Friday, with city employees allowed a full day off with pay for religious observance.

  • Infrastructure & Public Health Projects:

    Permits were issued for the expansion of gas lines across multiple city streets. Additionally, the new Nurses' Home at Brackenridge Hospital was officially completed and accepted, a significant Public Works Administration project.
