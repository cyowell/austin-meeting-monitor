Thursday, January 24, 1918 regular
Transcript
  • Comprehensive new rules were adopted for Oakwood Cemetery, dictating everything from grave decorations and plot transfers to visitor conduct and funeral procedures.
  • A new ordinance passed, establishing a 5% penalty for delinquent municipal tax payments.
  • Half of a fine for public intoxication was remitted for a citizen named Buster Anderson.
  • The City formally documented a long list of refunding bonds from 1901 that had recently been destroyed by burning.

All lots shall be held and used in accordance with the ordinances of the City of Austin and the bylaws and rules and regulations of Oakwood Cemetery and of Oakwood Annex now in force or hereafter adopted ,and lots shall not be used for any purpose than the burial of the human dead. 2. Lot holders shall not allow interments to be made upon their lots for a compensation; nor shall any transfer or assignment of any lot or interest therein be valid without the consent of the Mayor of the City of Austin first had and endorsed upon UNXMM such transfer or assignment and duly entered of record by the City Assessor & Collector of this City. 3. The corners of each lot will be permanently marked by the City of Austin. 4. No lot or grave shall be decorated by its owner, or others interested therein, with any tree, shrub, flower or plant, or the trees or scrubs disturbed in any way without first obtaining the consent of the Supt of Parks and Public property. This does not ,however, include the placing of cut flowers on the graves. 6. No lot shall be filled above the established grade ,nor shall any construction be allowed which will interfere with the proper care of the lawns. 7. Mounds above graves shall not exceed in height two and one-half inches ,when thoroughly settled. 8. No advertisement of any kind whatever will be permitted within the cemetery. 9. The City of Austin reserves the right to remove from any lot anything that conflicts with therules and regulations. 10. The Supt of Parks and Public Property, through the Cemetery Sexton, has entire charge of the cemetery and he is authorized to enforce all rules, to maintain order, to overlook all workmen ,visitors and drivers and to expell those who disregard his orders.
