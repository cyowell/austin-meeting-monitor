Austin COVID Update: Testing, Nursing Homes, Reopen

Tuesday, April 21, 2020 Austin City Council Work Session
  • City officials provided a comprehensive update on Austin's COVID-19 response, detailing current case numbers, expanded contact tracing, and efforts to boost community testing, particularly in underserved areas.
  • New "enhanced control orders" and "strike teams" were announced to combat significant COVID-19 outbreaks in local nursing homes, which have seen numerous infections and deaths, alongside ongoing support for the city's homeless population.
  • A new online platform (Salesforce) is rolling out to streamline public COVID-19 testing registration and speed up result delivery. Officials also issued a strong warning against fraudulent, inaccurate, and expensive antibody tests.
  • The city launched the RISE financial assistance program to help residents and non-profits impacted by the pandemic. Discussions began on a cautious reopening plan, emphasizing continued vigilance with social distancing and masks despite the Governor's relaxed retail and state park guidelines.
