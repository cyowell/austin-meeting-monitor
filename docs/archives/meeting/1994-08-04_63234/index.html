Austin's Future: Energy, Growth, and Housing

Thursday, August 4, 1994 regular

  • Energy and Utility Policy:

    The city committed to reducing operations at the Holly Street Power Plant, exploring new energy resources, and aggressively pursuing ways to curb proposed electric rate increases. New policies also removed restrictions on Municipal Utility Districts (MUDs) regarding utility rates and annexation surcharges.

  • East Austin Economic Development:

    A resolution passed to encourage economic development in East Austin, with a strong emphasis on a "bottoms-up, grassroots" approach to ensure genuine community involvement.

  • Affordable Housing Discussion:

    A public hearing was held to discuss the City's potential participation in Advanced Micro Devices' (AMD) proposal for affordable housing development.

  • Youth Safety Zones:

    New "Drug-Free Zones" were established around schools, playgrounds, youth centers, and public pools to support crime prevention programs.

  • Future Downtown Infrastructure:

    The City Manager was directed to investigate the feasibility of implementing a district cooling and heating system in the downtown area.
