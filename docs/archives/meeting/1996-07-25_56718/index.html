Austin's Future: Broadband & Climate Action

Thursday, July 25, 1996 regular
  • Voted to create a plan targeting a 20% reduction in greenhouse gas emissions below 1990 levels by 2010.
  • Advanced an ordinance for a franchise to build and operate a city-wide broadband telecommunications network.
  • Approved a significant construction contract for the extensive reconstruction and utility replacement of Lavaca Street.
  • Renamed the Pan American Recreation Center to Oswaldo "A.B." Cantu/Pan American Recreation Center.
