Austin's Early Steps: Fire, Schools, Water

Thursday, March 4, 1926 regular

  • New Fire Engine Acquired

    The city authorized the purchase of a 750-gallon pumping engine to strengthen fire protection services.

  • School District Expansion

    City limits were extended specifically for school purposes, allowing for tax collection in newly included territories to fund education.

  • Waste Water Survey Initiated

    A contract was approved for a waste water survey, aiming to identify and reduce water loss across the city.

  • Gasoline Storage Review

    Plans for installing underground gasoline storage tanks on South Congress Avenue were referred to the Safety Committee for examination.
