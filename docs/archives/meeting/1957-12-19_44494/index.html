Austin Expands, Invests, and Plans for Future Growth

Thursday, December 19, 1957 regular
  • Authorized selling $8.75 million in bonds to fund major city improvements across airport, parks, streets, and utility systems.
  • Planned for a five-foot lake lowering in January 1958 to allow residents to perform shoreline work and build piers.
  • Initiated discussions on a new paving policy, proposing property owner contributions for street improvements and exploring new financing methods.
  • Approved multiple land annexations totaling over 45 acres, a commercial zoning change, and a new drive-in gasoline station.
