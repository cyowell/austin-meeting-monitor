Austin's Poverty War, Lakefront, & Texas Dome

Thursday, August 26, 1965 regular

  • Poverty War Progress:

    Austin reported leading other cities in the federal War on Poverty, with new Day Care, Information, and Dental Centers scheduled to open the following month.

  • New Lake Austin Construction Rules:

    An ordinance was approved to allow certain businesses, enterprises, and living quarters to extend over Lake Austin through special permits, facilitating projects like the Maurice Doke lake development.

  • Downtown Stadium Debate:

    Citizens advocated against moving the University stadium from downtown and proposed a massive "Texas Dome" to preserve economic benefits, while the council decided not to intervene in University decisions.

  • Public Budget Hearing Set:

    A public hearing for the city's 1965-1966 annual budget was scheduled for September 9th.
