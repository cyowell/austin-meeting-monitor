Austin: Soccer Dreams, Green Plans, Big Projects

Thursday, March 6, 2014 Austin City Council Regular Meeting

  • Exploring a Major League Soccer Team:

    Directed city staff to research the feasibility of bringing a professional MLS franchise to Austin, alongside planning for crucial I-35 corridor improvements.

  • Boosting Green Initiatives & Energy Future:

    Established a task force to update Austin Energy's climate protection plan, adopted a city-wide Urban Forest Plan, and incentivized a new hotel to generate solar power.

  • Investing in Parks and Infrastructure:

    Approved over $8 million for major Shoal Creek Greenbelt restoration and stream improvements, along with over $7 million for airport taxiway expansion.

  • Essential Services & Regional Collaboration:

    Secured a $1.7 million share for regional wastewater system upgrades with neighboring cities and funded emergency medical transport and various public health programs.
