Austin's Power Future, Land for EMS, Bond Plans

Wednesday, October 15, 1997

  • Major Energy Investments:

    Significant contracts were approved for the Electric Utility, including multi-million dollar agreements for decommissioning the Seaholm Power Plant (addressing asbestos and lead) and securing a long-term, multi-million dollar natural gas supply for city power plants.

  • Nuclear Operations Shift:

    The city authorized the formation of a new entity, the STP Nuclear Operating Company, to take over as the licensed operator for the South Texas Project Electric Generating Station.

  • Critical Infrastructure & Services:

    Funds were allocated for essential city projects, such as constructing a new landfill liner, building a water quality pond, and acquiring land for a future Emergency Medical Service facility.

  • Planning for Future Growth:

    A briefing was held to discuss the public participation process for upcoming bond elections, outlining how residents will engage in future city investment decisions.
