Austin's Future: Solar Power, Housing, & Community

Thursday, March 27, 2014 Austin City Council Regular Meeting

  • Major Solar Power Boost:

    Committed to a 25-year, $525 million agreement for up to 150 megawatts of solar-generated electricity.

  • Smart Housing & Transit:

    Began reducing parking requirements for small apartments near transit lines to encourage density and affordability.

  • Supporting Vulnerable Residents:

    Authorized a partnership with Travis County for a new emergency shelter for homeless women and children.

  • Assessing SXSW Impact:

    Directed a comprehensive post-event evaluation of the South by Southwest festival, gathering public and commission feedback.

  • Future Water Security:

    Initiated analysis of costs and timelines for critical short- and long-term water infrastructure projects.
