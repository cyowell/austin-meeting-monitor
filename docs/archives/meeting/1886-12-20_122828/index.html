Austin 1886: Water & Infrastructure Plans

Monday, December 20, 1886 regular

• Water Infrastructure: Council approved placement of a water trough at Blanco and West Twelfth streets after residents petitioned for one, with instructions to install it immediately.

• City Survey Funding Debate: Two competing proposals were introduced to fund a topographical survey of Austin (one for $500, another for $3,000), referred to a special committee for further consideration.

• Syndicate Bridge Safety: New ordinance passed prohibiting pedestrians from crossing railroad bridges on East College and East avenues, with plans to post warning signs at the city's expense.

• Monthly City Operations: Reports showed solid financial position ($13,415 total receipts in November), 73 arrests made by police, and good overall public health with only 4 deaths and 18 scarlet fever cases reported.

• Staff Continuity: Council extended employment terms for key city positions including police sergeant, hospital physicians, and firefighters through the 1887 elections.
