Austin: Radio Waves, Tax Fairness, Street Rules

Thursday, June 8, 1922 regular

  • Pioneering Technology:

    Granted permission to the Texas Radio Corporation to build a radio station at 206 East 5th Street.

  • Modernizing Taxation:

    Approved a contract to implement a "unit system" for equalizing real estate values, aiming for fairer property tax assessments.

  • Street and Commerce Rules:

    Began discussions on new regulations for vehicles carrying passengers or freight for hire, outlining where they could operate and stand on city streets.

  • Urban Infrastructure:

    Heard proposals from the Chamber of Commerce regarding the widening of West 6th Street at the I&GN Railway bridge.
