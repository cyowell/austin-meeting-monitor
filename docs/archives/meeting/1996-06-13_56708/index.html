Thursday, June 13, 1996 regular
Transcript

  • Major Economic Incentives Approved:

    The city moved to boost the local economy by approving a $1.5 million fee waiver for the new Samsung semiconductor facility and providing over $240,000 in energy rebates for high-efficiency upgrades at apartments and a large tech company.

  • Enhanced Environmental and Development Oversight:

    New water quality regulations were adopted, and the Land Development Code was updated to establish an environmental review division and officer. Additionally, a significant planned development (PUD) was approved with conditions including the dedication of a 181-acre nature preserve.

  • Key City Services and Community Initiatives Advanced:

    A new cable television franchise generating $2.6 million in revenue was finalized, along with agreements for public access channels. Funds were also allocated for youth employment programs, HIV housing services, downtown revitalization efforts ("First Tuesday Downtown"), and a study to expand the popular Town Lake Trail.
