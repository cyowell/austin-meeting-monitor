Austin's Wartime Development & Land Sales

Thursday, March 15, 1945 regular

  • New Gas Line Construction:

    Approved plans for Texas Public Service Company to install new gas mains on several city streets, including East 12th, Holly, and Riverview.

  • Ridgetop Annex Re-Zoned:

    Changed zoning for properties on East 47th Street from residential to commercial, allowing for new business development in the area.

  • City Sells Land:

    Authorized the sale of two city-owned lots to private citizens through installment payment contracts.
