Austin Growth: Utility Bonds, Taxi Sale, Development Future

Thursday, June 1, 1995 regular

  • Massive Utility Refinancing:

    The Council approved the issuance of up to $151.77 million in revenue refunding bonds for the city's combined utility systems.

  • Taxi Service Changes & Public Safety Investments:

    The Yellow Checker Cab Company franchise sale to Greater Austin Transportation Company was approved, alongside significant contracts for new fire department hazardous materials and emergency rescue vehicles.

  • Major Redevelopment & Future Planning:

    Discussions began on the advisory board structure for the Robert Mueller Municipal Airport redevelopment, and public hearings were scheduled for the East 11th and 12th Street Revitalization projects and new floodplain electrical installation rules.

  • Community & Cultural Initiatives:

    Funds were allocated for elderly housing under a community grant program, support was voiced for juvenile justice system reforms, and June was officially declared "Jazz Month" in Austin.
