Thursday, January 21, 1943 regular

The budget for 1943 represents strictly an operating and maintenance budget since no permanent improvements can be made due to the war effort and the resultant inability to secure materials to carry on such improvements. You will note that the anticipated expenditures for the year correspond in a very large measure with the actual expenditures of the past year, 1942.
