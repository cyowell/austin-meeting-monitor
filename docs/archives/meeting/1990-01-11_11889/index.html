Austin's Environment, Bills, and Growth

Thursday, January 11, 1990 regular

  • Residents Received Financial Relief:

    Approved a refund of electric utility "windfall profits" and allowed extended payment options for December's utility bills. Wastewater billing was also adjusted to exclude December from winter averaging.

  • Major Environmental Initiatives Advanced:

    Significant funding was allocated to the recycling program, with new policies adopted for purchasing recycled goods. The city also established goals for protecting the ozone layer and affirmed support for the Texas Disposal System Landfill permit.

  • Key Infrastructure & Development Projects Progressed:

    Authorized land acquisition for the US 183/290 project, approved artist contracts for the Convention Center, and funded initial engineering for an 18-hole expansion at Jimmy Clay Golf Course.

  • Public Input and Future Policy Deliberations:

    Heard citizen concerns on sexually oriented business ordinances and postponed major decisions, including the full adoption of "AustinPlan" and setting a public hearing on new development fees (which included proposed discounts for affordable housing).
