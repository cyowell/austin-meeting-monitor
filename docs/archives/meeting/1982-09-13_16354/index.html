Austin's Future Funded: Utility & Public Works

Monday, September 13, 1982 special called
  • The Austin City Council met to officially certify the results of a special election held on September 11, 1982.
  • Voters overwhelmingly approved all 25 bond propositions, signaling a massive public investment in the city's future infrastructure.
  • The approved bonds, totaling hundreds of millions of dollars, are earmarked for critical upgrades across Austin, including electric, water, and wastewater systems, new and improved parks, street and drainage projects, emergency services, library facilities, and public parking.
