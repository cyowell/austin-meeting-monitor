Apple's Austin Expansion: Jobs, Costs, Community

Thursday, March 15, 2012 Austin City Council Special Called Meeting

  • Massive Apple Campus Proposed:

    Austin is considering a proposal from Apple to build a 1 million square foot "Americas Operations Center," investing $282 million.

  • Thousands of New Jobs:

    The project is projected to create 3,635 new jobs over 10 years, with an average annual wage of nearly $64,000, and retain 3,100 existing Apple employees.

  • City Incentives & Fiscal Impact:

    Austin proposes an $8.6 million property tax rebate over 10 years, with city staff estimating a net benefit of $6 million to the city.

  • Public & Council Concerns:

    Questions were raised regarding construction worker safety and wages, potential partnerships with local community colleges, and Apple's charitable giving practices within the community.

  • Decision Soon:

    A public hearing for final feedback and a decision on this significant economic development deal is scheduled for March 22nd.
