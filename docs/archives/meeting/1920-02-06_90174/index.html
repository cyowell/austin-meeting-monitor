Austin's New Sidewalks, Paved Alley & Budget OK

Friday, February 6, 1920 special called
  • City leaders officially approved the 1920 annual budget.
  • Property owners on multiple key streets, including North Congress Avenue, Rio Grande, and Guadalupe, were mandated to construct new 4.5-foot cement sidewalks.
  • An alley between 4th and 5th Streets was ordered paved with concrete, with the cost to be borne by adjacent property owners.

In the matter of assessment of Mrs. Marie Gross Estate, the Board of equalization erroneously increased the value of Improvements on the W 2/3 of lot 6 in Block 87 from $900 to $1500. We lost sight of the age of said improvements and failed them to conform to improvement of recent construction on lot in the immediate neighborhood. James Belger, Pres. Board.
