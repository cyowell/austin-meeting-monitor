Austin Priorities: Growth, Diversity, & City Services

Thursday, August 7, 1980 regular

  • Advanced Diversity & Inclusion:

    Adopted a comprehensive Affirmative Action Plan to increase hiring of handicapped, female, and minority individuals in city roles, establishing a citizen-staff oversight committee.

  • Managed Rapid Southwest Growth:

    Chose against an immediate subdivision moratorium near the Edwards Aquifer, instead funding $307,000 for urgent water service upgrades and creating a task force for a 5-year development plan.

  • Shaped Urban Landscape:

    Approved major downtown street modifications for a new bank, zoned the White-Springfield House for historic office use to encourage preservation, and moved to acquire land for McCarty Lane District Park.

  • Addressed Public Service Costs & Concerns:

    Scheduled public hearings on upcoming water, sewer, and sanitation rate increases, amidst strong public criticism regarding proposed public transportation fare hikes and service cuts.

  • Debated Specific Zoning Amendments:

    Set a public hearing to discuss amending zoning rules to specifically exclude "nude modeling studios" from "O" Office Districts.
