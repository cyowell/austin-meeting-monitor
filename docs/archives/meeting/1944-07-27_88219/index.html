Austin Secures Hospital Funds, Plans River Dam

Thursday, July 27, 1944 regular

  • Secured Federal Funds for Hospital Expansion:

    Accepted a $30,500 federal grant towards a $61,000 project to construct a Hospital Nurses Home and Training Facilities.

  • Planned River Development and Beautification:

    Scheduled a joint meeting to discuss the "Low Water Dam project" and plans to beautify the banks of the Colorado River.

  • Authorized City-Wide Gas Line Expansion:

    Approved permits for Texas Public Service Company to lay new gas mains across several city streets.

  • Granted Property Tax Forgiveness:

    Remitted penalties and half of the accrued interest on delinquent property taxes for two individual properties.
