Thursday, August 19, 1999 regular
Transcript

  • Protecting Water Quality & Development:

    New, stricter rules were approved for impervious cover and critical water quality zones in rural and suburban watersheds, directly impacting future development and environmental protection.

  • City Finances & Public Funds:

    Public hearings were scheduled for the proposed annual tax rate, and significant bond sales were approved to fund various city infrastructure improvements.

  • Securing Future Water Supply:

    A public hearing was set to discuss a major agreement to substantially increase Austin's firm water supply from the Lower Colorado River Authority.

  • Community & Youth Initiatives:

    Funding was committed for a new multipurpose community center/school project in partnership with the local school district, and juvenile day and nighttime curfews were extended.
