Austin Wartime Agendas: Airport, Rights, & Rates

Thursday, February 12, 1942 regular

  • Airport Bond Vote:

    An election was authorized for $600,000 in bonds to acquire and develop land for a new city airport.

  • Wartime Economic Adjustments:

    Planned electric rate reductions were deferred due to increased war-related costs, though a reserve fund was established for potential future consumer refunds.

  • Anti-Discrimination Policy:

    A "No Discrimination" clause, explicitly covering race, creed, color, or national origin, was added to a contract with a major corporation.

  • New Bus Service:

    A part-time bus route was approved for West Austin to the University area, largely in response to the growing "tire situation" during the war.
