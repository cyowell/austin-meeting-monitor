  • City Cultural Centers Face Widespread Issues:

    An audit revealed significant operational challenges at Austin's city-managed cultural centers (including Mexican-American and Asian-American centers), citing inadequate maintenance, underutilized spaces, inconsistent fees, and a lack of strategic planning and community input.

  • Millennium Youth Complex in Crisis:
