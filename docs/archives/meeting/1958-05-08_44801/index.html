Austin Debates Streets, Adopts New Rules

Thursday, May 8, 1958 regular

  • Controversial Street Closures:

    Despite significant public opposition from residents and professionals, a portion of Sabine Street and an adjacent alley were permanently closed.

  • New Business Regulations:

    The city adopted an ordinance to regulate massage parlors and scheduled a public hearing to consider new rules for itinerant vendors.

  • Neighborhood Health and Safety Concerns:

    Residents voiced concerns over severe drainage issues, open sewage, and traffic hazards in several areas, prompting a council inspection and planned improvements.

  • Key Infrastructure Projects Approved:

    Contracts were awarded for major upgrades, including paving West 1st Street and installing a new sprinkler system in East District Park.
