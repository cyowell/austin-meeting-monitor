Austin Curbs Street Gas Pumps

Wednesday, November 26, 1924 regular
  • Approved permits for two new gasoline filling stations at separate city intersections, reflecting the era's growing car use.
  • Passed a major ordinance banning all gasoline pumps and storage tanks from city sidewalks, streets, and public places starting April 1, 1925, revoking prior permissions.
  • Confirmed permanent appointments for key staff in the Tax Assessor and Collector's office due to a recent vacancy.
  • Authorized a payment of over $1,400 to the Travis County District Clerk for accrued court costs in city tax lawsuits.
