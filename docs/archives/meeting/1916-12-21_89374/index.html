City Debt, Gas Pump Rules & Christmas Clemency

Thursday, December 21, 1916 regular

  • Abolished City Debt

    The city officially wrote off nearly $97,000 owed to its Water, Light, and Power Department for services like street lighting and fire hydrants, citing financial strain.

  • Modernizing Roads & Rail

    Approved significant track changes for the Great Northern Railway on West 4th Street and ordered property owners on Lavaca St. and N. Congress Ave. to construct new cement sidewalks.

  • Regulating Early Gas Pumps

    Issued a detailed permit for a new gasoline pump, specifying requirements like a mandatory nightly illuminated electric light and proper hose storage at the curb.

  • Holiday Spirit & Budget Moves

    As a "Christmas Gift," fines were remitted for several individuals. The city also authorized $170,000 in refunding bonds and adjusted street department budgets.

The Mayor laid before the Council. Dec 2th 1916: ALL present: Whereas, by resolution adopted by the City Council of the City of Austin on the 12th day of April, 1909, the City of Austin agreed to pay the Water, Light and Power department the sum of $ 2,002.00 per month for Fire Hydrants, Flushing gutters sprinkling streets, water troughs, Parks and Public Buildings and lights for Public Buildings and streets, which sum was the price previously paid for such City acct. with service out of the general revenue, and W. L. & P. Dept. Whereas, the City of Austin has been unable to pay the greater part of the amount agreed to, and there has accumulated and is now due for such service the sum of $ 96,608.92; and Whereas, it will not be practicable for the City to pay said amount out of the general revenue without greatly crippling the other departments of the City. Now therefore, Be it resolved by the City Council of the City of Austin: That the Supt of Streets and Public property be authorized to draw from the books of the Water, Light and Power dept the said sum of $96,608.92, and that until further notice, the charge for the above mentioned service be made monthly as heretofore. The resolution was adopted by the following vote: Yeas Mayor Wooldridge, Councilmen Anthony, Bartholomew, Haynes & Powell 5 Nays none, Approved, A P Wooldridge, Mayor. A - P Wooldridge Jno. W. Johnson, City Clerk
