Millions for Austin Housing Programs Approved

Thursday, September 10, 2015 Austin Housing Finance Corporation (AHFC) Board of Directors AHFC Meeting
  • The Austin Housing Finance Corporation authorized crucial funding for the city's housing initiatives.
  • This included approving the operating and capital budgets, allowing the Neighborhood Housing Community Development department to administer various housing programs.
  • Multiple contracts for housing services were also greenlit, following the budget approvals.
  • Discussions clarified a substantial $17 million fund, comprised of federal, local, and capital contributions, dedicated to housing assistance.
