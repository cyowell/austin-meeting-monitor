Austin's Budget, Police, Arts, Housing & Jazz

Tuesday, September 12, 2000 Austin City Council Regular Meeting

Here's a summary of the Austin City Council meeting:

  • Fiscal Year Budget & Taxes Finalized:

    The city approved its annual operating and capital budgets, setting property tax rates for the upcoming fiscal year. Key adjustments included increased funding for mental health, libraries, and emergency management.

  • Major Bond Allocation Failed:

    A significant proposal to allocate funds from a $150 million bond package for projects like affordable housing, parks, and infrastructure did not pass, highlighting council divisions.

  • Housing & Cultural Arts Boost:

    Over $12 million in federal housing grants were accepted, bolstering the Austin Housing Finance Corporation's $16 million budget for city housing programs. Additionally, more than $3.7 million was dedicated to cultural arts, establishing a new music loan program.

  • Police Contract & Event Support:

    The city extended its "Meet and Confer Agreement" with the Austin Police Association. Local community events also received support, with fees waived for the East Side Jazz and Blues Festival.
