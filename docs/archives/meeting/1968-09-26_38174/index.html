Austin Expands; Model Cities & Land Sales

Thursday, September 26, 1968 regular

  • Austin Expands Borders & Solidifies Land Deals:

    The city approved the annexation of over 370 acres and finalized a significant land-sharing agreement with neighboring Sunset Valley, shaping future development.

  • New HQ for Model Neighborhood Program:

    Official office space was secured on East 6th Street, kicking off the city's participation in the federal Model Cities urban revitalization initiative.

  • Plan to Sell Surplus City Properties:

    A strategy was adopted to auction off or offer various city-owned surplus lands, with some tracts undergoing re-zoning for potential multi-family development.

  • Ongoing Urban Renewal Efforts:

    Progress continued with the sale of a parcel in the Kealing Urban Renewal Project and multiple houses from the Glen Oaks Project.
