City Recovers Dam, Boosts Infrastructure

Friday, December 1, 1933 regular

  • City Reclaims Dam Control:

    The Council rejected a proposal from Austin Dam, Inc. to continue their work, instead voting to immediately terminate their franchise and take full possession of the city dam, power house, and all related hydro-electric plant properties.

  • Gas Line Expansion Approved:

    A resolution passed allowing the Texas Public Service Company to construct new gas mains in Academy Drive, adding to the city's utility infrastructure.

  • Telephone Pole Installation:

    Southwestern Bell Telephone Company received approval to build new telephone pole lines in Haskell Street Alley and East Thirteenth Street.
