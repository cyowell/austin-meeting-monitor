AHFC Boosts Housing Funds and Tenant Help

Thursday, April 20, 2023 Austin Housing Finance Corporation (AHFC) Board of Directors Regular Meeting

  • More Funding for Housing:

    Approved utilizing 2022 voter-approved bonds and Project Connect funds to provide loans for new housing developments.

  • Tenant Relocation & Housing Aid:

    A $700,000 contract was approved to assist residents with relocation and finding new housing.

  • Future Public Discussion:

    A public hearing is scheduled to amend a bond agreement for the Los Portales del Guerrero housing project in District 3.
