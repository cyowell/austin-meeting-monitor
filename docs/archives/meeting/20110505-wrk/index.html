Driving Change: Austin's Budget Focus

Thursday, May 5, 2011 Austin City Council Budget Work Session

  • No immediate fee increases

    for drainage or transportation services this year, though future hikes for water, energy, and drainage are anticipated alongside projected property tax increases.

  • Major improvements in road conditions

    are underway, with more durable street overlays replacing less popular seal coating, aiming for 90% of city roads to be rated "satisfactory or better."

  • Watershed Protection faces growing challenges

    , including extensive erosion problems across 112,000 feet of waterways and the need to inspect 270 dams, pushing for more stable, dedicated utility funding over bonds.

  • New parking meter technology has been a huge success

    , dramatically reducing public complaints by over 90% since 2009 and improving revenue collection in downtown areas.
