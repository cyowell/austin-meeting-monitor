Thursday, July 30, 2015 Austin City Council Budget Work Session
Official City Record Agenda Watch Video Transcript

  • Homeowner Relief & Affordability Focus:

    The budget includes a new 6% general homestead exemption, providing an estimated $7.3 million in tax savings for qualified homeowners, alongside an additional $740,000 for tenant-based rental assistance programs.

  • Expanding Services for a Growing City:

    To address Austin's rapid growth, 347 new city positions are proposed, including 85 police officers, 19 911 call takers, 48 staff for the new central library, and 27 for development services.

  • Investments in Mobility & Quality of Life:

    Significant funding is allocated for infrastructure improvements, including $47.7 million for streets, bridges, urban trails, and bike/ped improvements, plus investments in parks and libraries.

  • Employee Compensation & Benefits:

    City employees will see pay increases (3% for civilian, 1% for police/EMS, 2.5% for firefighters), mid-year market adjustments for underpaid civilian jobs, and enhanced health benefits.
