Thursday, November 6, 1958 regular
Transcript
  • Doctors raised concerns about one-way streets hindering emergency access to Seton Hospital, with the Mayor proposing a new hospital drive from Rio Grande to improve vehicle flow.
  • Public input was heard on proposed zoning changes for the new Auditorium's vicinity, affecting building heights and uses, with many expressing opposition. Separately, an ordinance was passed allowing barbers and beauticians in office and residence zones.
  • The city approved significant contracts for major infrastructure projects, including new pavement for West Riverside Drive, storm sewers, and multiple component purchases for the Holly Street Power Station's new Unit 1.
  • Plans were finalized for the new Auditorium's grand opening, outlining three days of free public access, interfaith dedication services, and setting initial rental rates for local organizations.
