Austin Secures Funds for Power & Nuclear Energy

Tuesday, November 9, 1976 special called
  • Austin authorized $44 million in revenue bonds for significant infrastructure investments.
  • A notable $16 million portion of these funds is dedicated to Austin's 16% stake in the upcoming South Texas Nuclear Project.
  • An additional $28 million is allocated for extending and improving Austin's existing Electric Light and Power System.
  • Officials celebrated securing a favorable 5.81% effective interest rate on the bonds, marking the best financial deal for Austin in nearly three years.
