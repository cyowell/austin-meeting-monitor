Zilker Pool, Music Boost, Business Permits

Wednesday, November 21, 1945 regular

  • Zilker Springs Bathhouse Gets Green Light:

    Following public input and a petition to ensure original plans were kept, the city approved two significant contracts for a new bathhouse at Zilker Springs Pool, covering both plumbing/electrical ($21,153) and general construction (cost plus $10,000).

  • Summer Concerts Eye Expansion:

    The Austin Musician's Union requested a substantial budget increase (from $2,000 to $5,000) for 1946 to expand summer concerts from 12 to 15 events and nearly double the number of participating musicians.

  • Business Operations and Taxi Regulations:

    New wine and beer licenses were granted for the Delux Hotel and LaRumba Cafe. Additionally, a taxi driver's permit was revoked due to a "very bad traffic record," with another granted conditionally.

The Mayor assured Mr. Parkinson that consideration would be given his request when the 1946 budget for the Recreation Department was made up, and, if it was found possible to make this increase, it would be done. Mr. Richard Pryor appeared before the Council and presented a petition from a number of regular swimmers using Zilker Springs Pool requesting that the City clean and repaint the present bathhouse at the pool and use same until such time as a new bathhouse could be built without reducing the size of the proposed plans or cutting anything out of the project. After the Mayor explained that the City did not intend to cut anything out of the original plans for the bathhouse, Mr. Pryor withdrew his petition and said that he knew all of the signers would be very happy to have the new bathhouse. The Zilker Springs Bathhouse Committee from the Chamber of Commerce wase present and after hearing the Mayor state that the bathhouse would be build according to the original plans and specifications expressed their appreciation of the Council's decision.
