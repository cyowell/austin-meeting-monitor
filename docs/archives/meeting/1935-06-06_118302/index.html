Austin: New Sewers, Gas, Roads & City Funds

Thursday, June 6, 1935 regular

  • Infrastructure Growth:

    Approved new gas mains in Wethersfield Road, Nellie Street, and South Fifth Street, alongside the final acceptance of a major Public Works Administration (PWA) sewer line project.

  • Urban Development & Land Use:

    Authorized a curb setback on East 1st Street to improve traffic flow, permanently closed several long-unused alleys for private development, and moved forward with a zoning change on Rio Grande Street to allow for denser residential use.

  • City Finance & Governance:

    Elected a new City Treasurer and established rigorous conditions for Austin's official bank depositories, including significant collateral requirements for public funds.

  • Taxi Driver Regulation:

    Revoked permits for two taxicab drivers following hearings, while also approving several new and conditional permits for other operators.
