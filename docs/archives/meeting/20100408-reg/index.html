Pregnancy Center Rules, Energy Goals, Festival Funds

Thursday, April 8, 2010 Austin City Council Regular Meeting

Here's a summary of the Austin City Council meeting:

  • New Pregnancy Center Disclosure Rules Passed

    The council approved an ordinance requiring "limited pregnancy service centers" to clearly display signs stating they do not provide or refer for abortions or birth control, sparking debate over consumer information and religious freedom.

  • Austin Energy's 2020 Green Plan Briefed

    Officials presented an updated generation plan aiming for 35% renewable energy, 200 megawatts of solar, and a 20% carbon reduction by 2020, with discussions on potential rate increases and the plan's flexibility given changing energy forecasts.

  • Festival Funding Debate for East Austin

    A public hearing addressed a $45,000 grant for the Fiesta Patrias/Pachanga music festival, drawing support for its cultural contributions and community benefits, but facing opposition over concerns about event size, parking impacts on residents, and the appropriate use of mitigation funds.
