Austin Relief Plan: Homelessness, Kids, Evictions

Tuesday, May 18, 2021 Austin City Council Work Session

Here's a summary of the Austin City Council work session agenda:

  • The city discussed the allocation of $188.8 million in federal American Rescue Plan (ARP) funds, with a staff proposal recommending $84 million for homelessness initiatives.
  • Council members voiced strong concerns about the proposed homelessness funding, emphasizing the need for more detailed plans and commitments from regional partners (like Travis and Williamson counties) and private philanthropy.
  • Other key spending priorities debated included early childhood education (specifically the "Success by Six" program), workforce development (including potential funding for an Austin Civilian Conservation Corps), and relief for the cultural arts sector.
  • Many council members highlighted an urgent need for increased funding for rental assistance and eviction prevention programs, anticipating a potential crisis as eviction moratoriums are phased out.
  • There was a consensus to delay a final vote on the ARP framework, with calls for more detailed financial breakdowns and deeper discussion on how these investments will create "transformational change" for the community.
