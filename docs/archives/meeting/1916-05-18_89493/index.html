Austin's Early Transit & Urban Plans

Thursday, May 18, 1916 regular

  • Jitney Boom & Regulation:

    Six new licenses were granted for Ford jitneys (early shared taxis), establishing detailed routes from downtown to 31st Street, operating on fixed schedules and requiring financial bonds.

  • Flexible Transit Routes:

    Jitney services saw a temporary rerouting due to a bridge closure on Speedway at 29th Street, adjusting northern termini and adding downtown segments to maintain service during construction.

  • Urban Development & Land Use:

    The council passed an ordinance to vacate and dedicate streets in a re-subdivision of land near Pease Park, indicating active city planning and growth.

  • Investing in Public Safety:

    Significant personnel appointments were made across multiple Fire Department companies, including captains and hosemen, alongside appropriations for new equipment like a life net and fire horses.

  • Financial Adjustments & Citizen Redress:

    A large $1,586 property assessment declared in error was cancelled, and smaller refunds were approved for overpaid taxes, including compensation for a calf killed by a live wire.
