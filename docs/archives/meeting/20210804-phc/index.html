Austin's Future on Homelessness & Kids' Health

Wednesday, August 4, 2021 Public Health Committee Regular Meeting

  • Healthy Kids' Meals Proposed:

    Advocates urged a policy to make water or unflavored milk the default drink in kids' restaurant meals to promote child health and reduce sugary drink consumption.

  • Homeless Housing Wins:

    The city announced securing new permanent supportive housing units and successfully transitioning 268 individuals from protective lodging into permanent homes.

  • Encampment Relocation Success:

    The HEAL initiative relocated approximately 70 people from two major encampments into bridge shelters, connecting them with rapid rehousing services.

  • Designated Camps Debate:

    Discussion highlighted significant hurdles for city-run designated homeless encampments, including rezoning, community opposition, and high operational costs, with exploration of private partnerships suggested.

  • Mental Health Focus:

    Council members emphasized the critical need for a comprehensive mental health strategy and enhanced services as a cornerstone of the city's overall response to homelessness.
