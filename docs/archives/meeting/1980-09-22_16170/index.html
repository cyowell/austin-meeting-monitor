Austin Debates New Fees & Gas Rate Changes

Monday, September 22, 1980 special called

  • Public Hearing on City Fee Increases:

    A major public hearing was held to consider a broad range of proposed fee increases for the upcoming fiscal year, impacting services such as parks, golf courses, swimming pools, libraries, construction permits, and various utility-related charges. Numerous residents voiced opposition, expressing concerns about affordability and the extent of the increases.

  • Gas Rate Change Decision Delayed:

    The city decided to postpone for one week the timetable for considering Southern Union Gas Company's proposed rate adjustments.

  • Aquifer Development Moratorium Deferred:

    A planned vote on establishing a temporary 30-day moratorium for certain subdivision applications within the Williamson Creek Watershed Aquifer Recharge Zone was put off until a later agenda.
