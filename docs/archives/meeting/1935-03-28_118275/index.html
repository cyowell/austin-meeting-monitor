Austin: Park Land, Relief Work, Local Taxes

Thursday, March 28, 1935 regular

  • Parks & Public Works:

    Approved a land exchange to straighten Shoal Creek Park boundaries and accepted over $1,000 in former relief funds to be used for a work program to beautify city parks.

  • Tax Adjustments & Deadlines:

    Granted a tax assessment reduction for a major local merchandise store and extended the 1935 tax payment deadline by three days, allowing a grace period without interest.

  • Civic Services & Regulation:

    Renamed "Sidon Street" to "Liberty Street" to resolve public confusion and denied a new taxicab permit application due to the applicant's non-residency and sufficient existing taxi services.
