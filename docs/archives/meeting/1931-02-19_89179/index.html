City Debates Job Rules, Funds Infrastructure, Leases Land

Thursday, February 19, 1931 regular
  • A resolution proposing a "hire local" and "buy local" policy for city employees and contractors, including a 37.5 cents/hour minimum wage for common labor, was rejected.
  • Over $100,000 in contracts were awarded for significant infrastructure projects, including new bridges and storm sewers across the city.
  • A public hearing on property assessments for planned alley paving improvements saw resident protests, leading to a one-week postponement.
  • City property was leased for 10 years to establish new commercial stockyards, with the city receiving a percentage of revenue to support the municipal abattoir.
