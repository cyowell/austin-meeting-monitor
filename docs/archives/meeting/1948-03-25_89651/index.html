Austin's Coliseum Vision and Zoning Debates

Thursday, March 25, 1948 regular

  • Coliseum Vision Set:

    Council prioritized a new "all-purpose" coliseum for livestock exhibitions, rather than basketball or orchestras, but allowed for other public meetings.

  • Hot Zoning Debates:

    The Zoning Board recommended against several controversial changes, including allowing beer sales near the University of Texas campus and churches, and building apartments in a high-class residential area. The Council scheduled public hearings for these contentious proposals.

  • Infrastructure & Safety Upgrades:

    Approved a major railroad crossing protection project, updated city traffic signal and one-way street regulations, and initiated repairs for a dilapidated bridge on Riverside Drive.

  • Community & Property Matters:

    Addressed property tax appeal policies, granted permits for new gas mains, and greenlit a "Clean-up, Paint-up, Fix-up Week" proclamation and a new tennis center.
