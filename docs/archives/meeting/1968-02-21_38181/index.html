Shaping Austin: Lake, Health, and Justice

Wednesday, February 21, 1968 regular

Here's a summary of the Austin City Council's February 21, 1968 agenda:

  • Developing Town Lake:

    The Council advanced plans to beautify Town Lake, approving a new gazebo project and securing initial funding for a comprehensive development plan. They also approved a specific route for new power transmission lines along the lake, despite some public concerns.

  • Protecting Water Quality:

    A new ordinance was passed to strictly regulate sanitation across the city's watershed, addressing treatment plants, septic tanks, and watercraft to safeguard Austin's water supply.

  • Managing City Growth:

    Numerous zoning changes were approved, primarily allowing for denser residential, office, or local retail development. The Council also initiated the annexation of over 240 acres to expand city limits.

  • Reviewing Justice System:

    Amid concerns over vacant judge positions and ensuring fair treatment in the city's Corporation Court, a special meeting was called to investigate current police and court procedures.
