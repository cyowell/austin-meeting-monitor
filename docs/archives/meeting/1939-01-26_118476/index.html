Austin's Future: Zoning, Recreation, Gas, Tax Relief

Thursday, January 26, 1939 regular

Here's a summary of the Austin City Council meeting:

  • Future Growth & Zoning:

    The Council set public hearings for significant zoning changes, including expanding a commercial district on Chestnut Avenue and proposing a new community retail center near Ash Street and Robin Hood Trail, signaling a move away from isolated "spot zoning."

  • Recreation and Utility Expansion:

    Officials approved a strategic land swap to develop new recreational areas for boating near the recently completed Tom Miller Dam, and permitted extensive new gas main installations across various city streets.

  • Tax Relief & Public Service Oversight:

    The Council granted remissions for historical property taxes to several local churches and a private owner, and the City Manager formally protested federal claims of high electric rates in Austin.
