Austin Streets, Sidewalks, and Public Hearings

Thursday, August 20, 1914 regular
  • New city-wide standards were adopted for concrete sidewalks, curbing, and gutters, including a special permit for one business to adjust its sidewalk placement on 6th Street.
  • Major street paving projects were approved for 16th and 9th Streets using "Bitulithic" pavement, with property owners along these routes expected to cover a significant portion of the costs.
  • Public hearings are scheduled for September 3rd for property owners on 16th and 9th Streets to discuss and potentially challenge the proposed assessments for the new street paving.
  • An additional $1,500 was allocated to the East 9th Street filing and grading project, doubling its initial budget to $3,000.
