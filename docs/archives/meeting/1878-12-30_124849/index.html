617 Called Meeting of City Council Austin Texas December 30, 1878 Mayor De Gress presiding Present Aldermen Ergot, Brooker, Mil- lett, Metz, Fann and Strong. 6. Quorum present The object of the Call being stated by the Mayor the following resolution was introduced and unanimously adopted. Be it resolved by the City Council of the City of Austin, That the Mayor be and he is hereby authorized to telegraph to Genl Cogt Commanding the department of Texas to Genl Trevino commanding the Mexican forces on the Rio Grande, Mexico, inviting them and their party to visit the City of Austin; and if they accept this invitation, that the Mayor is further authorized to appoint a Committee of five to escort them to this city. On motion the Council then adjourned. Approved L. M. Bradley. Jany 6 1879 City Clerk
