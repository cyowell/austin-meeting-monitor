Austin: Free Rides, Tax Breaks, and Trailer Troubles

Thursday, June 28, 1945 regular
  • The city approved funding to support a community canning program.
  • Residents petitioned for the complete removal of a trailer camp on East 2nd Street, which was taken under advisement.
  • Permission was granted for free transportation services for delegates attending a convention at the Olivet Baptist Church.
  • The city council remitted penalties and half (or all, in one case) of the accrued interest for several individuals and businesses with long-standing delinquent property taxes.
