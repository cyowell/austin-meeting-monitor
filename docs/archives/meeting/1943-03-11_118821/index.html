Wartime Austin: Red Cross & Cab Drivers

Thursday, March 11, 1943 regular
  • City leaders officially proclaimed March 15 to April 1 as the period for raising the Red Cross War Fund quota for Austin and Travis County, supporting the national wartime effort.
  • Numerous new taxicab driver permits were approved, including applications from several individuals explicitly noted as "colored" in the records.
  • Dozens of existing taxicab driver permits were renewed, indicating a busy and vital local transportation sector during the war.
