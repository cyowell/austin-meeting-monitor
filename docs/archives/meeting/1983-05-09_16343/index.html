Austin Funds Housing, Jobs, Child Care

Monday, May 9, 1983 special called

  • Workforce Development Boost:

    Applied for official designation under the new federal Job Training Partnership Act to enhance local employment and training programs.

  • Major Community Grant Allocation:

    Approved over $7.4 million in Community Development Block Grant (CDBG) funds for upcoming projects, also finalizing and rolling over previous project balances into this new funding cycle.

  • Targeted Investments:

    The CDBG funds were earmarked for critical areas, including constructing new and rental housing, emergency home repairs, modernizing public housing, providing business assistance, accessibility improvements for the disabled, and launching a Child Care Voucher Program.
