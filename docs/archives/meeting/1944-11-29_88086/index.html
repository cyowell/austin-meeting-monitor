Zoning Battle, Utility Growth, Transit Shift

Wednesday, November 29, 1944 regular

  • Beer Sales Zoning Denied:

    Following significant public protest, a request to rezone a grocery store at 1401 Lavaca Street to allow packaged beer sales was rejected. Concerns were raised about setting a "spot zoning" precedent and potentially undermining city regulations on alcoholic beverages.

  • Major Gas Line Expansion:

    Permission was granted for Texas Public Service Company to lay new gas mains across at least ten city streets, including Preston, Bouldin, and Duval Avenues, expanding vital infrastructure.

  • Wartime Transit Route Discontinued:

    The city approved the permanent discontinuation of a specific Austin Transit Company service route, notably affecting transport to the International Minerals and Chemical Corporation's Magnesium Plant.
