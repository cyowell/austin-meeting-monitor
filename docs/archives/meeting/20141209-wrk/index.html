Austin's Future: Energy, Housing, Noise, Transit

Tuesday, December 9, 2014 Austin City Council Work Session

  • Austin Energy's Future Power:

    An independent study will evaluate Austin Energy's plan for new power generation (a gas plant vs. renewables) amid concerns about electricity rates and environmental goals.

  • Funding Social Services:

    A proposal seeks to increase social service funding through a new formula, sparking debate over long-term budgetary flexibility versus meeting critical community needs.

  • City-Wide Noise Management:

    New city-wide noise monitoring technology is planned to better manage sound in entertainment areas and address resident complaints, especially during festivals.

  • Affordable Housing Initiatives:

    Discussions include refining landlord exemptions in an anti-discrimination housing ordinance and exploring an Economic Development Corporation for workforce housing.

  • Post-Rail Transportation Strategy:

    Council debates allocating $20.4 million in leftover urban rail funds to Airport Boulevard improvements versus reserving them for a new city-wide transportation strategy.
