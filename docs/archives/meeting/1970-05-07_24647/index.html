Thursday, May 7, 1970 regular
Transcript

  • Student Protests Spark Debate:

    Amidst national unrest, university students' request for an emergency parade permit, related to the Kent State shootings, was denied after extensive debate, with the city stressing official channels for applications.

  • Push for Child Safety Law:

    A citizen urged a new law banning unattended children in cars, citing risks like suffocation and molestation, and proposed a minimum $100 fine.

  • New Theater on Park Land Reviewed:

    A public hearing was held for the Zachary Scott Theater's proposal to build on city park property, discussing contract provisions and land use before continuing the discussion.

  • Major Sidewalk Safety Initiative:

    The city approved a $200,000 program to construct sidewalks around elementary schools, prioritizing child safety and planning to cover costs without assessing property owners.

  • Cracking Down on Lake Noise:

    Officials addressed numerous complaints about excessive noise and extended hours from boat races and public address systems on Town Lake, calling for stricter enforcement of permits.
