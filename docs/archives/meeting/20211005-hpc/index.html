Tuesday, October 5, 2021 Housing and Planning Committee Regular Meeting
Official City Record Agenda Watch Video Transcript
  • Austin officials explored ways to ease housing affordability by reforming Accessory Dwelling Unit (ADU) regulations, often called "backyard homes."
  • Major barriers to building ADUs include restrictive zoning in many single-family areas, high utility connection fees (potentially tens of thousands of dollars), and current bans on converting garages.
  • Outdated rules also limit who can live in these units, with restrictions based on age (e.g., over 60) or on-site employment, preventing many families from creating intergenerational housing.
  • Discussions focused on streamlining permits, removing restrictive residency requirements, allowing more attached or internal ADUs, and leveraging prefabricated options to lower overall costs.
