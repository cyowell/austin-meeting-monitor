Eminent Domain, Rezoning & Urban Renewal

Thursday, June 20, 1957 regular

  • Property Rights & Infrastructure:

    The city authorized using eminent domain to acquire land for a new sanitary sewer line. Separately, a resident challenged the city's eminent domain process for airport expansion, citing concerns about fair pricing and property selection.

  • Rezoning Spree:

    Council reviewed a wave of zoning requests, primarily seeking to rezone residential properties for local retail, commercial, or office use. While many were approved, one was denied, and others were postponed for on-site inspections by the council.

  • East Austin Urban Renewal:

    A landmark decision initiated a new urban renewal program for East Austin, starting with applications for a small pilot area and a comprehensive study to assess costs and define future development.

  • City Growth & Paving:

    Austin officially expanded its boundaries by annexing over 14 acres of land. Additionally, the city agreed to proceed with two residential street paving projects following strong neighborhood support.
