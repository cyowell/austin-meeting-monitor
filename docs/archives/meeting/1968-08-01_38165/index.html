Emergency System, Legal Aid, & City Growth

Thursday, August 1, 1968 regular

  • Legal Aid Funding Request:

    The City heard a request for financial support for the Legal Aid Society, which provides free legal services to underprivileged residents and is partially funded by a federal grant. This request was tabled for future budget discussions.

  • City Expansion and Development:

    Plans moved forward to annex over 22 acres of land into the city, and a building permit was approved for an expansion of Steak Island restaurant near Town Lake. Property was also authorized for acquisition related to the future MoPac Boulevard.

  • Public Safety and Infrastructure Upgrades:

    Contracts were approved for new advanced emergency warning stations (partially federally funded, though concerns were raised about the single bidder) and for building new transmission line towers across Decker Lake for the electric department.
