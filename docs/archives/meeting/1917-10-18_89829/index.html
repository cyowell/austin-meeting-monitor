Austin's Sewer Fund, Drivers, Firefighters

Thursday, October 18, 1917 regular

  • Sewer Fund Financial Management:

    The city adjusted its financial policies to ensure all interest earned on the Sanitary Sewer Fund's daily balances would be directly credited to that fund, especially as its bond money was depleted.

  • Correcting Fund Allocation:

    A financial error was rectified by transferring $204.28 in previously misallocated interest earnings from another account back into the Sanitary Sewer Fund.

  • City Appointments & Early Licenses:

    The council confirmed the appointment of two "Hosemen" (firefighters) for city service and granted a driver's license.
