Austin's $112M+ Housing Budget Approved

Wednesday, August 16, 2023 Austin Housing Finance Corporation (AHFC) Board of Directors Regular Meeting
  • Austin's Housing Finance Corporation approved over $112 million for housing initiatives.
  • This includes a $37.7 million operating budget and a $75 million capital budget for Fiscal Year 2024.
  • These housing budgets are companion pieces to the recently adopted main City of Austin budget.
  • All agenda items were passed unanimously and quickly in a brief meeting.
