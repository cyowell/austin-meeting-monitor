New Housing Development Plans on Hold

Thursday, April 25, 2002 Austin Housing Finance Corporation (AHFC)
  • The Austin Housing Finance Corporation (AHFC) discussed backing a $1 million loan.
  • This loan was intended to finance infrastructure for Heritage Village, a proposed 56-unit "S.M.A.R.T. Housing" subdivision.
  • The planned mixed-income development was located near Highway 183 and Martin Luther King Blvd.
  • Ultimately, no official actions were taken on these housing proposals, as the special meeting itself was postponed.
