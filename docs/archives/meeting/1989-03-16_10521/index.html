Austin: Homeless Aid, Pollution Study, New Senior Center

Thursday, March 16, 1989 regular

  • Affordable Housing & Community Investment:

    The city accepted state grants to aid homeless programs and prepared to redirect over $800,000 in federal community development funds for capital improvements in the Rodriguez Subdivision, though key affordable housing proposals were postponed.

  • Environmental Protection:

    Austin prioritized environmental health by approving a household hazardous waste collection contract and seeking federal funding to study nonpoint source pollution in Town Lake.

  • City Facilities & Future Development:

    A new building was purchased for the Waterloo Senior Center. Meanwhile, major expansion projects for Robert Mueller Municipal Airport and a policy regarding city employee pay and benefits were deferred for later consideration.
