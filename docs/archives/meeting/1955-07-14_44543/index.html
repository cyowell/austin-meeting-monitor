Austin's Big Decisions: Dog Control, Gas, Downtown

Thursday, July 14, 1955 regular

Here's a summary of the Austin City Council agenda:

  • Dog Leash Law Delayed:

    Following public debate and Humane Society concerns about shelter readiness, Austin's new dog restraint law was postponed until January 1956.

  • Future Gas Supply Strategy:

    The city began planning for its long-term gas needs, addressing rising prices and exploring options for securing a stable supply for its power plant and industrial users.

  • Downtown Office District Rezoning:

    A public hearing was held to discuss reclassifying the downtown area, leading to mixed public feedback and a deferred decision pending council site visits.

  • New Park and Swimming Pool:

    Authorization was granted to purchase 31.1 acres in Northwest Austin for a new park and public swimming pool, subject to financing arrangements.
