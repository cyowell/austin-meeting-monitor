Austin Paves Ways, Secures Funds, Seals Wells

Thursday, August 29, 1912 regular

  • Public Health Emergency:

    City officials ordered 11 private wells to be sealed immediately due to severe contamination posing a health risk to residents.

  • Major Infrastructure Boom:

    Over $600,000 from municipal bond sales was dedicated to fund crucial street improvements, sewers, hospitals, and cemeteries. This included plans to pave University Avenue and begin paving East 6th Street.
