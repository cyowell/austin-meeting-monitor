City Approves Paid Fire, Creek Sewer, Archway

Thursday, April 27, 1916 regular

  • Major Infrastructure Investment:

    Approved plans and allocated $50,000 for a significant storm sewer project along Little Shoal Creek, aiming to improve drainage and address flooding concerns.

  • Transition to Paid Fire Department:

    Following a public election with overwhelming support, the city officially transitioned from a volunteer to a paid fire department and appointed its first paid Chief.

  • Street Name Changes:

    A section of "26th Street" was officially renamed "Archway," and a street previously known as "26 1/2 Street" was redesignated as "26th Street."
