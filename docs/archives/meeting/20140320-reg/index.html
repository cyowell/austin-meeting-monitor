Big Solar, New Parks, Housing, & City Policies

Thursday, March 20, 2014 Austin City Council Regular Meeting

  • Major Solar Power Investment:

    Approved a 25-year, $525 million agreement to purchase 150 megawatts of solar-generated electricity, significantly boosting the city's renewable energy supply.

  • Housing & Property Tax Relief:

    Enacted property tax exemptions for disabled and senior homeowners and supported multiple applications for new affordable and mixed-income housing developments.

  • New Public Lands & Recreation:

    The city acquired the 292-acre Grey Rock golf course and tennis facility for $9.6 million, and designated a new 23-acre parcel as Wunneburger Park.

  • Community & Social Initiatives:

    Directed the development of a "Housing First" permanent supportive housing project, a sobriety center, and a "Welcoming City" strategy for immigrants. The city also expanded its Community Health Paramedic Program and funded home energy efficiency improvements.

  • Zoning & Environmental Protection:

    Approved changes to occupancy limits for unrelated individuals in single-family homes and set future public hearings for new environmental protection zoning around Lake Austin, as well as the use of parkland for a solar project.
