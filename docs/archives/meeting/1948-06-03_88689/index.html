Wiring Rules Change, Rail Debate, Fire Dept Grows

Thursday, June 3, 1948 regular
  • A citizens' committee was formed to study a bold plan for municipal ownership, relocation of railroads to the east side of the city, and a new union station.
  • Following a public hearing and debate, the city's electrical ordinance was amended, impacting wiring rules for apartments, and the Electrical Board was expanded with new members.
  • Plans were approved to build two new fire stations, and a significant sanitary sewer expansion was authorized for South Austin.
  • Council made key zoning decisions, including establishing a new "D" Industrial District and approving a commercial zone change over Board of Adjustment recommendations.
