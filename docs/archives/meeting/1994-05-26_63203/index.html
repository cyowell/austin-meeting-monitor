Thursday, May 26, 1994 regular
Transcript

  • Cable TV Rate Cuts & Refunds:

    Approved significant rate reductions for basic cable services, ordering refunds for customers dating back to September 1993.

  • Downtown Safety & Community Initiatives:

    Funded a new Downtown Rangers Program with additional staff to boost safety, and allocated over $100,000 for a summer youth program.

  • Affordable Housing & Future Airport Plans:

    Committed over $1 million to develop affordable housing and advanced planning for the new Austin Airport, including expanding its advisory board.

  • Water Sales & Aqua Festival Updates:

    Authorized a wholesale water sales contract with Cedar Park for additional revenue and renegotiated the license for the Austin Aqua Festival, including new rates and a policy review for parkland use.
