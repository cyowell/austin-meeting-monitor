Wednesday, February 25, 1953 special called
Transcript
  • Austin's City Council held a special meeting to address critical urban planning and zoning needs.
  • Council members discussed the urgent need to appoint new experts to the city's planning and zoning bodies, as several current members were unable or unwilling to continue serving.
  • An interim Planning Commission was established, comprising nine primary members and five ex-officio advisors, appointed to guide city development until June 1st.
  • The City Manager was directed to draft the official ordinance required to formalize these planning commission appointments.
