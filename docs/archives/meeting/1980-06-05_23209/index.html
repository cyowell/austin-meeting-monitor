Austin Addresses Water, Growth, Recreation

Thursday, June 5, 1980 regular

  • Protecting Lake Austin's Environment:

    A 90-day moratorium was placed on new subdivision developments in the Lake Austin Peninsula to study low-density standards and safeguard drinking water quality and sensitive areas.

  • Barton Creek Water Concerns:

    Officials addressed worries about increased erosion and water quality impact from large projects like Barton Creek Square Mall and the MoPac highway extension, calling for environmental review.

  • Major Infrastructure Investments:

    Multi-million dollar contracts were approved for significant city projects, including the expansion of Brackenridge Hospital and extensive street, drainage, and sidewalk improvements in East Austin.

  • Community Safety and Recreation:

    The Council responded to public complaints about golf balls from the municipal driving range hitting nearby homes, postponing action and requesting a review of safety options.
