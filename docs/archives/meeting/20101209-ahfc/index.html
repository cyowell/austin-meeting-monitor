Thursday, December 9, 2010 Austin Housing Finance Corporation (AHFC) Board of Directors AHFC Meeting
Official City Record Agenda Watch Video Transcript
  • Approved millions in funding for numerous affordable housing projects across Austin, supporting hundreds of new and renovated homes for low- and moderate-income residents.
  • Key initiatives include new condominium construction, multi-family rental acquisitions, and a significant 120-unit single-room occupancy facility specifically for homeless and low-income individuals.
  • Set a public hearing for January 13, 2011, regarding $6 million in bonds to help finance the acquisition and rehabilitation of the 100-unit Marshall Apartments.
