Mount Bonnell, Penny Meters, Anti-Trust Wins

Thursday, June 17, 1965 regular

  • Mount Bonnell Preservation Denied:

    The Council voted against purchasing the "Connolly tract" on Mount Bonnell for preservation, allowing for potential development, despite significant public debate and a split committee recommendation.

  • Controversial Business Zoning Approved:

    Despite strong community opposition, the Council approved a C-2 commercial rezoning for a liquor store near the University of Texas. They also allowed a Pizza Hut to sell draft beer near a drive-in theater, overriding the theater owner's concerns about increased disturbances.

  • Million-Dollar Anti-Trust Payouts:

    Austin finalized settlements totaling nearly $750,000 from electrical equipment companies in anti-trust cases, accepting offers from Allis-Chalmers and McGraw-Edison, a decision some council members felt was too low.

  • New Penny Parking Meters Introduced:

    The city approved implementing new one-cent parking meters for 12-minute parking periods, alongside funding various street improvement projects and setting land for annexation.
