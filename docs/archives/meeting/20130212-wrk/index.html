Austin Funds Head Start, DNA Lab, Property Tax

Tuesday, February 12, 2013 Austin City Council Work Session

  • Emergency Funding for Cemeteries:

    Approved $476,000 to manage city cemeteries after the previous vendor ceased operations, covering interim staff and equipment leasing.

  • Support for Early Childhood Programs:

    Voted to consider over $600,000 in one-time grants for children's programs, including $557,000 for Child Inc.'s Head Start summer program and $50,000 for a successful bilingual daycare pilot.

  • Property Tax Relief Debated:

    A motion passed to set aside $4 million in one-time funds for a temporary property tax rate reduction next fiscal year, aiming to address city-wide affordability.

  • Addressing Police Forensic Backlog:

    Approved funding for three new forensic chemists for the police department to urgently address a critical backlog in DNA and drug testing, crucial for criminal case processing.

  • Tackling Permit Delays:

    Discussed and advanced plans to re-staff the Planning and Development Review department with senior planners and customer service representatives to significantly reduce backlogs in permit applications.
