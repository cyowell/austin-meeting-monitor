Austin's Big Changes: Zoning, Wells, House IDs

Thursday, November 17, 1932 regular

  • Urban Zoning Overhaul:

    Approved a specific property rezoning from residential to commercial near East 16th Street and East Avenue, making way for a new store and housing. This also sparked a broader review for expanding commercial zones along East Avenue.

  • Public Park Investment:

    Allocated additional funds to complete a vital artesian well project in Blunn Creek Park, ensuring sufficient water flow.

  • Modernizing City Addresses:

    Launched a major initiative to renumber homes across a significant section of East Austin, aiming to standardize the city's addressing system.

  • Community Support:

    Extended free water and light services to the Austin Garden Club for their Flower Show and to the Salvation Army's Bunk Hall, which provides overnight lodging for transient men.
