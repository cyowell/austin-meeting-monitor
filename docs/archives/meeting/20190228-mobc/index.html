Austin's Mobility Vision, Plus Scooter Laws

Thursday, February 28, 2019 Mobility Committee Regular Meeting

  • Future Mobility Plan Unveiled:

    The Austin Strategic Mobility Plan (ASMP) sets a long-term vision to shift 50% of trips to sustainable modes (transit, bikes, walking) by 2040, driven by extensive community feedback favoring balanced transportation options.

  • Controversial Road Concepts:

    The ASMP includes new road connections, such as the debated Red Bud Trail Connector, and a street network table for future right-of-way acquisition, which are presented as long-term concepts for discussion rather than immediate projects.

  • New Regulations for Dockless Scooters:

    Following millions of rides and reports of injuries and a fatality, the city proposes major changes for dockless mobility, including clearer "rules of the road," context-sensitive "slow-down" zones, a shift to a franchise model for operators, and increased fees to fund related infrastructure.

  • Public Hearings Underway:

    Both the ASMP and the proposed dockless mobility regulations will undergo further public hearings and discussions at various commissions and the full City Council in the coming weeks, inviting continued community input.
