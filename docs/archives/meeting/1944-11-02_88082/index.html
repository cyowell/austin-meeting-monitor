Thursday, November 2, 1944 regular
Transcript

  • Utility Expansion:

    The city approved a significant project for the Texas Public Service Company to lay new gas lines across multiple streets, including East 12th, East 14th, and South 2nd, signaling infrastructure development.

  • Business Operations:

    Several commercial licenses were granted, including new taxi driver permits and alcohol sales permits for establishments like Dinty Moore's Cafe & Bar. Two taxi drivers also saw their previously revoked permits reinstated under probationary terms.
