Austin: Sidewalks, Taxes, and Speeding Fines

Thursday, March 26, 1914 regular

  • Infrastructure & Paving:

    Authorized bids for new concrete sidewalks and curbing on West 22nd Street. Also approved a request to sublet excavation work for the West 8th Street paving project, requiring the subcontractor to provide an indemnity bond.

  • Taxation & Finance:

    Approved a new tax collector for the city. The council also adjusted a citizen's 1913 property improvement tax assessment, reducing it from $600 to $400.

  • Traffic Enforcement:

    Remitted speeding fines for two individuals, H.A. Wree and Mrs. M. Robertson.
