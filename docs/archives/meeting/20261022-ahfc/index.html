Thursday, October 22, 2026 Austin Housing Finance Corporation

Austin Invests $21M in New Affordable Homes

  • Approved major housing investments:

    The Austin Housing Finance Corporation authorized over $21 million in funding for five new affordable rental housing developments.

  • Creating deeply affordable units:

    These projects will collectively create 178 new rental units designated for residents earning 30% or 50% of Austin’s median family income.

  • Utilizing city bonds:

    Over $20 million of the funding came from the 2022 General Obligation bonds, allocated across four different housing initiatives.

  • Federal funds for one project:

    One development, the Austin Art Village in District 1, received over $1 million through federal Community Housing Development Organization (CHDO) funds, providing nine units at 30% median family income or below.

  • Citywide impact:

    The approved developments are spread across different parts of the city, with projects located in Districts 1, 4, 5, and 9.

  • Unanimous support:

    All six agenda items, including the approval of these housing investments and previous meeting minutes, were adopted without objection by the board.
