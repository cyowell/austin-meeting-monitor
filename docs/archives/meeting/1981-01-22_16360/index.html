Lake Austin Freeze, Gas Rates, 911 Update

Thursday, January 22, 1981 regular

  • Lake Austin Development Freeze:

    A 90-day moratorium was enacted on new subdivision applications along the Lake Austin waterfront to allow for a review of growth.

  • Cable TV Study Funded:

    The city allocated funds to conduct a community study regarding future cable television services.

  • New Park Land Acquired:

    An option was approved to purchase 100 acres for the future McCarty District Park.

  • Gas Rate Revisions Begin:

    The council began the process of revising Southern Union Gas Company's rates, with a preliminary vote favoring the lowest proposed increase.

  • Enhanced 911 System On Track:

    An update confirmed that the Enhanced 911 emergency telephone system is progressing on schedule for a December 1981 launch.
