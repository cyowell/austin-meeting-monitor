Austin's Dry Future: Water Cuts & New Rules

Tuesday, October 18, 2011 Austin City Council Work Session

  • Severe Drought Forces Major Water Policy Shift:

    Austin faces unprecedented water restrictions by spring 2012 due to historic drought and critically low lake levels. The city will overhaul its water use code to manage a chronic drought, potentially altering outdoor watering, fines for violations, and impacting businesses and power generation.

  • Protecting Trees & Supporting Businesses:

    As part of drought management, officials will focus on strategies to save the city's tree canopy and mitigate economic impacts on businesses like landscapers and homebuilders from long-term watering limits.

  • New Smoking Ban Proposed for Parks:

    A permanent ban on smoking in all city parks, including golf courses, is under consideration and will proceed through public review.

  • Fire Safety: Outdoor Cooking Rules:

    The Fire Chief may soon gain authority to restrict outdoor cooking at multi-family residences during periods of high fire risk.
