Austin Growth: Paving, Zoning & Public Spaces

Thursday, May 16, 1957 regular
  • Heard an appeal from "Club Heights" residents regarding mandatory paved streets and curbs, citing high costs for new homeowners.
  • Approved several zoning changes, notably converting a residential area on East 12th Street to commercial use, despite a Planning Commission recommendation against it.
  • Authorized a land deal for Cameron Road widening, committing the city not to charge homeowners for future expansion beyond current plans.
  • Greenlit the construction and operation of a new Gulf Oil filling station, outlining strict environmental and safety conditions.
