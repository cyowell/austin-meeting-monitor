Austin Energy's Solar Future, Campaign Finance Transparency

Thursday, June 25, 2015 Austin City Council Special Called Meeting

  • Boosting Campaign Finance Transparency:

    Approved an ordinance making campaign finance data electronically searchable and downloadable, increasing public access and accountability for contributions and expenditures.

  • Solar Power Costs Plummeting:

    Received an update on a major solar energy request for proposals (RFP), revealing record-low prices and sparking discussion on the best strategy to acquire more solar to meet renewable goals.

  • WAYA Parkland Agreement Extended:

    Finalized a 25-year use agreement with the West Austin Youth Association (WAYA) for parkland, incorporating flexibility for future city projects like the Pressler Street extension.

  • Key Energy Plan Review Moves Forward:

    Awarded a contract for an independent "gap study" consultant to verify Austin Energy's resource plan and evaluate the costs associated with the city's climate protection plan.
