Thursday, August 7, 1913 regular
Transcript

  • Urban Development & Infrastructure:

    Funds were approved to purchase land for extending Fifth Street, and permanent street layouts were established for Waller and Brazos Streets. Significant investment was also made in machinery for the West Austin sewer system.

  • Environmental & Street Design:

    A temporary curb line was set on First Street to preserve a block of "large and handsome trees," with future street width and planting guidelines noted.

  • City Services & Personnel:

    The Council fixed new prices for grave spaces at Oakwood Cemetery and appointed a new city Dog Catcher.

  • Financial Matters:

    Various financial claims were settled, including a refund for an unearned liquor license and payments for damages caused by a water main break.
