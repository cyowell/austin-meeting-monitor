Funding Schools and Protecting Austin Workers

Monday, November 15, 1926 regular
  • Approved a new group insurance plan for city employees through the Missouri State Life Insurance Company.
  • Transferred $15,000 from the Water, Light and Power Department's earnings to the city's general fund.
  • Accepted J. T. Bowman's bid to purchase $75,000 of Austin School Bonds, providing funding for local schools.
  • Voted for the city itself to invest $75,000 from its Interest and Sinking Fund into the remaining portion of the Austin School Bonds.
