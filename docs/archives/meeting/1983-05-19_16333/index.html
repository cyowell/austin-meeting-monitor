Thursday, May 19, 1983 regular
Transcript

  • Nuclear Project Funding:

    Approved $30 million in bonds for the controversial South Texas Nuclear Project (STNP), overriding public opposition and legal challenges, to meet obligations and prevent a major electric rate increase.

  • Utility Rate Hikes:

    Set a public hearing for a proposed 10% increase in water and wastewater rates, aimed at funding urgent maintenance and future city growth, including plant expansion.

  • Police-Community Relations:

    Addressed strong criticism from community groups regarding the Police Chief's "hoodlum" comments about KKK march protestors, prompting an apology from the City Manager and a commitment to review relations.

  • City Expansion:

    Advanced plans to annex 8.5 square miles of land in 1983, significantly expanding Austin's boundaries and future service areas.

  • Zoning & Senior Support:

    Discussed new zoning to restrict colleges and universities in certain residential areas, and supported state legislation for property tax relief for senior citizens.
