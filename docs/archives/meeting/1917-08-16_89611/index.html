City Bonds, Fire Upgrades, & Citizen Relief

Thursday, August 16, 1917 regular

  • City Bond Election Approved:

    An ordinance passed to hold an election for issuing new city bonds, despite internal concerns about the city's current debt load and the difficulty of selling bonds during the period of "distress and uncertainty."

  • Fire Department Boost:

    New leadership appointments were made, including an Assistant Fire Chief, and a contract was awarded for 1,000 feet of specialized fire hose to enhance firefighting capabilities.

  • Citizen Appeals & Infrastructure:

    Several residents received extensions to connect to the city's sanitary sewer system, and the council remitted portions or all of fines for various minor offenses, including "fishing with two poles" and "drunkenness."

Fire Hose Contract awarded The Mayor laid before the Council the following resolution : Whereas, on this day came on to be considered propositions from five firms or Corporations ,by their respective agents ,for the supply of one thousand feet of fire hose to the City of Austin, Texas, at various prices according to the quality of the hose ,from $ 1.30 per running foot down to 60 ½ cents per running foot ; and Whereas, the City Council has come to the unanimous conclusion that for the purposes of our present needs more than averagelsstrong hose for the use of our automatic pumping engine is required; and Whereas, such requirement would seem to indicate to the City Council that it should be best that we purchase what is known as Solid Multiple Woven hose ; and Whereas , there wer only two corporations offering this quality of fire hose; and Whereas, the price of the Baker Fabric hose,as offered to the City Council by the corporation of Walter Co of this City, being to-wit,four ply Baker Fabric solid multiple hose at $ 1.15 per running foot without couplings,is the lowest and best price offered for said above described hose; Now, therefore, Be it resolved by the City Council of the City of Austin,Texas: That the City Council hereby authorizes Clarence Woodward ,Chief of the Fire Department, to contract for the furnishing to the City of Austin of one thousandfeet of Solid Multiple Woven Hose ,to be furnished this City within a reasonable time at the cost of $ 1.15 per running foot ,without couplings,to be paid for on or before February 1st 1918, without interest upon the amount owing for such purpose.
