Austin: Gas Stations, Streetlights, & Holidays

Thursday, April 5, 1934 regular

  • Community Advocacy for Infrastructure:

    The Council addressed a petition from Parent Teacher Associations for the Colored, requesting street improvements on Leona Street and new streetlights near "Negro schools," directing a cost and location estimate.

  • Gas Station Boom & Strict Development Rules:

    Permits were approved for two new drive-in gasoline filling stations, with detailed conditions for construction, drainage, and street-side improvements, reflecting early urban planning and safety regulations.

  • Worker Holiday & Financial Scrutiny:

    City employees were granted a holiday for Good Friday, and a policy was adopted to withhold payments to any individuals or businesses indebted to the City.
