Public Transit Shake-Up: Austin Chooses New Path

Saturday, March 6, 1971 regular
  • Austin officials approved a contract with Austin Transit Corporation to operate the city's bus service, aiming to provide an updated fleet, including newer air-conditioned coaches.
  • The decision, estimated to offer a lower monthly cost to the city than the previous temporary service, focused on acquiring management expertise while allowing flexibility for Austin to eventually purchase its own buses.
  • Following the vote, the City Manager was immediately directed to pursue federal authorization to order municipal buses, signaling a clear long-term intent for city-owned and operated public transportation.
  • A concerned citizen voiced the need for more bus routes, less polluting vehicles, and a more thorough study before committing to a long-term contract, highlighting public desires for improved and sustainable transit.
