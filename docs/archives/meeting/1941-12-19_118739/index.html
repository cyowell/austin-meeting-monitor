Taxpayers Challenge Valuations, New Building Costs

Friday, December 19, 1941 special called
  • City leaders held a special session to hear numerous appeals from citizens disputing their 1941 property tax assessments.
  • In most instances, the original property valuations were upheld by city officials after reviewing the appeals.
  • A key discussion centered on property taxes for a "new type of construction," with arguments that it faced a disproportionately high tax ratio. City leaders decided to investigate these specific building costs further.
