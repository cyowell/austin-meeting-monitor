Oak Creek Housing Project Gets $13M Funding

Thursday, November 15, 2018 Austin Housing Finance Corporation (AHFC) Board of Directors AHFC Meeting
  • The Austin Housing Finance Corporation approved issuing up to $13 million in multi-family housing revenue bonds.
  • These funds are specifically designated to finance a new housing development at 3300 Oak Creek Drive.
  • All agenda items, including the significant housing bond approval, were quickly passed on a consent agenda.
