Thursday, December 13, 1956 regular
Transcript

  • New Industrial Fuel Plant:

    Approved a private underground gasoline and diesel fuel storage plant on East 4th Street for Kerrville Bus Company's fleet, subject to strict safety and operational guidelines.

  • City Limits & Growth Strategy:

    Began annexing additional land parcels and discussed challenges with non-adjacent properties, prompting a new focus on more careful consideration for small-parcel annexations.

  • Major Utility Infrastructure:

    Authorized Southern Union Gas Company to install new gas mains across several city streets and approved the purchase of over 105,000 lbs of aluminum wire for utility development.

  • Auditorium Rate Overhaul:

    Initiated a process to establish new, firm rental rates for the City Auditorium, particularly for conventions and trade shows, analyzing operating costs and concession revenue.
