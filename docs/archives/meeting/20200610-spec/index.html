Austin's Next Transit Move: Project Connect

Wednesday, June 10, 2020 Austin City Council Special Called Meeting

  • Big Transit Vote:

    The City Council will vote on officially adopting the "Project Connect" system plan, a massive public transit expansion for Austin.

  • Reshaping Mobility:

    This plan outlines proposed new rail and bus lines designed to transform how people get around the city.

  • Funding the Future:

    Discussions will cover the proposed governance structure and financial strategy for Project Connect, detailing how this large-scale project would be funded.
