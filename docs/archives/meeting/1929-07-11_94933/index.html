Thursday, July 11, 1929 regular
Transcript

  • Gas Station Permit Denied:

    Following a protest from the University Baptist Church citing pedestrian hazards, the Council refused a permit for a new gasoline filling station at 21st and Guadalupe Streets.

  • Major Street Paving Approved:

    Public hearings concluded with no protests, leading to the approval of ordinances to pave sections of South Congress Avenue and San Antonio Street, with costs assessed to abutting property owners.

  • Ninth Street Closure Proposed:

    The Council moved to draft an ordinance to close a portion of Ninth Street from Neches to Trinity, responding to a petition signed by 1365 community members from the School Board.

  • New Fire Limits & Building Rules:

    A significant new ordinance was introduced to establish and define fire limits and zones across the city, regulating the type and quality of building construction permitted within these areas.
