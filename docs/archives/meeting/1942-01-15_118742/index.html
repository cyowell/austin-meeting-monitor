Thursday, January 15, 1942 regular
Transcript

  • Wartime Airport Expansion:

    The City committed to acquiring up to 3,000 acres for a new U.S. Air Corps airport, a significant contribution to the national defense effort.

  • Defense Industry Water Supply:

    A major contract was approved to build a water line and supply water to a new magnesium production plant, vital for wartime manufacturing.

  • New Utility Rates & Bulk Discounts:

    Revised water rates were adopted, including a specially discounted rate for industrial customers using exceptionally large volumes.

  • Emergency Preparedness:

    The Council approved a mayoral proclamation for a city-wide trial blackout, signaling local readiness for potential wartime emergencies.

  • Taxi Driver Regulations:

    The Council denied license renewals for several taxi drivers and strengthened regulations to prohibit unlicensed drivers from operating through taxi terminals.
