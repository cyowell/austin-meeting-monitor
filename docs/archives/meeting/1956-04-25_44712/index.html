Austin: Vote Prep, Westlake Sewers, Land Trade

Wednesday, April 25, 1956 special called

  • Special Election Logistics

    The Council finalized a comprehensive list of election officials and polling places across numerous city precincts for an upcoming special election on May 12, 1956.

  • Key Board Appointments

    New members were appointed to the Planning Commission, Airport Committee, and Retirement Board to fill vacancies.

  • Infrastructure Planning

    The City Manager was directed to conduct a study and report on sewer development specifically concerning the Westlake Club and its surrounding areas.

  • Property Negotiations

    Discussions began regarding a potential property trade with the American Legion, with the Mayor suggesting appraisals for the parcels involved.
