Austin Energy: Rebates, Assistance, & Renewables

Tuesday, September 28, 2021 Austin Energy Utility Oversight Committee Regular Meeting

  • Boosting Utility Bill Assistance & Efficiency:

    Approved $6.55 million for energy efficiency rebates and extended a $7.25 million program helping customers with utility bill payments.

  • Considering Higher Bill Discounts:

    Discussed raising the Customer Assistance Program discount from 10% to 15% to further support residents and businesses.

  • Strong Renewable Energy Growth:

    Reported 52% of electricity came from renewable sources this quarter, with significant new solar and wind projects coming online.

  • Addressing Grid Reliability Concerns:

    Reviewed performance during the winter storm, clarifying "Black Start" capabilities, and noted recent reliability impacts from severe weather.

  • Future Focus on Wildfire Prevention & Small Business:

    Plans to discuss vegetation management near power lines to reduce wildfire risk and revisit charges affecting small commercial businesses.
