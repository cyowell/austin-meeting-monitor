Historic Austin: Dam Project, Taxes, Pauper Care

Thursday, December 28, 1911 regular

  • Segregated Public Services:

    Approved various city contracts, notably separate bids for handling "white pauper remains" and "colored pauper remains," along with general city and hospital supplies.

  • Major Infrastructure Update:

    Extended the deadline for the dam reconstruction contractor due to a city-mandated change in the power transmission line route; also officially set new street grades for Baylor Street.

  • Property Tax & Charity Policy:

    Authorized a deal to clear delinquent property taxes by transferring city liens in exchange for full payment; and allocated $750 from the general fund to charity.
