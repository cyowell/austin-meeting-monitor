Austin's 1904 Plumbing Rules and City Hall Funding

Monday, December 19, 1904 regular

• Major Plumbing Ordinance Passed: The city adopted new plumbing regulations requiring inspections and licensing fees after lengthy debate, setting official inspection fees at $75.00 and establishing rules for house connections to Austin's public sewer system.

• New City Hall Fund Created: Council voted to establish a dedicated "New City Hall account" and directed the city treasurer to deposit 10% of general revenue collections toward construction of a new municipal building, plus an initial $6,000 transfer.

• Street Improvements Approved: The council allocated funds for grading streets in the 7th Ward ($75) and approved various street-related petitions, including requests for work on Henderson Street and a new wagon scale on West 6th Street.

• Bond Payments and Municipal Finances: Council authorized $19,252.50 for upcoming bond interest payments due January 1, 1905, and approved purchase of $6,000 in city refunding bonds for the sinking fund.
