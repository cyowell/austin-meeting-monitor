Austin Drives Forward: Roads, Parks, Zoning

Thursday, December 3, 1931 regular

  • Street Infrastructure & Modernization:

    Approved new paving for Tenth Street, "flush coating" for Seventh, Eighth, and Second Streets, and initiated plans to extend East Ninth and Tenth Streets to improve city connectivity.

  • Significant Park & Riverfront Investments:

    Authorized over $29,000 in land acquisitions for the completion of Shoal Creek Boulevard and endorsed a major proposal for a recreational lake on the Colorado River.

  • Key Development & Zoning Decisions:

    Established new building lines on East First Street, denied one commercial zoning change to protect residential character, and approved another to guide urban growth.

  • Essential Utility & Financial Oversight:

    Permitted new gas main installations for Texas Public Service Company and settled several long-standing property tax disputes, including adjusting valuations and waiving penalties.
