Austin's Future: Parks, Utilities, & Resorts

Thursday, September 8, 1949 regular

  • Significant Funds for City Growth:

    City leaders secured over $8 million in bonds for essential upgrades to Austin's utilities (electric, water, sewer) and general city operations. They also approved an additional $930,000 for a new Police & Courts building and public school construction.

  • Lake Austin Land for Vacation Resort:

    A large portion (400 acres) of Lake Austin Metropolitan Park was optioned for sale to developers planning a major "Butlin Vacation Village" resort, with strict conditions to ensure tourism and economic benefits.

  • Protecting Shoal Creek Green Space:

    Citizens petitioned to protect city-owned land along Shoal Creek as public park space, pushing back against potential commercial or institutional development, prompting a commitment for further study.

  • Local Business & Development Decisions:

    The city approved new commercial zones and a gas station, debated Lake Austin dock access, and personally weighed in on individual taxi driver licenses and a beer permit, setting strict conditions for some.
