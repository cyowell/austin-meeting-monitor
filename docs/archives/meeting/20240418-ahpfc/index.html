Thursday, April 18, 2024 Austin Housing Public Facility Corporation (AHPFC) Board of Directors Regular Meeting
Official City Record Agenda Watch Video Transcript
  • Approved $35 million in private activity bonds for the "Escuela Nueva" project.
  • This development will create 114 affordable housing units in the Montopolis neighborhood.
  • The project uniquely combines housing with a new first-floor preschool, addressing critical childcare and displacement needs.
  • A public hearing showed strong community support for the development, leading to the board's approval.
