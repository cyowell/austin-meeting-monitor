Austin Property & Business Tax Cuts

Thursday, February 7, 1924 regular

  • Police Force Expanded:

    The city approved the appointment of two new police officers: a regular plain clothes officer and a special policeman for the City Hospital.

  • Public Works & Safety Requests:

    Citizens petitioned for street improvements on San Pedro Street, and a request to expand fire limits to Block 27 was referred for legal review.

  • Widespread Tax Valuation Adjustments:

    The council approved numerous reductions to 1923 property and business tax valuations. These adjustments addressed issues like damaged properties, incorrect assessments, inaccessible land, and reconsiderations of business inventory and machinery values.
