Austin Housing Funds: $20 Million Boost

Monday, September 11, 2017 Austin Housing Finance Corporation (AHFC) Board of Directors AHFC Meeting

  • Major Housing Investment:

    Nearly $20 million was approved to fund Austin's housing initiatives for the upcoming fiscal year.

  • Dedicated Funds:

    This includes over $10 million for capital projects related to housing and over $9 million specifically for housing grants.

  • Program Guidelines:

    The Board authorized the development of new program guidelines for housing initiatives and established administrative approval limits for financial oversight.

NOTICE OF MEETING The Austin Housing Finance Corporation, Board of Directors, will convene at 9:45 AM on Monday, September 11, 2017, in Council Chambers at Austin City Hall, 301 W.
