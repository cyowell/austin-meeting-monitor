    The City Manager declared a much-needed pay increase for city employees financially impossible due to tight budget constraints and unexpected costs, despite proposals to reallocate funds from other sources.

  • Aggressive Collection of Delinquent Accounts:

    Austin was intensifying efforts to collect over $1.5 million in delinquent taxes, including preparing 6,000 lawsuits. The city also actively pursued overdue utility and hospital bills, defending its high collection rates amidst a Councilwoman's concerns.

  • Infrastructure & Growth Strain:

    The city faced significant financial challenges, needing $750,000 to complete its water plant next year and grappling with higher-than-expected growth in new electric customers, which strained utility budgets.
