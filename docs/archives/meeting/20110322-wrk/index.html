Tuesday, March 22, 2011 Austin City Council Work Session
Official City Record Agenda Watch Video Transcript

Here's a concise look at key decisions and discussions from the meeting:

  • Protecting Austin's Water:

    Supported state legislation to restrict wastewater discharges in the sensitive Edwards Aquifer Drinking Water Protection Zone, a critical source of drinking water.

  • Investing in Public Well-being:

    Approved funding for free GED classes, school-based programs to combat obesity (including installing bike racks), and grants to assist female victims of domestic violence and sexual assault.

  • City Infrastructure Upgrades:

    Authorized millions in contracts for significant projects, including improvements at Austin-Bergstrom International Airport, construction of a new EMS station, and essential road and waterline work.

  • Planning for a Sustainable Future:

    Briefed on strategies for long-term recycling services and ratified a campaign to promote water conservation. A public hearing was also set for community input on future city programs.
