Monday, July 18, 1898 regular

• Bridge Repairs Approved: The city allocated $345 to repair a bridge in Fair View Park and $165.50 to build new bridges on West 2nd, West 5th Streets, and Congress Avenue.

• Fiscal Responsibility Measure Stalled: A proposed ordinance to prohibit spending money the city doesn't have in its treasury failed to advance, suggesting budget discipline concerns among council members.

• Tax Assessment Dispute Rejected: A local merchant's petition to overturn a property tax increase was denied, with the council voting to uphold the Board of Equalization's 1897 assessment.

• City Services Funded: The council approved $100 for sanitary workers to cut weeds and authorized payment for water and electric services provided by the city's utilities.
