Growth: Schools, Water, Developer Reimbursement

Wednesday, October 21, 1998

  • Boosted Infrastructure Development:

    Authorized significant investments, including over $1.5 million for water and wastewater services to the Howard Lane Industrial Park, a new water reservoir for Davenport Ranch, and expansion of the South Austin Regional Wastewater Treatment Plant.

  • Support for New High School:

    Approved over $2.5 million and granted special waivers to extend critical water and wastewater services to the proposed W. Charles Akins High School.

  • Key Development Policy Shift:

    Approved Moore's Crossing Municipal Utility District to issue $1.6 million in bonds and, notably, altered developer reimbursement for district infrastructure from 90% to 70% of costs, impacting future growth incentives.
