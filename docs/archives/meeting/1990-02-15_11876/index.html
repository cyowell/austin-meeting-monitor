Transcript

  • Future Airport Decisions:

    The city launched a major study into converting Bergstrom Air Force Base into a new civilian airport, while also continuing development plans for a separate site in Manor.

  • Lobbying Transparency:

    New rules were passed requiring lobbyists to disclose fees, report past activities, and submit quarterly updates, aiming for greater transparency.

  • Water Conservation Mandate:

    A public hearing was set to consider a new rule mandating low-flush toilets in all new construction to conserve water.
