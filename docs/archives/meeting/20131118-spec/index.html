Austin Voters OK Affordability Prop 1 Results

Monday, November 18, 2013 Austin City Council Special Called Meeting

  • Election Results Certified:

    The meeting's primary purpose was to officially approve and certify the results of the November 5th special election.

  • Proposition 1 Passed:

  • Affordable Housing Focus:
