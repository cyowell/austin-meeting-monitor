Austin's Future: Dell, Development, and Child Care

Thursday, February 26, 1998 regular

  • Major Economic & Infrastructure Boost:

    Approved multi-million dollar contracts for water and wastewater services in newly annexed areas and established incentives for the planned Dell Campus development on Parmer Lane.

  • Investing in Social Services & Urban Planning:

    Committed over $2 million to HIV-related services, directed studies on child care affordability, and planned for Traditional Neighborhood Design projects in East Austin.

  • Significant Land Use & Urban Redevelopment:

    Advanced large-scale industrial rezonings in areas like Parmer and Howard Lanes, and moved forward with a public-private development at the "Pole Yard Site" downtown.

  • Community Recognition & Advocacy:

    Renamed the Zaragosa Branch Library to honor Eustacio Cepeda and advocated for the restoration of Food Stamps for legal immigrants at the federal level.
