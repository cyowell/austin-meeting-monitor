Austin's Future: Oil Search, Gas Mains & Civic Art

Thursday, February 28, 1935 regular

  • Public Engagement:

    Citizens petitioned for the organization of a civic orchestra and requested new street lighting and a stop sign at a city intersection.

  • Major Infrastructure Development:

    Approval was granted for Texas Public Service Company to lay new gas mains across several city streets, expanding utility access.

  • Commercial Growth:

    Two permits were issued for the construction and operation of new drive-in gasoline filling stations, one including a garage, with strict environmental and safety conditions.

  • Unique Resource Exploration:

    A permit was approved for W.F. Johnson to drill a deep well for oil prospecting within city limits, located in a residential district, pending state approval.

  • Preserving City Assets:

    City officials were instructed to investigate and recommend plans to safeguard invaluable and irreplaceable City Hall records, estimated to be worth $250,000.
