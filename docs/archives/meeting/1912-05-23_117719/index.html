Major Street Paving and New Dog Rules

Thursday, May 23, 1912 regular
  • Citizens from Hyde Park addressed city officials with their concerns during a suspended session.
  • New dog regulations were advanced, passing their first reading.
  • Major street and alley paving projects received approval, including the award of a bid for alleyways and contracts for upgrading Colorado, Brazos, and 2nd Streets. Project costs will be shared between the city, local property owners, and railway companies.
