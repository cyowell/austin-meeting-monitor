In parting with you let me say that I do it with a twofold regret, first because of the kindred intercourse that has always existed between us, and secondly because I recognize the fact that the administration of the city government loses on account of the dissolution of the official relations between you and us an officer who has been faithful and able in the discharge of duty and who justly enjoys the confidence of the entire community.
