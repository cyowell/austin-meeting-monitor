Austin: Dam Safety, Public Land & Community

Thursday, June 19, 1941 regular

  • Enhanced Security at Dams:

    The city restricted public access near Tom Miller and Mansfield Dams, acting on federal requests to prevent potential sabotage during wartime.

  • Long-Term Public Land Lease:

    A 20-year lease for the 77-acre "Sand Beach Reserve" was renewed, securing the land for water protection, waterfront development, and recreational use.

  • Organizing Civilian Defense:

    The city moved to appoint someone to coordinate women's defense activities, highlighting early local efforts in wartime civilian mobilization.

  • Community Welfare Program:

    Austin adopted the federal "Cotton Stamp Plan," a program aimed at distributing surplus cotton goods to residents.
