Paving Disputes, Tax Relief, & Early Business Permits

Thursday, April 2, 1914 regular
  • Approved legal action against several property owners who refused to pay their share for recently completed street paving on major roads like East 5th, Neches, and Trinity streets.
  • Corrected numerous property tax assessments and canceled others due to errors, double counting, or notes being sold prior to the assessment date, offering financial relief to citizens.
  • Granted permits for new business infrastructure, including an underground gasoline tank and sidewalk pump for a tire company, and electric light signs for other local establishments.
  • Addressed minor citizen grievances, such as refunding a $3 impound fee for a horse that escaped due to "mischief or malice," and approved appropriations for general city operations.
