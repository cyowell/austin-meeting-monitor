Austin's Land Code: Density, Homes, & Displacement

Tuesday, February 11, 2020 Austin City Council Special Called Meeting

Here's a summary of the 2020-02-11 Austin City Council agenda:

  • Land Development Code (LDC) Overhaul:

    The Council continued discussions on the second reading of a major update to Austin's LDC, focusing on how it will reshape city development and housing policy for decades to come.

  • Intense Public Input & Future Hearings:

    Despite limited speaking times, numerous citizens testified, leading to a commitment from Council members to schedule a dedicated, longer public hearing day for the LDC's upcoming third reading to allow more community feedback.

  • Affordable Housing & Anti-Displacement Efforts:

    Many speakers urged the Council to strengthen provisions for affordable housing, tenant protections (like right to return and source of income protection), and robust anti-displacement strategies, particularly in vulnerable and gentrifying areas.

  • Neighborhood Character & Infrastructure Concerns:

    Residents voiced strong opposition to "upzoning" in their single-family neighborhoods, citing concerns about increased traffic, strain on aging infrastructure (water, drainage, sidewalks), loss of mature trees, and destruction of established community character.
