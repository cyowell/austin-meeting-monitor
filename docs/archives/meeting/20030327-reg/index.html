Austin: Neighborhood Growth, Hospital, Public Health

Thursday, March 27, 2003 Austin City Council Regular Meeting

  • Major Urban Planning Adopted:

    The comprehensive Govalle/Johnston Terrace Neighborhood Plan was approved, significantly redefining land use and zoning for a large area. The city also amended its duplex residential use code, repealing a prior moratorium.

  • Healthcare & Public Services:

    A new facility was officially named the "Austin Women's Hospital." Essential grants for HIV emergency relief were extended, and the city renewed the non-emergency medical transfer franchise.

  • Future Growth & Development:

    Public hearings were conducted regarding the annexation of the Pioneer Hill and McNeil Drive areas.

  • Upcoming Policy Debates:

    Public hearings were scheduled to consider a new city-wide smoking ordinance and revisions to the Northtown Municipal Utility District agreement.
