Austin's Mobility Future: Robots & Rail

Thursday, August 4, 2022 Mobility Committee Regular Meeting

  • Project Connect Light Rail Adjustments:

    Austin's ambitious light rail project is undergoing cost and design re-evaluation to ensure alignment with funding, with a commitment to the original vision and no new taxes. Other transit improvements (like MetroRapid) are already being implemented.

  • Robot Delivery on Austin Streets:

    The city received a briefing from companies like Refraction AI and Coco, who are deploying electric robots for local delivery, aiming to reduce traffic congestion and emissions while improving efficiency.

  • Enhanced Bike Lane Safety Proposed:

    Cyclists advocated for a new program to allow photo submissions of cars illegally parked in bike lanes, pushing for more effective enforcement to improve safety.

  • Westgate Blvd Traffic Calming Concerns:

    Residents voiced significant safety concerns regarding current traffic calming installations on Westgate Boulevard, reporting numerous accidents and calling for an urgent review.
