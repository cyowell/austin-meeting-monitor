Water Ahead: Resilience & Innovation

Tuesday, November 13, 2018 Austin City Council Work Session
  • The Council reviewed a detailed timeline and after-action report for the October 2018 boil water notice, discussing communication challenges with businesses and the decision-making process for using emergency alert systems.
  • A comprehensive "Water Forward" plan, developed over four years, was presented, outlining a 100-year strategy to secure Austin's water supply in the face of projected population growth (to 4 million by 2115) and climate change impacts.
  • Key strategies include a major investment in Aquifer Storage and Recovery (ASR) to store treated drinking water underground, new ordinances requiring alternative water sources (rainwater, reuse) in new developments, and expanding existing conservation and water reuse programs.
  • The plan emphasizes local water resource maximization and is projected to cost over $1 billion in utility and private development initiatives over the next two decades, funded through state loans, capital recovery fees, and debt retirement, with no immediate rate increases expected.
