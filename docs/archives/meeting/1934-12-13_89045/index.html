Jobs for Austin, New Gas Station, Street Paving

Thursday, December 13, 1934 regular

  • Prioritizing Jobs During the Depression:

    Instructed city management to work with the Public Works Administration (PWA) to ensure a new project exclusively used manual labor to employ local residents.

  • New Gas Station Development:

    Approved a new drive-in gasoline filling station, wash rack, and grease lift at West 24th and Rio Grande Streets, detailing strict construction and waste disposal requirements.

  • Infrastructure Upgrades:

    Awarded a contract to pave East Fourth Street, funded jointly by the city and property owners, and granted permits for the installation of new telephone pole lines and gas mains in several areas.
