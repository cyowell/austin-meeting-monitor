New Senior Housing & Land Acquisition

Thursday, May 24, 2001 Austin Housing Finance Corporation (AHFC)

  • Public Land for Affordable Housing:

    The Housing Finance Corporation accepted 208 acres in the Colony Park neighborhood to develop future low and moderate-income housing.

  • Public Input on Senior Housing Project:

    A public hearing was conducted regarding the issuance of up to $9 million in bonds to finance the Arbors at Creekside Apartments, a proposed 176-unit affordable senior citizen complex.

  • Major Senior Living Project Approved:

    The Arbors at Creekside Apartments, an affordable 176-unit senior living complex, was approved. This included authorizing up to $9 million in financing, official co-development by the Housing Finance Corporation, and securing the land through purchase and a long-term lease.
