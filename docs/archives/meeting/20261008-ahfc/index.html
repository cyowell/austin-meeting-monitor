Thursday, October 8, 2026 Austin Housing Finance Corporation

ATX Housing: $33M Bonds, Expanded Repair Aid

Here's what happened at the recent Austin Housing Finance Corporation meeting:

  • Affordable Housing Boost:

    Approved a resolution to issue up to $33 million in housing bonds for Elm Ridge Apartments at 1190 Airport Boulevard. This will help acquire and renovate the complex, ensuring all 130 units remain affordable for residents earning 30% of the median family income.

  • Home Repair Program Expanded:

    Increased funding for the "GO Repair" home repair program by an additional $10 million, bringing the total contract amount to $32 million. This money, from 2022 general obligation bonds, helps residents with necessary home improvements.

  • Non-Profit Contracts Extended:

    Extended contracts with key non-profit partners like Austin Habitat for Humanity and Meals on Wheels, who administer the GO Repair program, through September 30, 2028.

  • Public Hearing Held:

    A public hearing was held on the Elm Ridge Apartments bond issuance, but no members of the public offered comments.

  • Unanimous Approvals:

    All items on the agenda, including the approval of past meeting minutes and the major housing initiatives, passed unanimously as part of a consent agenda, with no objections from board members.
