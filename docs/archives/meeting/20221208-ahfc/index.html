Austin Greenlights Affordable Homes, Homeless Vouchers

Thursday, December 8, 2022 Austin Housing Finance Corporation (AHFC) Board of Directors Regular Meeting

  • Approved the purchase of nine affordable housing complexes

    in Central Austin (Districts 7 & 9), totaling 270 units, aimed at preserving housing affordability.

  • Launched a new local housing voucher program

    to provide permanent supportive housing for people experiencing homelessness, starting with properties like Pecan Gardens, Katy Lofts, and Seabrook Square.

  • Proceeded with the Candlewood Suites hotel conversion

    into permanent supportive housing, despite public opposition raising concerns about transparency and equitable distribution.
