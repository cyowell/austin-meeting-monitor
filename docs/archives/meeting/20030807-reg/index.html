Thursday, August 7, 2003 Austin City Council Regular Meeting

  • New Women's Hospital Funded:

    Significant budget changes were approved to establish the Austin Women's Hospital, reallocating funds from Seton Healthcare and refining its operational structure.

  • City Hall Transparency & Rules:

    Major amendments were passed to revise City meeting procedures, public hearing rules, and appeal processes, directly influencing how citizens can participate in local government.

  • Upcoming Fees and Development Debates:

    Public hearings were scheduled for the following week to discuss proposed increases to drainage and electric utility fees, along with new residential development regulations concerning garage and parking placement.

  • Expanding City Limits & Denying Historic Status:

    The city moved forward with annexing two large areas (Motorola PDA Area and Lexington Parke) and also voted to deny historic zoning for the DeWitt C. Greer House.
