Austin OKs 1% Property Tax Increase

Wednesday, September 29, 2010 Austin City Council Special Called Meeting
  • The city council approved a new property tax rate for the upcoming 2010-2011 fiscal year.
  • This decision resulted in an increase, setting the rate at 71 cents per hundred dollar valuation, which was an effective 1% rise.
  • The measure passed unanimously with a 6-0 vote by the present council members.
