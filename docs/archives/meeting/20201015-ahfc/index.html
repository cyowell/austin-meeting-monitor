Austin Greenlights Affordable Tower Funding

Thursday, October 15, 2020 Austin Housing Finance Corporation (AHFC) Board of Directors AHFC Meeting

  • Approved $37 Million for Affordable Housing:

    The Housing Finance Corporation voted to apply for $37 million in bonds to fund a major redevelopment project.

  • The Rebecca Tower Project:

    This funding targets "The Rebecca" (also known as "The Tower"), a second phase of the RBJ redevelopment, aiming for up to 277 units.

  • 100% Affordable Units:

    All units funded by these bonds in "The Tower" are designated as affordable housing, serving residents earning 30%, 50%, or 60% of the area's median family income.

  • Equitable Unit Placement Emphasized:

    Council members underscored the importance of ensuring affordable units are integrated throughout the building, including those with desirable views.
