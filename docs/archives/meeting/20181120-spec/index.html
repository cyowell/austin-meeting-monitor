Austin Election Results & Runoffs Declared

Tuesday, November 20, 2018 Austin City Council Special Called Meeting

  • November Election Results Certified:

    The city officially canvassed the 2018 general and special election results.

  • Mayor Adler Wins Outright:

    Mayor Steve Adler secured re-election without needing a runoff, as did Councilmembers Ann Kitchen (District 5) and Kathie Tovo (District 9).

  • Runoff Races Set for December 11:

    Three City Council districts (1, 3, and 8) will proceed to a runoff election, as no candidate won a majority of votes.

  • Key Ballot Propositions Decided:

    Voters approved Propositions A through I, while Propositions J and K failed, impacting various city initiatives and bond measures.
