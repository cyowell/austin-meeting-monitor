Austin: New Bus Routes, Tax Fights, & Big Growth

Thursday, April 17, 1947 regular

  • Student Bus Service Approved:

    The city approved a new, direct bus route for University of Texas students living in the Brackenridge and Deep Eddy Apartments, aiming to cut commute times significantly.

  • City Fights State Tax Exemption:

    Officials strongly opposed a proposed state bill that would exempt $3,000 of homestead value from property taxes, warning of significant financial losses (over $500,000 annually) for city services and public schools.

  • Major Infrastructure & Growth:

    Approved a vast expansion of gas mains across 32 streets and enacted significant zoning changes, converting hundreds of acres of recently annexed land from residential to commercial use.

  • Public Health & Fair Enforcement:

    Declared a "Clean-Up Week" to support polio prevention efforts and directed city officials to ensure stricter, non-discriminatory enforcement of taxicab regulations.
