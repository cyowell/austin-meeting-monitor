Urban Renewal and Austin's New Bond Program

Thursday, January 18, 1968 regular

Here are the key takeaways from Austin's January 18, 1968 City Council meeting:

  • Major Urban Renewal Project Approved:

    The council approved the Brackenridge Urban Renewal Project, a significant federal-backed initiative for "slum clearance," land reallocation for the University of Texas and state offices, and a detailed plan for resident relocation.

  • New Personal Bond Program:

    A new personal bond program was adopted to improve the system for releasing individuals from custody, with authorization for community-based oversight, including the appointment of a layperson to the board.

  • Key Land Use and Zoning Decisions:

    The council actively managed city development by approving a residential zoning change for a property on Beech Drive, denying a commercial rezone for a Rio Grande Street property, and referring a Glen Oaks development appeal to the Planning Commission.

  • Infrastructure & Property Updates:

    The city approved contracts for electric utility upgrades and equipment (including $105,960 for transformers), oversaw the sale of 18 houses for demolition or relocation, and permitted a private boat dock construction.
