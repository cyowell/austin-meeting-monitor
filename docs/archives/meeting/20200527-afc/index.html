Austin Police, Finances & Employee Appeals

Wednesday, May 27, 2020 Audit and Finance Committee Regular Meeting

  • Community Policing Under Scrutiny:

    An audit found mixed results and cultural challenges in police community engagement efforts, highlighting the need for better performance measurement and dedicated officer time for building community trust.

  • Boosting Financial Stability:

    New policies aim to increase the city's general fund reserves from 12% to 14% of operating costs and update property tax cap rules to align with state law, strengthening resilience against future economic downturns.

  • Key Employee Oversight Appointments:

    The committee re-appointed a member and scheduled interviews to fill a vacancy on the Municipal Civil Service Commission, which handles appeals related to city employee discharges, promotions, and disciplinary actions.
