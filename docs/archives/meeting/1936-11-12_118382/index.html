Thursday, November 12, 1936 regular
Transcript

  • Major Gas Infrastructure Projects Approved:

    Extensive new gas mains were authorized for installation by the Texas Public Service Company across numerous city streets, including Niles Road, Harris Avenue, and Burleson Road, significantly expanding the city's utility network.

  • Strict Rules for Building Site Safety:

    Contractors were granted permits to occupy public sidewalks and streets during construction, under strict conditions for public safety walkways, guard rails, night lighting, storm water management, property restoration, and a $5,000 surety bond.

  • Road Widening for Traffic Relief:

    Two property owners received permission to adjust curbs on East 1st Street and San Jacinto Boulevard, at their own expense, to widen travel lanes and alleviate traffic congestion.

  • Significant Zoning Change Recommended:

    The Zoning Board of Adjustment proposed rezoning an *entire block* from residential to commercial (8th to 9th Streets, Sabine to East Avenue) to address unsuitable residential conditions, including "shacks where no sanitary facilities are available," avoid "spot zoning," and align with nearby commercial development.
