Lakefront Tower, Sister City, Bond Sale Halted

Thursday, September 30, 1965 regular

  • New Sister City:

    Austin formally embraced Belo Horizonte, Brazil, as its sister city, with discussions on cultural exchange and a potential street naming.

  • Lake Austin Development:

    Approved the "Lake Austin Towers" project, a 72-unit high-rise with boat docks, to be partially constructed over the lake, under strict flood and environmental stipulations.

  • Bond Sale Halted:

    Delayed a $14 million bond sale for electric, water, and sewer projects due to rising interest rates, aiming to secure better financial terms later.

  • Property Tax Review:

    Council initiated a review of undeveloped property tax valuations after a citizen alleged inequities unfairly burdened homeowners.

  • Affordable Housing Rents:

    Scheduled a meeting with the Housing Authority to address concerns that housing project rents were too high for low-income residents.
