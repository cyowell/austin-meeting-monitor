Austin's Water Future: Meters & Conservation

Thursday, March 5, 2020 Austin Water Oversight Committee Regular Meeting

  • Smart Water Meter Rollout:

    A $95 million advanced metering infrastructure (AMI) system was recommended for approval, aiming to improve water conservation, quickly detect leaks, and provide residents with detailed water usage information.

  • Wastewater Plant Pollution Alert:

    Concerns were raised during a public hearing about potential pollution (dark water, chlorine smell) from the Dessau Wastewater Treatment Plant, prompting Austin Water to investigate and report on environmental conditions.

  • Funding Water Source Protection:

    Discussions explored new funding methods, such as a proposed $1/month water connection fee, to acquire and preserve land vital for long-term water quality and supply.

  • Nearly $1 Billion Infrastructure Plan:

    An overview of Austin Water's 2020-2024 capital plan outlined almost $1 billion in investments to maintain and upgrade the city's extensive water and wastewater infrastructure.
