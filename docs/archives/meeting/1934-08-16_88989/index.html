PWA Funds Water Overhaul, New Gas Mains

Thursday, August 16, 1934 regular

  • Massive Water System Upgrade:

    Secured a $160,000 federal loan and grant from the Public Works Administration (PWA) to finance extensive improvements to the city's water works, including installing several miles of new 20-inch and 12-inch main lines.

  • New Gas Lines on Hartford Road:

    Approved Texas Public Service Company to lay new 6-inch gas mains, with city oversight on construction and street restoration.

  • Architectural Standards Aligned with PWA:

    Adopted a revised contract for architects working with the city, mandating compliance with federal Public Works Administration regulations.
