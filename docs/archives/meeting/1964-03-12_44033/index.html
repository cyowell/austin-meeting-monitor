Austin's Heated Debate: Discrimination, Golf, Growth

Thursday, March 12, 1964 regular

  • Austin Confronts Racial Discrimination:

    The City heard a comprehensive report detailing racial discrimination within the community and city departments. A strong debate ensued over recommendations to establish a Human Relations Commission to combat inequality, weighing legal ordinances against voluntary efforts.

  • Golfers Protest Steep Fee Increases:

    Numerous citizens voiced strong opposition to recently implemented, significant hikes in city golf course fees, arguing the changes negatively impact seniors, youth, and low-income players, prompting the Council to reconsider the new rates.

  • Major Urban Planning Decisions:

    The Council approved new flexible zoning for apartment developments, began efforts to acquire land for future expansion of Brackenridge Hospital, and authorized the relocation of Rocky River Road to facilitate a new water treatment plant.
