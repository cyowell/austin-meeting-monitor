Tuesday, June 7, 2022 Austin Energy Utility Oversight Committee Regular Meeting
Official City Record Agenda Watch Video Transcript

  • Fayette Coal Plant Debate:

    Public speakers pressed Austin Energy to accelerate the closure of its share in the Fayette coal plant, urging City leaders to use financial oversight to halt funding and meet climate goals.

  • Utility Rate Hike Review:

    An ongoing review proposes increasing electric base rates and consolidating customer tiers, prompting calls from Council members for early public discussions on the potential impact to low-income and energy-efficient residents.

  • Customer Aid Program Scrutiny:

    Concerns were raised about Austin Energy's Customer Assistance Program allegedly enrolling ineligible wealthy residents, leading to a request for a city auditor investigation into the program's effectiveness.

  • Future Infrastructure Plans:

    Discussions began on potentially burying major electric lines along the Project Connect light rail corridor on North Lamar to optimize public space and support future transit development.
