Austin Approves Street, Cemetery & Garbage Facility Funding

Monday, January 1, 1906 regular

• Approved immediate spending on street improvements, including $162 for street sweeping and $150 for extra street labor

• Authorized $3,000 for a garbage crematory (an early waste management facility), pending budget committee review

• Approved $500 for cemetery repairs and received a positive sexton's report confirming proper maintenance

• Cleared police officer Ben Piper of misconduct charges after investigating a December 25th incident where he struck a suspect with his pistol; the committee found his actions justified

• Approved six-month supply contracts for city provisions including beef, groceries, and other goods for municipal operations, prisons, and hospitals
