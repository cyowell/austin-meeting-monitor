Austin's Parking Meter Debate and Growth

Thursday, September 23, 1937 regular

  • Heated Debate Over Parking Meters

    Hundreds of citizens protested the installation of parking meters, while others spoke in favor. The City took the matter under advisement, postponing a decision.

  • New Commercial Center Approved

    A significant zoning change was enacted, transforming all four corners of the Chicon and Haskell Streets intersection from residential to commercial to establish a new community business hub.

  • Expanding Gas Infrastructure

    Permits were granted to Texas Public Service Company for new gas main installations across several city streets, enhancing utility services for residents.

  • Retroactive Tax Reductions

    Property tax assessments for a specific house on state land were significantly reduced for over ten years, correcting what was deemed "excessive" prior valuations.
