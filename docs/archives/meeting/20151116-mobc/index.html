Austin's Mobility Vision & Road Debates

Monday, November 16, 2015 Mobility Committee Regular Meeting

  • Future Mobility Vision:

    Austin partners with the Rocky Mountain Institute to develop a cutting-edge "mobility as a service" future, focusing on electric, autonomous vehicles and city redesign.

  • Mopac South Update:

    An update on the Mopac South project presented six express lane options, with projections showing traffic will still worsen by 2035, but less severely than doing nothing.

  • Traffic Management Modernization:

    A $2.25 million contract is proposed to expand and outsource the city's Transportation Management Center, aiming for 24/7 monitoring, improved incident response, and greater travel reliability.

  • Coyote Policy Debate:

    Citizens voiced strong opposition to proposed coyote management recommendations, warning they could lead to trail closures and increased danger to children and pets.

  • Road Use Conflicts:

    Public comment highlighted ongoing community debates about road restriping for bike lanes versus car traffic capacity on key routes like East Riverside and Mesa Drives.
