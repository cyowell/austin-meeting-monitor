Austin Airport Named, New Telecom, Key Policy Changes

Thursday, November 3, 1994 regular

  • Austin-Bergstrom Airport Inaugurated:

    Following a public hearing, the city's new airport was officially named Austin-Bergstrom International Airport. Millions in contracts were also approved for its development, covering services like recruiting disadvantaged businesses, insurance, and land acquisitions.

  • Key Policy & Infrastructure Advancements:

    The council finalized a Campaign Finance Reform Ordinance, approved an independent audit for the Electric Utility, and initiated steps to partner on an advanced city-wide telecommunications network.

  • Community Planning & Public Engagement:

    Public hearings were scheduled to discuss a Central Urban Redevelopment (CURE) proposal and new regulations for sidewalk cafes. The council also approved funding for public health programs, including school-based clinics and parenting education.
