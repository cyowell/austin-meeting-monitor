Austin Greenlights 234 New Affordable Homes

Thursday, October 10, 2024 Austin Housing Finance Corporation (AHFC) Board of Directors Regular Meeting
  • Approved funding for three major affordable housing developments, creating nearly 234 new units across the city.
  • Key projects include Ovata at Rosedale, a 70-unit development in District 10 featuring on-site childcare, The Sasha with 60 units in District 3, and The Bailey at Berkman, offering 104 supportive housing units.
  • These projects are vital for utilizing a recently awarded 9% Low Income Housing Tax Credit, bridging financing gaps for crucial affordable housing initiatives.
  • The Ovata at Rosedale project was specifically highlighted as the first major affordable development in District 10, bringing much-needed housing near parks, transit, and medical facilities.
