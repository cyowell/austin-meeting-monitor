Transcript

  • Major Utility Infrastructure Approved:

    The city granted permits for Southwestern Bell Telephone Company to install new underground phone conduits and for Texas Public Service Company to lay new gas mains across several streets, expanding essential services.

  • Public Protest Against Street Name Change:
