Thursday, February 26, 1914 regular
Transcript
  • New safety regulations were adopted for storing gasoline and other flammable liquids, exempting authorized steel tanks and private garages.
  • Major infrastructure improvements were approved, including a $22,000 investment for a new water main in East Austin and extensive paving projects on West 8th Street.
  • Significant property tax assessments were cancelled or reduced for both a large mortgage company and individual homeowners based on over-assessment or legal interpretations.
  • Funding was appropriated for a nurse providing health services to Austin's Public Free Schools.
