Austin Boosts Roads, Parks, Water

Monday, October 11, 1948 special called
  • City leaders authorized $1,183,000 in public funding for major infrastructure upgrades.
  • Significant investments were approved for improving streets, roads, bridges, and acquiring land for state highway rights-of-way, totaling $443,000.
  • The largest allocation, $640,000, was designated for expanding and enhancing the city's water works and system.
  • Public parks and playgrounds also received $100,000 for new facilities and improvements.
