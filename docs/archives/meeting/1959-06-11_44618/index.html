Thursday, June 11, 1959 regular
Transcript

  • Community Recreation Prioritized:

    Initial support was given for a new playground in the St. Elmo area, and a policy was approved for the city to provide utilities to local ball teams under specific conditions.

  • Major Street and Zoning Changes:

    East 9th Street was permanently closed for Interregional Highway development, a portion of Duval Street was vacated and sold, and several properties underwent significant zoning reclassifications.

  • Urban Renewal Expansion:

    Funding for surveys and plans for the Thomas Jefferson Heights/Kealing Urban Renewal Project was increased, advancing a major city development initiative.

  • FAA Halts Road Project:

    A planned extension of Kleberg Lane was stopped by the Federal Aviation Administration due to concerns about interference with flight landing systems, prompting a search for a new route.
