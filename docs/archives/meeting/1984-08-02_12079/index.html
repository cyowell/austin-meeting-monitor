Thursday, August 2, 1984 regular
Transcript

  • Protecting Capitol Views:

    Enacted a new ordinance to protect scenic views of the State Capitol, following strong public support.

  • Major Bond Election Set:

    Scheduled a September 8, 1984 bond election to fund significant capital improvements, including water system upgrades, new fire stations, road projects, and the crucial acquisition of 2.6 acres adjacent to Zilker Park for preservation.

  • Taxi Service Overhaul:

    Determined the allocation of taxi permits to several local companies (Airline, Roy's, Yellow, Capitol) after a lengthy public hearing, impacting city transportation services.

  • Electoral System Challenge:

    Voted to negotiate a settlement in the *Overton v. City of Austin* lawsuit, which challenged the city's electoral system and could lead to the implementation of single-member districts.

  • Housing Project Funding Denied:

    Declined to fund the Nueva Vida Low-Cost Housing Project, with the City Manager citing its origins as a private enterprise.
