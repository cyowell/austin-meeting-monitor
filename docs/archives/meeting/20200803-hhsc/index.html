Central Health Board & Sobriety Center Discussed

Monday, August 3, 2020 Health & Human Services Committee Special Called Meeting
  • The committee advanced the process for appointing new members to the Central Health Board of Managers, which oversees healthcare services for Travis County residents.
  • A final vote on the board appointments was deferred to allow for collaborative discussions with partner agencies before selecting the final candidate.
  • A future meeting was scheduled to address a vacancy at the local sobriety center, indicating ongoing attention to public health resources.
