Austin: Half-Holidays, Fines, Property Tax

Thursday, May 20, 1915 regular

  • City Employees Secured Summer Half-Holidays:

    City workers were granted Saturday half-holidays for the summer months, from June 5th to September 4th.

  • Infrastructure Growth:

    Approved $150 for the extension of a sanitary sewer line on West 25th Street.

  • Council Shows Clemency:

    Multiple fines were remitted or suspended, notably for a motorcyclist deemed of "excellent character" and a drunkard whose fine was held in suspense, contingent on future good behavior.

  • Property Tax Relief:

    A significant reduction was granted on property assessments for Lewis Hancock in Aldridge Place, lowering his tax burden.

Regular meeting of the City Council: Austin, Texas, May 20, 1915: The Council was called to order by the Mayor: Roll called : Present Mayor Wooldridge, Councilmen Anthony, Bartholomew, Haynes & Powell 5 Absent none: Reports of City Officers: The Mayor laid before the Council the report of the Sanitary Inspector for the month of City Employees Petitioned holiday Saturday half holiday June 5 to Sep 4, '15 April 1915, which was read and ordered filed : The Mayor laid before the Council a petition from Employees of the different Departments, asking that the offices of the City Government be closed on Saturday at 12 O'clock ,from June 5th to Sept 4th 1915.
