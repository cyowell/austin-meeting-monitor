Airport, Tax Abatement, Zoning & Seaholm Future

Thursday, November 7, 1991 regular

  • Economic & Land Use Policy:

    Adopted new tax abatement guidelines for 1991-93, including requirements for hazardous materials disclosure, and established an Industry Expansion Area. The council also closed public hearings on major annexations, affecting areas like Walnut Creek Business Park and the Barton Creek Greenbelt.

  • Infrastructure & Development:

    Approved a consultant for the Bergstrom Airport Master Plan, initiating long-term development strategies. Additionally, the council heard and conditionally approved significant zoning changes for large commercial projects, including specific restrictions on access and setbacks.

  • Community & Environment:

    Citizens raised concerns regarding the Edwards Aquifer and city finances. The council committed to preserving the historic Seaholm Power Plant site for future cultural and civic use and scheduled a public hearing for upcoming social service agency funding.
