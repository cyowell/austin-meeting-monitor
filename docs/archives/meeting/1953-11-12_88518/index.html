Austin: Phone Rate Hike, Roads & Water Deals

Thursday, November 12, 1953 regular

  • Telephone Rate Increase Demand:

    City leaders were pressed to finally schedule a public hearing on Southwestern Bell's request for a phone rate increase, a matter pending for months.

  • Road & Infrastructure Investments:

    Over $110,000 in contracts were approved for significant road improvements, including paving on Jefferson Street and Oakmont Boulevard, and widening West 1st Street.

  • Strategic Water Service Expansion:

    A contract was approved to supply water and services to a new Travis County Water Control District, including provisions for future city annexation of the area.

  • Zoning Debates & Changes:

    The council heard pleas from a resident to maintain residential zoning for family land against an industrial recommendation and approved a commercial zoning change on West 5th Street.
