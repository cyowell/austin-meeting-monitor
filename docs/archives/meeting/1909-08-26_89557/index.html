Austin Budget: Fire Racks, Mules, and Tax Collection

Thursday, August 26, 1909 regular

• Fire Department Equipment: Council approved $360 to build drying racks for the city's fire companies.

• Sanitary Department Purchases: The city budgeted $400-$350 for pairs of mules to support street cleaning operations.

• Tax Collection Authority: Council instructed the city attorney to demand that the county sheriff turn over all money collected from the city's court judgments and provide itemized billing for court costs.

• City Clerk Salary: An ordinance setting the city clerk's salary was passed after its third reading.

• Administrative Adjustments: Councilman Gracy took a two-week leave beginning August 28, with the Mayor temporarily assuming his financial oversight duties.
