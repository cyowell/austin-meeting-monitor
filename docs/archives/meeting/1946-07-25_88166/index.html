Austin Debates Trailer Parks, Zoning, Paving

Thursday, July 25, 1946 regular

  • Trailer Park Controversy:

    Following significant public protest over potential unsanitary conditions, the city rescinded a previously approved zoning change for a trailer park on Barton Springs Road and scheduled a new public hearing.

  • Grocery Store Rezoning Battle:

    A contentious proposal to rezone a residential area for a grocery store faced strong neighborhood opposition and a negative recommendation from the Board of Adjustment, yet a public hearing was still set.

  • City Pledges Street Paving:

    Residents on Red River Street successfully advocated for city-funded paving to mitigate dust from heavy municipal truck traffic, with property owners agreeing to cover curb and gutter costs.

  • Industrial Expansion & Utility Upgrades:

    A large tract near Georgetown Road was officially rezoned for industrial development, and the city moved to acquire a private water system for the Theodor Low Heights Addition, alongside approving new gas line installations across several streets.
