Monday, January 6, 1908 regular
Transcript

• Street improvements contract awarded – The city approved a $1,745 contract with J.W. Rutledge to cut down (grade) Colorado Street and West 8th Street near the new City Hall building.

• Mayor vetoes land purchase over neighborhood concerns – Mayor Maddox rejected a $100 property acquisition deal after residents petitioned against it, saying the city's intended use would harm their homes; the council upheld his veto.

• New City Hall furnishings approved – The council accepted a $1,190 bid from R.A. Plumbe to supply a rostrum and other furniture for the new City Hall.

• Sewer safety measure ordered – City officials were instructed to ensure the Austin Sewerage Company properly connects to City Hall's sewer system to prevent backup problems.

• Various street and infrastructure petitions referred – The council received requests to close an alley, open E. 5th Street, gravel Barton Springs Road, and refund fees to citizens, all sent to appropriate committees for further review.

Unfinished business; President Haynes in the chair; The Chair laid before the Council the following veto message from the Mayor; Austin, Texas, January 3rd 1908. Mayors Veto To the Honorable, The City Council of Austin, Texas . Gentlemen: I return you, without my approval, your ordinance appropriating the sum of one hundred dollars ( $ 100.00 ) for the purpose of closing trade with Stacy-Robbins-Covert Company for part of outlet No 11, Division " Z " City of Austin. My reason for this veto is that a goodly number of the residents in the neighborhood have filed a petition with the City Clerk in which they claim that the use of said lot for the purposes the City intends "would be a detriment to our homes and families ".If such is the case these Citizens should be given an opportunity to be heard before the trade is finally closed. Respectfully F M Maddox, Mayor. Alderman Crooker moved that the vote by which the ordinance was passed be reconsidered, which motion prevailed.
