Austin Housing Projects Secure Funding Steps

Thursday, January 26, 2023 Austin Housing Finance Corporation (AHFC) Board of Directors Regular Meeting
  • Approved an initial step (an "inducement resolution") for securing private activity bonds for the Stage at Franklin Park housing project.
  • Scheduled upcoming public hearings to discuss issuing private activity bonds for two other developments: Libertad Austin at Gardner and Woodway Square.
  • These actions are key steps toward financing new affordable housing options in Austin through the use of private activity bonds.
