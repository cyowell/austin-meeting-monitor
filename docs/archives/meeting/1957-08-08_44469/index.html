Big Water Fix, Zoning Boosts Growth

Thursday, August 8, 1957 regular

Here's a concise look at Austin's agenda:

  • City Expansion & Development:

    Major zoning changes were approved for recently annexed areas, defining land use and building height. Specific industrial-to-commercial rezonings were also passed, a portion of St. Johns Avenue was vacated, and plans progressed to acquire land for a future highway loop.

  • Critical Infrastructure Upgrades:

    An emergency project was approved to replace a broken 24-inch water main under the Colorado River. Additionally, essential repairs were funded for a key water filter plant, and contracts were awarded for new electrical voltage regulators, paving, and a storm sewer.

  • Financial Stability & Airport Business:

    The city's tax rate was tentatively kept steady at the previous year's level. Discussions also covered a new rent-a-car company seeking space at the airport, anticipating a new terminal.
