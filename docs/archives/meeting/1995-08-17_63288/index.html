Austin Votes on Stadium, Airport, and Green Future

Thursday, August 17, 1995 regular

Here's a look at key decisions from the August 17, 1995 Austin City Council meeting:

  • Massive Airport Investment & Stadium Vote:

    Approved over $400 million in bonds for critical airport system upgrades and called a special election to decide on $10 million in bonds for a new baseball/multi-use stadium.

  • Pledged to be an "Energy Smart City":

    Committed Austin to joining cities worldwide in the "Cities for Climate Protection Campaign."

  • Boosted Affordable Housing:

    Authorized managing a state grant to provide down payment assistance for first-time homebuyers and applied for $23 million in bond authority for future housing initiatives.

  • Funded Key Public Amenities:

    Approved construction for a new pool and bathhouse at Dick Nichols Park and allocated funds for ADA-compliant curb ramps in East Austin.
