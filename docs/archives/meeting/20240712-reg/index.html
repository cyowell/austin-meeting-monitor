Austin Budget: Higher Costs, Key Services, Staff

Friday, July 12, 2024 Austin City Council Regular Meeting
  • Austin's proposed $5.9 billion budget for 2025 focuses on essential city services, major infrastructure projects, and employee investments.
  • Typical homeowners face a 5.7% property tax increase, with average monthly utility and fee bills rising by $22.70 in 2025.
  • Key investments include opening new park facilities, funding affordable housing programs, establishing new fire and EMS stations, and significant utility system upgrades.
  • The budget boosts civilian staff wages by 4%, increases the city's living wage to $21.63 per hour, and expands paid paternity leave.
  • Public input emphasized urgent needs for climate resiliency, homelessness support, and enhanced park maintenance, with some residents questioning proposed police budget increases.
