Austin's Big Road Plan & Debt Relief

Thursday, February 21, 1935 regular

  • Major Roadway Expansion Approved:

    The city greenlit extensive land purchases, including numerous private lots and portions of existing streets, to facilitate the "southerly extension of South Congress Avenue" and improvements to the "San Antonio Road (Post Road)." This signals significant urban planning for future transportation needs.

  • New Bridge for Monroe Street:

    Construction of a bridge over Bouldin Creek at Monroe Street was authorized, with a contract awarded for $2,338.70.

  • Significant Debt Repayment:

    Over half a million dollars in matured city bonds and interest coupons were approved for destruction, indicating substantial progress in paying off municipal debt.
