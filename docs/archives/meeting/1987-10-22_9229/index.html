Thursday, October 22, 1987 regular
Transcript

  • Utility Rates Set & Key Land Policy Shifts:

    Following a public hearing, the city finalized gas rates for Southern Union Gas, largely reinstating previous charges while allowing for service relocation increases. Separately, the Council initiated immediate proceedings to remove Steiner Ranch from the city's limited purpose area.

  • Major Development & Environmental Rules Passed:

    New "Development Processing" and "Performance Overlay" ordinances were adopted, establishing updated guidelines for city growth. A contentious waiver was also granted for a subdivision within a sensitive watershed area.

  • Community Health & City Branding Initiatives:

    Plans were approved to establish new pharmacies at the Rosewood-Zaragosa and East Austin Health Centers to expand public services. The "Absolutely, Positively Austin" marketing campaign also received official endorsement.

  • Transportation & Infrastructure Investments:

    A five-year franchise was granted to Kerrville Bus Company for charter services, and dedicated sales tax funds were approved for essential roadway improvements across the city.
