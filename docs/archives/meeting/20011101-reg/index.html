Austin Grows, Goes Green, Boosts Safety

Thursday, November 1, 2001 Austin City Council Regular Meeting

  • Growing Austin & Green Energy:

    The city significantly expanded its boundaries through multiple land annexations, while also committing to a greener future with a multi-million dollar contract for wind-generated electricity and an investment in an innovative fuel cell power plant.

  • Enhanced Public Safety & Services:

    The council approved increased police staffing, bolstered the 9-1-1 emergency system, and funded critical water infrastructure upgrades. They also approved a rate increase for TXU Gas utility services after a public hearing.

  • Community & Economy Focus:

    Members discussed the significant role of music in Austin's economy, approved renovations for public wading pools, and advanced plans for a safe pedestrian crossing on Barton Springs Road in Zilker Park.
