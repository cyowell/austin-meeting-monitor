Austin Housing: New Homes & Rental Aid

Thursday, August 28, 2025 Austin Housing Finance Corporation (AHFC) Board of Directors Regular Meeting

  • New Affordable Housing Planned:

    Approved land for "Sycamores at Pleasant Valley," a 75-unit affordable housing community in District 2 designed for lower-income families.

  • Funding Greenlit for Housing:

    Authorized $25 million in revenue bonds to finance the "Sycamores at Pleasant Valley" affordable housing development, following a public hearing.

  • Expanded Rental Assistance Program:

    Approved a $1.4 million annual contract to provide tenant-based rental assistance, utilizing federal funds to help more residents afford their homes.
