Millions for Water & Wastewater Upgrades

Thursday, May 30, 1985
  • Approved a $3.7 million contract for new wastewater treatment facilities (Package Plants 5 & 6) at Williamson Creek, significantly boosting sewage processing capacity.
  • Authorized a $546,743 special change order (a 26% increase over the original contract) to construct a major 16-inch water line for Jollyville Road, Phase I.
  • These critical infrastructure upgrades were fast-tracked through an emergency meeting as part of the Capital Improvements Program.
