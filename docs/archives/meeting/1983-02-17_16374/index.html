Austin: Water, Mobile Homes, Public Lands

Thursday, February 17, 1983 regular

  • New Water Conservation Rules:

    The city adopted an ordinance and administrative policies to manage water use during potential shortages.

  • Protecting Neighborhood Zoning:

    The Council unanimously instructed lobbyists to oppose state legislation that would limit Austin's control over placing mobile homes in residential subdivisions, aiming to protect local zoning.

  • Economic Growth & Urban Renewal Debates:

    "Blighted area" designations were approved for projects like a new furniture showroom (promising 80 jobs) and a printing press expansion (17 jobs), though these decisions sparked debate over the criteria for such designations.

  • Public Votes & Unique Projects:

    A public vote was set for using city parkland for livestock shows, and a proposal to paint the Seaholm Power Plant stacks in primary colors, funded privately, was expedited.
