Austin Energy: New Solar, Market Risks, Renter Aid

Monday, November 13, 2017 Austin Energy Utility Oversight Committee Regular Meeting
  • Austin Energy reported strong financials, added 11,000 customers, and increased renewable energy to 36% of its power supply, with plans to add another 150 MW of utility-scale solar capacity.
  • The utility is closely monitoring significant changes in the Texas energy market, including power plant closures expected to drive up summer electricity prices by 20%, and potential state legislative efforts to deregulate municipal utilities.
  • Plans are moving forward for a new $1.4 million downtown substation to support city growth and reliability. A study also confirmed that Austin Energy's multi-family energy efficiency rebates save residents, including low-income households, about $100 annually on utility bills without causing rent increases.
