Austin's Energy Future and Green Goals

Thursday, March 7, 1996 regular

  • Debated the future of the city's electric utility,

    exploring options like potential sale or public-private partnerships, though no formal request for proposals was approved at this meeting.

  • Prioritized environmental protection with new studies and programs,

    directing initiatives for Bull Creek Watershed monitoring, Barton Springs/Edwards Aquifer dye tracer studies, and promoting eco-friendly products.

  • Scheduled public hearings for significant community plans,

    including new exemptions for community gardens and the adoption of the 1995 Bicycle Plan.

  • Revised wastewater billing methodology,

    a change expected to reduce city revenues by approximately $600,000 but could impact resident bills.

  • Delayed improvements to emergency services

    after failing to approve a construction contract for additions to several EMS stations, requiring the project to be rebid.
