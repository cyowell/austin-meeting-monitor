Oak Hill Land Use Overhaul

Wednesday, May 7, 1986 special called
  • Austin officials held a special public hearing to discuss the future development of the Oak Hill Area.
  • Major zoning changes were advanced for a large section of Oak Hill, converting land from interim residential to a diverse mix of permanent residential, retail, and commercial uses.
  • These significant land-use reclassifications, many requiring conditions like joint access or water quality controls, received initial approval.
  • The comprehensive plan covers a broad area of southwest Austin, including sections near US 290 West and Convict Hill Road.
