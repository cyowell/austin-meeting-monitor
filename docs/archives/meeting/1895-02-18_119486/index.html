Austin Water Project Approved, Fire Department Upgrades

Monday, February 18, 1895 regular

• Water and Light System Funding Approved: The council appropriated $13,905.22 for engineering costs and General Electric Company payments related to Austin's new water and light system.

• Fire Truck Purchase Reduced and Passed: Despite initial concerns, the council approved a $1,800 fire truck purchase (originally proposed at $2,500) to modernize the fire department.

• Theater Safety Requirements Added: New ordinances passed requiring all theatres and public amusement venues to have 3-foot-wide emergency aisles kept clear during performances to prevent fire hazards.

• County Hospital Funding Dispute: The council voted to pressure Travis County to cover half the costs of maintaining the city and county hospital, which was currently costing Austin $4,000-5,000 annually.

• Cemetery and Tax Collection Updates: Minor ordinances adjusted cemetery services and reduced the fee for executing tax sale deeds from $2 to 50 cents.
