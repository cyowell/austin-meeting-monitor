Austin Rezones Hancock Tract for Retail

Tuesday, October 27, 1959 special called

  • Major Zoning Change Approved:

    City leaders rezoned approximately 38 acres of the Hancock tract from residential to general retail use.

  • Strategic Location:

    This significant parcel of land is situated at the intersection of Red River Street, East 41st Street, and the Interregional Highway (now I-35).

  • Opens Door for Commercial Growth:

    This policy decision paves the way for new retail development in a prominent area of the city.
