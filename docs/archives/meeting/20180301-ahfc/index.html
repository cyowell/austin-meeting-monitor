Funds for Housing, New Leader, Community Job Fair

Thursday, March 1, 2018 Austin Housing Finance Corporation (AHFC) Board of Directors AHFC Meeting
  • Secured $20 million in housing revenue bonds to finance the development of Nightingale at Goodnight Ranch, a new multi-family housing project.
  • Appointed Spencer Cronk as the new general manager for the Austin Housing Finance Corporation.
  • A major public job fair was announced for this Saturday at Eastside Memorial, featuring many employers including HEB, AT&T, and city departments like APD and Austin Fire.
