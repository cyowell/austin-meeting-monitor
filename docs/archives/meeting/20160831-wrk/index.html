Wednesday, August 31, 2016 Austin City Council Budget Work Session
Official City Record Agenda Watch Video Transcript

  • Tight Budget Forecast & Resident Costs

    Austin faces a projected $1 million deficit in FY18, with long-term financial strain expected. The average household will see a $10.24 monthly increase (3.3%) in city bills, partly to fund new transportation staff.

  • State Recapture Outrage

    City leaders voiced strong concern over $406 million in local property taxes being sent to the state for school district "recapture" payments, arguing it deprives Austin of essential local funds.

  • Proposed Savings & Staffing Debates

    To address budget shortfalls, proposals included finding $6-6.5 million in savings by potentially phasing in EMS workweek changes, shifting SXSW event funding from the general fund, or delaying new library and fire station projects. Debate also flared over projected city staff wage increases and calls for departments to explore cuts to new hires.

  • Non-Profit Funding & Process Review

    Questions arose about the allocation of a "Community Development Incentives Fund" for non-profit capacity building, specifically whether it's effectively reaching smaller, emerging organizations. The Mayor urged an earlier, more in-depth budget review process for next year to better align spending with affordability and workforce development priorities.
