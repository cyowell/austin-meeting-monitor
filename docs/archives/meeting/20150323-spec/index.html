Fixing Austin's Citizen Service & Code Issues

Monday, March 23, 2015 Austin City Council Special Called Meeting

  • City Services Under Review:

    Austin's 311, Code, and Police departments presented their systems for handling citizen requests, detailing efforts to improve responsiveness, tracking, and transparency for public inquiries and complaints.

  • Austin Energy Customer Service Criticized:

    Residents voiced significant frustration with Austin Energy's customer service, citing issues like disconnection threats and lack of clear communication. Calls were made to establish a public ombudsman or dedicated citizen advocate to help resolve utility disputes.

  • Code Enforcement & Development Transparency:

    Concerns were raised regarding lengthy code enforcement processes, potentially misleading "resolved" status updates, and a lack of notification to neighborhood planning teams about major new development projects.

  • Public Information Access Debated:

    A discussion occurred about the city's policy on releasing public information, particularly for code enforcement cases that proceed to municipal court, prompting questions about citizens' ability to access records without legal counsel.
