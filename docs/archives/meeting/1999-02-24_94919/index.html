Austin Tackles Environment, Health, & Growth

Wednesday, February 24, 1999

  • Barton Springs Zone Legal Issues:

    Discussed ongoing litigation related to development regulations in the environmentally sensitive Barton Springs Zone, including legal fees.

  • Community Health & Planning:

    Received updates on the local HIV/AIDS situation and a presentation on the Chestnut Neighborhood Plan.

  • Green Energy & Climate Action:

    Approved grants for climate protection programs and energy-efficient appliance rebates for a large apartment complex.

  • Airport Development:

    Authorized a contract for demolition and remediation work at the Austin-Bergstrom International Airport.
