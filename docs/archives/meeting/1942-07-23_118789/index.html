Thursday, July 23, 1942 regular
Transcript

  • Significant Utility Savings Approved:

    The City Council passed an ordinance reducing natural gas rates for residents and businesses, estimated to save consumers $52,000 annually through a compromise with the gas company.

  • Future Electric Rate Cuts & Refunds:

    Council initiated a plan to lower light and power rates by an additional $76,000 annually, retroactive to February 1942, with the City Manager instructed to prepare for consumer refunds.

  • Updated Taxi Regulations:

    New rules were approved for the operation of taxicabs and their drivers within the city.

  • Infrastructure Development:

    Permitted the Texas Public Service Company to install new gas mains on Fairmount Avenue.
