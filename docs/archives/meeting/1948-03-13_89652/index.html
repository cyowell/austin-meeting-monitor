Saturday, March 13, 1948 special called
Transcript

  • Colorado River Bridge Funding:

    Austin leaders approved amendments to the funding plan for a $50,000 low-water bridge project slated for construction across the Colorado River below Lake Austin.

  • Tax Levy Adjustment:

    The primary change involved modifying the annual tax amount collected to cover the interest payments and establish a sinking fund for the bridge's general obligation bonds.

  • Unanimous Approval:

    The ordinance amendment passed with full consensus from the city's leadership, moving forward this significant infrastructure project.
