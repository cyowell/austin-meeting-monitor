Tourism Reimagined, Tree Saved

Thursday, January 31, 1991 regular

  • Convention & Tourism Restructuring:

    The City reorganized its convention and tourism efforts, merging the Visitors Bureau into the Convention Center Department and dedicating funds to promote minority and historical tourism.

  • Major Economic Incentives:

    A significant tax abatement package, including fee waivers and a rebate program, was approved for Applied Materials, a key tech company.

  • A Tree's Victory:

    The Council voted to save a Cedar Elm tree at 807 E. 32 1/2 Street, overturning staff recommendations for its removal.

  • Supporting Local Music:

    Funding was allocated to conduct a feasibility study for a new Music Business Incubator.
