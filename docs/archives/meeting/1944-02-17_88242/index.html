Austin: Crime Reward, Postwar Youth, New Gas Lines

Thursday, February 17, 1944 regular

  • Public Safety & Workforce:

    A $500 reward was offered for information leading to the arrest of a taxi driver's murderer, while several taxi driver permits were approved, including two on a probationary basis after initial denial.

  • Postwar Planning & Youth:

    The city planned a meeting with civic organizations to establish a committee focused on youth welfare and post-World War II development.

  • Infrastructure & Utilities:

    New gas main installations were approved for several city streets, and a "beneficial" contract with the Lower Colorado River Authority was authorized.

  • Financial Relief:

    Penalties and some interest were remitted for various overdue city tax accounts, providing relief for taxpayers.
