New Smoking Rules, Housing Growth, East Austin Plans

Thursday, May 8, 2003 Austin City Council Regular Meeting

  • Public Smoking Policy Revised:

    New regulations restricting smoking in public places were approved, with specific exemptions carved out for certain bingo parlors, fraternal organizations, and billiard halls (age 21+, no food service).

  • Major Zoning & Housing Development:

    Significant rezonings and land use plan amendments were passed for East Austin's Holly and Govalle/Johnston Terrace neighborhoods, shifting former industrial areas toward mixed-use development. Rules for duplex residential construction were also updated to allow slightly larger structures.

  • City Limits Expansion & Infrastructure:

    Several new areas, including parts of Pioneer Hill and Avery Ranch, were fully annexed, expanding the city's boundaries. New funding was also approved for critical infrastructure projects like low water crossing safety gates and further development of bike routes.

  • Community & Environmental Initiatives:

    The city accepted grants for energy efficiency and low-income appliance replacement programs. A new Asian American Resource Center Advisory Board was established, and water quality variance determination rules were updated.
