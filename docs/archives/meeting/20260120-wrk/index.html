City Leadership Roles & Affordability Revamp

Tuesday, January 20, 2026 Austin City Council Work Session

  • New Committee Assignments:

    Austin is shaking up its internal leadership, with council members confirming their roles on various city committees and intergovernmental entities for the year.

  • Revitalizing Affordability Efforts:

    A key focus was reigniting the Regional Affordability Committee to tackle issues like housing, transportation, childcare, and workforce wages holistically.

  • Role Shifts & Openings:

    Specific changes discussed include new vice-chair appointments, a council member joining Public Safety, and an open spot on the Public Health Committee. A request to join the Audit and Finance Committee as a voting member was also debated.

  • No Action on Planning Commission:

    A listed item concerning Planning Commission appointments was mistakenly added to the agenda and was not discussed or acted upon.
