Austin's Future: District Votes & Campaign Rules

Monday, February 20, 1978 special called
  • Voters will consider a major shift to electing City Council members by district (an "8-1 plan" with 8 district representatives and an at-large Mayor), aimed at better reflecting city demographics and growth.
  • A controversial proposal to lift restrictions on political activity for city board and commission members in local elections will be placed on the public ballot.
  • The Council decided against expanding absentee voting to multiple locations, opting to keep it solely at the City Clerk's office to avoid additional costs.
  • Efforts to grant the Council more administrative power, such as allowing members to hire their own staff, were rejected and will not appear on the ballot.
