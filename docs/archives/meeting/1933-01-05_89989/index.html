City Growth: Taxis, Trains & Taxes

Thursday, January 5, 1933 regular
  • Approved the city's tentative budget for the year 1933.
  • Granted numerous individual licenses for taxicab operations to various drivers and companies, overseeing public transportation.
  • Authorized the construction of a new railroad spur track through West Fourth Street and approved a permit for a gasoline filling station with specific development and operating conditions.
  • Adjusted property tax records for residents, including removing an assessment for a demolished building and lowering past valuations for a doctor's office equipment due to claimed over-assessment.
