Austin Funds Big Projects & Local Groups

Monday, September 25, 1978 special called

  • Massive Infrastructure Investment:

    Approved a $554 million five-year Capital Improvements Program (CIP), earmarking funds for essential services like electric, water, and transportation, though some new service extensions were scaled back or deferred.

  • Hospital Expansion & Southwest Traffic Solutions:

    Greenlit a substantial $17 million for hospital upgrades and funded a $75,000 independent study to address pressing traffic and transportation challenges in the city's southwest quadrant.

  • Boost for Community & Parks:

    Allocated over $1.1 million to local social service agencies and $322,000 to arts organizations. Significant investments were also approved for parks, including Walnut Creek, Waller Creek, a new softball complex, and a 16-court tennis center.

  • Balanced City Budget & Employee Raises:

    Adopted the annual operating budget, which included widespread departmental reductions to prioritize core services, while also approving a 6% cost-of-living increase for most city employees.
