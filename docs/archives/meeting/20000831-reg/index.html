Barton Springs, Housing & Austin's Future Growth

Thursday, August 31, 2000 Austin City Council Regular Meeting

  • Barton Springs Zone Lawsuit Settled:

    Approved a settlement in a significant lawsuit concerning development rules in the environmentally sensitive Barton Springs Zone.

  • New Housing & Development Rules:

    Adopted new design and site standards for two-family homes and secondary apartment units, aiming to shape future housing options. The city also moved forward with annexing the 228-acre Canterbury Trails Area.

  • Community Investment & Support:

    Allocated funds for urban revitalization projects along East 11th Street, increased electric utility conservation rebates, and accepted a state grant to establish a police outreach program for immigrant families.

  • Key Infrastructure & Airport Growth:

    Authorized major construction for a water main as part of the Convention Center expansion and approved a new off-airport parking service for Austin-Bergstrom International Airport.

  • Utility Rate Review:

    Scheduled a public hearing to gather community input on proposed rate changes for the city's Water and Wastewater Utility.
