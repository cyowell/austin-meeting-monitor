Firefighter Deal, Downtown Issues, New Website

Thursday, December 17, 2009 Austin City Council Regular Meeting

  • Landmark Firefighter Contract Approved:

    A new 4-year agreement passed, introducing a "lateral entry" process to boost diversity, though some raised concerns about its $25.5 million cost amidst economic hardship.

  • Downtown Austin's Strengths & Struggles:

    A community survey revealed downtown is loved for its culture and walkability, but faces significant challenges with traffic, parking, rapid growth, and preserving historic character.

  • New Online City Services & Transparency:

    Plans for a revamped city website will include an online "checkbook" for public spending, a unified payment portal, and earlier public access to meeting agendas.

  • Toilet Rebate Program Paused:

    Austin's highly popular water-saving toilet rebate program was temporarily halted due to overwhelming demand, pending re-evaluation for future efficiency.
