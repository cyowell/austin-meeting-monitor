New Gas Station, Utilities, Public Funds Secured

Thursday, August 26, 1937 regular

  • New Drive-In Gas Station Approved:

    A drive-in gasoline station was permitted on Lower Georgetown Road, despite its residential zoning, subject to detailed construction and drainage requirements.

  • Major Utility Expansion:

    The Texas Public Service Company received approval to lay new natural gas mains across several city streets, expanding critical infrastructure.

  • City Fund Security Enhanced:

    Updated policies were enacted for depositing city funds in local banks, clearly defining collateral requirements and increasing oversight by the City Manager.

  • Public Use of Streets:

    Permission was granted for an Evangelical Free Church to hold open-air religious services on city streets and for two property owners to install flagstone sidewalks instead of standard concrete.
