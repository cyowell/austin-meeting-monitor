Austin: Car Fines, Public Sleepers, Damaged Heifer

Thursday, January 20, 1916 regular

  • Early Auto Regulation:

    The council issued new automobile driver licenses and notably remitted a $24.80 fine for a resident caught "driving an automobile without tail lights."

  • Social Order Fines Remitted:

    Fines were remitted for various minor infractions, including $14.80 for "sleeping in a public place" and the balance of a $12.80 fine for assault, based on a judge's recommendation and time served.

  • Infrastructure & Park Funding:

    A contract was approved for 16 tons of reinforced steel bars, and $1,000 was appropriated from the Park fund for Alamo Park materials and labor.

  • Dam Compensation:

    The city paid $53.95 to settle a lawsuit for a heifer allegedly lost due to backwater from the city's dam.

Regular meeting of the City Council; Austin, Texas, January 20, 1916. The Council was called to order by the Mayor: Roll called: Present Mayor Wooldridge, Councilmen Anthony, Bartholomew, Hyanes & Powell 5 Absent none. The minutes of the regular meetings of Jany 6th & 13th, and subsequent recessed meeting were read and adopted. Applications for Licenses as Drivers of Automobiles were received from the following named persons, viz: Harry M Elam, 1709 E 13th street A D Jones, Manor, Route 5. The applications were granted by the following vote: Yeas Mayor Wooldridge, Councilmen Anthony, Bartholomew, Haynes & Powell 5 Nays none Councilman Anthony offered the following resolution: Resolved by the City Council of the City of Austin, Texas: That, for good reasons unto the City Council shown, as is set forth more particularly in the attached statement filed with this resolution, the fine and cost imposed upon Roy Modrall in cause No 10238, in the Corporation Court of the City of Austin, Texas, for driving an automobile without tail lights, and amounting to $ 24.80, be and the same is hereby remitted. The resolution was adopted by the following vote: Yeas Mayor Wooldridge, Councilmen Anthony, Bartholomew, Haynes & Powell 5 Nays none. Approved, A P Wooldridge, Mayor: Councilman Anthony offered the following resolution: Resolved by the City Council of the City of Austin, Texas: That the balance of the fine and costs, which balance is $ 12.80, in the case of the State of Texas vs Gabriel Solis, No 10298, in the Corporation Court of the City of Austin, Texas, for assault, be and the same is hereby remitted. This resolution is chiefly based upon the recommendation of the Corporation Judge and facts learned subsequently to the imposing of the fine upon said Gabriel Solis, and the further fact that he served out more than one-half the fine and costs imposed. The resolution was adopted by the following vote: Yeas Mayor Wooldridge, Councilmen Anthony, Bartholomew, Haynes & Powell 5 Nays none. Approved, A P Wooldridge, Mayor: Councilman Powell offered the following resolution: Whereas, the bid of the Walter Tips Co for sixteen tons of re-inforced steel bars, from the attached bid, is the lowest and best bid for said re-inforcing steel. Now, therefore, Resolved by the City Council of the City of Austin, Texas: That the contract to furnish sixteen tons, more or less, of re-inforced steel, of the size set forth in the attached bid, and at the prices named in such attached bid, be and the same is hereby accepted. For information and comparison the bid of W H Richardson & Co, for the same material is filed with the bid of Walter Tips Co.
