Austin Growth: Utilities, Rent, Pet Rules, Zoning

Thursday, May 5, 1949 regular

  • Bond Election for City Upgrades:

    Voters will decide on a $7.15 million bond to improve Austin's water, sewage, and electrical infrastructure, which is facing inadequacy.

  • Rent Control Debate on Hold:

    Builders urged an end to rent controls, but the city awaits state legislative action before considering changes.

  • Stray Dogs & Leash Law Push:

    Following over 5,000 stray impoundments, the Austin Kennel Club recommended a permanent leash ordinance for all dogs, which the city will study.

  • Major Zoning Changes Spark Debate:

    While many properties, especially along a proposed interstate, were approved for commercial rezoning, a request for commercial use on a narrow, dead-end street was met with strong resident protests and delayed for further review.

  • New Utility Projects & Drainage Woes:

    Gas mains were approved for several streets and a sanitary sewer project expanded, yet a significant drainage issue in another area was deemed too expensive for immediate, full resolution.
