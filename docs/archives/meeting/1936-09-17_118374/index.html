Austin: Taxis, Schools, and Public Works Updates

Thursday, September 17, 1936 regular

  • Enhanced Taxicab Safety & Service:

    Approved temporary permits for five additional taxicabs to serve the growing demand from the University and Legislature, and strengthened public safety by requiring all taxis to carry insurance covering death, personal injury, and property damage.

  • School District Expansion:

    Extended the city's boundaries *for school purposes only* to include the St. Elmo Common School District No. 43, allowing for the assessment and collection of school taxes in the newly added territory.

  • Infrastructure and Public Works:

    Authorized the installation of new gas mains in several city streets and finalized contracts for interior painting at John T. Allan and Austin Senior High Schools, funded in part by a federal Public Works Administration (PWA) project.
