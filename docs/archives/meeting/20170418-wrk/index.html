ATX CodeNEXT Maps: Density, Character, Housing

Tuesday, April 18, 2017 Austin City Council Work Session

  • New Zoning Maps Released:

    The CodeNEXT team unveiled the first draft of new zoning maps for Austin's land development code, marking a major step in reshaping the city's future growth.

  • Boosting Housing Supply:

    The new maps project the creation of 143,900 new housing units over the next 10 years, introducing diverse housing options like "house-scale" multi-unit buildings and duplexes designed to fit into existing neighborhood character.

  • Creating Walkable Communities:

    New mixed-use zoning categories are planned for corridors and activity nodes, encouraging more walkable commercial areas and connecting new developments with improved streets, sidewalks, and trails.

  • Public Feedback Opportunities:

    Residents can now view the proposed maps online, compare existing and new zoning for their properties, and provide direct comments through an interactive tool. Public workshops and office hours are scheduled to gather community input.
