Austin Fine Remission & Drivers Licenses

Thursday, August 5, 1915 regular
  • Approved the remission of a Recorder's Court fine for citizen Ed Johnson, following a petition.
  • Granted new drivers' licenses to three applicants: E.B. Earnest, Geo. Fiegel Jr., and J.M. Benson.
  • Received and filed routine reports from city departments, including the Assessor & Collector, Superintendent of Streets & Sewers, and the Marshal.

Full Transcript

321 .Regular meeting of the City Council : Austin, Texas, August 05, 1915. Hon E C Bartholomew, Mayor, pro tem,presiding:Roll called : Councilmen Anthony, Bartholomew, Haynes & Powell 4 Absent excused, Mayor Wooldridge 1 The minutes of the last regular and subsequent recessed meetings were read and approved. Reports of City Officers: The Chair laid before the Council the following reports,which were read and ordered filed: Assessor & collector, Supt Streets & Sewers,Marshal Councilman Anthony to whom was refered the petition of Ed Johnson to have finein Recorders Court remitted, presente a verbal report recommending that the petition be granted.The recommendation was adopted by the following vote : Yeas Councilmen Anthony, Bartholomew, Haynes & Powell 4 Nays none. Drivers license wre granted to the following named applicants; E B Earnest, 1701 Chicon Street. Geo Fiegel Jr 606 Guadalupe street. J M Benson , 1403 Garden street. The Council then adjourned. Ed Johnson Fine remitted Drivers license Jno. Johnson City Clerk
