Austin Funds Utilities, Reverses School Deal

Thursday, April 30, 1936 regular

  • Infrastructure Investment:

    To fund essential repairs and extensions for the city's water, electric, and sewer systems, $404,000 in old bonds were cancelled and new revenue bonds were issued at a lower 3% interest rate.

  • School Contract Reversal:

    A plumbing contract for high school additions was re-awarded to the lowest bidder, Austin Air Conditioning, after federal public works administrators rejected the initial award to a more expensive contractor.

  • Utility Expansion Approvals:

    Permits were granted for Southwestern Bell to install new telephone pole lines and for Texas Public Service Company to lay new gas mains across several city streets.
