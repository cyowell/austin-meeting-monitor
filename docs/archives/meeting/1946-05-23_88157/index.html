Thursday, May 23, 1946 regular
  • Approved a $336,000 bond for new fire stations and equipment, with the final vote uniquely held at a sick councilman's home.
  • The Director of Public Safety was granted a year's leave to serve with the Inter-Allied Control Commission in post-war Austria.
  • Initiated significant infrastructure upgrades, including the reconstruction of State Highway 29 and widespread gas main installations.
  • Adopted a new policy for residential subdivisions to share street construction costs, addressing rapid housing growth.
  • Denied a zoning change from residential to commercial for a Salina Street property after neighborhood protests.
