Wednesday, March 1, 1950 regular

  • Urban Planning & Transportation:

    The City Plan Commission was tasked with developing plans for future east-west boulevards and major thoroughfares (like North Guadalupe to Airport Boulevard and Koenig Lane) to guide city growth and subdivision development.

  • Infrastructure Expansion:

    Southern Union Gas Company received approval for extensive projects to lay new gas mains across numerous streets throughout the city, with the work under city supervision for installation and restoration.

  • Zoning Hearings:

    Public hearings were scheduled for notable zoning change requests, including a proposal to rezone property for the Boy Scouts from residential to commercial use.

  • Traffic Rule Updates:

    Amendments were passed to existing traffic ordinances, specifically updating regulations for 15-minute parking zones and loading zone locations.
