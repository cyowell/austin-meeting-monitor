Lobbying, Coyotes & Energy Aid Shake Up ATX

Wednesday, October 28, 2015 Audit and Finance Committee Regular Meeting

  • Coyote Management Debated:

    Citizens and an advisory committee clashed over Austin's coyote control, questioning the city's current lethal methods and proposing an audit of wildlife management contracts.

  • Low-Income Energy Program Audited:

    An audit uncovered significant issues with Austin Energy's low-income weatherization program, including unspent funds, inconsistent data, and some ineligible participants.

  • Downtown Business Fund Overhaul:

    Staff proposed expanding and reforming a downtown business loan program, funded by a $2.4 million settlement, to better support local businesses and music venues.

  • Ethics Resolution on Hold:

    A controversial resolution to enhance lobbyist registration and transparency faced strong opposition from design and building professionals, delaying further action.
