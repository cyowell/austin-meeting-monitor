Power Bills, Lamar Bridge & City Growth

Thursday, March 6, 1997 regular

Here's what happened at the Austin City Council meeting on March 6, 1997:

  • Electric Bill Savings:

    The council approved a significant $4.2 million annual decrease in electric utility rates, providing direct financial relief for customers.

  • Lamar Bridge's Future:

    After a contentious debate, the council prioritized plans for improved pedestrian and bicycle facilities on the historic Lamar Bridge, rejecting proposals to add more car lanes.

  • Public Health Investments:

    Substantial grant funds were secured to expand critical HIV prevention and care programs, alongside the approval for purchasing a new mobile dental vehicle to serve the community.

  • Key Development & Environmental Debates:

    Public hearings were scheduled for sensitive issues, including water quality in Barton Springs and the Barton Creek Watershed, and several proposed annexations reflecting the city's ongoing growth.
