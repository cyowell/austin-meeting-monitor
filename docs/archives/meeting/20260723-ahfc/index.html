Thursday, July 23, 2026 Austin Housing Finance Corporation

Here's what the Austin Housing Finance Corporation (AHFC) board, which includes City Council members, actually did at its meeting on July 23rd, 2026:

  • Approved the acquisition of 4800 Bowman Road:

    The AHFC board voted to buy the property at 4800 Bowman Road from the City of Austin for nearly $4 million ($3.95 million), using bonds from 2022. This site is slated for housing for residents earning 60% of the area's median income or less.

  • Passed unanimously despite strong public opposition:

    This acquisition, along with the previous meeting's minutes, was approved as part of the "consent agenda" without any formal objections or separate votes from board members.

  • Heated debate over environmental concerns:

    A resident, Zenobia Joseph, spoke passionately against the Bowman Road purchase. She highlighted the site's history as a "brownfield" condemned due to environmental racism.

  • Unaddressed contamination worries:

    The resident expressed serious concerns that placing low-income Black and Brown families on this site would expose them to contamination in an industrial zone. She questioned if the Texas Commission on Environmental Quality (TCEQ) had cleared the site, but there was no board discussion or response to these environmental concerns during the meeting.

  • Approved previous meeting minutes:

    The board also formally approved the minutes from its meeting held on May 28th, 2026.
