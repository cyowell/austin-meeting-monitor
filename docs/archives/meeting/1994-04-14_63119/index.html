Austin Eyes Salamander Protection, Skateboarding Ban

Thursday, April 14, 1994 regular

  • Environmental Initiatives:

    The city is taking proactive steps to protect the Barton Springs Salamander, implementing a ban on electric water heaters as a primary source in new large homes for energy efficiency, and launching a new commercial recycling program.

  • Public Health & Accessibility:

    The council renewed grants to combat tuberculosis and approved significant funding for Americans with Disabilities Act (ADA) improvements across multiple health and municipal facilities.

  • Skateboarding Public Hearing:

    A public hearing was scheduled to gather community input on a potential ban of skateboarding in certain public areas within Austin.
