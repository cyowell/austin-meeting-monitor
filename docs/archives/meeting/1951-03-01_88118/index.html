Austin: Rabies Scare, City Growth, Key Election

Thursday, March 1, 1951 regular

  • Public Health Emergency:

    Responded to a rabies outbreak by implementing a city-wide dog quarantine until May 31st.

  • Urban Development & Land Use:

    Approved contracts for new sewage and sanitary sewer systems, granted temporary street space for a significant building alteration on Congress Avenue, and enacted several commercial zoning changes.

  • Community & Future Planning:

    Permitted local circuses to operate for two days and tasked the Planning Commission with studies on city beautification, river improvements, and a major street extension.

  • Civic Engagement:

    Scheduled a general election for April 2nd to elect five new Councilmen, but rejected a proposal to include a public vote on increasing police and firemen's salaries.
