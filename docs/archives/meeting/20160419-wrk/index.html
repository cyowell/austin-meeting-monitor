ATX Water Future: Loans, Smart Meters, Debt Relief

Tuesday, April 19, 2016 Austin City Council Work Session

  • Austin Water pursued major financial moves

    to manage debt and fund future projects, including applying for over $167 million in low-interest state loans.

  • Key projects targeted for funding

    include a "smart meter system" to help detect leaks and manage water use, as well as improvements to wastewater treatment and expanding the reclaimed water system.

  • The Council debated fiscal transparency

    , with concerns raised about the cost-benefit analysis of reclaimed water projects, the city's existing water rights, and whether "unaffordable water bills" were adequately prioritized as a management concern.

  • It was clarified that applying for smart meter funding

    is an initial step, and final approval for the project itself would come back to the Council after detailed feasibility studies.
