Thursday, August 27, 1998 regular
Transcript

  • Financial & Utility Costs:

    Discussed proposed rate increases for water, wastewater, and drainage fees, alongside a new $30 fee for driver's license renewals tied to missed court appearances.

  • Major Airport Upgrades:

    Approved over $2.5 million in contracts for Austin-Bergstrom International Airport, including new roadway signage and a fire water line.

  • Conservation & Culture:

    Advanced land acquisition for the Balcones Canyonlands Preserve and considered a management agreement for the Austin Music Network.

  • Community Planning & Street Names:

    Adopted the Dawson Neighborhood Plan and ultimately denied the proposed renaming of Rosewood Avenue to Dorothy Turner Boulevard.
