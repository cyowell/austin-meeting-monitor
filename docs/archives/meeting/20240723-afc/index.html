Austin Addresses Streets, Gas, Homelessness, Future

Tuesday, July 23, 2024 Audit and Finance Committee Regular Meeting

  • Dark Roads & Safety Risks:

    Public outcry for immediate budget priority on fixing hundreds of broken freeway and interstate streetlights, citing safety hazards and difficulties reporting outages.

  • Natural Gas Rate Debate:

    Discussions on city oversight for natural gas rates, conservation, and methane leaks were postponed. City staff will develop a new public input process for ongoing rate negotiations and future franchise agreements.

  • Homelessness Efforts Reviewed:

    An audit follow-up noted progress in data collection and housing units, but highlighted gaps in long-term success measurement, contract accountability for providers, and clear prevention program strategies.

  • Planning Future City Bonds:

    City leaders discussed the process for a large upcoming bond package, aiming for early community and council input on projects ranging from mobility to parks, with a focus on comprehensive prioritization criteria.

  • Climate Research Funding:

    The vital UT City Climate Colab, a partnership informing Austin's climate resilience and infrastructure planning, seeks sustained funding to continue its work on preparing the city for environmental changes.
