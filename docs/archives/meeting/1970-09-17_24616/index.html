Austin Tackles Traffic, Student Protests, Elder Care

Thursday, September 17, 1970 regular

  • Student Protest Approved:

    Granted a parade permit for an estimated 10,000 student protestors from the UT campus to the Capitol, despite council members expressing concerns about traffic disruption and the protest's impact.

  • Elderly Care & River Development:

    Endorsed plans for a multi-million dollar Geriatric Center for the elderly on the former Fish Hatchery land, while also advancing a proposal for the Centex Corporation to develop 65 acres along the Colorado River, involving changes to the river's flood plain.

  • Major Traffic System Upgrade:

    Adopted the "TOPICS Program," committing to over $5 million in federal, state, and local funding for significant improvements to urban arterial streets, aiming to boost traffic capacity and safety.

  • City Finances & Employee Pay:

    Discussed potential reforms to property tax evaluation with a shift to a two-year appraisal cycle, and debated special 7-8% pay raises for police and fire departments to address rising living costs and job demands.
