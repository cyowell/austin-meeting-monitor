Thursday, March 6, 1980 regular
Transcript

  • Electric Bills Reduced:

    Approved a new electric rate ordinance and adopted a plan (Lovaca Settlement Trust Option I) projected to reduce customer utility bills by 2.5% to 3% over eight years.

  • Barton Creek Watershed Debate Continues:

    Held a lengthy public hearing and work session on new development controls for the Barton Creek Watershed, discussing approaches like density limits versus performance standards for water quality protection, and setting further work sessions to refine the ordinance.

  • MoPac Sound Barrier Approved:

    Authorized a $346,560 agreement with the State to construct a sound barrier wall along portions of Loop 1 (MoPac Boulevard) between Enfield Road and RM 2222.

  • Support for Community Services:

    Approved a lease agreement for city-owned property for the Center for Battered Women, and authorized a contract to implement a Residential Architectural Barriers Removal Program to improve accessibility.
