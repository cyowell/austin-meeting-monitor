Urban Expansion & Key City Projects

Thursday, June 3, 1954 regular

  • Firefighter Work Week Debate:

    The Council held a lengthy and heated discussion regarding a proposal for a 63-hour work week for firefighters and a 40-hour week for other city employees, ultimately deferring a decision until a full budget review could assess the financial implications.

  • Major Highway Development:

    Plans advanced for the "Interregional Highway" (likely I-35), with the City authorizing several eminent domain lawsuits to acquire necessary land for this significant infrastructure project.

  • Extensive Urban Rezoning:

    Multiple residential properties throughout Austin were officially rezoned for commercial and industrial use, indicating a period of significant urban growth and development.

  • New Water Plant & Local Permits:

    The City Manager was directed to expedite the operation of a new water treatment plant, and individual permits were approved, including one for a day nursery in a residential area and temporary construction space for downtown buildings.
