Facing Austin's $54M Shortfall: Smart Solutions

Wednesday, May 14, 2003 Austin City Council Work Session
  • Austin faced a projected $54.7 million budget shortfall for the upcoming fiscal year, requiring the city to find major cost savings and revenue adjustments.
  • Despite the significant deficit, city leaders aimed to balance the budget without introducing new fees and committed to maintaining core services such as police staffing, public health, and social programs.
