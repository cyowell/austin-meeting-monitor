Internet Access, Adult Venues, & Park Use

Thursday, March 5, 1998 regular

  • Internet Freedom in Libraries:

    Approved a 60-day pilot program to remove internet filtering software (CyberPatrol) from public library computers, exploring open access.

  • New Adult Business Oversight:

    Scheduled a public hearing to define and establish regulations for emerging "adult health clubs, adult-physical fitness studios, and adult spas."

  • Major Infrastructure & Environmental Projects:

    Greenlit significant water and wastewater upgrades (including a $2.9 million rehabilitation project), authorized parkland use for a new water main, and funded a $377,000 environmental cleanup at the Austin-Bergstrom International Airport.

  • Efficient City Operations:

    Approved a $2 million purchase of new city vehicles and finalized joint election agreements projected to save $175,000 in costs.
