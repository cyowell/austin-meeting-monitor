Local Taxes, Fees, and Cultural Arts

Wednesday, September 17, 1997 special called

  • New Annual Budget Adopted:

    The city approved its operating budget for the 1997-98 fiscal year, including adding staff to areas like Eastside Story, IT support, and development review.

  • Property Tax Rate Finalized:

    The municipal property tax rate was set at 54.01 cents per $100 valuation, with exemptions approved for agricultural, historic, disabled, and elderly properties.

  • Public Facility Fees Rolled Back:

    Some proposed fee increases were reversed, specifically restoring the Ziker Clubhouse fee to $500 and rescinding increases for Auditorium Shore and Waterloo Park.

  • Millions for Cultural Arts:

    Over $2.28 million was approved for cultural arts services contracts, providing significant funding to local arts organizations.
