Austin's Water Future, Downtown & City Issues

Thursday, May 7, 1992 regular

  • Voters to Decide Springs Fate:

    The Council approved placing the citizen-initiated "Save Our Springs" (SOS) water quality ordinance on an August 8th public ballot, alongside the Council's own alternative ordinance for voters to consider.

  • Major Downtown Investment:

    A significant $650,000 was approved for the purchase of a key downtown block, with plans initiated to explore establishing a Downtown Improvement District.

  • Public Scrutiny & Legal Actions:

    Citizens voiced concerns over city management and bond debt, while the Council also approved a $250,000 settlement for a wrongful death lawsuit.
