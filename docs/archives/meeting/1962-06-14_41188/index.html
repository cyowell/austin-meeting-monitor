Austin: New Gas Rates and Urban Growth

Thursday, June 14, 1962 regular

  • Utility Rate Hikes & Oversight:

    The City approved a rate increase for Southern Union Gas Company despite public calls for state oversight on utility rates.

  • Urban Development & Housing:

    A five-member Urban Renewal Agency was established, and new city-wide minimum housing standards were advanced to help qualify for federal urban renewal programs.

  • Public Safety & Infrastructure Boost:

    New pool safety rules were adopted to prevent non-swimmers from entering city pools, and major contracts were awarded for a 48-inch water main and extensive street resurfacing.

  • City Expansion & Property Disputes:

    The Council approved several annexations, including parts of Allandale Park, and addressed a private retaining wall encroaching on city property, raising traffic safety and utility concerns.
