City OKs Worship Street & Fire-Safe Sheds

Saturday, August 17, 1918 special called

  • Shed Construction Approved:

    A permit was granted for G. Flurry to build sheds.

  • Fire Safety Requirements:

    These new sheds were specifically required to feature fire-resistant sheet metal and corrugated iron roofs, a measure backed by the Fire Chief and Fire Marshal.

  • Street Closure for Worship:

    For two weeks, a section of 39th Street was temporarily closed to public vehicles from 8 PM to 10 PM nightly to allow for religious worship, following consent from local property owners.
