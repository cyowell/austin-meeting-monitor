Boosting Austin Homebuyer Aid and Senior Living

Thursday, December 12, 2002 Austin Housing Finance Corporation (AHFC)
  • Approved leadership appointments for the non-profit overseeing Primrose at Shadow Creek, a 176-unit senior living community.
  • Increased the maximum home purchase price limits for the Mortgage Credit Certificate program.
  • This expansion allows homebuyer assistance for existing homes up to $175,593 and newly constructed homes up to $205,677.

Approve the re-appointment of Michael C. Approve modification of the maximum allowable purchase price limits on the Austin Housing Finance Corporation's current Mortgage Credit Certificate program to equal $175,593 for existing homes and $205,677 for newly constructed homes.
