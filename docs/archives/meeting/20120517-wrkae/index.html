Austin Energy Rates & Bond Rating Debate

Thursday, May 17, 2012 Austin City Council Austin Energy Rates Work Session

  • Urgent Bond Rating Warnings:

    Financial experts cautioned that Austin Energy faces a bond rating downgrade due to declining financial margins and low cash reserves, which could significantly increase future borrowing costs for the utility.

  • Call for Rate Increases:

    Council members were urged to approve immediate and adequate rate increases to restore financial stability and prevent a credit rating reduction.

  • New Power Cost Allocation Method:

    The Council adopted a "four coincident peak" (4CP) method with a +/- 5% adjustment for allocating power production costs, influencing how different customer types will share the burden.

  • Debate Over "Off-System Sales":

    Discussions explored whether to incorporate volatile revenue from selling surplus power (off-system sales) into base rates to help reduce customer bills, facing concerns about its unpredictable nature.
