Austin Growth: Planning, Pensions, & Permits

Thursday, January 7, 1971 regular

  • Future City Planning:

    A 17-member committee was formed to strategize for a convention center and boost tourism, alongside continued city expansion through several land annexations.

  • Major Infrastructure Investments:

    Over $600,000 was allocated for large-scale water mains, sewer lines, road improvements, and channel upgrades across the city.

  • City Employee Benefits Enhanced:

    The retirement and pension system for city employees was significantly updated, including increased contributions, adjusted retirement ages, and new medical insurance for retirees.

  • Contentious Land Use Decisions:

    A major zoning case on I-35 allowed a 40-acre tract to be rezoned for commercial use without requiring an immediate subdivision, sparking debate on large-scale development and property access. Legal action was also authorized for numerous substandard structures.

  • Public Health & Services Adjustments:

    The city removed specific "raw milk" classifications from its code and postponed a hearing on a taxicab company's franchise cancellation amidst concerns over unpaid taxes.
