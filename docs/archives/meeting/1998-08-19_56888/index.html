Budget, Smart Growth, Tax Rate, Bond Talk

Wednesday, August 19, 1998
  • Extensively reviewed the 1998-99 operating and capital budgets, with briefings on key initiatives including Smart Growth, Neighborhood Planning, Housing, and environmental services.
  • Scheduled a public hearing for September 3rd to gather input on a proposed increase in the effective tax rate to 51.42 cents per $100 value for the upcoming fiscal year.
  • Discussed ordering an election for Revenue and General Obligation Bonds, though the ordinance authorizing the election was ultimately pulled from the agenda for this meeting.
  • Received a presentation on proposed guiding principles for Town Lake from the Town Lake Advisory Group.
