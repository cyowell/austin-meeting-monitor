Austin Plans for Security, Growth, & Environment

Thursday, February 14, 2002 Austin City Council Regular Meeting

  • Boosted Homeland Security & Public Safety Funding:

    Approved significant budget reallocations and debt for post-9/11 security, increasing funds and staff for police, fire, EMS, and health departments, while also allocating funds for community social needs.

  • Major Land Annexation and Development:

    Finalized the annexation and regulatory plan for the 1,957-acre Wildhorse Ranch, a large development near US 290 E and proposed SH 130, including directives for future infrastructure and environmental considerations.

  • Combating Racial Profiling:

    Accepted a federal grant to fund a program aimed at reducing racial profiling by the Austin Police Department, addressing a critical social justice issue.

  • Prioritizing Environmental Stewardship:

    Directed staff to negotiate comprehensive environmental protection for development near Barton Creek (Stratus Properties) and mandated stronger environmental criteria for all future city concrete contracts.
