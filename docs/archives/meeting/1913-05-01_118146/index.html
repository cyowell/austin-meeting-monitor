Austin: New Treasurer, Infrastructure, Events

Thursday, May 1, 1913 regular

  • New City Treasurer Appointed:

    Chester Thrasher was elected City Treasurer after a competitive bidding process, offering the highest interest rate (3.51%) on daily city fund balances. His substantial $300,000 bond was subsequently approved.

  • Infrastructure Improvements & Maintenance:

    The city approved funding for new electric lines to meet increasing power demands in east Austin and officially accepted the completed paving of Lavacca Street from 13th to 19th streets.

  • Event Permits and Business Engagement:

    The Retail Merchants Association received permission for temporary signs, electric lights, and fencing on 5th Street for an upcoming exposition. The council also began reviewing Quality Mills' proposal to use waste water from the city power plant for a nominal annual fee.

  • Unique Property & Resident Actions:

    An unused alleyway easement, originally established in 1896, was officially closed and returned to private ownership. Separately, a resident successfully petitioned for a $5.25 refund for a water meter, due to price reductions since his original purchase years prior.
