Austin Election Judge Appointments May 1890

Monday, May 5, 1890 special called

• Election judges reassigned: The council held an emergency meeting to replace election judges who declined their appointments, including new judges for the Fourth Ward (Ed Jurok), Third Ward (Jos. Harrell Jr.), and Seventh Ward (John P. Kirk).

• Low attendance required intervention: Only 9 of 21 aldermen initially showed up, forcing Mayor John McDonald to formally call the council into session to establish a quorum.

• Staff confirmations approved: The council confirmed G. D. Walker and J. B. Hutchison as election clerks and assistants for the Eleventh Ward.
