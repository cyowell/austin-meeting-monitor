Austin's Growth, Golf, and City Services

Thursday, July 20, 1972 regular

Here's a summary of the Austin City Council agenda from July 20, 1972:

  • City takes control of anti-poverty programs:

    The Human Opportunities Corporation's designation was revoked, and the City of Austin officially took over as the Community Action Agency for the area.

  • Modernizing city finances:

    A $140,000 contract was awarded to implement a new computer system for property appraisal, tax billing, and accounting, projected to save $50,000 annually.

  • Future growth and development debated:

    Council discussed a $575,000 water line extension far outside city limits, referring it for further planning review amidst concerns about unplanned growth. They also set hearings for zoning changes and annexations.

  • Investing in recreational spaces:

    Plans advanced for new public golf courses and improvements, including $80,000 for a clubhouse at Jimmy Clay Golf Course and $50,000 to plan a new 18-hole course.

  • Community land dispute mediation:

    A public hearing on a special permit for Carrington Homes was delayed, allowing time for residents to explore purchasing the land for a club or park.
