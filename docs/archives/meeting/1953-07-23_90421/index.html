Austin's Growth: Elections, Phone Rates, Safety

Thursday, July 23, 1953 regular

  • Voting Precinct Redesign:

    New boundaries for voting precincts were approved, specifically adjusting the E-1 Ward, Precinct 35 area in Travis Heights for a more equal distribution of voters.

  • Southwestern Bell Seeks Rate Hike:

    The telephone company formally requested a rate increase, arguing current rates were insufficient to fund essential infrastructure expansion needed to serve Austin's growing population and reduce a lengthy waiting list for service. The Council will review this request later.

  • Updated Fire Safety Rules Introduced:

    A new ordinance was proposed, detailing regulations for installing and maintaining fire protective devices and licensing fire extinguisher businesses. A citizen committee was recognized for two years of work on these important safety updates.

  • New Power Plant Showcased:

    The city finalized boiler and machinery insurance for its new electric and water plant. An open house was announced for August 17th, inviting the public and civic groups to tour the state-of-the-art facility.

  • Major Subdivision Ordinance Public Hearing:

    A public hearing was scheduled for August 3rd to discuss a "fundamental change" to the Subdivision Ordinance, with calls for widespread public awareness.
