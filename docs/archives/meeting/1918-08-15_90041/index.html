Transcript
  • A public service car license was revoked after a hearing found the owner used his vehicle to transport women for "immoral conduct."
  • Council members discussed the proposal for a "woman welfare worker" to combat immorality among local girls and soldiers.
  • A new ordinance was passed, making loud animal noises that disturb residents a public nuisance with penalties.
  • The city formalized payments for a $100,000 sewer bond sale and associated banking charges to fund infrastructure.
