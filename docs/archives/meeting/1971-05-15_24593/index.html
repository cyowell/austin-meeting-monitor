Austin's New Leaders Sworn In with LBJ

Saturday, May 15, 1971 regular

  • New Leadership Takes Office:

    Roy Butler was inaugurated as the new Mayor, alongside a newly sworn-in slate of six council members.

  • Mayor Pro-Tem Elected:

    The new council unanimously elected Dan Love to serve as the Mayor Pro-Tem.

  • High-Profile Guests:
