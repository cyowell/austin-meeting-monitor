Guadalupe Paving, Bonds, New Parks

Thursday, February 1, 1934 regular

  • Zoning Board Salaries Abolished:

    City officials voted to eliminate all salaries for members of the Zoning Board of Adjustment.

  • $678K in Old Bonds Destroyed:

    The City authorized the "cremation" (destruction) of $678,000 worth of paid and canceled bonds, which originally funded various public works like street improvements, hospitals, schools, and sewers.

  • Guadalupe Street Paving Approved:

    A project was approved to pave a section of Guadalupe Street and an adjacent alley, with the costs to be covered by the owners of the abutting properties.

  • Major Park & Street Land Acquisition:

    The City purchased several tracts of land for $2,682.52 to expand parks and develop new streets, including settlement of related lawsuits and agreement to specific development conditions.
