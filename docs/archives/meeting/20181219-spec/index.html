Austin Elects New District 1, 3, and 8 Reps

Wednesday, December 19, 2018 Austin City Council Special Called Meeting
  • Austin's local government officially certified the results of the December 11th special runoff election.
  • New representatives for three city districts were formally confirmed: Natasha Harper-Madison for District 1, Sabino Pio Renteria for District 3, and Paige Ellis for District 8.
  • This brief, special meeting focused exclusively on validating the election outcomes with no additional agenda items or public hearings.
  • The resolutions declaring the winners were approved unanimously.
