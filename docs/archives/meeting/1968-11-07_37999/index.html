Austin's Global Ties, Transit, & Library Tech

Thursday, November 7, 1968 regular

  • Forging International Bonds:

    Austin officially launched a sister city partnership with Saltillo, Mexico, to foster cultural, educational, and economic exchanges.

  • Zoning Debates for Community Life:

    Public hearings continued on significant zoning revisions, particularly discussing new rules for operating nursery schools in residential areas and clarifying requirements for club sites.

  • Modernizing Public Transit:

    The Council reviewed proposed changes to the bus franchise ordinance, including a new formula for automatic fare adjustments and greater flexibility for route modifications.

  • Library Goes High-Tech:

    The Public Library moved to automate its book lending process by acquiring new "photochargers" (microfilm technology) to simplify check-outs and reduce the need for traditional library cards.

  • Planning Future Energy:

    The city advanced plans for the new Decker Creek power plant, with officials actively negotiating contracts for its natural gas supply.
