Austin: Air Noise, New Roads, Military Police HQ

Thursday, September 30, 1943 regular

  • Airport Noise & Flight Paths:

    City officials addressed concerns about low-flying military planes and explored changes to the municipal airport's flying patterns to reduce noise.

  • Major Highway Expansion:

    Approved the dedication of 28 tracts of city-owned land for the development and expansion of State Highway 29.

  • Wartime Support:

    Authorized the lease of city property to the United States government for use as headquarters for the Negro Military Police.

  • Downtown Construction Safety:

    Granted temporary use of public sidewalk and street space to a contractor for building alterations, with strict safety regulations and a required $1000 bond.
