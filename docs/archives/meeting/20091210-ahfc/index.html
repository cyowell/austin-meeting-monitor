Austin Housing: Elm Ridge, Mueller, South Congress

Thursday, December 10, 2009 Austin Housing Finance Corporation (AHFC) Board of Directors AHFC Meeting
  • Approved up to $6 million in bonds to finance the acquisition and rehabilitation of Elm Ridge Apartments, providing 130 units of affordable housing.
  • Authorized a $2 million loan for the development of 201 units of mixed-income senior housing within the Mueller Planned-Unit Development District.
  • Approved a $3 million loan for FOUNDATION COMMUNITIES to acquire and rehabilitate a 238-unit mixed-income apartment complex on South Congress Avenue.
  • Increased the overall funding for affordable housing initiatives by over $2 million, bringing the total budget to nearly $18 million.
