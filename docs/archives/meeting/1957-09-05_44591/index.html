Austin Tackles Transit, Zoning, & New Growth

Thursday, September 5, 1957 regular

  • Public Transit in Crisis

    The Austin Transit Corporation requested financial relief, prompting a public hearing on potential fare increases (e.g., 16 cents for adults, 7 cents for students) and service cuts. The city decided to study the proposed fare adjustments.

  • Key Zoning Decisions

    Multiple land tracts in the Area 6 development were officially rezoned for Local Retail and Commercial use, shaping future urban development and land use.

  • Infrastructure Upgrades

    Over $100,000 in contracts were awarded for significant storm and sanitary sewer construction projects across the city, including improvements on West 49th Street and along Waller Creek.

  • Neighborhood Paving Dispute

    Despite local opposition citing drainage concerns and lack of benefit, the city approved the full paving of Alta Vista Alley, with costs to be assessed to abutting property owners.
