Austin's Zoning Overhaul & New Gas Stations

Thursday, April 11, 1935 regular

  • City-Wide Zoning Update:

    A new comprehensive ordinance was enacted, establishing updated zoning regulations and districts for the entire city.

  • Debate Over Commercial vs. Residential Zones:

    The council held a public hearing regarding a proposal to change commercial properties near 45th and Red River to residential use, deferring a decision for 45 days contingent on a property sale. They also scheduled a future public hearing to consider expanding commercial zoning on Sabine Street, notably advising against "spot zoning" a single lot and citing the nearby new City Market and unsuitable land for residential use.

  • New Gas Station Approved with Strict Rules:

    Plans for a drive-in gasoline filling station on South Congress Avenue were approved, but came with extensive conditions. These included requirements for proper drainage, waste oil disposal, specific construction materials, and adherence to new building and safety ordinances.
