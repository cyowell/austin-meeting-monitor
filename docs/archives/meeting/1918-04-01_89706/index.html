Wartime Holiday, Taxi Licenses, Fines Suspended

Monday, April 1, 1918 special called
  • New regulations were adopted, requiring licenses for automobiles operating as for-hire taxis.
  • Fines for two individuals, Lillie Cameron and Bettie Lee Tony, were conditionally suspended, contingent on them leaving the city and not re-offending.
  • A half-holiday was declared for Saturday, April 6th, and several downtown streets were ordered closed to facilitate a parade promoting the Third Liberty Loan, a national wartime bond drive.
