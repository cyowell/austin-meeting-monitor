ATX Funds Mueller & Aldridge Affordable Homes

Thursday, April 23, 2015 Austin Housing Finance Corporation (AHFC) Board of Directors AHFC Meeting

  • Boosted Affordable Housing:

    Approved a $2 million loan for a development at Mueller Airport, providing units for families at 30-50% median income, and greenlit 11 Austin Habitat for Humanity homes.

  • Funded Key Projects:

    Authorized over $36 million in bonds to finance the development and rehabilitation of multi-family affordable housing, including the Aldridge 51 apartments.

  • Debated Tax Exemptions:

    Discussed the use of property tax exemptions for affordable housing projects, with some concerns raised about the impact on other taxpayers.

  • Streamlined Project Structure:

    Created a new non-profit entity to manage affordable housing developments like Aldridge 51, aiming to limit liability and secure tax benefits.
