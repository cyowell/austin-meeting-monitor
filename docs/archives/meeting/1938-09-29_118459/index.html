Austin's Urban Challenges: Zoning to Dairies

Thursday, September 29, 1938 regular

Here's a summary of the Austin City Council agenda from September 29, 1938:

  • Major Zoning Debates:

    The council deliberated significant re-zoning near the Colorado River, weighing residential development against industrial use and flood plain concerns. Another commercial rezoning request was postponed to prevent "spot zoning" at a key intersection.

  • Public Health & Dairy Removal:

    A resident petition prompted an investigation into an "insanitary" dairy at 37 Waller Street, referred to the Health Department for further action.

  • Infrastructure Expansion & New Development:

    The city approved new gas mains and telephone pole lines, plus permits for commercial driveways and a drive-in gasoline filling station, signaling ongoing urban growth.

  • Street Name Standardisation & Construction Safety:

    A section of Navajo Drive was renamed Pecos Street to reduce public confusion. Additionally, strict safety and site management rules were set for construction projects using public sidewalk space.
