Austin's Growth, Zoning, and Unique Infrastructure Deals

Thursday, December 11, 1947 regular

  • Rapid Expansion:

    Over 246 acres were annexed, and numerous zoning changes approved to facilitate new commercial and residential developments across the city.

  • Innovative Infrastructure:

    A contract was approved for water and sewer mains in a new subdivision, with the developer's upfront costs to be reimbursed through future water utility sales.

  • New Businesses & Public Facilities:

    Permits were granted for multiple new taxicabs (including a probationary permit after an appeal), wine/beer licenses, two gasoline filling stations, and a public floating boathouse on Lake Austin.

  • City Operations:

    Amendments were made to traffic and parking meter ordinances, a public hearing was set for the 1948 budget, and condemnation proceedings began for a new sewage pump station site.
