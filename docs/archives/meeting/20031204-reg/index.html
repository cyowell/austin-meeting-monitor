Thursday, December 4, 2003 Austin City Council Regular Meeting

  • Protecting Barton Springs:

    New restrictions on retail development in the sensitive Barton Springs Zone were approved, and an existing moratorium on new retail permits was extended. A public hearing was also set for a contentious Lowe's development settlement in the area.

  • Bold Green Energy & Air Quality Goals:

    An Austin Energy Strategic Plan was adopted, setting an ambitious goal of 100 megawatts of solar power by 2020. Additionally, strategies and an action plan were approved to improve regional air quality and meet ozone standards.

  • Downtown Revitalization Investment:

    A $1.3 million budget was approved, and assessments were levied for the Downtown Public Improvement District, funding ongoing enhancements and revitalization efforts in Austin's city center.
