Austin: New Airport, Policing, Bridge Design

Wednesday, September 25, 1996

  • Upcoming Public Hearings on Key Issues:

    The agenda previewed future public hearings on the design and impact of the Lamar Bridge Project, the broader future of Lamar, and a Downtown Access Study. Citizens will also have a chance to discuss the 1996-97 budget's impact on neighborhood and community policing initiatives.

  • Major Airport Updates:

    Significant discussion revolved around the new Austin-Bergstrom International Airport, including construction and financial updates, management reserves, and future contract awards for critical facilities like the Central Heating and Cooling Plant. An update was also provided on the redevelopment plans for the old Robert Mueller Municipal Airport.

  • Workforce Diversity Efforts:

    Presentations focused on ongoing initiatives to promote workforce diversity within several city departments, specifically the Municipal Court, City Clerk's office, and Solid Waste Services.
