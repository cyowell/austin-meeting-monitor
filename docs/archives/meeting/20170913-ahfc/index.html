Wednesday, September 13, 2017 Austin Housing Finance Corporation (AHFC) Board of Directors AHFC Meeting
Official City Record Agenda Watch Video Transcript
  • The Austin Housing Finance Corporation (AHFC) adopted its annual budget for the upcoming fiscal year.
  • An $8.5 million capital budget was approved, notably a $2 million reduction from the initial proposal.
  • Over $9.4 million was allocated for housing grants and direct housing assistance programs.
  • The budget also established guidelines for housing programs and administrative spending limits.
