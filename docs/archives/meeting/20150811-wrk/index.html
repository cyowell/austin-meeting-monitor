Austin Airport Expansion, Contractor Debate

Tuesday, August 11, 2015 Austin City Council Work Session

  • Airport Capacity Crisis:

    An urgent plan to reactivate the South Terminal with a private partner was debated, aiming to quickly add three new gates to address severe capacity shortages and accommodate airline growth.

  • Contracting Fairness Concerns:

    The city's "rotation list" system for hiring professional services faced scrutiny over a perceived lack of prime contract opportunities for minority and women-owned businesses (MWBEs).

  • Price vs. Qualifications in Contracts:

    Debate arose over state law that prevents price from being the primary factor in awarding professional services contracts, leading some to question the system's efficiency for taxpayers.

  • Committee Review vs. Project Urgency:

    A key discussion point was whether to send the complex airport lease to a committee for more detailed review and stakeholder input, or approve it immediately due to the pressing need for increased gate capacity.
