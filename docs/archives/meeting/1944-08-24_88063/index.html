V-DAY Planning, Land Sales, and Tax Fixes

Thursday, August 24, 1944 regular

  • Anticipating V-DAY:

    Approved a mayoral proclamation designating a day of thanks and urging businesses to close when Germany unconditionally surrendered to the Allies.

  • Property and Tax Adjustments:

    Addressed erroneous tax assessments by canceling taxes on non-existent property improvements and remitted penalties and half of the interest on other overdue taxes.

  • City Real Estate Transactions:

    Authorized the sale of multiple city-owned land parcels to private individuals (including one in exchange for cash and legal services) and approved the permanent closing of a public alley.
