Austin: Speed Limits, Internet, Growth

Thursday, December 7, 1995 regular

  • Major Highway Speed Limits:

    Set new 55 mph speed limits on key highways including Mopac, I-35, Loop 360, and Highways 183/290 within city limits, also directing further study for potentially higher speeds.

  • Urban Expansion & Downtown Future:

    Approved the annexation of the Mt. Bonnell area and discussed creating a "Central Urban Redevelopment District" (CURE) to stimulate downtown development.

  • Pioneering Public Internet:

    Supported a grant for Austin Free-Net to establish free public internet access within city libraries.

  • Pipeline Safety Concerns:

    Held a public hearing to address critical public safety, health, and environmental issues regarding a proposed gasoline pipeline in South Austin.
