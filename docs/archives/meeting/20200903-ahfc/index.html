Austin Approves Supportive Housing

Thursday, September 3, 2020 Austin Housing Finance Corporation (AHFC) Board of Directors AHFC Meeting

  • Major housing funding:

    Approved a $75.6 million service agreement between the City of Houston (as stated in transcript) and the Austin Housing Finance Corporation.

  • New supportive housing project:
