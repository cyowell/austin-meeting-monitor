Austin's Growth: Dam Power, Schools & Downtown Zoning

Thursday, July 15, 1937 regular

  • Major Dam Agreement:

    A significant 30-year lease was approved with the Lower Colorado River Authority (LCRA) to reconstruct the Austin Dam, providing the city with hydroelectric power and 40 million gallons of water daily.

  • School District Expansion:

    City limits were extended for school purposes only to include the St. Elmo Common School District No. 43.

  • Downtown Zoning Changes:

    Recommendations were approved to rezone areas near the State Capitol and in Block 91 from residential to commercial, partly to accommodate a demand for governmental agency office space.

  • New Parking Meters:

    Bids were received and taken under advisement for the installation of parking meters, indicating a move towards new traffic management.

  • City Employee Salaries:

    A revised salary schedule for city employees was authorized to be effective for the latter half of 1937.
