Thursday, June 7, 1917 regular

788 .Regular meeting of the City Council: Austin, Texas, June 07, 1917: The Council was called to order by the Mayor : Roll Called: Present Mayor Wooldridge ,Councilmen Anthony, Bartholomew, Haynes & Powell 5 Absent none: The minutes of the last two regular meetings ,with OKINNK Adjourned meetings were read and adopted : Reports of City Officers: The Mayor laid before the Council the report of the Assessor & Collector, the Public Weigher, The Secretary United Charities, and the Plumbing Inspector, which were read and ordered filed: Fine &c Kreisle remitted Councilman Anthony offered the following resolution : Whereas, it appears from the information derived from Police Officer, Ed Nitschke, and from the verbal recommendation made to the Mayor of thid City by A S Phelps, Asst City Attorney, it appears that there might have been a mistake in the imposition of a penalty upon E C Kreisle in cause No 11954, in the Corporation Court of the City of Austin, Texas, Now, therefore, Resolved by the City Council of the City of Austin, Texas : That the fine and costs in this cause, amounting to $ 19.80,be and the same same are hereby remitted. Fine Due Stebney remitted Councilman Anthony offered the following resolution : Whereas, it appears from the attached statement of W S Walsh that Tom Stbney ,colored, was fined in cause No 11739, in the Corporation Court of the City of Austin, Texas, for gaming,in the amount of $ 19.80; and Whereas, the said Tom Stbney ,Colored,has paid of this amount the sum of $ 11.50,leaving a balance due of $ 8.30; and Whereas, the said Tom Stebney, colored, died on the 24th day of May past and can no longer continue to pay the monthly installments due upon his hiring bond ,upon which W S Walsh, was a surety. Now Therefore, Be it resolved by the City Council of the City of Austin, Texas : That in consideration of the extreme equities of the case,the balance of the fine and costs in this case,amounting to $ 8.30,be and the same is hereby remitted. The resolution was adopted by the following vote: Yeas Mayor Wooldridge, Councilmen Anthony, Bartholomew, Haynes & Powell 7 Nays none, Approved, A P Wooldridge, Mayor.
