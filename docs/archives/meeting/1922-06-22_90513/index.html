Austin's Tax Fixes and Automobile Shed Permit

Thursday, June 22, 1922 regular

  • Tax Roll Clean-Up:

    Officials approved multiple resolutions to correct errors on the tax rolls, including canceling assessments for properties that were double-rendered or taxed on assets already replaced.

  • Property Tax Refund:

    Authorized a refund of $27.72 to a property owner who overpaid taxes due to an uncorrected property valuation error.

  • New Auto Building:

    Granted approval for Dr. Z.T. Scott to build an "automobile shed" (a garage or carport) at 700 Brazos Street, reflecting the growing presence of cars.
