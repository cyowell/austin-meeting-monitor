Austin Rails Get Green Light; City Budget Approved

Monday, June 16, 1902 regular

• Railway expansion approved: The Austin Suburban Railway Company received permission to operate railroad lines using electricity or steam on city streets, pending review by the Ordinance Committee and City Attorney.

• Budget allocations passed: The council approved three spending ordinances totaling over $2,200 for bond interest payments, street department expenses, and hospital accounts (with a drug store charge removed).

• Infrastructure improvements funded: $280 allocated to remove a culvert on 16th Street and construct drainage ditches to improve city infrastructure.

• Bridge damage claim filed: A resident sought damages for injuries caused by a defective bridge, referred to the Street Committee for investigation.

• Hospital affordability issue addressed: The council intervened on behalf of a long-term hospital patient unable to afford new rates, allowing her to maintain her previous $10/month payment pending committee review.
