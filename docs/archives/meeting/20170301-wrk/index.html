Tackling Austin's Health, Homelessness, Data Gaps

Wednesday, March 1, 2017 Austin City Council Budget Work Session
  • Austin officials initiated budget discussions for Health and Human Services, setting the stage for future sessions focused on "tough policy questions" concerning current spending and long-term strategies, especially given rising poverty, increasing homelessness, and health disparities in certain parts of the city.
  • A key policy discussion highlighted the critical need to improve data sharing and collaboration across city departments and external partners (including medical, social services, and law enforcement) to boost efficiency and outcomes for vulnerable residents, while acknowledging and working through regulatory barriers like HIPAA.
  • The Council also reviewed social service contracts, particularly those for children and youth, calling for stronger outcome-based metrics and addressing cultural competency and potential duplication of services. Updates were provided on major capital projects, including the Animal Center kennel expansion and the ongoing construction of the Women and Children's Shelter.
