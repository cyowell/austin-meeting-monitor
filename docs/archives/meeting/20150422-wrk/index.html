Austin Budget: Economic Surge, Housing Squeeze

Wednesday, April 22, 2015 Austin City Council Budget Work Session

Here's a summary of the 2015-04-22 Austin City budget work session:

  • The city kicked off its 2015-16 budget process, reviewing Austin's booming economy, robust job growth, and vibrant tech and tourism sectors, forecasting continued positive trends.
  • Despite economic prosperity, significant concerns were raised about the rapidly tightening housing market, declining affordability for residents, and the risk of over-reliance on rapidly appreciating property values for city revenue.
  • Discussions focused on the nuances of property tax policy, including the impact of effective and rollback rates, and the increase in property tax bills for homeowners.
  • Key budget drivers identified include projected increases in employee wages and substantial rises in health insurance costs, alongside plans for a homestead exemption analysis.
