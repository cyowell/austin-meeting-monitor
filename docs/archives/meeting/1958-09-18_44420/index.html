Austin Funds Future with $2.5M Bond Sale

Thursday, September 18, 1958 regular

  • Major Financial Decision:

    The city approved the sale of $2.5 million in General Obligation Bonds.

  • Funding City-Wide Projects:

    These bonds were intended to finance various public works and essential city services.

  • Awarded to First Southwest Company:

    The bid for purchasing these bonds was accepted from First Southwest Company, at an effective interest rate of 3.64%.
