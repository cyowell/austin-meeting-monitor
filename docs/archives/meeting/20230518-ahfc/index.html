Austin Housing: $780K Loan Sparks Displacement Row

Thursday, May 18, 2023 Austin Housing Finance Corporation (AHFC) Board of Directors Regular Meeting
  • The Austin Housing Finance Corporation (AHFC) board convened to approve several housing-related financial items.
  • AHFC approved a controversial $780,000 loan for a fourplex at 3306 Vintage Hills Cove, intended to be funded by Project Connect anti-displacement funds.
  • A community member strongly opposed the loan, arguing the anti-displacement funds were misapplied to an area not impacted by Project Connect and accused the corporation of fraud, stating they had reported AHFC to the IRS.
  • Despite this public opposition and some board member dissent, all proposed items, including the $780,000 loan, were ultimately adopted by the board.
