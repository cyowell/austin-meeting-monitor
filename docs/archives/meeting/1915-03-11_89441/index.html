Thursday, March 11, 1915 regular
Transcript

  • Major Utility Budget Approved:

    The city adopted its 1915 budget for the Water, Light, and Power Department, committing over $407,000 to city services.

  • Shift to Hydro Power:

    A significant policy decision was made to transition the city's utilities from steam to water (hydro) power, with an initial $100,000 allocated for a new dam, power house, and reservoir.

  • Key Infrastructure Investments:

    The budget also funded critical water infrastructure projects, including $11,200 for rebuilding the existing dam and $12,000 for securing an additional water supply.
