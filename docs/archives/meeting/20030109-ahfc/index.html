New Lead Safety Program, 160 Affordable Units

Thursday, January 9, 2003 Austin Housing Finance Corporation (AHFC)

  • Boosted Lead Hazard Control:

    Approved over $1.8 million in funding and three new staff positions to establish a Lead Hazard Control Program, aimed at protecting residents from lead-based paint hazards.

  • Greenlight for Affordable Housing Project:

    Endorsed plans for "Villas on Sixth Street," a proposed 160-unit apartment complex on East 6th Street, dedicated to providing much-needed affordable housing.

  • Seeking Key Housing Funds:

    Authorized the application for crucial Low Income Housing Tax Credits, a vital funding source, to help finance the development of the "Villas on Sixth Street" affordable housing community.
