Austin Recommends New Court Clerk

Friday, September 11, 2015 Public Safety Committee Regular Meeting
  • The Public Safety Committee met to discuss a key city personnel appointment.
  • The committee nominated and recommended Mary Jane Grubbs for the Municipal Court Clerk position.
  • An annual salary of $125,000 was approved for the role.
  • This recommendation will be presented to the full city council for final approval on October 1st.
