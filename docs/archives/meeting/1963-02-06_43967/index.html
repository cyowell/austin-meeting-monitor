Austin's Cable TV: Competition & Local Control

Wednesday, February 6, 1963 special called
  • Austin issued its first contract for cable television service to "TV Cable of Austin," explicitly making it non-exclusive to ensure a competitive market.
  • The new service was required to offer at least 8 VHF channels, carry all local broadcasts, and include features for civil defense alerts and content moderation.
  • Initial residential subscriber rates were capped at $4.95 per month with no installation fee, and the company committed to paying 2% of its gross earnings to the city.
  • City leaders emphasized their commitment to preventing a TV monopoly, encouraging more companies to apply, and discussed the possibility of blocking out unwanted out-of-town advertisements.
