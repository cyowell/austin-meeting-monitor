Shaping Austin: Bonds, Civic Center, Key Zoning Rules

Thursday, October 3, 1985 regular

  • Future Funding & City Infrastructure:

    Approved a December 1985 bond election, committed funds for new Civic Center studies, and moved forward with an option agreement for a Municipal Office Complex development.

  • Major Development & Environmental Policy:

    Adopted key zoning changes for the North Lamar area (with further public hearings planned for exceptions), approved amendments to Capital Recovery Fees for development, and passed updates to Watershed Ordinances.

  • Public Safety & Community Support:

    Accepted state grants for enhanced Driving While Intoxicated (DWI) and speed limit enforcement, and authorized significant funding proposals for neighborhood centers.

  • Boosting Local Tourism:

    Awarded substantial contracts totaling over $950,000 to local chambers of commerce for convention and tourism promotion services.
