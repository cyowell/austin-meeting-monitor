Austin Housing Plans: Bonds and More

Wednesday, April 9, 1997
  • Approved $15 million in new bonds and established an $8.28 million Mortgage Credit Certificate program to help low and moderate-income residents purchase homes.
  • Held a public hearing and postponed a decision on $8.5 million in bonds for the acquisition and rehabilitation of the Woodland Heights Apartments.
  • Authorized the transfer of ownership for the Cypress Apartments, an existing affordable housing project financed by bonds.
