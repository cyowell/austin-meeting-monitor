Austin: New Schools, Motorized Firepower

Thursday, February 15, 1912 regular

  • School Funding:

    A $150,000 bond issue was proposed to taxpayers for new school construction, building repairs, and land purchases.

  • Fire Department Modernization:

    The city accepted and paid for a new motorized "Auto Fire Apparatus" (early fire truck) and appointed its drivers, upgrading emergency services.

  • City Infrastructure:

    Approved repairs for the City Hospital and formally established the precise center line for West 12th Street.

  • Taxpayer Reimbursement:

    Addressed erroneous tax assessments, including a refund for taxes paid in 1891 on land that was not legally within Austin's city limits.
