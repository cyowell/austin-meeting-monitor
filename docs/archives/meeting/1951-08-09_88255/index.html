Austin's Growth: Paving, Zoning & Gas Lines

Thursday, August 9, 1951 regular

  • Infrastructure & Utilities:

    The council approved a nearly $100,000 contract for new storm sewers and expanded the street paving program by 20 blocks. They also granted permission for the Southern Union Gas Company to install new gas mains across multiple city streets.

  • Zoning Battles:

    A request to rezone a Scenic Drive property from residential to commercial was denied after strong opposition from neighborhood residents concerned about commercial encroachment. Another commercial rezoning request was postponed for a council site visit.

  • Traffic & Parking Updates:

    Multiple ordinances were passed to amend existing traffic regulations, including new one-way streets, updated stop sign placements, adjustments to various "no parking" zones, and revisions to parking meter areas.

  • Community Services & Oversight:

    The council requested a progress report from the Austin Transit Company on its reorganization and fleet expansion. They also initiated a study to evaluate the future and effectiveness of the city market and discussed challenges in hiring labor for public clean-up programs.
