Austin Eyes Water Quality, Blocks Annexations

Tuesday, March 7, 1989 special called

  • Financial caution halts expansion:

    The city voted *not* to annex three large land tracts, including a significant 2,200-acre parcel, due to financial concerns.

  • Prioritizing water quality:

    A joint meeting with the Lower Colorado River Authority (LCRA) Board was scheduled to discuss water quality goals for the future.

  • Airport development update:

    An "anti-lobbying clause" was removed from the Request for Proposal (RFP) related to the Austin airport project.

  • Key development hearing canceled:

    A scheduled public hearing and decision regarding an agreement with the University of Texas for the future development of the Brackenridge Tract was called off.
