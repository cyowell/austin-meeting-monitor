Austin Approves Fire Relief Fund for Paris

Monday, September 10, 1877 special called

• Emergency aid passed: Austin City Council approved a $500 appropriation to help residents of Paris, Texas, whose city had been largely destroyed by fire—demonstrating early municipal disaster relief efforts.

• Attendance problems: The council struggled with quorum issues across multiple sessions, requiring the marshal to track down absent aldermen to conduct official business.

• New licensing authority: The mayor was authorized to issue licenses for night scavengers (sanitation workers), establishing a system for regulating this essential city service.

• Quick action: Despite initial procedural delays, council passed the Paris relief ordinance on its second reading within the same meeting, showing urgency in responding to the disaster.
