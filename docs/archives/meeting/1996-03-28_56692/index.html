Affordable Homes, Green Streets, Digital Austin

Thursday, March 28, 1996 regular

Here are the key takeaways from the March 28, 1996 Austin City Council agenda:

  • Affordable Housing Boost:

    Approved over $3 million in federal grants for the Austin Housing Finance Corporation, supporting programs for down payment assistance, single-family loans, and the "Welcome Home Program" to make homeownership more accessible.

  • Green City & Active Living:

    Adopted new policies to promote urban livability, including a tree protection ordinance requiring funds for new plantings, temporary exemptions and water fee waivers for community gardens, and plans for a dedicated pedestrian coordinator and a new city-wide Bicycle Plan.

  • Modernizing Public Services:

    Advanced efforts toward a unified public safety communications system and secured grants for victims' assistance programs. Notably, a grant was accepted to enhance public library access to the Internet and World Wide Web, marking an early step into digital inclusion.

  • Shaping Austin's Future:

    Explored alternative uses for the decommissioned Seaholm Power Plant and officially supported plans for Austin Community College to locate its new south Austin campus on Huston-Tillotson College land, directing the preparation of an annexation plan.
