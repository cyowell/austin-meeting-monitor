Austin Funds Arts & Services; MLK Holiday Debate

Thursday, September 13, 1984 special called

  • City Finances & Spending

    Public hearings focused on the proposed 1984-85 financial plan, including city taxes, fees, and the allocation of federal revenue-sharing funds.

  • Cultural Arts Support

    Organizations such as Ballet Austin, the Paramount Theatre, and Laguna Gloria Art Museum presented their cases for city funding.

  • Major Social Services Funding

    After extensive discussion, city leaders approved over $2.7 million for human services, supporting critical groups like the Rape Crisis Center, Big Brothers/Big Sisters, and the Capital Area Food Bank.
