Ballot Box: Districts, Term Limits, Campaign Funds

Tuesday, March 19, 2002 Austin City Council Special-Called Meeting

Here are the key takeaways from the Austin City Council meeting:

  • Big Ballot Decisions:

    Voters will decide on significant proposed changes, including moving to a hybrid election system with both district and city-wide council members.

  • Term Limits & Campaign Finance:

    Proposals to repeal term limits for the Mayor and City Council, and to remove the 1997 $100 campaign contribution limit and other regulations, were approved for the ballot.

  • Consumer Protection:

    A measure to create an Electric Utility Consumer Advocate, appointed by the City, will also go before voters.

  • Rejected Ideas:

    Notable proposals for a police monitor and oversight board, and for the City Council to appoint the City Attorney, were not approved to appear on the ballot.
