Zoning Shifts, New Roads, City Taxes Set

Thursday, September 24, 1959 regular

  • Urban Development & Drainage Concerns

    Addressed a resident's complaint about potential flooding affecting a local business due to new development, and moved forward with over a dozen street paving projects across the city after a public hearing.

  • Major Infrastructure Projects

    Advanced plans for the new Ben White Boulevard, including authorizing land acquisition, and approved significant contracts for the Holly Street Power Station and the Sewage Treatment Plant, saving over $35,000 on power station insulation.

  • Property Zoning Shifts

    Approved numerous zoning changes for various properties, converting some residential areas to office or retail districts, and granted permits for new public and private gasoline stations.

  • 1959 Tax Rate Set

    Officially approved the city's 1959 property tax assessment rolls, totaling over $484 million in valuation, and formally levied municipal taxes for the year.
