Austin's Development: Water, Environment, & Transit

Thursday, February 20, 1975 regular

  • Major Infrastructure Investments:

    The city approved significant contracts, including a $1.4 million water reservoir and related mains, a $204,000 electric substation, and a $741,000 parking structure and training facility for the Police Department. Land acquisitions for the MoPac Expressway, Waller Beach, and Fiesta Gardens also moved forward.

  • Barton Creek Environmental Policy:

    A zoning agreement was approved for a development in the Barton Creek watershed despite citizen concerns about potential water quality degradation. The Council accepted the developer's commitment to "best possible" water control under current technology, as endorsed by city staff.

  • Lake Austin & Annexation Debates:

    The Council decided against lowering Lake Austin, upholding an earlier recommendation. They also considered an appeal from a resident seeking deannexation due to significantly increased property taxes and perceived lack of city services after being annexed, deferring the request for further study.

  • Expanding Mass Transportation:

    Steps were taken to advance Austin's public transit system, including authorizing the submission of an annual program to qualify for federal funds and approving a contract for transportation planning consulting services.
