Tuesday, November 18, 2025 Austin Energy Utility Oversight Committee Regular Meeting
Official City Record Agenda Watch Video Transcript

  • $735 Million Grid Upgrade:

    Austin Energy is launching a 10-year, $735 million plan to harden the electric grid against extreme weather, improve reliability, and pilot undergrounding projects in select areas.

  • Urgent Search for New Power:

    The city is issuing an "all-resource" request for proposals for new local power sources, including solar, storage, and gas "peaker" plants, with council members emphasizing the critical need to secure these units quickly due to long lead times and aging infrastructure.

  • Boosting Clean Energy Storage:

    A new 100-megawatt, four-hour battery storage system is now under contract, and Austin Energy is seeking bids for new utility-scale wind and landfill solar projects.

  • Solar Program Improvements Requested:

    Public speakers raised concerns about slow permitting processes and low financial incentives for small commercial and residential solar installations, urging Austin Energy to review and adjust these programs.
