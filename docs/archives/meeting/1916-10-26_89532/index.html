Free Sewer Access, Traffic Updates, Street Upgrades

Thursday, October 26, 1916 regular

  • Free Sewer Service for All:

    A landmark decision was made to provide free sanitary sewer service to all residents, businesses, and industries, effective January 1, 1917.

  • Updated Traffic Rules:

    An existing ordinance regulating street traffic, including rules of the road and penalties, was amended and passed.

  • Infrastructure Investments:

    Significant funds were approved for public works, including new specifications for repairing street openings, a major drain pipe project on West 12th Street, and mandating new cement sidewalks on San Jacinto Street.

  • Accountability & Leniency:

    The city required surety bonds for employees handling public funds to ensure financial accountability, and notably, remitted an $11.80 fine for a young boy, Arthur Tucker, citing his youth.
