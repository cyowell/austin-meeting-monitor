Austin Residents Challenge Property Taxes

Monday, April 26, 1948 regular
  • Austin citizens packed a city meeting to directly appeal their individual property tax valuations, claiming their assessments were unfairly high.
  • Residents cited issues like old or poor property conditions, unusable land (e.g., gulleys), and frozen rental income that didn't align with increasing taxes.
  • City officials generally agreed to personally inspect disputed properties and conduct further reviews before making final decisions on valuations.
  • This meeting was part of a larger trend, as the city had already scheduled an additional 70 property tax appeal hearings for later that week.
