Austin: Sunday Law, Traffic & Growing Pains

Thursday, June 15, 1950 regular

  • Sunday Law Enforcement Debate:

    The city grappled with enforcing a "Sunday Law" that mandated grocery stores close, prompting debate from independent grocers and the nascent 7-11 chain.

  • Urban Growth & Infrastructure:

    Officials approved new gas mains and a drive-in gasoline station, while also accepting the plat for the new Oakdale subdivision, reflecting Austin's rapid post-war expansion.

  • Traffic Management & Public Spaces:

    To ease congestion, plans began for dedicated rush-hour lanes on the Congress Avenue Bridge. The city also explored selling land for a new mausoleum and funding long-term care for existing cemeteries.

  • Waste Management & Public Health:

    Following citizen complaints about a city dump, the council initiated studies into compost manufacturing as a potential alternative to incinerators, hinting at early environmental considerations.
