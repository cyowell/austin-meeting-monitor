Paving Streets, Paying Workers, Baseball!

Thursday, January 22, 1914 regular
  • A new wage policy was proposed for city day laborers, setting pay at $2 per day for eight hours of work, with preference for long-term residents and heads of families.
  • Major street improvements were underway across Austin, including final payment for Brazos Street paving and ongoing assessments for property owners along Lavacca Street, University Avenue, and West 19th Street. Legal steps were initiated for owners who did not agree to the assessments.
  • The city approved a land exchange to extend West 5th Street and officially closed an alley in the Grooms Addition to allow for property re-subdivision.
  • Permits were issued for a new electric business sign on Congress Avenue and for a temporary banner across Congress Avenue to advertise University of Texas baseball games.
