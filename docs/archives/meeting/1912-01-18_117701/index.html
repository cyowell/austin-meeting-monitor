Austin Tackles Streets, Approves Budget, & Grants Permits

Thursday, January 18, 1912 regular

  • Significant Infrastructure Investment:

    An ordinance was passed to dedicate 30% of property taxes towards improving streets and building bridges, with a portion specifically set aside for permanent paving.

  • Annual City Budget Approved:

    A comprehensive budget totaling over $312,000 for most city departments was officially approved.

  • Fire Department Modernization Discussed:

    The possibility of transitioning the Fire Department from an all-volunteer service to a paid department was on the agenda for future consideration.

  • City Hospital Deficit Addressed:

    Funds were reallocated from other departments to cover shortfalls at the City Hospital, specifically for essential supplies like food and ice.

  • Urban Development and Regulation:

    The city reviewed various permits for new construction and renovations, including approving a sleeping porch and an electric sign, while denying a large bulletin board sign.

Austin, Jan 22nd 1912. The Council was called to order by the Mayor with all members present; Street fund The Mayor laid before the Council an ordinance setting apart thirty per centum of the bridges paving advalorem taxes of this City for the improvement of streets and building of bridges within the City; segregating twenty per centum of said thirty per centum for Permanent Paving ; making this ordinance re-troactive ,and repealing all ordinances and parts of ordinances in conflict with the provisions hereof.
