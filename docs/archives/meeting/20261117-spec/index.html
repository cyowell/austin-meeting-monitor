Tuesday, November 17, 2026 Special Called Meeting

ATX Election Results: Bonds Pass, Runoffs

Here's what happened at this week's city meeting:

  • Bond Election Results Approved:

    The results of the November 3rd General Obligation Bond Election were certified. Voters approved Proposition A (222,003 for / 142,337 against) and Proposition B (217,143 for / 145,491 against) for investments in city parks and libraries.

  • General Election Certified, Runoffs Loom:

    The November 3rd General Election results were officially accepted. Celina Xia was declared the winner for District 8, and Zo Qadri for District 9. However, runoff elections are needed for Districts 1, 3, and 5, as no candidate in those races secured a majority of votes.

  • Special Charter Election Results Finalized:

    Voters approved Propositions C, F, G, H, and J, while rejecting D, E, and I from the November 3rd Special Charter Election. For example, Proposition I (the independent performance audit measure) failed with 155,750 votes for and 189,750 votes against. During public comment, residents voiced concerns about confusing and incomplete information provided to voters regarding these charter amendments.

  • December 12th Runoff Election Ordered:

    An ordinance was approved to hold a runoff election on December 12th to decide the City Council Members for Districts 1, 3, and 5.

  • City Attorney Discussions:

    A closed-door executive session was held to discuss legal and personnel matters related to the appointment of the City Attorney.
