  • Utility Rate Adjustments:

JOE CROW appeared before the Council regarding some property he and MR. J. E. ROBERTS owned at 17th and Brazos Streets on which a zoning application was pending. He stated they had owned the property since 1956, some few months before the State had announced its expansion program. He said he had been before the State Officials to get an answer as to whether or not this property would be included in this expansion program, and had not been able to get a definite answer. The Mayor reviewed a former plan in 1948 for Capitol expansion. On this particular property, the Mayor stated he had discussed the matter a number of times with the officials, and that he would again make inquiry of them and ask for a decision. Councilman Long moved that the Mayor be empowered to officially represent the City Council before these officials and get an answer within the next 30 days pro or con on this particular piece of property and others.
