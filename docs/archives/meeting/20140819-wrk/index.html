Austin Budget: Tax Rate, Parks, Energy

Tuesday, August 19, 2014 Austin City Council Budget Work Session

  • Property Tax Rate Set:

    The Council approved setting the maximum property tax rate for FY 2014-15 at 48.09 cents per $100 valuation, which is $11.9 million lower than the rollback rate.

  • Parks Initiatives & Challenges:

    Discussions included a controversial proposal for an RFP to modernize the 50-year-old Butler Pitch & Putt, requests for additional funding for the Umlauf Sculpture Garden expansion, and ongoing challenges with tree maintenance and senior transportation services.

  • Austin Energy Rate Adjustments:

    Austin Energy announced a 1.9% overall utility rate increase for residents, emphasizing no base rate hike, but adjusting for power supply costs and regulatory charges.

  • New Park Developments:

    Highlights included the acquisition of Grey Rock Golf Course, successful redevelopments of Bartholomew and West End pools, and high citizen satisfaction with park services.
