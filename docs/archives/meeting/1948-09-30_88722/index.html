Austin Expands, Residents Resist Zoning

Thursday, September 30, 1948 regular

  • Major Infrastructure & Property Sales:

    The city council approved the installation of new gas mains across 18 streets and a railway track extension, while also moving forward with the sale of multiple city-owned houses.

  • Neighborhood Zoning Battles:

    Multiple zoning change requests were reviewed, with a significant proposal for a "Community Center" in Hyde Park being rejected due to strong resident opposition against "spot zoning."

  • New Commercial Regulations & Public Amenities:

    Discussions advanced on regulating jewelry auction sales, and a public hearing was scheduled for a renewed proposal to install bus stop benches across the city.

  • Taxi Driver Oversight:

    The council actively regulated public transportation by granting a probationary taxi driver permit and revoking another.
