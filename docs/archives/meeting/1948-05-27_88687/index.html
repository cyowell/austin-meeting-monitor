Austin: School Taxes, Charter, Fire Stations, Work Week

Thursday, May 27, 1948 regular

Here's what was on Austin City Council's agenda for May 27, 1948:

  • School Tax Increase Proposed:

    The council set the stage for a public vote on June 18 to potentially raise the public school maintenance tax from 70 cents to $1.15 per $100 of taxable property, significantly impacting education funding.

  • City Charter Amendments:

    Voters will also consider several city charter changes on June 18, including updates to annual tax levy powers and requiring voter approval for new city bonds.

  • Major Zoning Decisions & Debates:

    A large tract of land was approved for "DA Industrial" use. Public hearings were scheduled for other contentious zoning changes, including a proposal to convert residential areas near Duval and East 51st Street to commercial, and a discussion on careful planning for new community centers to avoid traffic congestion.

  • New Fire Stations & Employee Benefits:

    Architectural services were approved for two new neighborhood fire stations, aiming for cost savings by reusing an existing design. City Hall employees also secured a trial 5-day, 9-hour summer work week.

  • Over $483,000 in Old Bonds Destroyed:

    The City Manager presented a certificate detailing the destruction by cremation of over $483,000 worth of cancelled bonds and interest coupons, representing retired debt from various city projects dating back to 1910.
