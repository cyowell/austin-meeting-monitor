Austin tackles development, housing, utilities

Thursday, June 1, 1989 regular

  • Approved a major new development:

    A large, contentious zoning change for the "Perot Group PUD" on North F.M. 620 Road received final approval, with conditions for parkland, water quality monitoring, and pesticide use.

  • Advanced affordable housing efforts:

    Authorized an action plan to purchase University of Texas property in the Blackland Neighborhood to develop affordable housing.

  • Invested in utilities and infrastructure:

    Approved $16.3 million in bonds for the North Austin Municipal Utility District No. 1 and expanded city construction fees to all projects requiring inspection by the Transportation and Public Services Department.

  • Funded community services:

    Allocated $10,000 from the Social Services Contingency Fund to the University Y.W.C.A., reversing an earlier recommendation to deny the request.
