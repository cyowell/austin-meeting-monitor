Austin: Major Rezonings, Lake Plans, Utility Scrutiny

Wednesday, December 31, 1958 regular

  • Approved significant land use changes,

    converting numerous residential areas into commercial, retail, and office districts, reflecting city growth and development, alongside annexing several new territories.

  • Set a plan to temporarily lower Lake Austin

    by 12 feet for a week in January, impacting recreational use, and approved a new agreement for state-funded highway lighting installation, with the city responsible for maintenance.

  • Addressed consumer interests by engaging an economist

    to advise on proposed telephone rate increases, while also celebrating the opening of the new City Auditorium and advancing plans to accept a donated tract of land for a public park.
