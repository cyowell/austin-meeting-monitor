Thursday, April 11, 2002 Austin City Council Regular Meeting

Here's a summary of the key actions and discussions from the meeting:

  • New Leadership Appointed:

    Toby Futrell was officially named the new City Manager, with her compensation and benefits package approved.

  • Major Urban Redevelopment & Growth:

    Discussions began for the massive redevelopment of the former Robert Mueller Municipal Airport site, and several new areas, including parts of Williamson and Hays Counties, were considered for annexation.

  • Downtown Homeless Support & Public Safety:

    Over $6 million in federal loans were approved to fund the construction of a new downtown homeless shelter, resource center, and health clinic. Additionally, a new Emergency Notification System (Reverse 9-1-1) was purchased for the police department.

  • Debate on City Governance:

    A public hearing was held and a contentious vote took place regarding a proposed ordinance to divide the city into eight single-member districts for future council elections.
