Austin: City Growth, Tobacco Ban, Road Funds

Thursday, February 18, 1988 regular

  • Strategic Growth & Infrastructure Funding:

    Advanced a strategic annexation program for future city expansion and reallocated nearly $5 million to improve existing city roads, a move made possible by county commitments for an outer parkway.

  • Increased Code Violation Penalties:

    Approved higher maximum criminal penalties for violations of various city codes and ordinances.

  • Banned Free Cigarette Giveaways:

    Prohibited the free distribution of cigarettes on public streets and sidewalks, addressing a public health and nuisance concern.

  • Explored Economic & Electoral Changes:

    Approved nominating "South" and "East" areas as Enterprise Zones to spur economic development, and moved forward on drafting ballot language for a potential shift to single-member districts for city council elections.
