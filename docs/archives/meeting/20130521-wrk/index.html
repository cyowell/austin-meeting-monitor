Redistricting, X Games, Austin Energy Future

Tuesday, May 21, 2013 Austin City Council Work Session

  • Redistricting Commission:

    Officials discussed legal issues and the selection process for the new independent commission tasked with drawing Austin's council districts, including how applicants were vetted.

  • Austin Eyes X Games:

    The city is a finalist to host the X Games for three years (2014-2016), an event projected to bring significant economic impact and global media exposure, with potential for new public facilities like a permanent skate park.

  • Austin Energy Governance Overhaul:

    A major debate centered on restructuring oversight for the city's electric utility, with proposals ranging from creating a more independent board to refining council oversight through a new subcommittee, but no final decision was reached.
