Minutes of a Called Meeting of the City Council. Austin, Texas, March 08, 1897 Hon. H. A. Linn, President of the Council, presiding. Roll Called. Present, Aldermen Bruin, Haynes, Kuhn, Linn, Maddox, Platt, Powell, Roberdeau, Rosengren, Stumpf, Saylor and Townsend 12. Absent, Aldermen Beaty, Fischer, Horton, Lawless, Miller, Nithing, Rudd, Schneider, Shelley, and Zilker. 10. The following call of the Council was then read: Austin, Texas, March 8th 1897. We, the undersigned members of the City Council hereby call a special meeting of the Council to assemble in the City Council Chamber, on this Monday, March 8th, 1897, at 8 o'clock P.M., for the purpose of considering the matter of Street and Bridgework and the manner in which same shall be done and to make an appropriation therefor. J. W. Maddox, H. E. Haynes, R. L. Roberdeau, J. W. Townsend, J. M. Beaty, Joseph Stumpf Austin, Texas, March 8th 1897. Marshal's Certificate of Serving Call. J. C. W. I hereby certify that the following named Aldermen have been served with the above call by having it read to each of them in person, viz: Beaty, Belgin, Fischer, Haynes, Kuling, Lawless, Linn, Maddox, Miller, Nitching, Platt, Powell, Rudd, Roberdeau, Rosengren, Schneider, Shelley, Stumpf, Saylor, Townsend and Zilker, and That Alderman Horton has been served by a true copy, having been left at his place of business he being out of the City. James E. Lucy, City Marshal. Mr. Alderman Townsend moved a roll of the Council, which was seconded and ordered. Alderman Maddox moved to excuse Alderman Horton & Shelley which motion prevailed. The Marshal was then directed to bring in the absentees. On motion the Council took a recess of 10 minutes. At the end of the recess the Council was called to order.
