New Adult Business Rules & Big Energy Plans in Austin

Thursday, May 22, 1980 regular

  • New Zoning for Adult Businesses:

    Passed an ordinance restricting sexually oriented commercial establishments (like adult bookstores and massage parlors) from locating within 500 feet of residential zones, schools, churches, or parks.

  • Major Environmental Initiatives:

    Approved funding for critical mapping to implement the Barton Creek Watershed Ordinance and accepted the plan for the Onion Creek Wastewater Treatment Plant, aiming for federal grants.

  • Future Energy & Utility Planning:

    Entered a joint agreement to bid on Camp Swift lignite reserves for long-term power generation and enacted a new energy ordinance for new construction.

  • Community Investment & Housing:

    Allocated millions in federal Community Development Block Grant (CDBG) funds to support local housing rehabilitation, neighborhood improvements, and social services.

  • Expanding Civil Rights & Advocacy:

    Amended public accommodations and fair housing laws to include "mental handicap" as a protected classification and began the process to hire a city lobbyist for the state legislature.
