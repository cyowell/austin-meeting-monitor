Austin Debates Traffic & Zoning Changes

Thursday, April 26, 1951 regular

  • City Debates One-Way Street Plan:

    A major public hearing addressed a proposed one-way street plan, facing strong opposition from local merchants and property owners concerned about business impact. Many residents suggested street widening and improved parking as alternatives.

  • Future Zoning Law Review Scheduled:

    A public hearing was set to consider amending zoning laws. Proposed changes include permitting professional organization headquarters in residential districts and defining terms like "club" and "profession."

  • Traffic Congestion and Parking Concerns:

    The Mayor proposed an ordinance to prohibit railroad car switching during peak traffic hours (8 AM, 5 PM), while citizens also highlighted the urgent need for more downtown parking solutions.
