Friday, July 22, 1949 special called
Transcript
  • A special meeting was held to finalize the sale of bonds intended for significant city utility improvements.
  • An earlier resolution to sell $2.85 million in water, electric, and sewer revenue bonds was canceled.
  • The city approved advertising for bids on the full $7.15 million in Electric Light and Power, Water Works, and Sewer System Improvement and Extension Revenue Bonds. These funds, previously authorized by voters, are designated for major upgrades and expansions to Austin's critical utility infrastructure.
  • Bids for the $7.15 million bonds are scheduled to be opened on August 18.
