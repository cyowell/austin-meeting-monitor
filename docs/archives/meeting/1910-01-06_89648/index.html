Austin City Budget Approvals & Street Improvements 1910

Thursday, January 6, 1910 regular

• Major budget allocations passed — Council approved $37,500 in spending across departments including $25,000 for water and light accounts, $10,000 for general fund, and $2,500 for street maintenance.

• Congress Avenue lighting preserved — Citizens petitioned to keep existing streetlights on Congress Avenue, with the request referred for investigation, reflecting early public investment in infrastructure.

• New city staff hired — Two new positions were added to municipal payroll: an Assistant Foreman for the Street Department ($50/month) and an Assistant to the City Engineer ($75/month), both effective January 1st.

• Trash can program approved with conditions — The city authorized street trash cans through the Bonear-Tarr agency, but only with property owner consent—an early effort at public sanitation.

• Property dispute resolved — City attorney was authorized to file a lawsuit against a property owner accused of encroaching on Garden Street to establish the city's ownership rights.
