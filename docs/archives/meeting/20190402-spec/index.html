Austin's Downtown Future: Parks, Community & More

Tuesday, April 2, 2019 Austin City Council Special Called Meeting
  • Austin City Council received a comprehensive UT study outlining future possibilities for the downtown convention center district, focusing on integrating urban design and public benefit.
  • The study proposed expanding the convention center to the west and redeveloping the existing site to create new public parks, active street-level retail, arts, and music venues.
  • Scenarios suggested raising exhibition halls to free up ground-level space for community uses and potentially adding significant city office space to the district.
  • Financing for expansion could come from an increased hotel occupancy tax (HOT) rate, with a portion of the new revenue also allocated to arts and historic preservation.
