Austin Debates Littlefield Fence, Courts Convention Bids

Thursday, April 7, 1910 regular

• Littlefield Building Fence Dispute: City council heard protests from residents opposing a fence being erected around the Littlefield building, though the specific outcome wasn't immediately decided.

• Passageway Approval: Council approved a wooden pedestrian bridge/passageway connecting two buildings on East 6th Street, to be built and supervised by the city engineer and electrician.

• Convention Bids: Austin extended invitations to both the State Dental Association and the Federation of Labor to hold their 1911 annual conventions in the city.

• Ice Company Pipeline: The Lone Star Ice Company requested permission to lay pipes across West First and Colorado streets; the request was referred to the city attorney for review.

• 1910 Budget: Mayor Wooldridge presented the city's annual budget for discussion and consideration.
