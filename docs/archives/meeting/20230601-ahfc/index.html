ATX Housing Funds & Equity Debate

Thursday, June 1, 2023 Austin Housing Finance Corporation (AHFC) Board of Directors Regular Meeting

  • Massive Investment in Affordable Housing:

    Austin approved over $65 million in loans for local affordable housing initiatives, generating more than 850 new units across the city.

  • Support for Ongoing Projects:

    A portion of the funding includes additional awards for existing developments, acknowledging increased construction, labor, and borrowing costs.

  • Community Concerns Raised:

    Public speakers highlighted worries that some new affordable housing sites might concentrate poverty and lack sufficient public transportation, potentially violating fair housing regulations.

  • Future Development on the Horizon:

    A public hearing took place for up to $50 million in bonds to fund the Seabrook Square housing project, a major new development near Mueller.
