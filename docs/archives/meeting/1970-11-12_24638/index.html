Development Debates, Transit Changes, Urban Aid

Thursday, November 12, 1970 regular

  • Public Transit Modernization:

    The Council approved new bus rates and launched a 30-day trial for a revised East End bus route, also discussing how to separate city and University of Texas transit services.

  • Significant Land Use Decisions:

    A special permit was granted for a new day care center despite neighborhood opposition, and 39 acres were rezoned from residential to manufacturing for the Walnut Hollow Business Park.

  • Urban Growth & Federal Funding:

    A public hearing was held regarding the annexation of a 400-acre tract near Spicewood Springs Road, which saw both support and opposition (with no immediate decision). The city also formally approved its participation in the federal Model Cities Program for urban development.
