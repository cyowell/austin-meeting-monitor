Austin Tax Relief for Properties & Businesses

Monday, December 14, 1931 special called
  • The meeting focused entirely on property and business tax appeals, resulting in widespread reductions to 1931 assessments.
  • Large property tax cuts included a $34,815 credit for a major downtown building that had been torn down and a nearly $28,000 reduction for a 146-acre tract, along with a tax refund and penalty waiver for past years.
  • Merchandise inventory valuations for several local businesses were significantly lowered, some due to identified clerical errors and others reversing prior assessment increases.
  • Numerous individual property owners also received adjusted, lower valuations for their land and home improvements across various city subdivisions.
