Austin's Infrastructure Boom

Thursday, March 21, 1929 regular

  • Major Infrastructure Contracts

    Approved significant contracts for new sanitary sewer systems across northwest, northeast, and south Austin, alongside accepting completed street paving and bridge projects.

  • Urban Development & Safety

    Granted a detailed permit for a new gasoline filling station with extensive safety and construction regulations, and authorized land purchases to widen Barton Springs Road.

  • Electoral and Land Policy

    Officially set new ward boundaries for voting and resolved a land shortage issue by adjusting the width of Front Street.

  • Citizen Engagement & Tax Review

    Heard protests from property owners regarding street paving projects and approved multiple adjustments to property tax valuations following investigations.
