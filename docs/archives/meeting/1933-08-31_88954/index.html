Growth, Gas Leaks, & School Annexation

Thursday, August 31, 1933 regular

  • School District Expansion:

    Approved the annexation of territory from the Esperanza Common School District for school purposes, despite local protests over potential tax revenue loss for the rural school.

  • Shoal Creek Development:

    Kicked off a plan to develop and sell 21 lots along Shoal Creek Boulevard, including funding for water, electricity, and sewer lines, and establishing terms for sales like down payments and interest rates.

  • Major Gas Leak Repairs:

    Authorized the Texas Public Service Company to dig up streets for extensive repairs on multiple leaking gas mains, with the city setting strict standards for pavement restoration.

  • Utility Head's Efficiency Hearing:

    Debated the proper procedure for a public hearing on inefficiency charges against the Electrical Division's General Superintendent.

  • New Dam Land Appraisal:

    Formed a committee to appraise and identify land needed for overflow areas if a new low water dam is constructed.
