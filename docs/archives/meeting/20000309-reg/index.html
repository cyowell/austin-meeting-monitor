Barton Springs, Smart Growth, & Digital Expansion

Thursday, March 9, 2000 Austin City Council Regular Meeting

  • Major Environmental & Development Decisions:

    Approved significant land acquisitions to protect the Barton Springs Zone, settled large development lawsuits (including Circle C and Bradley tracts), and adjusted the Save Our Springs Initiative while annexing thousands of acres for future planning.

  • Expanding Digital Connectivity & Infrastructure:

    Granted multiple new franchises for cable and broadband internet services to enhance digital access across the city. The Council also funded upgrades for water/wastewater systems and public safety communications, and designated a future passenger rail corridor.

  • Strategic Urban Planning for Growth:

    Set public hearings for new "Smart Growth" guidelines, aiming to shape future development through master planning and infill/redevelopment proposals.

  • Community Initiatives & Energy Programs:

    Approved funding for a Children's Health Insurance Program outreach, energy efficiency rebates for residents, and initiated a study for a proposed hotel/golf course development at Decker Lake.
