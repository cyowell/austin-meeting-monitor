Airport, Towing, Skateboarding

Thursday, April 21, 1994 regular

  • Major Development & Conservation:

    Advanced the Balcones Canyonlands Conservation Plan for endangered species and scenic views. Approved initial ordinances, including height and land use limits, for the upcoming Austin Airport at Bergstrom.

  • New City Regulations:

    Established new citywide regulations for vehicle towing, including setting maximum fees.

  • Community & Energy Initiatives:

    Funded an adolescent violence and drug abuse prevention program. Held a public hearing on potentially banning skateboarding in certain areas, and approved significant rebates for energy-efficient upgrades for homes and businesses.
