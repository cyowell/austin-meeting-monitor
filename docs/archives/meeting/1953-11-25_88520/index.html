Austin: Fridge Safety, Charity, and Creek Issues

Wednesday, November 25, 1953 regular

  • Child Safety Ordinance:

    Passed a new law requiring the removal of doors or locking mechanisms from abandoned refrigerators and iceboxes to prevent child entrapment.

  • Charity Solicitation Approved:

    Despite technical issues and a board's recommendation against it, the council granted a permit for the Muscular Dystrophy Association to solicit funds.

  • Infrastructure & Development:

    Authorized the sale of multiple buildings for the future Interregional Highway right-of-way and approved extensive new gas main installations across the city.

  • Environmental Concerns:

    Addressed ongoing illegal filling of Shoal Creek, instructing the City Manager to recommend action, including potentially purchasing drainage rights-of-way.
