Austin's Big Hospital & Infrastructure Push

Thursday, August 15, 1940 regular

  • Hospital Expansion Funded

    Approved $450,000 in bonds to significantly improve city hospital facilities, with funds specifically allocated for both charity and pay patient services.

  • New Commercial Development

    Granted permission for the construction and operation of a drive-in gasoline filling station and allowed H. E. Butt Grocery Company to modify curbs and build commercial driveways.

  • Utility Infrastructure Agreements

    Authorized a key agreement for a 69,000-volt power transmission line to cross International-Great Northern Railroad tracks.

  • City Asset & Service Management

    Initiated the sale of the North Austin Fire Station and instructed officials to prepare a contract for selling city water to Travis County Water Control District No. 2.
