Zoning Disputes, Street Upgrades, and Showdowns

Thursday, November 5, 1931 regular

  • Entertainment Showdown:

    The Council denied a request to extend the Harley Sadler Show's permit, siding with local movie theaters that claimed the traveling show harmed their business, despite an offer to donate proceeds to the Community Chest.

  • Zoning & Street Planning:

    Officials addressed a zoning "oversight" on East 1st Street, granting a temporary building permit to a homeowner while planning an official district change. The City Engineer also advised against costly street widening due to existing buildings, recommending establishing fixed building lines instead.

  • Key Infrastructure Expansion:

    New utility projects were approved, including extensive water line installations on 19th Street and surrounding areas, alongside the construction of gas mains by Texas Public Service Company across several neighborhoods.

  • City Improvements & Maintenance:

    Austin's fire insurance rating improved due to a new aerial truck and stricter electrical code enforcement. The Council also approved contracts for "flush coating" (resurfacing) downtown streets like Lavaca, 9th, and 10th.
