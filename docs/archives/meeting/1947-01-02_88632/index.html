Austin's Growth: Zoning, Utilities & Waste

Thursday, January 2, 1947 regular

  • Expanded Urban Zones:

    Approved multiple zoning changes for commercial, industrial, and residential areas. A new commission was also appointed to plan zoning for recently annexed tracts of land, signaling future city growth.

  • Utility Expansion:

    Granted permits for the Texas Public Service Company to lay new gas mains across six different city streets.

  • Waste Management:

    Authorized a one-year lease for a 50-acre site to be used as the city's official garbage and trash dumping grounds.

  • Construction Safety:

    Imposed strict safety regulations and a $5,000 surety bond on a contractor utilizing public sidewalk and street space for building alterations on Congress Avenue.

  • Property Dispute:

    Addressed a citizen's claim regarding her property allegedly taken by the city for an electric plant substation, referring the matter for further investigation.
