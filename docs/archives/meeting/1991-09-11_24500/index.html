Austin City Council MINUTES For HOUSING SUBCOMMITTEE MEETING September 11, 1991 - 11:30 A.M. MUNICIPAL BUILDING ANNEX Mayor Pro Tern Urdy, Councilmembers Nofziger, Larson and Garcia met as members of the Housing Subcommittee. After approving minutes of the August 14, 1991 meeting the subcommittee discussed and heard staff reports on the following: Discussion and Recommendation on Managing Non-Profit Program Income Discussion on Contract and Lease with FREEE Discussion on Residential Tax Abatement Monthly Housing Report A more detailed set of minutes is on file in the Department of Planning and Development. Copy of those minutes is also on file in the City Clerk's office. At 1:06 P.M. the meeting was adjourned.
