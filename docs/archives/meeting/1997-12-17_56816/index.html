Austin Airport Gets Big Boost

Wednesday, December 17, 1997

  • Massive Airport Expansion Approved:

    The new Austin-Bergstrom International Airport (ABIA) is set for significant growth with approvals for a $24.2 million budget increase to expand the passenger terminal from 20 to 25 gates. This is part of a larger $35 million terminal project.

  • Key ABIA Construction Projects Funded:

    Major contracts were awarded for essential airport infrastructure, including a $27.6 million parking garage and a $3.8 million aircraft fuel storage facility.

  • Secured $30 Million in FAA Airport Grants:

    The city accepted up to $30 million in federal grants from the Federal Aviation Administration (FAA) to support ongoing development at the Austin-Bergstrom International Airport.

  • Local Infrastructure Upgrades:

    Five storm sewer improvement projects across various Austin watersheds were approved with a total contract value of $245,186.
