Paving Plans, Staff Pay, and Failed Bus Stop Benches

Thursday, October 18, 1951 regular

  • Massive Street Paving Project:

    Approved a significant $450,000+ contract for paving 64 specific streets across the city, with a public hearing scheduled to determine how the costs will be shared between property owners and the city.

  • Debate Over Bus Stop Advertising:

    A proposed ordinance to allow advertising benches at bus stops failed to pass after a split among members regarding the precedent it would set for commercial use of public spaces and concerns for property owners.

  • Employee Compensation and City Projects:

    Discussions were held on city employee salaries, weighing bonuses against general pay raises, with plans for a 1952 salary schedule focusing on incremental increases and a 75¢ minimum wage. Updates were also given on major projects like the new hospital (awaiting federal approval) and the Police and Courts building.
