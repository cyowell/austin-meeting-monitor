Austin City Hall Construction & Street Improvements 1906

Monday, September 17, 1906 regular

• New City Hall Project Moving Forward – Council approved a $2,875 excavation contract with James Bergen and authorized $362.25 for architectural plans and specifications for the new city hall building.

• Street and Infrastructure Upgrades – Funded $100 for cemetery improvements and authorized a work crew (1 team and 2 men) to clean gutters and spread dirt throughout the city.

• Failed Skating Ban – A proposed ordinance prohibiting skateboarding on sidewalks was defeated, with 7 aldermen voting against the restriction.

• Multiple Budget Approvals – Council approved approximately $2,057 in various expenditures, including $518.23 and $1,177.21 for approved street department accounts and $40 to compensate a prisoner guard.

• Business Permits Mixed Results – Council denied one platform construction request (W.S. Walkers & Sons on West 3rd Street) but approved another for W.B. Wackert & Sons at their main business location under street committee supervision.
