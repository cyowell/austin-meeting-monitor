Austin Funds Affordable Homes & Homeless Support

Thursday, November 18, 2021 Austin Housing Finance Corporation (AHFC) Board of Directors Regular Meeting

  • Major Housing Investment:

    The board approved over $20 million in loans for various housing projects, including senior housing, new affordable homes for families, and units for first-time homebuyers.

  • New Permanent Support for Homeless:

    A significant $2.85 million loan was approved for "Redfield 34," a project creating 34 units specifically for people experiencing homelessness, including necessary support services.

  • Record Homeless Housing Progress:

    A council member highlighted that with this approval, over 800 permanent supportive housing units have been approved in the last two years, a substantial increase from previous periods.

  • Community Donations Celebrated:

    A specific project for senior affordable housing received praise for a private family's property donation to a non-profit developer.
