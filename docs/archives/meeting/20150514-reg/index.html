Thursday, May 14, 2015 Austin City Council Regular Meeting
  • A controversial appointment to the Municipal Civil Service Commission sparked debate over a nominee's union affiliations and impartiality.

  • Residents voiced significant concerns about traffic safety from new commercial road access on a residential street and potential noise from the proposed kennel near Bull Creek.
