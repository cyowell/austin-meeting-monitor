Thursday, May 3, 1917 regular

  • Sanitary Sewer Funding Boost:

    $25,000 was transferred from the water and light fund to improve the city's sanitary sewer system, highlighting a focus on public health infrastructure.

  • Modernizing City Services:

    The Sanitary Department acquired a new "automobile truck" for $3,600 from Tiffin Wagon Co. (via Walter Tips Co.) after an expert committee evaluated multiple bids.
