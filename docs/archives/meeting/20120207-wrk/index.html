Tuesday, February 7, 2012 Austin City Council Work Session
Official City Record Agenda Watch Video Transcript

  • Ambulance Staffing Changes:

    Austin's EMS department proposes shifting ambulance crews from two paramedics to one paramedic and one EMT. This aims to improve patient care, address staff vacancies, and create a career ladder for medics.

  • Boosting EMS Coverage:

    Potential savings from the new staffing model would be reinvested to expand ambulance services into underserved areas of Travis County and high-demand zones within Austin.

  • Investing in Youth & Schools:

    Council members plan discussions on strengthening youth programs and fostering better partnerships between the city and school district, particularly after recent budget cuts.

  • Reevaluating Police Presence:

    A significant topic for an upcoming retreat includes reassessing the city's police officer-per-resident metric and its implications for public safety and the budget.
