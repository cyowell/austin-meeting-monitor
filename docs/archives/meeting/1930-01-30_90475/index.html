Electric Rates, Drive-Ins, & Urban Growth

Thursday, January 30, 1930 regular

  • Major Overhaul of Electricity Rates:

    New, detailed electricity rates were established for residential, commercial, industrial, and off-peak power services, directly impacting all city consumers.

  • Significant Utility Infrastructure Upgrades:

    The city approved bids exceeding $75,000 for equipment for its electric distribution substations and granted permits for the construction of new gas mains across various streets.

  • Regulating Emerging Businesses:

    A drive-in auto spring repair shop received a permit with extensive conditions covering public safety, traffic flow, waste management, and building codes.

  • Investing in Public Works & Spaces:

    Funds were allocated for a new Fire Department practice tower and land was approved for purchase to develop Shoal Creek Boulevard, alongside decisions to manage city alleys and construction site access.
