Thursday, January 29, 1953 regular
Transcript

  • Denied Application for Home for the Aged:

    City Council rejected a proposal for a home for the aged on San Gabriel Street following strong opposition from neighbors.

  • Significant Utility Infrastructure Expansion:

    Approved plans for new 11,800-volt power lines and extensive gas main installations across multiple city streets.

  • Zoning Debates and Changes:

    Discussed a resident's appeal regarding commercial building setbacks on Lamar Boulevard and approved a zoning change for a specific lot from restricted to general commercial use.

  • Addressing Unserved Water Access:

    Initiated an investigation into a complaint about 11 homes on Goodwin Avenue that lacked water service due to complicated property circumstances.
