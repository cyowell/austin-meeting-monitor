Austin Civil Service Commission Nominations

Monday, May 4, 2015 Audit and Finance Committee Regular Meeting
  • The Audit and Finance Committee interviewed multiple candidates for positions on the Municipal Civil Service Commission.
  • The committee recommended Michael Murphy and Late Detaily to the full City Council for nomination to three-year terms on the commission.
  • Commissioner Lancaster was recommended to serve as the next Chair of the Municipal Civil Service Commission.
