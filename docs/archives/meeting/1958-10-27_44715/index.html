Monday, October 27, 1958 special called
Transcript

  • New Prevailing Wage Scale Adopted:

    After a lengthy discussion with contractors and labor unions, the council formally approved a detailed prevailing wage scale for numerous construction trades (e.g., bricklayers, electricians, plumbers). This scale would apply to city projects, including an upcoming reservoir.

  • Ben White Boulevard Expansion Funding:

    The city agreed to a cost-sharing plan with Travis County to acquire land for the Ben White Boulevard right-of-way, with Austin committing to pay 3/5ths of the property acquisition costs.

  • Proposed Gas Contract Reviewed:

    Council members held a detailed discussion with an advisory committee regarding a new proposed gas contract, reviewing its terms and gathering feedback, though no final decision was made during this meeting.
