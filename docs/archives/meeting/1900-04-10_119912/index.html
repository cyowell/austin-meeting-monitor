Austin Bridge Repairs and Creek Infrastructure Plan

Tuesday, April 10, 1900 special called

• Avenue Bridge Funding Approved: The city council unanimously approved a $2,000 appropriation to repair the Congress Avenue bridge approaches over the Colorado River, with the county covering additional costs to bring the total project to $4,500.

• Cost-Sharing Agreement with County: The county commissioners agreed to complete the bridge work if Austin covered $2,000 of the estimated $4,500 total cost, representing a shared infrastructure investment between city and county.

• New Creek Bridge Study Ordered: Council directed the city engineer to assess the cost and feasibility of constructing a new bridge across Shoal Creek on West 12th Street to handle water drainage.

• Unanimous Vote: All six aldermen present voted in favor of the bridge appropriation with no opposition, showing strong council consensus on the infrastructure improvements.

To the Honorable Mayor and Board of Aldermen of the City of Austin! Gentlemen! Your Committee on streets to whom was referred the matter of Conferring with the County Commissioners Court on the subject of repairing the Long Ave bridge across the Colorado river and its approaches, beg leave to report that said Commissioners Court has agreed to put said Long Ave road bridge in first-class condition provided the Council will pay to said County the sum $2000. Estimated total Cost of the work is $4500. We hereby recommend that the City accept said proposition and appropriate said sum of $2000 for said purpose Francis Discher Joseph Stumph H.E.Nolen Committee --- Page break --- 296 By Alderman Fischer an ordinance appropriating the sum of $2000. for the purpose of paying for repairing the southern approach to the Congress Avenue bridge across the Colorado River under the direction of the County Commissioners Court and the City Engineer The ordinance was passed under suspension of the rules by the following vote Yeas Aldermen Dunham, Fischer, Glass, Mortmen, Nolen, Rittsoff, & Nays None. Alderman Fischer offered the following resolution which was read and adopted "Be it resolved by the City Council of the City of Austin; That the City Engineer be and is hereby instructed to make an estimate of the cost of a bridge across Shoal Creek on West 12th Street adequate to carry the water of said Creek and report to the next meeting of the Council On Motion the Council adjourned.
