Austin Election Update: Runoffs for Mayor & Seats

Monday, April 4, 1977 special called
  • Officials finalized the results of the April 2 municipal election, formally validating votes cast across all precincts despite a minor ballot discrepancy in one.
  • Three City Council seats were definitively filled, with Betty Himmelblau (Place 2), John Trevino, Jr. (Place 5), and Jimmy Snell (Place 6) declared the winners.
  • A runoff election was promptly scheduled for April 30 to determine the next Mayor and Councilmembers for Places 1, 3, and 4, as no candidate achieved a majority vote in those key races.
