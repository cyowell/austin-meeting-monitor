Jitneys, Land Deals, and City Growth

Thursday, July 1, 1915 regular

  • Regulating "Jitneys":

    A new ordinance was passed to define, license, and regulate "Jitneys," an early form of shared public transportation.

  • Major Infrastructure Investment:

    Approved compensation for land submerged by the new dam and allocated funds to acquire land for a new sewer disposal plant.

  • Modernizing City Operations:

    Authorized the purchase of an automobile for a department head and hired engineers to oversee significant bridge rebuilding projects.

  • Honoring a Fallen Firefighter:

    Funds were appropriated for the burial expenses of Tom Quinn, who heroically died saving lives during a recent flood.
