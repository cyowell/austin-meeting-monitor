Wednesday, February 11, 1998
Transcript

  • Prioritized Sustainable Energy:

    The council received a briefing on sustainable energy and approved significant investments, including a $50,400 rebate for high-efficiency air conditioners at a local apartment complex and a $6.8 million contract for broader energy services performance.

  • New Utility Branding:

    A new logo and business name were officially approved for the City of Austin Electric Utility Department.

  • Watershed Protection Initiative:

    A regional study of Onion Creek was authorized, partnering with the U.S. Army Corps of Engineers to enhance environmental and flood protection efforts.

  • Essential Utility Upgrades:

    Several contracts were approved for critical electric utility infrastructure and supplies, including battery systems, optical power ground wire, and street lighting, to ensure reliable service.
