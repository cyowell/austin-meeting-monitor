Austin: New Building, Taxis, & Fire Safety Rules

Wednesday, November 23, 1938 regular

  • New Municipal Building Unveiled

    The City Council held its inaugural meeting in the newly completed Municipal Building, receiving praise from the Austin Chamber of Commerce.

  • Taxi Services Expanded

    Several new licenses were approved for taxi operators and drivers, including additional cabs for an existing proprietor, indicating active regulation of public transport.

  • Basement Smoking Ban for Safety

    A strict "no smoking" policy was enforced in the Municipal Building's basement to protect valuable and irreplaceable city records from fire hazards.

  • Tax Penalty Relief Offered

    A property owner was granted a remission of penalties and half the interest on several years of overdue taxes, conditional on prompt payment of the remaining amount.
