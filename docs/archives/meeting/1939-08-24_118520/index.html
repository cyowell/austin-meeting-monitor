Austin: Zoning, Utilities, and Social Issues

Thursday, August 24, 1939 regular

  • Zoning Change with Conditions:

    A segment of East 7th Street was rezoned for "C-2" Commercial use, with the specific stipulation that any future liquor establishments in the area must only have entrances on 7th Street, not Brazos Street. This decision followed public input from an adjoining property owner.

  • Major Utility Infrastructure Advances:

    The city formally accepted the completed Electric Power Plant & System improvement project. Additionally, permits were granted to the Texas Public Service Company to lay new gas mains across several city streets, expanding critical services.

  • Community Addresses "Subversive" Activities:

    The American Legion presented a resolution commending the city for denying public parks for "communistic gatherings" and offered their services in combating "subversive groups," reflecting social anxieties of the era.

  • Healthcare Funding Approved:

    Funds were appropriated to purchase an Electro Surgical Unit for Brackenridge Hospital, enhancing the city's medical services.
