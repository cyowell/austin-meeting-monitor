Austin: Gas Lines, Relief, & Big City Budget

Thursday, January 28, 1932 regular

  • Approved Austin's entire 1932 operating budget

    , allocating over $2.7 million for city services including police, fire, parks, and major investments in water and electric utilities.

  • Authorized Texas Public Service Company to lay new gas mains on Cross Street

    , with specific conditions for installation and liability regarding other underground utilities.

  • Dedicated funds to "General Relief" efforts within the Charity Division

    , providing essential support for residents during the challenging economic climate.
