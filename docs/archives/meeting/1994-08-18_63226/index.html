Austin's Future: Land, Health, & Infrastructure

Thursday, August 18, 1994 regular

  • Development & Land Use:

    Initiated the annexation of multiple new areas, approved significant rezonings, and committed over $6.4 million to expand the Barton Creek Wilderness Park.

  • Healthcare & Social Services:

    Allocated increased funding for HIV programs, approved major equipment upgrades for Brackenridge Hospital, and purchased Meadow Glen Apartments for low-income housing.

  • City Infrastructure & Modernization:

    Authorized an $805,000 mainframe computer for the Police Department and approved substantial investments in electric utility plant maintenance and upgrades.

  • Community Initiatives & Financial Planning:

    Named the city animal shelter "Town Lake Animal Center" and initiated discussions to potentially redirect a portion of sales tax toward healthcare and road maintenance.
