Over $64M for Austin Housing Developments

Thursday, October 10, 2002 Austin Housing Finance Corporation (AHFC)
  • Approved preliminary resolutions to seek over $64 million in state bond financing for six new multi-family apartment developments.
  • These projects, including Villas on Sixth Street and Travis Park Apartments, aim to create new housing options across Austin.
  • Potential development locations include East 6th Street, the Highway 71 area, and East Oltorf Drive.
