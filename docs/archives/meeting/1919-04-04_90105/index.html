Burning Bonds and Nurse Car Costs

Friday, April 4, 1919 special called

  • Financial Housekeeping:

    City officials publicly incinerated $417,000 worth of various municipal bonds (including refunding, water works, and sewer bonds) in the City Hall furnace.

  • Property Tax Correction:

    Approved the cancellation of $100 in excessive property assessments for two lots in the Welch Subdivision, instructing the assessor to provide credits.

  • Public Health Support:

    Allocated $135 to the Municipal Nurse to cover car expenses like gasoline and oil, aiding her in city service.

  • Infrastructure Compensation:

    Authorized a $3,000 payment as a "profit fee" to the contractors, Potts, Moore & Prentice, for their work on the city's sewage disposal plant.
