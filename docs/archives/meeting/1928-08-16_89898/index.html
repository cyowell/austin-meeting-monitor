Fueling Austin: Safety, Parks & Public Works

Thursday, August 16, 1928 regular

  • Five Gas Stations Approved with Strict Safety Rules:

    Permits were granted for multiple new or upgraded gasoline filling stations across the city, all subject to comprehensive safety regulations. These included detailed conditions for construction, fire prevention, electrical wiring, waste disposal, and pump placement to protect the public.

  • Investing in Public Parks and Playgrounds:

    The city moved forward with plans to landscape and improve East Avenue, including hiring a specialist for complex sections. Preparations were also made to purchase property for a new neighborhood playground near Winn School, and the leveling of an old reservoir site was authorized, potentially for future public use.

  • Expanding City Infrastructure and Transportation:

    Permits were issued for the installation of new gas mains and significant upgrades to the telephone network, including underground conduits and pole lines. The city also established specific parking and maneuvering agreements for Greyhound buses, reflecting evolving urban transportation needs.
