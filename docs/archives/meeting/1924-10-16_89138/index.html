Austin: New Incinerator, Road Roller & Parades

Thursday, October 16, 1924 regular

  • Major Infrastructure Investment:

    Approved a contract for a new garbage incinerator plant, marking a significant upgrade in the city's waste management.

  • Street & Fleet Upgrades:

    Authorized the purchase of a 10-ton gasoline-operated road roller for improving city streets and a new Ford touring car for municipal use.

  • Community Events & Permits:

    Granted permission for an Armistice Day parade on Congress Avenue and approved a permit for the "Cuban Shows" carnival, conditioned on a sanitation deposit.

  • Public Health Response:

    Addressed payroll for guards enforcing a quarantine at the State Lunatic Asylum, highlighting contemporary public health concerns.
