Austin's Future: Streets, Health, and Growth

Thursday, August 22, 1963 regular

  • Major Transportation Planning:

    The city reviewed a comprehensive $85,000 study to guide future street and traffic development, with a focus on new expressways and key thoroughfares.

  • Urban Renewal and Land Decisions:

    Plans moved forward for "slum clearance" and redevelopment in the Kealing Project. Debates occurred over selling city land near Town Lake and a contentious zoning request highlighting disagreements over street paving costs.

  • Public Health and New Rules:

    A $30,000 study was approved to assess community hospital and health needs. An ordinance was introduced to license and regulate pool halls, and officials were tasked with investigating bus idling causing air pollution.

  • Inclusive Leadership Efforts:

    The Mayor proposed future appointments of Negro and Latin American members to the Urban Renewal Agency Board to broaden community representation.
