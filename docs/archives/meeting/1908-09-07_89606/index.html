Austin City Infrastructure and Budget Decisions 1908

Monday, September 7, 1908 regular

• Street Improvements Approved: Council authorized opening West 30th Street from Speedway to Guadalupe and approved $200 for Duval Street grading/graveling work, plus $57.95 to complete drainage at the 5th Street Fire Hall.

• Fire Department Expansion Request: Members of the 7th Ward Fire Company petitioned to be admitted into the city's Fire Department, with their request referred to the Board of Fire Commissioners for consideration.

• Public Lighting and Utilities: West Austin Improvement Club successfully petitioned to install a street light on Blanca Street between 8th and 9th streets, and the council directed sewerage system extensions to be made by the Austin Sewerage Company.

• Budget Allocations: Council approved nearly $1,957 in accounts payable, plus $40 for a park keeper's August salary and $329.01 for street department expenses.

• Administrative Actions: The city attorney was granted 30 days leave, and council directed committees to investigate livestock feed costs for city departments.
