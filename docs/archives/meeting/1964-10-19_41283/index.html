Austin Property Values & Zoning Shift

Monday, October 19, 1964 special called

  • Property Tax Fairness Under Fire:

    A major public hearing centered on citizen Nelson Puett's extensive appeal of property tax assessments. He argued for fair, market-value based appraisals and questioned a recent $50 million increase in the tax roll, highlighting the lack of a city-wide reappraisal since 1947.

  • Calls for Tax Reform & Oversight:

    Puett urged the city to establish a Citizens Committee to scrutinize appraisal methods and tax changes. He also suggested exploring alternative revenue sources like a sales or sewer tax to reduce reliance on property taxes.

  • Detailed Valuation Disputes:

    Puett presented numerous examples of his properties, contending some were valued too low by the city, while many others—particularly newly built cottages—were assessed significantly above their construction cost or recent sales prices.

  • Contentious Zoning Change Advances:

    The council debated an ordinance to rezone a portion of the F. Haster Subdivision from residential to commercial. A motion to postpone the decision failed, and the ordinance passed its second reading, moving towards final approval.
