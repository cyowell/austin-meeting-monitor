Grand Jury Eyes Austin Court; New Judges Sought

Friday, February 23, 1968 special called
  • A city review into the Corporation Court's operations was postponed because a Grand Jury initiated its own investigation, subpoenaing key officials and court records.
  • City leaders emphasized supporting the Grand Jury's impartial inquiry to avoid any appearance of political influence in the sensitive matter.
  • The city is actively interviewing candidates to appoint new Corporation Court judges, with plans to establish improved court procedures once they are in place.
