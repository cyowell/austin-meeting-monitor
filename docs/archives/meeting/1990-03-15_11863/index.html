Sixth Street Boost, New Airport Planning Begins

Thursday, March 15, 1990 regular

Here's a look at key decisions from a March 1990 Austin agenda:

  • Sixth Street Safety & Improvements:

    New police officers, a public urination ban, and plans for improved street lighting were approved to enhance safety and cleanliness in the East 6th Street entertainment district.

  • Major Infrastructure Growth:

    Land acquisition moved forward for the U.S. Highway 183 expansion, and a contract was awarded for appraisal services, signaling the start of planning for a future new Austin Airport.

  • Affordable Housing & Community Programs:

    The city revised its affordable housing policy, approved funding for an Urban Homesteading Program, and addressed financial details for emergency shelters and community development grants.

  • Anti-Drug Efforts & Tourism Promotion:

    Applications for over $1.4 million in state grants were authorized to fund police anti-drug units, alongside continued support for ethnic/minority marketing to boost tourism and conventions.
