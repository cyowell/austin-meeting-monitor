Austin's Housing Crisis: Gaps in Shelters & Support

Tuesday, August 6, 2024 Austin City Council Budget Work Session

  • Urgent Shelter Needs:

    A $2.8 million budget gap threatens the full operation of Northbridge and Southbridge homeless shelters, while Austin's overall shelter capacity remains significantly lower than comparable cities.

  • Massive Rapid Rehousing Shortfall:

    Over 1,700 individuals are currently on a waitlist for rapid rehousing services, highlighting an annual funding gap estimated at more than $60 million.

  • Long-Term Permanent Supportive Housing Crisis:

    Austin needs 5,050 new permanent supportive housing units over the next decade to address chronic homelessness, a goal significantly underfunded by current capital sources.

  • High-Impact Prevention Underfunded:

    Homelessness prevention programs demonstrate a 97% success rate at a fraction of the cost ($2,353 per household) compared to rehousing efforts, yet city investment in this area is minimal.
