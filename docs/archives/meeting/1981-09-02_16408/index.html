Transit Boost, Senior Swim, Health Care Debate

Wednesday, September 2, 1981 regular

Here's a summary of the Austin City Council's September 2, 1981, agenda:

  • Public Health & Affordability

    Scheduled a public hearing for major changes to the Medical Assistance Program's pharmacy services, potentially introducing co-pays and new clinic pharmacies, alongside approving a new, reduced swim fee for senior citizens.

  • Strategic Development

    Granted retail zoning for Fairway Estates with strict conditions, including significant tree buffers, privacy fences, and mandatory council approval for site and traffic plans to mitigate neighborhood impact.

  • Infrastructure & Transit

    Accepted a federal grant to purchase an additional transit bus and acquired land necessary for vital road widening and new water line projects.

  • Regulatory Review

    Set future public hearings to consider proposed amendments to the city's taxicab ordinance and recommendations from the Fire and Life Safety Committee.
