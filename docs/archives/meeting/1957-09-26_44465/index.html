Austin Utility Budget: Power Plant Expansion Ahead

Thursday, September 26, 1957 regular

  • $11.4 Million Utility Budget Proposed:

    Austin's Utility Fund projects $11.4 million in total receipts for 1957-58, anticipating significant revenue growth driven largely by increasing electricity sales.

  • Major Power Plant Expansion:

    Over $1.6 million is earmarked for capital improvements, with a substantial portion dedicated to expanding the electric power plant, including significant investments in new boiler and turbo-generator equipment.

  • Supporting City Services:

    A considerable $3.9 million from utility revenues is allocated to the City's General Fund, contributing significantly to broader municipal services.

  • Water and Sewer Infrastructure Growth:

    While direct utility fund capital spending for water and sewer is modest this year, major infrastructure improvements are planned for both, funded primarily through revenue bonds.
