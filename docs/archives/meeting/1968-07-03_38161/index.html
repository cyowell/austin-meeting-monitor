    City leaders approved a major update to police guidelines on the use of force, firearms, and reporting procedures for incidents involving injury or discharge of a weapon, incorporating specific state statutes.

  • Bus Service Regulation Debated:

    An extensive public hearing was held on a proposed ordinance to regulate bus operations, sparking debate on requiring "public convenience and necessity" for new services versus concerns about creating monopolies for existing companies.

  • City Expansion and Green Space:

  • International "Sister City" Program:
