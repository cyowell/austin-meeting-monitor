Austin Debates Tax System, Health, Sewer Plans

Wednesday, April 25, 1917 regular
  • Approved funding for detailed plans for a new sewerage disposal plant.
  • Rejected a proposal to overturn the "Sommers Unit System" for property tax valuations, retaining the current assessment method.
  • Failed to centralize public health administration under a single officer, preserving the existing structure of various health and inspection roles.
  • Initiated a significant transfer of $25,000 from Water & Light funds to the Sanitary Sewer department.
