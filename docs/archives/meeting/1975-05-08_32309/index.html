Austin funds nuclear power, debates bond bids

Thursday, May 8, 1975 special called
  • Austin approved the issuance of $7.5 million in revenue bonds to upgrade its electric, waterworks, and sewer systems.
  • A key part of this funding was allocated for Austin's 16% investment in the South Texas Nuclear Project, a new power plant in Matagorda County.
  • During the bidding process for these bonds, only one out of six submitted proposals fully conformed to the city's requirements.
  • Despite a non-conforming bid being $200,000 cheaper, the city council chose to accept the only compliant bid to uphold its financial integrity and avoid future market speculation.
