Transcript

Here's a summary of the Austin City Council's April 26, 1979 meeting:

  • Major Urban Development & Equity Planning:

    The council initiated planning for a central city redevelopment district. Critically, they also expanded an economic development market analysis to include underserved East and South Austin areas, addressing concerns about equitable growth across the city.

  • Elimination of Utility Standby Charges:

    Following a public hearing, the council voted to eliminate the monthly water and wastewater standby charges, noting that administrative costs exceeded the revenue collected. They also authorized the final execution of the complex LoVaca natural gas supply settlement plan.

  • Key Public Projects & Community Services:

    An architect was selected for the Phase III expansion of Brackenridge Hospital. The council also reprogrammed Community Development Block Grant (CDBG) funds for various community improvements (like water adjustments and street lighting) and approved a day of free public transit for senior citizens.
