May Election Certified: Runoffs & Charter Defeat

Monday, May 9, 1988 special called
  • Certified the official results of the May 7 municipal election.
  • Smoot Carl-Mitchell and Charles Urdy were declared winners for City Council Places 2 and 6, respectively.
  • A runoff election was scheduled for May 28 for races where no candidate received a majority, including the Mayor's office (between Frank C. Cooksey and Lee Cooke) and Council Place 5 (between Sam Guzman and Robert Barnstone).
  • Voters rejected a proposed City Charter amendment that would have expanded the governing body to a nine-member council with district representation.
