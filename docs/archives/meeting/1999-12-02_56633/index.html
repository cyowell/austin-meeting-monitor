Austin eyes new City Hall, major developments & utility growth

Thursday, December 2, 1999 regular

  • Major Development & Land Use:

    Approved a comprehensive rezoning plan for the "Champion Property" with strict conditions on traffic, environmental buffers, and building sizes. A public hearing was also held for the annexation of the 1,630-acre Avery Ranch.

  • Infrastructure & Planning Initiatives:

    Authorized initial architectural planning for a new City Hall and acquired 457 acres for expanding the Hornsby Bend Biosolids Management Plant. Briefings were also given on the Town Lake Master Plan and the Bull Creek Study.

  • Public Services & Regulations:

    Denied a request for a taxi fare increase, requesting further analysis. The council also set a public hearing to regulate valet parking services and funded a mobile unit for child care outreach.

  • Significant Financial Settlement:

    Approved a $1.08 million settlement in the "John Ross v. City of Austin" lawsuit.
