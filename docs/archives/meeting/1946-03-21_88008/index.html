Thursday, March 21, 1946 regular
Transcript

  • Major Zoning Changes:

    Several properties across the city were rezoned from residential to commercial and industrial districts, signaling a push for urban development and business growth.

  • Public Safety Concerns:

    Congress Avenue businesses appealed for help against a "gang of hoodlums" (juveniles) disrupting customers; the Police Chief was instructed to provide additional protection.

  • Construction Boom:

    Permits were granted for multiple building projects, allowing temporary use of public sidewalks and streets, with strict requirements for public safety and access.

  • Recreation Management:

    Concessions for popular recreational spots, Zilker Springs and Deep Eddy, were awarded to a private operator for the upcoming year.
