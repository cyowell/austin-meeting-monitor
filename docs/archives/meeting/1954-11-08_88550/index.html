1150 1150 CITY OF AUSTIN, TEXAS MINUTES OF THE CITY COUNCIL CITY OF AUSTIN, TEXAS Recessed Hearing of the SOUTHWESTERN BELL TELEPHONE COMPANY Request for Rate Increase November 8, 9, 10, 1954 Council Chamber, City Hall Roll Call: The meeting was called to order with Mayor McAden presiding.
