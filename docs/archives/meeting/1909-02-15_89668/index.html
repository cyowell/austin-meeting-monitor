Monday, February 15, 1909 regular
Transcript

• Building permits approved – The council granted several construction permits, including permission for the B.P.O. Elks to install a galvanized roof and for D.H. Doom to add a frame building addition, though the latter faced three opposing votes.

• Cemetery improvements funded – Allocated $400 to compile, letter, and number cemetery lots at Oakwood Cemetery, with the Cemetery Committee directed to obtain a detailed map of the grounds.

• Infrastructure and maintenance spending – Approved expenditures for sewerage work in West Austin ($235), street force payroll ($350), and mapping/engineering projects totaling over $40.

• Damage claims settled – Approved payments including $45 to Will Ziller for property wall damage and reimbursement to Steve Burland for an awning destroyed by a fire truck.

• Interurban railroad extension – Extended the deadline for an interurban railroad company to commence construction on their road through the city.
