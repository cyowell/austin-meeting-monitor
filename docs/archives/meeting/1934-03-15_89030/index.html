Austin: Water Rate Cuts, Street Changes, Music Plans

Thursday, March 15, 1934 regular

  • Summer Water Rates to Be Reduced:

    The City Manager was instructed to draft a resolution for significant discounts (10-25%) on domestic water bills for the upcoming summer, with an estimated city revenue reduction capped at $18,000.

  • New Driveways and Curb Changes Approved:

    Two separate permits were granted for private property owners to construct new driveways and modify curbs, requiring adherence to strict city-mandated concrete and construction standards.

  • Austin High School Bands Offer Concerts:

    A proposal from the Austin High School Bands to furnish summer concerts for $300 was submitted and taken under advisement.
