Austin Energy Rates: A Controversial Deal

Friday, March 1, 2013 Austin City Council Special Called Meeting
  • The Austin City Council approved a settlement regarding Austin Energy's electricity rates, aimed at resolving a lengthy "rate contest."
  • A key point of contention was the proposed rate structure, which included specific discounts for out-of-city customers.
  • Critics argued the deal was unfair to Austin residents, potentially offering electricity below cost to wealthier suburbanites, and decried a lack of public transparency in its negotiation.
  • Supporters emphasized the settlement as a vital compromise to prevent prolonged litigation and protect core city initiatives like energy efficiency, renewable programs, and multi-tiered rates.
  • Despite public debate and concerns over fairness, the settlement was ultimately approved by a unanimous 7-0 vote.
