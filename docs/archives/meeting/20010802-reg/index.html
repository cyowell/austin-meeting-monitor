Thursday, August 2, 2001 Austin City Council Regular Meeting

Here's a concise summary of the Austin City Council's agenda from August 2, 2001:

  • Shaping Austin's Growth & Budget:

    The council began reviewing the proposed 2001-2002 annual budget and set public hearings for a new property tax rate. They also scheduled hearings for numerous large-scale annexations, including areas like Del Valle and Avery Ranch, significantly expanding the city's boundaries.

  • Downtown & Waterfront Development:

    Approved rezoning for a major urban redevelopment project on Congress Avenue and continued discussions on "The Vintage on Town Lake," a large multifamily development proposed along the waterfront.

  • Key Infrastructure & Environmental Policies:

    Directed city staff to analyze a new ordinance regarding the routing and location of liquid petroleum pipelines, and approved revisions to the city's drainage utility fees, which impact property owners.

  • Community Housing & Parks:

    Allocated over $13 million in federal and local funds for housing assistance and community development programs through the annual HUD Action Plan, alongside approving new contracts for renovating West Austin Park and Dottie Jordan Playground.
