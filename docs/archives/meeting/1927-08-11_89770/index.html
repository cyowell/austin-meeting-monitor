Austin: Health, Fire Limits, and Street Ads

Thursday, August 11, 1927 regular

  • New Public Health Regulations:

    A significant ordinance was passed to regulate the construction, maintenance, and use of human waste disposal facilities within Austin and its police jurisdiction.

  • Expanded Fire Limits:

    The council established and passed an ordinance for new fire limits across the city, enhancing building safety.

  • Street Paving Accepted:

    New paving, curbs, gutters, and sidewalks on Fifth, Neches, and Trinity Streets were formally accepted after being completed by the Southwest Bitulithic Company.

  • Advertising on Traffic Posts:

    The Texas State Exposition received permission to place advertising posters on city traffic signal posts, under the supervision of the City Engineer.

  • Speeding Fines Remitted:

    Costs were remitted for two individuals whose speeding fines had already been paid, a direct council intervention in specific legal cases.
