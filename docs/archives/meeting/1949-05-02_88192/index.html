Austin Elects New Mayor, Staff

Monday, May 2, 1949 special called

  • New Leadership Takes Office:

    A new group of elected officials was sworn in, including a new Mayor, Taylor Glass, and Mayor Pro Tem, W.S. Drake. This marked the end of Mayor Tom Miller's 16-year term.

  • Key City Officials Appointed:

    The newly formed leadership team made several critical appointments, including Guiton Morgan as City Manager, Joe B. Roberts as Judge of the Corporation Court, and Dr. Ben Primer as City Health Officer.

  • Rapid City Growth Highlighted:

    Outgoing Mayor Tom Miller noted Austin's substantial population increase from 50,000 to 150,000 residents during his tenure, underscoring the expanding challenges and future opportunities for the growing city.
