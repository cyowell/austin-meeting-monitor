Paving, Pipes, and Property Tax Relief

Thursday, February 21, 1929 regular

  • Infrastructure Projects:

    Approved contracts for new storm sewers, a culvert, and a bridge. Also, granted permits for extensive new gas main installations across several city streets.

  • Urban Development Permits:

    A downtown building project on West Eighth Street secured approval to temporarily occupy street space and excavate under the sidewalk, subject to strict safety and restoration requirements.

  • Automotive Growth:

    Two new drive-in gasoline filling stations received permits at key intersections, reflecting the growing auto industry and requiring adherence to detailed safety standards.

  • Financial Oversight:

    City funds were reallocated to support street and sewer projects, and numerous property and vehicle tax valuations were adjusted downward after being deemed excessive.
