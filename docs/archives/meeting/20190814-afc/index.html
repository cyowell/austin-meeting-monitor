City Tackles Wildfire Risk, Permit Delays

Wednesday, August 14, 2019 Audit and Finance Committee Regular Meeting
  • An audit revealed Austin is behind on wildfire preparedness, having only treated 11% of high-risk public lands and still debating adopting a Wildland-Urban Interface (WUI) code for safer building standards.
  • The city's development permitting process remains slow, costly, and inconsistent, with concerns that up to half of residential projects may bypass proper permits.
  • New state law (HB 3167) imposes challenging 30-day review deadlines for subdivision and site plans, requiring city departments to significantly adjust operations, potentially through overtime.
  • An update detailed progress on implementing a 2017 audit's recommendations for improving investigations into city employee harassment, discrimination, and retaliation claims.
