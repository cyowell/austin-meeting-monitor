Austin Gas Contract Debate & New Airport Plans

Thursday, February 18, 1960 regular

  • Natural Gas Contract Overhaul:

    The city decided to re-open the bidding process for a crucial 20-year natural gas supply for its power grid, following public controversy and an offer from a current vendor to restart negotiations.

  • New Airport Terminal Concessions:

    Contracts were approved for a restaurant, gift shop, and newsstand at the new airport terminal, outlining operational terms and revenue sharing.

  • Auditorium Rates & City Development:

    Discussions began on adjusting convention rental rates to attract more events, while several zoning changes were approved to allow new retail and office spaces.

  • Water Plant Upgrades & Downtown Parking:

    The city authorized purchasing new sludge removal equipment for a water treatment plant and established new hourly and monthly parking fees for a downtown lot.
