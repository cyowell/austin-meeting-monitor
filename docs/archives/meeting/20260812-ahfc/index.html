Wednesday, August 12, 2026 Austin Housing Finance Corporation

Here's a summary of what the Austin Housing Finance Corporation (AHFC) Board of Directors did at their August 12, 2026 meeting:

  • Approved Major Operating Budget:

    The Board adopted a grant operating budget of nearly $42 million for AHFC and an additional $1.7 million for the Housing Assistance Fund for the upcoming fiscal year (FY 26-27). This money will fund the day-to-day operations and various housing programs.

  • Approved Capital Budget for Housing Projects:

    A separate capital budget of over $48.5 million was approved for FY 26-27. This funding is dedicated to significant housing development projects.

  • Authorized Management of City Housing Programs:

    The Board approved an agreement for AHFC to manage and operate various housing programs on behalf of the City of Austin for FY 26-27, with a budget not to exceed $90.5 million. This ensures the continued operation of important housing services for residents.

  • All Items Passed Unanimously:

    All three items were approved together as part of a "consent agenda" without any public speakers, discussion, or objection from the board members, indicating quick and unanimous support.
