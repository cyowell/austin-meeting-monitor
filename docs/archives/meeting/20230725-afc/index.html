Tuesday, July 25, 2023 Audit and Finance Committee Regular Meeting
  • An audit revealed significant data reliability issues across city departments, affecting the public "Open Data Portal" with outdated or inaccurate information, prompting calls for a citywide strategy and centralized oversight.
