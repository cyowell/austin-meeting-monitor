Thursday, August 21, 1997 regular
Transcript

  • Airport Transition:

    The city prepared for its aviation future by approving new parking fees at Robert Mueller Municipal Airport and awarding rental car concessions for the upcoming Austin-Bergstrom International Airport.

  • Innovative Building & Home Businesses:

    New building code requirements for sustainable "straw bale" construction were adopted, alongside updated rules to support home-based businesses, addressing aspects like employee parking.

  • Community Planning & Identity:

    A pilot program for neighborhood-led planning was launched in areas like Dawson and East Cesar Chavez, and a portion of 26th Street was officially renamed Dean Keeton.

  • Regional Rail Investment:

    The city committed to guaranteeing a loan for Amtrak, aiming to support the expansion of rail services across Texas.
