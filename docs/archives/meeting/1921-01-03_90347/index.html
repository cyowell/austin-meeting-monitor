Austin Takes On Gas Co., Licenses "Service Cars"

Monday, January 3, 1921 special called

  • City to Roll Back Gas Rates:

    The council moved to force the Austin Gas Light Company to revert to its 1919 gas rate schedule, accusing the company of making "excess profits" and arbitrarily raising prices without justification. This decision followed a committee report that challenged the company's valuation and accounting practices.

  • "Service Car" Operations Approved:

    Several individuals were granted licenses to operate "service cars," marking an early step in the city's regulation of private for-hire transportation services.

  • City Hospital Contracts Awarded:

    Essential supply contracts for the City Hospital were approved, including bids for drugs, meat, and laundry services.
