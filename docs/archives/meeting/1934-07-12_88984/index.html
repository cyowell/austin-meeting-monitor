Utility Bonds, Hospital Staff, River Rules

Thursday, July 12, 1934 regular

  • Major Utility Upgrade:

    The city approved $857,000 in revenue bonds to fund significant repairs and expansions for Austin's water, electric, and sewer systems, with repayment secured by utility revenues.

  • City Hospital Staffing:

    Funding was allocated to hire a bookkeeper, a charity investigator, and a drug clerk for the City Hospital.

  • Community Requests:

    Citizens submitted petitions requesting a new recreation park and an ordinance to regulate fishing in the Colorado River, with the latter scheduled for a future public hearing.
