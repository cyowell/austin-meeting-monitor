Here's a summary of the Austin City Council agenda from August 28, 1969:

  • Massive Future Development Planned:

    A 5-6 year Capital Improvements Report was presented, outlining over $200 million in projected spending for major city projects including a new municipal building, hospital upgrades, police facilities, airport improvements, and continued development of Town Lake.

  • Mo-Pac Highway & City Expansion:

    The city moved forward with land acquisition, including authorizing eminent domain for several properties, to develop the crucial Mo-Pac Boulevard (now Loop 1). Extensive annexations of numerous parcels were also planned to expand Austin's boundaries and tax base.

  • Blackshear Urban Renewal Sparks Debate:

    Residents from the Blackshear area petitioned for a public hearing to reconsider urban renewal and neighborhood development projects, expressing concerns about displacement. A motion to set a specific public hearing for the Neighborhood Development Program ultimately failed.

  • New Road Name & Traffic Rules:

    A portion of Highway 183 was officially renamed Research Boulevard. Additionally, ordinances were passed to adjust school zone speed limits and modify parking regulations for alleys and loading zones.
