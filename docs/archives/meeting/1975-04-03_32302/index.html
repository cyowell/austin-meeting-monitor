MLK Blvd Renaming, Preserving History & Green Space

Thursday, April 3, 1975 regular

  • Historic Street Renaming:

    Voted to partially rename East 19th Street to Martin Luther King Boulevard, sparking public debate over the scope and impact, with a follow-up hearing planned for the full street.

  • Zilker Park Development:

    Approved running a wastewater main across a section of Zilker Park to serve a nearby neighborhood.

  • Protecting Heritage Sites:

    Designated multiple properties, including commercial buildings and residences, with historic zoning to preserve their character.

  • Community & Environmental Initiatives:

    Supported a new community garden project by leasing city land and enacted a temporary ban on large billboards near MoPac and Loop 360.

  • Neighborhood Zoning Battles:

    Denied several requests for commercial or office zoning changes, prioritizing the preservation of residential neighborhood character.
