Austin's Animal Welfare, Autism Aid, & City Growth

Thursday, March 26, 2015 Austin City Council Regular Meeting

  • Animal Welfare & Health Benefits:

    Citizens urged the city to hire more staff for dog care at the Austin Animal Center and to add Applied Behavior Analysis (ABA) therapy for autism to the city's employee health plan.

  • Water Fluoridation Questioned:

    Public hearings included expert testimony from dentists and environmentalists who raised health and environmental concerns, calling for an open discussion on Austin's water fluoridation policy.

  • Bouldin Creek Development Delayed:

    A proposal to vacate streets for a large Bouldin Creek real estate project faced significant neighborhood opposition, leading to a council decision to delay action for further review of traffic, environmental, and connectivity impacts.

  • Protest Lawsuit Settled:

    The city approved a nearly $200,000 settlement for a civil rights lawsuit stemming from a 2011 protest at City Hall, prompting calls for an update on policies for handling future demonstrations.
