Thursday, May 21, 1992 regular
Transcript

  • Future Airport & Environmental Focus:

    Initiated environmental studies for a potential air carrier airport at Bergstrom Air Force Base and scheduled a public hearing on new fees for ozone-depleting chemicals.

  • Youth & Community Investment:

    Secured over $1 million in federal grants for critical youth programs, including initiatives for at-risk individuals and gang prevention.

  • Major Infrastructure & Legal Action:

    Committed over $6 million to a new Convention Center parking facility and approved a $150,000 settlement in a civil rights lawsuit against the city.

  • Cultural & Public Safety Initiatives:

    Re-established the Mexican American Cultural Center Task Force and funded expanded police walking patrols for the University of Texas and Sixth Street areas.
