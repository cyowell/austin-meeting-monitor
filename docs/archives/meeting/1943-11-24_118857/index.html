Austin Roads, Utilities, and Tax Relief

Wednesday, November 24, 1943 regular

  • Infrastructure Upgrade:

    Approved $12,750 to pave several streets surrounding the State Cemetery, including sections of Navasota, Comal, and 11th Streets.

  • Utility Expansion:

    Authorized Texas Public Service Company to lay new gas mains on Rosewood Avenue and Chestnut Avenue, with specific installation guidelines.

  • Tax Penalty Waived:

    Remitted a penalty and half the interest on several years of overdue property taxes for a resident.
