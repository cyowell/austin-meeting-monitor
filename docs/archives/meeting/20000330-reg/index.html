Growth & Green Space: Austin Policy Actions

Thursday, March 30, 2000 Austin City Council Regular Meeting

  • Securing Austin's Power Future:

    Approved an $86 million partnership with Enron for a new gas turbine power plant to expand energy supply and updated Austin Energy's purchasing rules following state legislation.

  • Protecting Green Spaces & Managing Growth:

    Authorized a $6.9 million purchase of 60 acres for conservation, adapted development rules for the sensitive Barton Springs Zone, and advanced the limited-purpose annexation of the 1,630-acre Avery Ranch.

  • Investing in Community Health & Equity:

    Committed over $2 million to local agencies for HIV/AIDS services, funded mental health support for Austin's music community, and adopted a plan to increase minority and women-owned business participation.

  • Planning for Affordable Housing & Development:

    Set public hearings for significant federal grant allocations (over $12 million for housing and community development) and for new "SMART Housing Initiative" policies to guide future growth.
