Austin Funds Affordable Housing & Parks, Bonds

Thursday, November 5, 2026 Regular Meeting

Austin Funds Affordable Housing & Parks, Bonds

  • Housing & Parks Funding:

    Dedicated future tax revenue from Tax Increment Reinvestment Zone No. 21 (known as the "Dog's Head" area) to the City’s Housing Trust Fund and Parks Enhancement Fund. This passed despite public concerns and two dissenting votes.

  • Major Utility Bonds:

    Authorized the issuance of up to $695 million in bonds for Austin Energy to fund electric utility system improvements and refinancing.

  • Housing Blueprint Update:

    Approved an updated Strategic Housing Blueprint, which will now require detailed reporting on the loss of naturally occurring affordable housing (NOAH) units and measuring displacement impacts (an amendment for this reporting passed 8-2).

  • Infrastructure & Parks Investment:

    Committed significant funds for city projects, including $5.4 million for land acquisition to create a new park along the Rutland to Northgate Trail and $1 million for a dedicated transit lane on Dean Keeton Street.

  • North Lamar Zoning Boost:

    Approved major zoning changes for the North Lamar at Beaver Street area, allowing for more mixed-use and increased density development.

  • Postponements & Withdrawals:

    Public hearings for a site-specific Save Our Springs amendment (5811 Southwest Parkway) and a rezoning case (3601 Davis Lane) were postponed to November 19. An eminent domain item for a Tarrytown wastewater project was withdrawn.
