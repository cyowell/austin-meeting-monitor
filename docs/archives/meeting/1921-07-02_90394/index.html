Tax Evasion Crackdown and New Car Service OKs

Saturday, July 2, 1921 special called

  • Hunt for Hidden Property & Fairer Taxes:

    A special commission was formed to survey all city properties, determine their true value, and uncover any untaxed real estate or personal belongings to aid in the 1921 tax equalization process.

  • Daily Pay for Assessors:

    Each member of this new property valuation commission will receive $5 per day for their services.

  • New Ride Services Approved:

    The council granted permits to George Quinton and W. B. Cleveland (both identified as "Colored") to operate service cars, an early version of today's taxi or ride-share.
