Thursday, January 18, 1996 regular
Transcript

  • Expanded Homelessness Support:

    Established a partnership with local churches, offering drainage fee exemptions for those committing funds towards a new $500,000 annual homeless assistance program.

  • Affordable Housing Boost:

    Approved fee waivers and $1,500 in home ownership grants for up to 100 first-time, low-income buyers in the Fairway Ridge Subdivision.

  • Environmental Protection & Future Planning:

    Voted to support strengthening Edwards Aquifer regulations and initiated public hearings for the potential annexation of 300 acres in Jourdan Crossing.

  • Advocating for Homeless Funding:

    Passed a resolution calling on Congress to reallocate defense budget funds to directly support housing for the homeless.
