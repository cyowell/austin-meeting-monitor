Austin's Wartime Priorities: Hospital, Taxes, Gas

Thursday, June 14, 1945 regular
  • The city committed to funding the land acquisition for a new federal hospital, agreeing to deposit money for condemnation proceedings if necessary.
  • The 1945 city tax rates were officially levied, setting the financial course for the year.
  • Plans were approved for the Texas Public Service Company to install new natural gas mains in several areas, outlining specific locations and construction requirements.
  • A taxi operating license was granted to a discharged veteran, explicitly referencing regulations from the Office of Defense Transportation.
