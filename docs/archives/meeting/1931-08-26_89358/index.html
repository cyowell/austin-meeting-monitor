Austin: Awnings, Alleys & New Streets

Wednesday, August 26, 1931 special called
  • An unsafe wooden awning was condemned and ordered removed by its owner, enforcing city safety ordinances.
  • Property owners were levied reassessments for recent improvements to an alley in Block 112 and connecting streets.
  • New street improvement projects were approved for Bellevue Place, and an existing contract was modified to extend work on Alice Avenue.
  • Plans advanced for future street improvements on Waller Creek Boulevard and other areas, with upcoming public hearings and property assessments scheduled.
