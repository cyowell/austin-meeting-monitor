Austin's Nuclear Debt & Downtown Shuttle Future

Thursday, May 10, 1984 regular

  • Austin's Controversial Nuclear Bonds Pass:

    City leaders approved $605 million in Combined Utility Systems Revenue Bonds, primarily for the South Texas Nuclear Project, despite public opposition and warnings about financial repercussions from breaching existing agreements.

  • Major Community & Infrastructure Funding:

    Over $5.4 million was allocated for Community Development Block Grant programs, supporting affordable housing, rehabilitation, and local business. New wastewater treatment capacity for the Williamson Creek area was also approved.

  • Downtown Shuttle Becomes Permanent & Water Protection:

    The popular "Armadillo Express" downtown shuttle service was made permanent. Additionally, the city sought enhanced regional authority to protect the Edwards Aquifer recharge zone.
