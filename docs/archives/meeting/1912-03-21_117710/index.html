Austin Paves Streets, Fights High Insurance

Thursday, March 21, 1912 regular
  • Approved major paving projects for Brazos, Colorado, and 8th Streets, requiring property owners to pay a portion of the cost. Public hearings are scheduled for April to discuss these assessments.
  • Pledged city resources to support community events, including donating water, light, and power for a charity carnival and appropriating funds for an upcoming Fireman's Convention.
  • Authorized legal action to challenge and reduce fire insurance rates in the city on behalf of citizens.
  • Remitted fines for several impounded animals, including a cow and a horse, acknowledging extenuating circumstances.
