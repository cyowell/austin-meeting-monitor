Austin's Modern Moves: Taxis, Bridges, Gas Stations

Thursday, June 29, 1916 regular
  • Adjusted routes and license fees for jitney (early taxi) car operators, including refunds due to changes in city ordinances.
  • Awarded a contract for the construction of a new concrete bridge over Waller Creek and accepted the completion of paving on West 9th Street.
  • Authorized a significant $1.17 million bond trip to New York City for city officials to manage refunding bonds.
  • Approved a permit for a gasoline tank and pump installation under a downtown sidewalk, specifying detailed conditions for its operation.
