Fair Chance Hiring & CodeNext Development Plans

Monday, October 12, 2015 Economic Opportunity Committee Regular Meeting
  • Debated new "Fair Chance Hiring" policies for private employers, focusing on conviction history in job applications.
  • Reviewed nominations for the CodeNext Land Development Advisory Group, a key step in the city's major zoning rewrite.
  • Discussed the long-term master plan for the Austin Convention Center.
  • Received a briefing on potential changes to Credit Access Business (payday loan) regulations.
  • Heard an update on programs supporting minority and women-owned businesses.

THE ECONOMIC OPPORTUNITY, COMMITTEE WILL MEET ON MONDAY, OCTOBER 12, 2015 AT 2:00 PM, AUSTIN CITY HALL 301 W. SECOND STREET AUSTIN, TEXAS Ellen Troxclair, Chair Ora Houston, Vice Chair Gregorio Casar Leslie Pool REVISED AGENDA AGENDA A quorum of the City Council may be present at this meeting. Action will only be taken by the members of the committee. The City Council Economic Opportunity, Technology, Innovation & Creative Industries Committee may go into a closed session under the Open Meetings Act, Section 551.071, of the Texas Government Code, to receive advice from legal counsel on any item on this agenda. Call to Order Approval of Minutes 1. Approve the minutes of the Economic Opportunity Committee meeting of September 14, 2015. Citizen Communication: General 2. The first five speakers signed up prior to the meeting being called to order will each be allowed three minutes to address their concerns regarding items not posted on the agenda. Discussion and Possible Action 3. 4:00 PM: Consider and develop a recommendation regarding Fair Chance Hiring Practices concerning the use of conviction history in hiring and fair chance hiring practices by private employers in the City of Austin. 4. Consider and develop a recommendation on the Convention Center's Long- Range Master Plan. 5. Discussion and recommendation regarding nominees to the CodeNext Land Development Advisory Group. Briefing 6. Briefing by the Small and Minority Business Resource Department regarding the Minority-Owned and Women-Owned Business Enterprise Procurement Program. 7. Briefing from Telecommunications and Regulatory Affairs department regarding program overview including possible amendments to the Credit Access Business Ordinance.
