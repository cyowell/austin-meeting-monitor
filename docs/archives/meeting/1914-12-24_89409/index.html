Austin: Fines, Dam Land, and Telegraph Lines

Thursday, December 24, 1914 regular

  • Leniency for Citizens:

    The City Council suspended outstanding court fines for four individuals (Mary Johnson, Bessie Gray, Lillie Fields, and Julia Hubbard) conditional on their good behavior.

  • Colorado River Dam Impact:

    Approved the purchase of "overflow rights" for $300 from John A. Darter, allowing the city to flood parts of his land due to a dam being built on the Colorado River.

  • Utility Expansion:

    Granted Western Union Telegraph Company permission to dig up city streets (West 6th and Colorado) to install new underground conduits and manholes, requiring a $250 deposit to ensure proper pavement repair and maintenance.

Cuts for trenches not to exceed 24 inches in width and conduit to be laid at such a depth as will clear existing conduits and pipe lines. That the Western Union Telegraph Company shall be held liable for any damages to property or personal injuries that may occur due to the prosecution of this work , and that said Western Union Telegraph Company shall deposite with the Mayor of the City of Austin a certified check in the amount of $ 250.00, guaranteeing the replace-ment of paving in first class condition ,and the maintenance of same for a period of five years.
