Austin's 1904 Sidewalk & Park Plans

Monday, March 21, 1904 regular

• Congress Avenue Sidewalk Dispute – Citizens petitioned to repeal an ordinance widening sidewalks on Congress Avenue. The council appointed a 14-member citizen committee to develop improvement plans for the avenue, with a report due at the next meeting.

• Pease Park Improvements – The council approved $300 for street improvements leading to Pease Park and granted firefighters permission to hold their annual picnic there on April 21st.

• Water Company Payment – The city approved $2,250 to pay the second installment due to the old Water Company on April 1st, 1904.

• Property Tax Redemption – New ordinance allowed property owners to reclaim real estate that the city had purchased at tax sales, under certain conditions.

• New Plumbing Standards – A new ordinance establishing proper construction standards for plumbing and house drainage within Austin was introduced and referred to committee for review.
