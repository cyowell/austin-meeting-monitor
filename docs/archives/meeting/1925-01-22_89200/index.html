Property Tax Adjustments & Public Land Exemptions

Thursday, January 22, 1925 regular

  • Tax Assessment Correction:

    The city council corrected an error in property tax assessments for Mrs. Ada Maddox and Mrs. Ione Whitlock, lowering their valuation from $8385 to the correct $6400 for 1922 and 1923.

  • Church Property Tax Relief:

    A request from MK&T Land Company for tax remission on certain church property was referred to the City Attorney for legal review.

  • Public Land Tax Cancellation:

    The council formally canceled 1921 tax assessments on a section of property in Block 109, recognizing it as public land owned by Travis County.
