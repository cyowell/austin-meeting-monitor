Thursday, August 29, 2024 Austin Housing Finance Corporation (AHFC) Board of Directors Regular Meeting
Official City Record Agenda Watch Video Transcript

  • Housing Development Funding:

    Approved the issuance of $64.8 million in private activity bonds to finance an affordable housing property known as Sage at Franklin Park.

  • New Affordable Homes:

    Established a new nonprofit (Doris Hathaway nonprofit corporation) and ground lease to develop 25 affordable community land trust units.

  • Housing Budget & Anti-Displacement Concerns:

    Adopted a $117 million operating budget for housing initiatives, but faced public criticism regarding the management and allocation of anti-displacement funds, especially those connected to Project Connect.
