Transcript

  • Major Property Transaction:

    The city agreed to conditionally donate a church building and its contents to the African Methodist Church, pending legal review, as part of a $5,000 land purchase.

  • New Urban Infrastructure:

    A permit was granted for a public gasoline tank and pump on East 5th Street, requiring city engineering oversight and being revocable at any time.

  • Public Health & Safety Reforms:

Anderson & Benson Gasoline Tank The Mayor laid before the Council the following resolution : Be it resolved by the City Council of the City of Austin : That the petition of Anderson & Benson to establish and maintain in front of the premises at 111-13-15 East 5th street a Gasoline tank with a gasoline pump attachedbe and the same is hereby granted . The Gasoline Tank herein and hereby authorized must be placed under the written direction of the City Engineer of this City, and the pump placed upon the sidewalk curb must like wise be located by the City Engineer , and the work done under his supervision and approval . Be it further resolved: That the permit hereby granted is revocable at any time by the City Council with or without assignment of any reasons therefor. The resolution was adopted by the following vote : Yeas Mayor Wooldridge, Councilmen Anthony, Bartholomew, Haynes & Powell 5 Nays none. Approved , A P Wooldridge, Mayor. The Council then adjourned.
