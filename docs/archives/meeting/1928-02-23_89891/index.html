Austin's Automotive Boom and Utility Growth

Thursday, February 23, 1928 regular

  • Fueling the Auto Age:

    Approved permits for new drive-in tire and gasoline stations, along with a private home gas tank installation, reflecting the growing demand for automotive services.

  • Major Infrastructure Upgrades:

    Authorized Southwestern Bell Telephone to lay extensive new underground conduits and approved Austin Gas Company for two new gas main installations to expand utility services.

  • Community and Property Decisions:

    Granted special utility rates and a free all-night yard light to St. John's Orphan Home, and adjusted several property tax valuations due to previous overassessments.
