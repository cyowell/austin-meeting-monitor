Austin Airport Expansion & Council Staffing Debate

Wednesday, March 25, 2015 Audit and Finance Committee Regular Meeting

  • Airport Parking Expansion:

    The committee approved using a specialized "Construction Manager at Risk" method for a new, large, and complex parking garage at Austin's airport, aimed at managing risks and improving efficiency for a project vital to handling future passenger growth.

  • City Staffing Debate:

    Discussions continued on adding staff to support the city's new 10-1 council, considering proposals like adding one to two full-time positions to each council office and the Mayor's office (totaling up to 12 new staff).

  • Budget & Open Meetings Act Concerns:

    A key debate point was the estimated $555,000 cost for these new staff for the rest of the current fiscal year (projected $1.2 million annually) and ensuring budget neutrality. Council members also raised significant concerns about potential Open Meetings Act violations.

  • Proposals Sent to Full Council:

    The committee recommended that the full council consider two main staffing options: either placing staff directly within each office or centralizing them within the City Auditor's office to address the council's increased workload.
