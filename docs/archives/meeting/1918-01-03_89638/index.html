Austin's Barton Springs Purchase & City Funds

Thursday, January 3, 1918 regular

  • Barton Springs Land Acquisition Finalized Amidst Debate:

    The city approved the purchase of the Barton Springs property from A.J. Zilker, authorizing payment via promissory notes and a new property tax levy. Two council members dissented, questioning the legality of the debt approval (requiring a two-thirds voter majority for bonds vs. simple majority for notes), discrepancies in the advertised acreage (42.5 vs. 50 acres), and a shift in funding from utility earnings to direct taxation.

  • Emergency Funding for Key City Operations:

    Interim appropriations totaling nearly $23,000 were approved to cover immediate operational expenses for the Water, Light, and Power Department, as well as the General Street Department. These funds will cover salaries, labor, materials, and fuel until the full 1918 budgets are adopted.

  • Settlement for Flood Damages Approved:

    The city allocated $500 from its contingent fund to settle a lawsuit for property damages to Henry Sauer's estate, caused by an overflow of Waller Creek in 1915.
