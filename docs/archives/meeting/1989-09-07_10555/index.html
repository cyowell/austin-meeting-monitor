Thursday, September 7, 1989 regular
Transcript

  • Major Infrastructure Investments:

    Approved $30 million in bonds for airport system expansion and committed over $3.3 million for the Convention Center's design and land acquisition, also establishing a new department for its operation.

  • City Development & Fees:

    Enacted changes to land development codes, including new zoning and theater parking rules, designated William Cannon Drive a scenic arterial, and set new water/wastewater development fees.

  • Key Social & Economic Policies:

    Supported a feasibility study for a Central Texas Foreign Trade Zone and postponed discussions on strengthening the city's Anti-Apartheid Ordinance.

  • Significant City Contracts:

    Authorized major operational spending, including $1.68 million for Brackenridge Hospital supplemental staffing and over $763,000 for streetlight luminaries.
