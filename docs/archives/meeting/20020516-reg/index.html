Adding Police, Golf Clubhouse, Pipeline Lawsuits

Thursday, May 16, 2002 Austin City Council Regular Meeting

  • Boosted Public Safety:

    Approved a grant to hire 60 new police officers and sought federal funding to recruit volunteers for local homeland security and emergency management efforts.

  • Community Input on Development:

    Scheduled public hearings to collect citizen feedback on the city's proposed plan for federal housing and community development funding.

  • City Infrastructure & Projects:

    Authorized $500,000 for street improvements on South Congress Avenue. They also approved an architect for a new clubhouse at the Jackrabbit Run Golf Course after a split vote.

  • Key Legal Discussions:

    Held private discussions on ongoing lawsuits concerning hazardous pipelines and legal matters related to sales taxes on airport services.
