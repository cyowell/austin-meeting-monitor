Austin: Paving Protests, Park Buys, Pool Operations

Thursday, June 11, 1931 regular

  • Paving Protests Faced:

    Approved major street improvements and levied assessments on West 11th and Colorado Streets, despite local residents on Colorado Street formally objecting due to cost, noise, and the prevailing economic hardship.

  • Bathing Pool Trial Run:

    Authorized the city to operate the Westenfield Bathing Pool for one month, with continued operation contingent on generating at least $250 in revenue.

  • Unique Park Land Deal:

    Purchased 1.15 acres for parks for $20,000, but with an unusual clause allowing the seller to repurchase the property in two years if the city found it unsuitable, including any improvements.

  • Utility Expansion & New Business:

    Granted permits for Texas Public Service Company to install new gas mains on multiple streets and for a new filling station on Guadalupe Street, both subject to specific city regulations and conditions.
