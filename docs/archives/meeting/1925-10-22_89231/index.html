Austin's Fuel Deals, Traffic Rules, and Tent Shows

Thursday, October 22, 1925 regular
  • Long-term contracts were approved to secure Austin's lignite coal supply from three companies, ensuring fuel for the city's steam power plant for the next year.
  • The Kiwanis Club appeared before the council to request stricter enforcement of headlight laws.
  • Merchants petitioned to abolish parallel parking on East 6th Street, with the request laid over for future discussion.
  • A permit was granted for a "Mexican tent show" to operate on East 6th Street.
