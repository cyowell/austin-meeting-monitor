ATX Budget Deep Dive: Wages, Taxes, IT, Fire

Wednesday, April 4, 2018 Austin City Council Budget Work Session (09:00AM)

  • Strong Financial Position, Future Worries:

    Austin's budget shows healthy reserves and a projected surplus, but future growth relies heavily on property taxes as sales tax and utility revenue growth slow.

  • Employee Pay & Benefits Debated:

    Council discussed significant wage increases for city staff, especially police, fire, and EMS, alongside concerns about civilian pay equity. Health insurance costs are projected to be flat next year.

  • Key City Service Funding:

    Discussions covered increasing IT infrastructure costs, ensuring development services are self-sufficient through fees, and allocating funds for urgent fire station repairs.

  • Property Tax Dependence & State Risk:

    Concerns were highlighted regarding the city's reliance on property tax growth, homeowner exemptions, and the potential negative impact of future state-imposed revenue caps.

  • Early Public Input for Budget:

    The budget process is opening up earlier to include more input from citizen commissions and advisory groups.
