New Gas Lines, City Dump, Taxi Permits

Thursday, June 8, 1944 regular
  • Approved the expansion of natural gas infrastructure, allowing Texas Public Service Company to lay new gas mains in multiple city streets.
  • Secured a long-term solution for waste management by authorizing the lease of 94 acres for a new city dump, with annual renewal options.
  • Issued several new taxicab licenses and driver permits, facilitating public transportation services.
  • Referred a zoning change request for a property on Cliff Street to the Board of Adjustment for review.
