Paving Debates, Auditorium Plans, City Growth

Wednesday, June 29, 1955 regular

Here's what Austin's City Council addressed on June 29, 1955:

  • Infrastructure & Development:

    Advanced plans for extending water services to residential areas (contingent on resident deposits) and approved assessments on property owners for street improvements on Westview and Burrell Drives.

  • Airport Business:

    Authorized a five-year lease for the cafe operating at the Municipal Airport.

  • New Auditorium Design:

    Consulted with an expert on plans for a major new city auditorium, receiving recommendations to cut costs, optimize layouts, and maximize city revenue from concessions.

  • Paving Policy Debate:

    Held a significant discussion on an experimental, lower-cost paving initiative for East Austin, debating its durability, financing, and a policy to assess property owners if the paving failed. A decision was postponed.
