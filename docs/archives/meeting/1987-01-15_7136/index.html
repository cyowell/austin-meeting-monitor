Duplexes, Airport Plans, & Watershed Rules

Thursday, January 15, 1987 regular
  • Citizens raised diverse concerns, including lack of heat in low-income apartments, city-funded abortions, and plans for a new convention center; the council later advanced an ordinance on subdividing duplexes.
  • Key environmental policy decisions included approving a variance to the Lake Austin Watershed Ordinance and requiring site plan approvals for development along Hill Country Roadways.
  • Significant infrastructure planning moved forward, with approved design work for Robert Mueller Airport's future, land acquisition for Stassney Lane improvements, and contracts for water/wastewater plant expansions.
  • Federal funds were allocated to local organizations for housing and economic development, with some funding decisions postponed for further consideration.
