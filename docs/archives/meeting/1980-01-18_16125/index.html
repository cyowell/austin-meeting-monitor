Austin's Growth: New MUD, Water & Power Rates

Friday, January 18, 1980 special called

  • New Utility District Approved with Conditions:

    City leaders approved the creation of a new Municipal Utility District (MUD) for 251 acres of undeveloped land, allowing it to develop its own wastewater treatment system instead of connecting to the city's main lines.

  • Wastewater Project Funding Denied:

    The city denied the MUD's request to use bond funds to cover interest payments during the construction of its independent wastewater system.

  • Electric Rate Overhaul Discussions Begin:

    Officials initiated a discussion on restructuring electric rates, including cost allocation, customer categories, and rate design, with council members raising concerns about residential rates, large commercial users, and the potential for "lifeline" rates for low-usage customers. A final decision was postponed.
