Thursday, September 20, 1962 regular
Transcript

  • Gas for City Power:

    Major bids were opened for a 20-year natural gas contract to fuel Austin's electrical system, sparking complex discussions and debate over long-term pricing and supplier terms.

  • Expanding Austin's Map:

    The city approved numerous zoning changes to allow for commercial and retail development, and officially annexed over 50 acres of land to expand the city's boundaries.

  • Future Recreation & Roads:

    Plans advanced for a proposed "Austin Aquatic Gardens" leisure complex on Town Lake, and a key alignment was approved for the future Barton Skyway. A new branch library was also slated for northeast Austin.

  • Modernizing City Pensions:

    The Council moved to allow up to 20% of the city's pension funds to be invested in corporate bonds and stocks, aiming to diversify investment strategy.
