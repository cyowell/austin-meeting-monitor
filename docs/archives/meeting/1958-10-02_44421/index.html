Austin Funds Parks, Airport, Colorado River Dam

Thursday, October 2, 1958 regular
  • The council finalized approval for approximately $2.5 million in bonds to fund major city infrastructure projects.
  • This included significant investments in upgrading and expanding public parks and playgrounds.
  • Funds were also allocated to improve municipal airport facilities and build/enhance streets, bridges, and drainage systems.
  • A substantial portion of the funding was specifically for constructing a new low-water dam, reservoir, and hydroelectric power plant on the Colorado River.
