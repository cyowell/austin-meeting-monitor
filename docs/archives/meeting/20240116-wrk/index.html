Tuesday, January 16, 2024 Austin City Council Work Session
Official City Record Agenda Watch Video Transcript

  • Tackling Homelessness

    Austin is hiring a consulting firm to audit and streamline how the city and its partners deliver services and allocate funds to address homelessness, aiming for greater efficiency and collaboration.

  • Climate Action Accountability

    Council members raised concerns about the lack of regular public updates on Austin's Climate Equity Plan and broader environmental initiatives, pushing for more consistent reporting and focused committee oversight.

  • Emergency Winter Response

    City staff received commendation for their swift and effective efforts during the recent cold weather, successfully providing warming shelters and support to a record number of vulnerable residents, including the homeless.
