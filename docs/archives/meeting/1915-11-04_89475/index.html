Austin Forfeits Rail, Plans Paving, Adds Fuel

Thursday, November 4, 1915 regular

  • Interurban Rail Franchise Revoked:

    A major interurban railway franchise was forfeited, and a $5,000 deposit seized, due to the developers' failure to comply with project terms.

  • Major Street Paving Initiatives:

    Significant funds were reallocated and a past university-area paving debt forgiven to prioritize new street projects on East 1st, Red River, 11th, Colorado, and Brazos streets.

  • New Utility and Auto Infrastructure:

    An ordinance to establish a natural gas utility for the city advanced, and an early permit was granted for a gasoline pump on South Congress Avenue.

  • Bridge Repairs:

    Funds were approved for essential repairs to the stone arch bridges across Shoal Creek, including instructions to clear obstructing utility lines.
