City Tackles Graffiti, Traffic, Airport Future

Wednesday, April 29, 1998

  • Graffiti Abatement:

    A new program to combat graffiti was presented, highlighting city efforts to address urban blight.

  • Mueller Airport Redevelopment:

    Briefings were held on the long-term planning process for the future reuse and redevelopment of the former Robert Mueller Municipal Airport land.

  • Barton Springs Road Design:

    A decision on the Barton Springs Roadway design was postponed. City staff were directed to evaluate four alternative designs and hold a public meeting for community input.

  • Community Development Funds:

    Public hearings were scheduled to discuss proposed amendments to reprogram nearly $1.8 million in federal Community Development Block Grant (CDBG) funds for housing and community programs.
