Austin: Cabs, Gas Lines, Street Closures

Thursday, March 1, 1945 regular
  • An appeal by a taxicab driver to reinstate their revoked permit was denied, upholding the Police Chief's recommendation.
  • Approved the installation of new gas mains by Texas Public Service Company in Newton and West Annie Streets.
  • Permanently closed several public alleys and streets, including Anita Street, with a new street dedicated elsewhere as a replacement for Anita Street.
  • Authorized the sale of a city property parcel to private citizens, with payments structured into monthly installments.
