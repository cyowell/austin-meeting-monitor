Austin Debates Growth, Re-bids Waste Contract

Thursday, March 4, 1982 regular

Here's a summary of the key happenings at the Austin City Council meeting:

  • A heated public hearing was held on a significant Municipal Utility District (MUD) proposal (North Central Growth Corridor MUD #1), addressing future growth, utility expansion, and annexation questions, with the discussion continued to a later date.
  • The Council passed the first reading of a Community Development Block Grant (CDBG) ordinance, despite mayoral concerns about it limiting the city's flexibility in administering federal funds.
  • Members voted to re-bid a municipal solid waste disposal contract due to procedural concerns raised about the initial proposals.
  • Council rejected a plan to close James Casey Street south of Radam Lane, opting instead to keep it fully open to traffic after a debated 4-3 vote.
  • Funding was approved for a cooperative hydrologic study with the U.S. Geological Survey to monitor Austin's ground and surface water quality and quantity.
