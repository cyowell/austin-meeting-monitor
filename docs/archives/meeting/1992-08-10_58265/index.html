JUSTIN Austin City Council MINUTES - August 10, 1992 SPECIAL CALLED MEETING COUNCIL CHAMBER 10:00 Α.Μ. - Mayor Todd called to order the Special Called Meeting of the Council, noting the absence of Mayor Pro Tem Urdy and Councilmember Reynolds. The Mayor announced the purpose of the meeting was to to canvass the election of August 8, 1992. 1. Consider a resolution canvassing the returns and declaring the results of the August 8, 1992 Special Municipal Election Motion The Council, on Councilmember Nofziger's motion, Councilmember Larson's second, adopted a resolution canvassing the returns and declaring the results of the August 8, 1992 Special Municipal Election at 10:10 A.M. C.S.D.T. Motion passed on a 5-0 Vote, with Mayor Pro Tem Urdy, and Councilmember Reynolds absent. 2. Adjournment Motion The Special Called Meeting of the Council was adjourned at 10:14 A.M., on Councilmember Epstein's motion, Councilmember Garcia's second, 5-0 Vote, Mayor Pro Tem Urdy and Councilmember Reynolds absent.
