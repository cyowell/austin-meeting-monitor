Austin Housing Project Sparks Affordability Debate

Thursday, March 21, 2024 Austin Housing Finance Corporation (AHFC) Board of Directors Regular Meeting

  • Major Land Purchase Approved

    Austin's housing corporation approved a $27 million acquisition of land on East Riverside for future housing development.

  • Affordability Concerns

    While intended for "affordable housing," public speakers questioned if the project would truly serve low-income residents, fearing it might be priced as "workforce housing," which is often unaffordable for those most in need.

  • Racial Equity & Displacement History

    Critics urged the city to consider the area's history of displacement, calling for genuinely affordable units, a study of displaced residents, and a racial equity assessment before proceeding.

  • Funding Questions

    Concerns were raised that the $27 million purchase price might exceed the previously allocated Project Connect anti-displacement land fund cap.
