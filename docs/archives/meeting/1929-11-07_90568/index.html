Austin Street Upgrades and Utility Expansion

Thursday, November 7, 1929 regular

  • Extensive Utility Expansion:

    Approved numerous new gas mains by Texas Public Service Company across various Austin streets, detailing construction requirements and city oversight for these vital infrastructure projects.

  • Major Street Projects:

    Accepted the completed paving of South Congress Avenue and finalized plans for improvements to East First Street, including contract approval and funding.

  • Water & Light Plant Investment:

    Appropriated $10,000 to finance labor and materials for ongoing construction at the city's Water and Light Plant.

  • Public Nuisance Investigation:

    Directed the City Attorney to investigate citizen complaints regarding a nuisance caused by the Missouri Pacific Railway switching yards.
