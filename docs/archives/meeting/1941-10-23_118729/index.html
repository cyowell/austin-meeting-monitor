Austin's Wartime Moves: Gas, Roads, Phone Rates

Thursday, October 23, 1941 regular

  • New Utility Infrastructure & Services:

    Authorized the laying of new gas mains across several city streets and approved a temporary, wartime $2 party-line telephone rate for new residential subscribers.

  • Traffic and Road Improvements:

    Approved a private owner's plan to widen a section of East Avenue and set back a curb to ease traffic flow, and implemented 30-minute parking meters near the Citizens Industrial Bank.

  • City Land Transaction:

    Agreed to sell a 200-foot strip of city land on East Side Drive for $500 to a resident seeking property access.
