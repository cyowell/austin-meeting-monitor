Monday, July 3, 1882 regular

155 By the following vote yeas Kinney, Schuber, Cook, Radkey, Kupping, Plath & Wilson Nays, Bruggerhoff. The resignation of D F Iglehart and J A Cooper, as members of the Board of Equalization was received and accepted. Mayor nominated J Warrenberger and Mr Brush to fill the vacancy. The Council confirmed the nominations After hearing some suggestions for and against alderman cook moved that the council adjourn On motion the Council adjourned July 1. 1882 Thomas E.
