ATX Water: 100-Year Plan, Drip Limits, Leak Fight

Tuesday, November 12, 2024 Austin Water Oversight Committee Regular Meeting

Here’s what happened at the Austin Water Oversight Committee meeting:

  • Long-Term Water Strategy Approved:

    The committee recommended the Water Forward 2024 plan, a 100-year blueprint for Austin's water future, which includes new water supply projects and updated conservation and drought contingency plans for council approval.

  • Stricter Conservation Rules:

    New regulations are coming, including a first-time two-day-per-week limit on drip irrigation and earlier drought response triggers to preserve water supplies.

  • Cracking Down on Water Loss:

    Austin Water will significantly boost efforts and investment to find and fix hidden leaks in the distribution system, especially targeting aging service lines, to prevent millions of gallons of water from being wasted annually.
