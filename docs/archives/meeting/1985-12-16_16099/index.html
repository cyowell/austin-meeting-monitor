Voters Greenlight Huge City Investments

Monday, December 16, 1985 special called
  • The city officially certified the results of a recent municipal bond election, with all 16 propositions overwhelmingly approved by voters.
  • Over $650 million in bonds were authorized for significant upgrades and expansions across the city's electric, water, and sewer utility systems.
  • Additional voter-approved funding was allocated for key community enhancements, including a new senior activity center and the acquisition and development of public parkland.
