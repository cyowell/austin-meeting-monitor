Austin Funds Utilities, Shapes Growth, Tax Appeals

Thursday, January 9, 1969 regular

  • Funded $9 Million in Utility Bonds and Approved New Gas Lines:

    Authorized significant revenue bonds for electric, water, and sewer system upgrades, and permitted Southern Union Gas to install new natural gas mains across various streets.

  • Expanded Boundaries and Adjusted Zoning:

    Moved forward with several annexations and approved multiple zoning changes for commercial, office, and residential development, while notably rejecting one large annexation proposal.

  • Addressed Property Taxes and Urban Renewal:

    Set hearings for tax appeals, clarifying that appeals must first go through the Equalization Board, and accepted bids for the sale of 13 houses acquired through urban renewal.
