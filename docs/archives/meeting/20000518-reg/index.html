Intel's Downtown Deal, New Police & Recycling

Thursday, May 18, 2000 Austin City Council Regular Meeting

  • Major Downtown Development Incentives:

    Approved over $15 million in fee waivers and infrastructure investments to bring Intel's main campus to downtown. The city also adopted new design guidelines to shape future downtown projects.

  • Boosting Public Safety & Community Programs:

    Authorized applying for a grant to fund 35 new police officers, allocated funds for a day labor program, supported a new youth activity center, and enhanced school zone safety with reduced speed limits.

  • Significant Environmental and Infrastructure Upgrades:

    Approved a $1.265 million upgrade to the city's recycling facility for a new sorting and baler system, and formally supported legislation for labeling genetically manufactured food.
