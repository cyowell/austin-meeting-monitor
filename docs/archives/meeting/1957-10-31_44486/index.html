Lake Austin Rules & Gas Station Approved

Thursday, October 31, 1957 regular

Here's a summary of the key actions and discussions from the meeting:

  • Lake Austin Shoreline Protection:

    New regulations were passed to prohibit filling and control earth moving along the shores of Lake Austin.

  • Waterfront Development Approved:

    A specific boat dock construction on Lake Austin was authorized, with strict conditions covering materials, size, safety lighting, and prohibiting any commercial activity.

  • New Drive-In Gas Station:

    Approval was granted for a new gasoline filling station at E 19th Street and Walnut Avenue, with detailed requirements for its construction, drainage, and safety features.
