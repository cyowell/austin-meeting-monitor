Austin: Protecting Nature, Planning Downtown

Thursday, May 4, 1995 special called

  • Major Environmental Conservation Plan Adopted:

    The Balcones Canyonlands Conservation Plan (BCCP) was approved, moving forward with an application to the U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service for a regional endangered species permit and related implementation agreements.

  • New City Hall Location Postponed:

    A public hearing concerning the specific location for a new City Hall was deferred for one month.

  • Downtown Revitalization Efforts Delayed:

    Broader downtown redevelopment plans, including creating a reinvestment zone and forming a non-profit development corporation, were also put on hold for a month.
