Urban Growth, Wind Energy, Parks, & Baseball Plans

Wednesday, March 1, 1995 special called

Here's a summary of the March 01, 1995 Austin City Council agenda:

  • Major Urban Revitalization Initiatives:

    The Council approved the creation of the Austin Redevelopment Authority (ARA) and the Central Urban Redevelopment Proposal (CURE) to drive significant urban development, including discussions for improvements on East 11th Street.

  • Future Energy and Wind Power:

    Significant debate occurred regarding the replacement of the Holly Street power plant, alongside consideration for a landmark 25-year contract to purchase energy from the Texas Wind Power Project.

  • Parks and Utility Lines Public Hearing:

    A public hearing was scheduled to address a contentious proposal for an overhead electric transmission line crossing Waller Beach, Festival Beach, and Fiesta Gardens Parks.

  • Baseball Stadium Study:

    A viability study for a potential new baseball stadium was presented to the Council for review and discussion.
