Austin Housing: ADU, STR Enforcement, CodeNEXT

Monday, June 15, 2015 Planning and Neighborhoods Committee Regular Meeting
  • Austin is moving forward with a phased approach to Accessory Dwelling Unit (ADU) reforms, aiming to increase housing options while addressing concerns about affordability, short-term rental (STR) overlap, parking, and lot size.
  • Significant concerns were raised regarding Short-Term Rental (STR) enforcement, with staff proposing immediate actions and ordinance changes to address issues like over-occupancy, illegal event advertising, and limited inspection authority.
  • The city officially affirmed "CodeNEXT Approach 2.5," committing to continue the multi-year overhaul of the land development code, with an emphasis on integrating green infrastructure and sustainable water management.
  • Discussions began on reforming the Density Bonus Program to ensure more affordable housing is built directly on-site, rather than through fee contributions, and exploring its application to commercial buildings.
