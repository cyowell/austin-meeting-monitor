Austin Orders Street Work Funds and Plumbing Rules Review

Monday, March 19, 1906 regular

• Street Labor Funding: The council approved $300 for extra street work labor, passing the measure quickly under emergency procedures.

• Plumbing Ordinance Under Review: A proposal to repeal the existing plumbing regulations was introduced but delayed for further discussion at the next meeting.

• Assessment Correction Requests: Multiple residents petitioned the city to reduce or correct their property tax assessments, with cases referred to the claims committee for review.

• Water Company Payment: The council authorized $22.50 to pay the fourth annual installment owed to the old water company.

• Tax Collection Support: The city assessor was authorized to hire temporary clerical staff during the busy tax collection season.
