Stadium, Hospital & Airport Real Estate Talks

Thursday, March 30, 1995 special called
  • Austin leaders held a private executive session to discuss major real estate matters, including a potential baseball stadium at Colorado River Park.
  • Discussions also centered on properties related to Brackenridge Hospital and critical lease agreements with airlines for Austin Bergstrom International Airport.
