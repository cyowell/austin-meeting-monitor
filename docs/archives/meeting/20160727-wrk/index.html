Austin Budget: Homes, Roads, & Health Priorities

Wednesday, July 27, 2016 Austin City Council Budget Work Session
  • The proposed FY2017 budget emphasizes fiscal restraint, aiming to restore conservative spending, resulting in an average 4% increase ($12.48/month) for a typical resident's combined city bill.
  • Key investments target affordability (increased homestead and senior tax exemptions, doubled Housing Trust Fund, higher living wage) and infrastructure (significant funding for street/bridge repair, new transportation positions for mobility projects).
  • Public safety receives substantial boosts with additional EMS medics, police officers (including reallocating sworn personnel to patrol), and increased overtime for the fire department due to vacancies.
  • Extensive public engagement revealed top citizen priorities for increased spending in behavioral/mental health services and street/bridge maintenance, while traffic flow and planning for growth remained significant concerns.
