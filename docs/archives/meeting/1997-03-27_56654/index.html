City Growth, Downtown Parking, Public Safety Boost

Thursday, March 27, 1997 regular

  • New Development and Future Annexation:

    Approved a wholesale wastewater contract for the Davenport Ranch area, conditional on environmental plans and explicitly setting the stage for potential future city annexation, a decision passed by a narrow 4-3 vote.

  • Boost for Downtown Businesses:

    Voted to exempt small businesses (under 6,000 sq ft) in the Central Business District and Downtown Mixed Use District from parking requirements, a move aimed at spurring urban core development.

  • Investments in Public Services:

    Allocated funds for a new aerial ladder fire truck, directed staff to develop a plan for "walking beat" police patrols in several key corridors, and approved a flood control project impacting Williamson Creek Greenbelt parkland.

  • Support for Social Programs & Legal Costs:

    Authorized grants and contracts for childcare initiatives, elderly care, and homeless support services, while also approving significant funding for external legal counsel to handle two ongoing lawsuits against the city.
