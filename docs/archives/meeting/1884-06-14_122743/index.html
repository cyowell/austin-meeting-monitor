Austin Elects New Mayor in 1884

Saturday, June 14, 1884 special called

• New Mayor Elected: John W. Robertson won the mayoral election with 811 out of 2,055 votes cast, taking over from resigned Mayor W.A. Saylor. He was sworn in immediately that evening.

• Alderman Resignation: J.W. Robertson's resignation from the 5th ward alderman position created a vacancy, prompting the city to schedule a special election for June 27th to fill the seat.

• Leadership Changes: L.M. Crooken was elected President of the Board of Aldermen, and the council appointed a special committee to review city appraisal and equalization procedures.

• Election Details: The vote count shows Miller received 654 votes and Dummings received 588 votes, making Robertson's victory clear but not overwhelming.
