Austin Plans Major Government Reform & Audit

Tuesday, May 27, 1924 special called

  • Major Governance Overhaul Proposed:

    The City Council intends to hold an election on August 9, 1924, for voters to consider 22 charter amendments. These amendments would fundamentally restructure city government, moving towards a Council-Manager system where an appointed City Manager would handle daily administration, and the Mayor's role would become primarily ceremonial.

  • City Hospital Financial Crisis:

    An audit of the City Hospital revealed over $4,000 in previously unknown bills and a significant overdraft, prompting questions about financial oversight and the management structure of the institution.

  • Citizen Demands Stricter Traffic Enforcement:

    A resident appeared before the Council to request more stringent enforcement of existing city traffic laws.
