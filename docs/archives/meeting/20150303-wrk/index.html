Flood Buyout, Green Energy, Promise Zone & Sidewalks

Tuesday, March 3, 2015 Austin City Council Work Session

  • Green Energy Rebates Under Scrutiny:

    City officials debated the effectiveness and affordability of Austin Energy's solar and energy efficiency rebate programs, particularly concerning their impact on utility bills. A policy review is planned, but existing project approvals will proceed.

  • Major Floodplain Buyout for Onion Creek:

    Plans advanced for the buyout of 240 homes in the 100-year Onion Creek floodplain, a significant public safety effort. The program offers fair compensation and relocation assistance for affected homeowners and renters.

  • Austin Seeks Federal Promise Zone Status:

    The city is applying for a HUD Promise Zone designation, aiming to unlock additional federal grant opportunities for high-need areas. Discussions focused on community engagement and the grant's approach to local challenges.

  • Sidewalk Projects & Contracting Methods:

    Discussion included crucial sidewalk construction and repair projects. Questions were raised about the city's "indefinite delivery, indefinite quantity" contracting approach, which staff defended as flexible and cost-effective.
