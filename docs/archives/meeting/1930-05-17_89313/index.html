Early Austin: Gas Stations, Paving, Library

Saturday, May 17, 1930 special called
  • A public hearing was set for a San Jacinto Street paving project, detailing costs and assessments for property owners.
  • Permits were granted for two gasoline filling stations, with extensive safety and construction conditions, including specific rules for pump placement and waste management.
  • Despite objections from fire safety officials, a permit was approved for a combined residence and filling station, with strict conditions to address safety concerns.
  • An ordinance was reviewed for its second reading, proposing the creation of a new Public Library Commission.
