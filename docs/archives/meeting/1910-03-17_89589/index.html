Austin's Dam Deal and Spring Elections

Thursday, March 17, 1910 regular

• Dam Reconstruction Proposal: City Council approved submitting a major proposition to voters on April 5, 1910, regarding a 65-foot-high dam across the Colorado River to be built and operated by the Dumont-Holmes Steel Concrete Company, with the city receiving water, light, and power services in return.

• Bridge Repairs Rejected: Council rejected all bids for repairs to the Bouk-dins bridge in South Austin and returned the submitted bids and checks to contractors Ray McDonald and J.O. Polhemus.

• Budget Approvals: Council allocated $250 for cemetery accounts, $250 for parks maintenance, and $5,000 for street department expenses to pay approved bills.

• Fees Waived: Several fines totaling over $36 assessed in recorder's court were remitted, and a $3 pound fee against a resident was forgiven.
