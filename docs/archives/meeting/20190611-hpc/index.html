Tuesday, June 11, 2019 Housing and Planning Committee Regular Meeting
Official City Record Agenda Watch Video Transcript
  • Austin is evaluating 17 new affordable housing projects using recently approved bond funds and a more competitive review process to maximize units and affordability.
  • Staff highlighted rising construction and land costs, alongside federal tax changes, are creating larger funding "gaps" for projects, often requiring the city to be the primary gap funder.
  • Council discussed critical policy challenges like funding risky pre-development stages, balancing city partnerships that may reduce tax revenue, and preventing "double-dipping" where projects seek city funds for affordability already promised through zoning.
  • A significant focus was placed on increasing and clearly defining Continuum of Care (CoC) units for homeless residents, streamlining collaboration with organizations like ECHO, and ensuring sufficient rental assistance.
