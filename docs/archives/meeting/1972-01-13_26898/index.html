Austin Growth: Buses, Phone Bills, Sewers

Thursday, January 13, 1972 regular

  • Investing in Public Transport:

    The city advanced a $3.2 million federal grant application to develop a "beautiful new public transportation system," marking a significant step for Austin's transit future.

  • Scrutinizing Utility & Service Rates:

    Public discussions were held regarding potential rate hikes from Southwestern Bell Telephone and Brackenridge Hospital fees, with a public hearing scheduled for a petition to adjust taxi charges.

  • Emergency Needs & Infrastructure Expansion:

    An emergency purchase of 500,000 gallons of fuel oil was approved to cover a cold spell. The Council also debated a major sewer main extension for Southeast Austin's rapid development, tying it to federal funding and future user fees.
