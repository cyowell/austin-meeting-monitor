City Debates Library, Utility Funds, & Naming Rules

Thursday, July 22, 1976 regular

  • Preserving Austin's Heritage:

    Recognized property owners for their commitment to preserving historic landmarks across the city.

  • Massive Infrastructure Investment Proposed:

    Reviewed a proposed $92.7 million bond program to significantly upgrade water and wastewater treatment and distribution systems, with public input on priorities and funding.

  • Central Library Plans Scaled Back:

    Voted against adding a fifth floor to the new Central Library, opting instead to prioritize neighborhood branch needs and evaluate future-proof elevator systems.

  • New Rules for Naming Public Facilities:

    Adopted a policy allowing public facilities to be named after deserving *living* citizens, setting guidelines for such honors.

  • Utility & Employee Policy Updates:

    Denied a city purchase plan for apartment electrical systems for individual meters, shifting the cost to owners, and affirmed the revision of city personnel policies, including grievance procedures and performance evaluations.
