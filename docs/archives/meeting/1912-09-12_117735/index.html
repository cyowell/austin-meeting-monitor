Austin: Paving Progress, Special Recognition

Thursday, September 12, 1912 regular
  • City officials passed a unique resolution recognizing Miss Lole McSween for her extensive charity work and congratulating her on her upcoming marriage.
  • Major infrastructure initiatives were launched, including adopting paving specifications and ordering new paving for key streets like North Congress Avenue, West 6th Street, Lavaaca Street, and University Avenue.
  • The council began the process to close and vacate a 48.5-foot strip of land located north of Wooldridge School.

318 . Regular meeting of the City Council: Austin, Texas, Sept 12th 1912. The Council was called to order by the Mayor: Roll called: Present Mayor Wooldridge, Councilmen Haynes & Powell 3 Absent excused Councilmen Bartholomew & Hart 2 The minutes of the last regular and subsequent recessed meetings were read and adopted. . Petitions ,etc: Fine Wm Powell The Mayor laid before the Council a petition from W M Powell, asking the Council to remit a fine assessed against Willie Powell in the Recorders Court, which was read and the petition granted by the following vote : Yeas Mayor Wooldridge, Councilmen Haynes & Powell 3 Absent excused Councilmen Bartholomew & Hart 2 Building permits The Mayor laid before the Council petitions from Yates & Cornwell, Yates & Cornwell W L Hamilton & W L Parsley, asking for permission to erect certain W L Hamilton buildings within the Fire limits, which were read ordered filed. W L Parsley Unfinished business: The Mayor laid before the Council a resolution appropriating the sum of $ 1,340.97 ,to pay the claim of J W Maxwell for commissions collecting delinquent taxes, which was passed by the following vote : Yeas Mayor Wooldridge, Councilmen Haynes & Powell 3 Absent Councilmen Bartholomew & Hart 2 New Business: Mayor Wooldridge offered the following resolution: Resolution on Miss Lole Mc Sween, an Austin girl, today marries Mr Scott McGuire, a marriage of Kentucky gentleman, and after today Mrs McGuirewill probably cease to Miss McSween live among us. Miss McSween inspired by the Council and example of her good mother, Mrs C H Wilcox, whose life for many years in Austin has been largely devoted to deeds of unpaid love and charity, has herself like her noble mother gone in and out amongst the poor ,the ignorant, the afflicted, the sorrowing and even the vicious of our people, unselfishly minister, to their needs ,loved and respected for her life and appreciated for her unselfish philanthropy. Resolved, therefore, by the City Council of the City of Austin: That the Council congratulates Miss McSween upon her impending happy marriage, and wishes for her in her new abode a continued life of unselfishness and love, and wishes that when she is married ,such domestic felicity will be added to the happiness she has so largely gotten and has so richly deserved from her public service. Resolved that this resolution be entered upon the minutes of the City Council and a copy thereof, oved the seal of the City, be transmitted with the compliments of the City Council to Miss McSween. The resolution was adopted by the following vote : Yeas Mayor Wooldridge, Councilmen Haynes & Powell 3 Absent Councilmen Bartholomew & Hart 2 The Mayor laid before the Council an ordinance closing and vacating against public use ,traffic and travel a strip or piece of ground forty eight and --- Page break --- Ordinance Vacating Street - a half foot wide by one hundred and eighty six foot long, north of Wooldridge school in outLot 43 Divison "D", of the City of Austin, Texas, and repealing all ordinances or resolutions in conflict with the provisions hereof.
