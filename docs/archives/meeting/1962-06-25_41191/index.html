New Faces on Austin Planning Board

Monday, June 25, 1962 special called
  • A special meeting was called specifically to appoint new members to the city's Planning Commission.
  • W. A. Wroe, Edgar Jackson, and Barton D. (Pat) Riley were appointed to serve on the commission until June 1964.
  • The city acknowledged and expressed appreciation for the service of two resigning Planning Commission members.

Full Transcript

CITY OF AUSTIN, TEXAS MINUTES OF THE CITY COUNCIL CITY OF AUSTIN, TEXAS Special Meeting June 25, 1962 11:00 Α.Μ. Council Chamber, City Hall 614 The meeting was called to order with Mayor Palmer presiding. Roll call: Present: Councilmen Armstrong, Perry, Shanks, White, Mayor Palmer Absent: None MAYOR PALMER announced this was a Special Meeting called for the purpose of appointing members to the Planning Commission for terms to extend to June 1, 1964. Councilman Shanks moved that the Council appoint the following to serve as members of the Planning Commission for terms extending until June 1, 1964: MR. W. A. WROE MR. EDGAR JACKSON MR. BARTON D. (PAT) RILEY The motion, seconded by Councilman Perry, carried by the following vote: Ayes: Councilmen Armstrong, Perry, Shanks, White, Mayor Palmer Noes: None The Council noted with regrets the resignations of MR. FRED BARKLEY and MR. NOBLE DOSS and expressed appreciation for their past services on the Planning Commission. There being no further business, Councilman White moved that the Council adjourn. The motion, seconded by Councilman Shanks, carried by the following vote: Ayes: Councilmen Armstrong, Perry, Shanks, White, Mayor Palmer Noes: None ATTEST: CITY OF AUSTIN, TEXAS 615 The Council adjourned at 11:30 A.M., subject to the call of the Mayor. By oster City Clerk APPROVED Mayor
