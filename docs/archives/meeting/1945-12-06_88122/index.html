Austin's Post-War Boom: Zoning, Vets & Fireworks

Thursday, December 6, 1945 regular

  • Major City Expansion Planned:

    A commission was established to determine future zoning for 1,901 acres recently annexed north of the city, significantly increasing Austin's footprint. Minor adjustments to existing residential and commercial zoning were also approved.

  • Support for Returning Veterans:

    The city allocated $15,000 for utility extensions to temporary housing being built by the University for veterans returning to complete their education.

  • Infrastructure & Construction Growth:

    Permits were granted for installing new gas mains in several streets, indicating utility expansion. Strict new guidelines were also adopted for contractors using public sidewalks and streets for building projects, including safety walkways and financial bonds.

  • Fireworks Restrictions Introduced:

    A new ordinance was introduced to regulate the discharge, possession, and sale of fireworks within city limits, aiming to enhance public safety.
