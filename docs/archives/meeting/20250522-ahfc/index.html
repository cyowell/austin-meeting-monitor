New Nonprofit for Austin Homes

Thursday, May 22, 2025 Austin Housing Finance Corporation (AHFC) Board of Directors Regular Meeting
  • Austin's housing agency approved forming a new 501(c)(3) nonprofit corporation.
  • This new entity aims to secure more funding for affordable housing, primarily by accessing additional tax credits and providing greater flexibility in managing housing assets.
  • A public speaker strongly opposed the plan, raising concerns about transparency, the agency's past performance on other city projects, and whether current affordable housing efforts adequately serve low-income communities.
  • Despite the opposition, the proposal was unanimously approved as part of the meeting's consent agenda.
