Austin Addresses Aid, Equity, Development

Thursday, August 27, 2020 Austin City Council Regular Meeting

  • Emergency Response:

    Activated shelters and provided critical resources for over 3,000 Hurricane Laura evacuees, managing the complex effort in a COVID-19 environment.

  • Health & Housing Equity:

    Approved funding for mobile health clinics targeting East Austin's Black and Latinx communities, allocated COVID-19 relief to high-risk areas, and acquired land for transit-oriented affordable housing.

  • Public Safety Debate:

    Heard strong public opinion on police funding and accountability, including calls to fire leadership and reallocate funds, as well as support for existing police programs.

  • Development & Environment:

    Approved a long-term aquifer water storage plan and debated a controversial floodplain variance request for a building's ground-level parking, along with the use of bond funds for golf course maintenance.

  • City-University Collaboration:

    Established a new agreement to streamline research partnerships with the University of Texas, aiming to leverage academic expertise for city challenges.
