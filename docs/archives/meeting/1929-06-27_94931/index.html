Austin: Fares, Drive-Ins, & Church Tax Relief

Thursday, June 27, 1929 regular

  • Public Transportation Fares:

    The City Council stalled a proposed streetcar fare increase, declining to advance the ordinance for a third reading despite the company's detailed plea of financial hardship and obligations to improve city infrastructure.

  • Early Auto Infrastructure:

    A permit was granted for a new "drive-in" gasoline filling station, with numerous strict safety and construction conditions, reflecting the growing importance of automobiles.

  • Community Tax Relief:

    The Council voted to remit years of accrued interest and penalties on delinquent property taxes for the parsonage of Wesley Chapel, M. E. Church, Colored, acknowledging an undecided tax question.
