Austin Pushes Limits, Regulates Trade, Fights State Law

Thursday, October 19, 1933 regular
  • The city voted to expand its corporate limits and approved several major gas and telephone line expansions across town.
  • New rules are in the works to license seafood vendors and protect local merchants from unfair competition by street vendors. A temporary permit for a material storage yard was also approved.
  • Officials voiced serious concern that a new state law could block federal funding for critical municipal water, light, and sewer system projects, potentially halting essential work.
  • A police officer who died in the line of duty was honored, and $3,400 was appropriated for a county welfare and employment project. Strict safety requirements were also set for sidewalk construction.
