Austin Debates Segregated Library, Boosts Parking

Thursday, February 23, 1933 regular

Here's a summary of the Austin City Council meeting from February 23, 1933:

  • A committee representing Black citizens discussed plans for a public library, with the City proposing to convert an existing temporary building (currently used by white citizens) into a dedicated library for the Black community, rather than waiting for funds for new construction.
  • Approved a permit for a property owner to set back a curb on West 6th Street, improving parking and street width, with specific construction and material requirements.
  • A request for tax exemption on a property deeded to University Baptist Church for a parsonage, but still occupied by the donor, was referred for legal opinion.
  • Adjusted a 1931 property tax assessment, reducing it after determining the initial construction completion estimate was excessive.
