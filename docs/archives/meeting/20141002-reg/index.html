ATX Housing, Parties & Property Rules Debated

Thursday, October 2, 2014 Austin City Council Regular Meeting

  • Renters Gain Protections (Initially):

    A new fair housing rule was tentatively passed, making it illegal to deny housing based on income source like Section 8 vouchers. However, a debated amendment exempting smaller rental properties (up to four units) was included.

  • West Campus Event Overhaul:

    The city is re-evaluating how it permits student parties and large events in West Campus, aiming to balance vibrant student life with residents' quality of life, while strictly enforcing safety and noise ordinances.

  • Landlord Cooperation & Code Enforcement:

    Plans for a "Good Landlord" incentive program moved forward, and the council pushed for improvements in how the city identifies and addresses repeat code violations in problematic rental properties.
