Austin's Growth: $60M Bonds & River Plans

Thursday, June 30, 1960 regular
  • Voters will consider a massive bond package totaling over $60 million to fund extensive improvements across the city's electric, water, and sewer systems, airport, fire stations, libraries, parks, and streets.
  • A major river development project was authorized, involving deepening the Colorado River channel (to 500 feet wide and 17 feet deep) and creating a "new town lake" as part of the Pleasant Valley Road dam and bridge construction.
  • Multiple residential properties were rezoned for general retail use in areas like Manor Road, Wheless Lane, and East Avenue, signaling a significant shift towards commercial development.
  • The city approved substantial contracts for ongoing public works, including new sanitary sewer systems for the McKinley Heights Area and Airport Terminal Building, and the paving of 42 city blocks.
