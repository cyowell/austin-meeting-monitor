Leon Street Closure for Charity Event

Tuesday, September 28, 1982
  • An emergency meeting was called to quickly address a specific item.
  • Council approved the temporary closure of the 2500 block of Leon Street.
  • The street closure, scheduled for October 1, 1982, from 3:00 p.m. to 8:00 p.m., is for a street party hosted by Delta Upsilon Fraternity.
  • The event is being held to benefit the Bluebonnet Youth Ranch Charity Drive.

Mayor McClellan stated that this was an Emergency Special Called Meeting for the purpose of considering a resolution temporarily closing the 2500 block of Leon Street on October 1, 1982 from 3:00 p.m. to 8:00 p.m., as requested by James Powell, representing Delta Upsilon Fraternity. Motion The Council, on Councilmember Deuser's motion, Mayor McClellan's second, adopted a resolution temporarily closing Leon Street from 25 1/2 Street to 26th Street on October 1, 1982 from 3:00 p.m. to 8:00p.m. for a street party for Bluebonnet Youth Ranch Charity Drive. (4-0 Vote, Councilmembers Goodman, Mullen and Urdy absent) ADJOURNMENT The Council adjourned at 5:10 p.m.
