City Budget Set, Alley Upgrades, Sewer Refund

Thursday, February 8, 1917 regular

  • Annual Budget Approved:

    Austin City Council formally adopted its 1917 budget, allocating over $1.8 million for various city departments (excluding public schools).

  • Alley Improvement Hearings:

    Plans moved forward for improving multiple city alleys, with public hearings scheduled for property owners to discuss assessment costs for these upgrades.

  • Sewer Fee Refund Issued:

    A resident received a $3 refund for sewer inspection fees after their property was found unsuitable for a sewer connection due to topographical conditions.
