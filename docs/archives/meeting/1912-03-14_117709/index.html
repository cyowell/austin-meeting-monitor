Austin: Tackling Taxes, New Signs, Auto Fuel

Thursday, March 14, 1912 regular

  • Aggressive Tax Enforcement:

    Authorized multiple legal suits to collect delinquent property taxes and against property owners refusing to pay for completed street improvements on Sixth Street.

  • Early Urban Regulation:

    Approved permits for new public signage, including an electric "CAFE" sign on the Littlefield Building and a "Men and Religion Forward Movement" banner across Guadalupe Street.

  • City Operations & Citizen Appeals:

    Approved municipal funds for employee automobile fuel and oil, and remitted several $1.50 horse impound fees following citizen appeals regarding escaped horses.
