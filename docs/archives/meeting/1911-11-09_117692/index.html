City Paving, Rail Lines, and Community Concerns

Thursday, November 9, 1911 regular

  • Major Street Paving & Roadwork:

    Finalized and paid for extensive paving on West Sixth Street, including special assessments for property owners, and approved bids for new sidewalks city-wide, alongside a large gravel contract for South Congress Avenue.

  • Railroad Expansion:

    Granted a significant right-of-way to a railroad company to construct a new switch track across city streets.

  • Cemetery Bond Election Halted:

    Repealed an earlier ordinance to hold a public election on issuing bonds for purchasing and improving a city cemetery.

  • Public Welfare & Compassion:

    Approved compensation for a citizen whose property (mattresses) was destroyed by health officials, and remitted a fine for a destitute youth.
