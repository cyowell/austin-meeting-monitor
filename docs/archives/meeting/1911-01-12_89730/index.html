Austin Funds City Services, Plans Water Trench

Thursday, January 12, 1911 regular
  • Major funding for city operations: The council approved significant appropriations to cover salaries and accounts for nearly all city departments for January and February.
  • The Water & Light Department received the largest share, with $25,000 allocated for staff and operational expenses.
  • Plans were authorized to seek bids for a 500-foot filtering trench, a key water infrastructure project near the city's pumping station.
  • A $14.80 fine assessed against Grant Jennings in Recorder's Court was remitted (waived).
