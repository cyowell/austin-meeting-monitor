Monday, February 20, 1899 regular
Transcript

• Fire Department Upgrades Approved – The council passed funding ($1,520 and $550) to purchase new hose wagons and 1,000 feet of fire hose for multiple fire companies across Austin, with unanimous support.

• Infrastructure Projects Rejected – Two proposed bridge and culvert projects totaling $1,800 failed to advance, with aldermen voting against moving them to the next stage of approval.

• Hose Company Driver Hired – The council authorized hiring a driver for North Austin Hose Company No. 6 at $50 per month, plus $25 monthly for food and equipment repairs.

• Sewer Connection Initiative – A three-member committee was formed to negotiate connecting the Driskill Hotel's drainage system to the city sewer.

• Ceremonial Invitations Extended – Austin extended a formal invitation to the Knights of Pythias to hold their 1900 state convention in the city, and the mayor accepted an invitation to a Governor's reception.
