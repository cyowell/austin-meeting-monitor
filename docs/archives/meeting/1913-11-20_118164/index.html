Thursday, November 20, 1913 regular
Transcript

  • Public Health:

    Typhoid, typhus, and related fever diseases were declared reportable, requiring all city physicians to report cases to the City Physician, with penalties to be drafted for non-compliance.

  • Pease Park Boundaries:

    The official boundary lines for Pease Park were formally established and recorded, addressing the disappearance of original reference trees from its 1875 deed.

  • City Utility Fuel:

    A major contract was awarded to Ryan & Perry for lignite fuel to power the Water & Light Plant for 1914.

  • Temporary Public Signs:

    The Beiderwolf Tabernacle people were granted temporary permission to place up to six "A" signs in public areas until January 1, 1914.
