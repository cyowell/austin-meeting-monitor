Austin Eyes Future: Art, Housing, Green Initiatives

Thursday, August 3, 2000 Austin City Council Regular Meeting

  • Future Funding & Growth:

    The proposed 2000-2001 city budget was presented, and public hearings were scheduled for significant general obligation bonds for transportation, parks, and affordable housing projects.

  • Downtown Transformation:

    Plans advanced for a new Austin Museum of Art downtown via a land exchange, and a "Great Streets Master Plan" was approved to revitalize city center streets and sidewalks.

  • Community & Green Initiatives:

    Over $13 million in federal grants were accepted for affordable housing and social services, complemented by a $1 million loan for housing development. The city also moved forward with ozone reduction strategies, solar energy promotion, and land management for nature preserves.

  • Public Safety Tech Boost:

    Funds were approved to upgrade emergency services with new mobile data terminals and equip police vehicles with recording systems.
