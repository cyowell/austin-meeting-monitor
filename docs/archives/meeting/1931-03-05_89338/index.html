Paving Austin, Funding Sports, New Traffic Rules

Thursday, March 5, 1931 regular

  • Extensive Street Upgrades Approved:

    Accepted several recently finished paving projects and greenlit new contracts for paving dozens of additional streets throughout the city, with costs shared by adjacent property owners.

  • Fueling Recreation and Community Life:

    Funded travel for the city's winning basketball teams to compete in state tournaments and permitted new gas main installations for essential utility services.

  • New Downtown Regulations & Development:

    Passed an ordinance restricting vehicle standing on Congress Avenue and approved special permits for private sidewalk construction on Congress and West Sixth Street.

  • Setting Up City Elections:

    Formalized the process for a general election to elect five new officials and updated the boundaries of voting wards.
