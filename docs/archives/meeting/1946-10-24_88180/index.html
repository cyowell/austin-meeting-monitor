Austin's Future: Highways, Drive-Ins, & Growth

Thursday, October 24, 1946 regular

  • Major Infrastructure Projects:

    Plans were accepted for the routing of US Interstate Highway 81 through East Avenue, alongside approvals for extensive new gas mains, water/sewer lines, and underground telephone conduits across the city.

  • Drive-In Theater Regulations:

    New rules were enacted for the construction and operation of drive-in theaters, including specific seasonal adjustments for late-night music playing hours.

  • City Expansion & Development:

    The city council approved the annexation of significant acreage into the city limits and granted a key commercial zoning change for property near Barton Springs Road.

  • Taxi Permit Decision:

    A taxicab driver's permit, initially recommended for denial by the Chief of Police, was granted on a 90-day probationary basis to an applicant identified in the records as "colored."
