Austin's Carbon-Free Path, Rates, and Plant Closure

Wednesday, February 26, 2020 Austin Energy Utility Oversight Committee Regular Meeting

  • Accelerated Green Energy Goal:

    Austin Energy is set to achieve a carbon-free power portfolio by 2035 or sooner, a major step forward for the city's environmental commitments.

  • Utility Rates Under Scrutiny:

    Discussions highlighted the impact of energy-efficient homes and the current tiered rate structure on revenue and affordability, signaling a comprehensive rate review process beginning in 2021.

  • Decker Plant Decommissioning:

    Plans are in motion to retire the Decker Creek Power Station's steam units by the end of 2021, with robust career support programs for affected staff.

  • Sustainability Recognition:

    The city was honored as "2020 Sustainability City of the Year" for its leadership in electric vehicles, renewable energy, and green building initiatives.
