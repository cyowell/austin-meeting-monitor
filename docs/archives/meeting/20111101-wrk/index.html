Austin: Public Speak Rules & Seaholm Design

Tuesday, November 1, 2011 Austin City Council Work Session

Here's a summary of the Austin City Council's November 1, 2011 agenda:

  • Public Speaking Rules Under Review:

    The Council debated proposed changes to citizen participation in meetings, focusing on limiting the number of consent agenda items individuals can speak on and improving meeting efficiency to prevent delays.
  • **Seaholm Substation Wall Redesign:

** Plans for a wall at the new Seaholm substation were halted after concerns about misleading design images, inadequate pedestrian access, and the area's overall "pedestrian-friendly" vision were raised by council members. The City Manager committed to revisiting the design.

  • Shoal Creek Project Speed-Up:

    An ongoing and disruptive flood control project in the Shoal Creek Allandale neighborhood, which includes new curbs and driveways, will have its construction schedule re-evaluated to find ways to accelerate completion and address resident complaints about property damage and limited access.
