Austin's Urgent Breaker Buy Saves Big

Thursday, October 6, 1955 special called

  • Crucial Equipment Purchase:

    The City Council approved the immediate acquisition of five oil circuit breakers, vital components for the city's infrastructure.

  • Smart Financial Move:

    Council acted swiftly to secure the equipment at a favorable price of $10,000 per unit under an existing contract.

  • Taxpayer Savings:

    This quick decision was made to preempt an anticipated price hike, saving the city a significant amount by avoiding a potential increase to $12,500 per unit.

1031 CITY OF AUSTIN, TEXAS 1031 NOTICE OF SPECIAL MEETING TO THE MEMBERS OF THE CITY COUNCIL OF THE CITY OF AUSTIN: Notice is hereby given that a Special Meeting of the City Council of the City of Austin will be held on the 6th day of October, 1955, at the Municipal Building, Eighth and Colorado, in Austin, Texas, at 11:45 A.M. for the purpose of authorizing the purchase of 5 Oil Circuit Breakers under our existing contract PA 89 for the sum of $10,000 each. Council Chamber, City Hall The meeting was called to order with Mayor Miller presiding. Roll call: Present: Councilmen Palmer, Pearson, White, Mayor Miller Absent: Councilman Long Present also: W.T. Williams, Jr., City Manager; Doren Eskew, City Attorney The Mayor announced that this meeting was called at 11:45 was for the purpose of authorizing the purchase of five oil circuit breakers under our existing contract, PA 89 for the sum of $10,000 each. The Mayor explained the necessity of extending the contract to include these, as the price had gone up to $12,500 each. Councilman White moved that the City Manager be instructed to go ahead and buy these five oil circuit breakers at $10,000 each. The motion, seconded by Councilman Pearson, carried by the following vote: Ayes: Noes: Councilmen Palmer, Pearson, White, Mayor Miller None Absent: Councilman Long There being no further business the Council adjourned this special meeting at 12:00 Noon subject to the call of the Mayor.
