Austin Housing Boost: $2.5M, 7 New Homes

Thursday, June 23, 2005 Austin Housing Finance Corporation (AHFC) Board of Directors AHFC Meeting
  • Approved a nearly $2.5 million increase to the Austin Housing Finance Corporation's operating budget for fiscal year 2004-2005.
  • The budget boost included substantial funding for the Anderson Hill Program and the Rental Housing Development Assistance Program.
  • Authorized a construction contract of over $448,000 to build seven new single-family homes in the Anderson Hill neighborhood.
