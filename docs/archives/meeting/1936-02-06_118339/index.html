Byrd, Infrastructure, & Austin's Expansion

Thursday, February 6, 1936 regular

  • Celebrate an Explorer:

    Declared February 12th as "Admiral Byrd Day" to honor explorer Richard E. Byrd's lecture on his Antarctic expedition.

  • Key Infrastructure Growth:

    Approved significant water line extensions and granted permits for new gas mains to be laid across several city streets.

  • City Planning for the Future:

    Introduced new ordinances for expanding city boundaries (annexation) and updating existing zoning regulations.

  • Ensuring Public Safety in Construction:

    Granted temporary public space to contractors for building projects, with strict requirements for public walkways, safety barricades, and financial bonds.
