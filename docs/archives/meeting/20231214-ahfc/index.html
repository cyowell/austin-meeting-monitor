ATX Housing: Homeless Aid & Community Land

Thursday, December 14, 2023 Austin Housing Finance Corporation (AHFC) Board of Directors Regular Meeting

  • Rental Assistance Program Renewed:

    The board approved a contract to continue a program providing rental subsidies for over 100 people annually transitioning out of homelessness, despite one director voting against it.

  • Affordable Housing Land Secured:

    A 0.3-acre property in District 4 was purchased at a deep discount for $10,000, with plans to develop it into a community land trust for future affordable housing.

  • Public Challenges Aid Rules & Notices:

    A speaker raised concerns that the rental assistance program's direct payments to landlords might violate federal rules requiring tenants to apply directly, and also alleged improper public posting of the meeting notice.
