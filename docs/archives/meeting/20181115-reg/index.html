Austin Policy: Police, Housing, Fire Chief Confirmed

Thursday, November 15, 2018 Austin City Council Regular Meeting
  • Advanced discussion on a new police oversight office ordinance and scheduled the contentious police contract for later.
  • Confirmed Joel Baker as Austin's new Fire Chief and approved a key milestone for building the Del Valle Fire/EMS station, anticipating a June 2019 groundbreaking.
  • Debated optimizing city-owned land for affordable housing, highlighting current zoning limitations, and postponed an intergovernmental agreement concerning environmental protections in the ETJ.
  • Approved a cemetery services contract, despite concerns over a single bidder and operational issues, with plans to transition services in-house; public speakers also raised issues regarding the animal shelter and gas utility rates.
