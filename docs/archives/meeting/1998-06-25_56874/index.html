Downtown Moves, Convention Center Grows

Thursday, June 25, 1998 regular

  • Downtown Traffic Transformation:

    Public discussion concluded on converting several major downtown streets, including Cesar Chavez, 2nd, 5th, and 6th, from one-way to two-way traffic flow.

  • Convention Center & Waller Creek Expansion:

    Approved significant funding and authorized property acquisitions, including through eminent domain, for the $135 million Convention Center expansion and a related Waller Creek flood control tunnel.

  • Housing & Environment Investments:

    Backed a $20 million bond for a new 256-unit affordable housing complex and resolved a legal case impacting development in the critical Barton Creek Water Quality Zone.

  • Infrastructure & Community Updates:

    Allocated funds for new bike and pedestrian paths near Town Lake, approved grants to upgrade the city's traffic signal systems, and initiated a task force to review solid waste operations.
