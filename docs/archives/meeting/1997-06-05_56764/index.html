Austin's Future: Land, Names, Services

Thursday, June 5, 1997 regular

  • Major Urban Development & Renewal:

    Discussions began for an Urban Renewal Project for East 11th and 12th Streets, alongside progress on the Central City Entertainment Center and commissioning new ordinances for 'Traditional Neighborhood' and 'infill' development.

  • East Austin Business Moratorium Exemptions:

    Several public hearings were scheduled to address requests for exemptions from the East Austin Moratorium for local businesses, including a daycare and a used car dealership.

  • Honoring Austin Icons:

    Public hearings were set to consider naming the Austin-Bergstrom International Airport terminal after Barbara Jordan and renaming 26th Street for Dean Page Keeton.

  • Homelessness & Water Policy:

    Citizens raised concerns about homelessness and the "No Camping" ordinance, while the council approved a study on water demand pricing to inform future utility strategies.
