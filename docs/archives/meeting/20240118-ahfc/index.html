Austin's Housing Budget Boosts Vouchers

Thursday, January 18, 2024 Austin Housing Finance Corporation (AHFC) Board of Directors Regular Meeting

  • Expanded Housing Vouchers:

    Approved an additional $1 million to the local housing voucher program, helping more residents afford housing.

  • Pecan Gardens Hotel Support:

    Allocated funds for a contract related to the city-owned Pecan Gardens Hotel, signaling continued investment in this property, often used for housing assistance.
