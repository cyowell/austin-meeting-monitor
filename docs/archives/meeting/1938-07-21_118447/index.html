Austin Advances: New Zoning, Incinerator Site

Thursday, July 21, 1938 regular

Here's a summary of the Austin City Council agenda from July 21, 1938:

  • Zoning Overhaul & Key Re-zonings:

    The Council adopted a comprehensive amendment to the city's zoning regulations, establishing new districts and rules for building height, land use, and density. This included re-zoning specific properties along Dam Boulevard, such as University of Texas land for residential use and M.H. Goldsmith's tract for commercial development, following a public hearing.

  • Major Infrastructure & Utility Upgrades:

    A site was approved for a new incinerator as part of a Public Works Administration project. Permits were also issued for Southwestern Bell to install new telephone pole lines and for Texas Public Service Company to lay gas mains across several city streets.

  • Strict Construction & Street Use Permits:

    Special permits were granted for private flagstone walks to extend into public sidewalks, with detailed construction requirements. Additionally, a contractor using street space for building repairs faced stringent conditions, including safety measures like guard rails and red lights, and was required to post a $5,000 surety bond.

  • Traffic Regulations Updated:

    An ordinance revising the city's traffic rules for public streets received its final reading and was adopted.
