Guadalupe Paving Fight, Barton Springs Restored

Thursday, January 9, 1930 regular

  • Guadalupe Street Paving Sparks Debate:

    Property owners vehemently protested the proposed repaving of a section of Guadalupe Street (between 5th and 6th Streets), arguing the existing pavement was still in good condition and new assessments for the project were unjust. The decision on this segment was postponed.

  • Barton Springs Park Gets Major Upgrade:

    A significant $14,045 plan was approved for the restoration and preservation of Barton Springs, including new retaining walls, pool maintenance, and gravel removal. An immediate $2,500 was allocated for urgent winter work, with a specific request for natural rock in new construction.

  • New Gas Station Approved with Strict Safety Rules:

    A permit was granted for a new gasoline filling station at First and Red River Streets, but only under numerous stringent safety and operational conditions, including specific setbacks, fire prevention, and drainage requirements, partly due to its proximity to Palm School.

  • Other Street Projects Move Forward:

    Despite protests on one block, the city accepted completed paving projects on other major segments of Guadalupe Street (between 6th and 19th Streets), finalizing payments to the contractor and issuing assessment certificates to property owners.
