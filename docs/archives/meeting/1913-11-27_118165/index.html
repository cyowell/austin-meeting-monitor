Austin: Sewer Fees, New Paving, Tax Relief

Thursday, November 27, 1913 regular

  • New Sewerage Billing:

    Property owners, not tenants, will be billed directly for sewerage connections starting January 1914.

  • Infrastructure Upgrades:

    An alley between 5th and 6th Streets was ordered paved with concrete, a contract for the work was accepted, and payment for a new sewerage lift station was approved.

  • University Tax Exemption:

    Taxes from 1910 on land donated to the University of Texas were cancelled, recognizing the institution's public benefit and use of the property.

  • Temporary Advertising Permits:

    Local businesses received permits to hang temporary cloth signs promoting Christmas or "Cost" sales.

November 28th 1913: The Council was called to order by the Mayor with all members present: The minutes of the stated meeting of Nov 20th and subsequent recessed meetings were read and adopted. The Mayor laid before the Council the report of Dr S J Swift, Health Inspector which was read and ordered filed. Councilman Powell offered the following resolution : Be it resolved by the City Council of the City of Austin : Sewerage Rents To be charged to property owner That on and after January 1st ,1914, service charges for sewerage connections will be charged to the owner of Property so connected and the City's sewerage Dept will look to property owners or their agents for payment of such charges and not to the tenant or occupant of the premises, The resolution was adopted by the following vote : Yeas Mayor Wooldridge, Councilmen Anthony, Bartholomew, Haynes & Powell 5 Nays none.
