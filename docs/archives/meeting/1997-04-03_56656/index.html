Major Telecom Deals, Park Use, & Development Talks

Thursday, April 3, 1997 regular

  • Telecommunications Expansion Approved:

    The council approved multiple new and amended telecommunications service franchises, notably with Southwestern Bell, projected to generate over $2 million in annual franchise fees.

  • Key Land Use Hearings Scheduled:

    Public hearings were set for significant land use decisions, including using a portion of Walsh Boat Landing parkland for a wastewater project and the relocation of state and National Guard facilities to the new Austin-Bergstrom International Airport.

  • Annexation Policy and Environmental Concerns:

    A new policy was adopted to link city annexations with service extension capabilities, while citizen communications raised concerns about supporting the endangered Barton Springs Salamander.

  • Infrastructure and Community Updates:

    Speed limits were adjusted on FM 2222, and funding was approved for a new mobile police center to serve community neighborhoods. Other citizen discussions included affordable housing and youth job programs.
