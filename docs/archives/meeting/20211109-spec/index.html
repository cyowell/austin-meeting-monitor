ATX COVID: Boosting Kids, Bridging Gaps

Tuesday, November 9, 2021 Austin City Council Special Called Meeting

  • Pediatric Vaccinations for 5-11s are Underway:

    Initial rollout sees strong parent interest, with most planning to vaccinate their children quickly. Efforts prioritize doctors' offices, pharmacies, and pop-up clinics at 12 school sites, especially in underserved communities.

  • COVID-19 Cases Plateau, But Holiday Surges Feared:

    While case and hospitalization numbers are stable, health officials warn of potential increases during holiday gatherings. Booster shots for eligible adults and flu vaccinations are strongly recommended to protect hospital capacity.

  • Strong Focus on Vaccine Equity and Outreach:

    Mobile clinics, community partnerships, and "boots on the ground" teams continue to successfully close vaccination gaps in underserved zip codes, bringing services directly to residents and Title I schools.

  • Planning for Long-Term Health & Preparedness:

    Discussions are beginning on creating a permanent strategy for health education and service access in areas historically lacking medical facilities, and incorporating winter emergency preparedness messaging into ongoing vaccine outreach.
