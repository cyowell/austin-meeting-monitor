346 City Council Room Austin Texas May 09, 1873 The Board of Aldermen sitting as a Court of Appeals Ald. T. E. Sneed as president called the Court to order, the Roll was called. Present: Ald. T.E. Sneed E. Bremond, " " R.H. Boardman W.m Brueggerhoff " J.H. Robinson J.W. Bruning Absent : A. Scholz Geo. Susemann. On motion of Ald. Robinson the improvements on North half of Lot 2, in Block 69, improvements on South half of Lot 2 in Block 69, improvements on South half of 3 in Block 69 were revised to four thousand dollars each half, and improvements on Lot five in Block 69 were raised to eight thousand dollars. Ald. Robinson moved to reduce the Assessment on Lots 1 & 2 in Block 173 from $600 each to $750 each, which motion was carried. On motion of Ald. Bremond : Lot 4 in Block 81 was raised to $750 and Lots 5 & 6 in same Block to $100 each. Ald. Bremond moved that the Assessment of the old city limits for the year 1873 now stands as corrected, and the Court of Appeals adjourn semi die; the President thereof shall certify to the same, and deliver the Assessment to the Mayor, which motion was unanimously carried. Fred Sleying Recorder City Council Room Austin Texas May 9th 1873 Called meeting His Honor J. W. Wheeler called the Council to order, the Roll was called. Present: Ald. T.E. Sneed, E. Bremond J.H.Robinson " W.m Brueggerhoff R.H. Boardman J.W. Bruning Absent: Geo. Susemann A. Scholz Ald. Sneed moved that a Committee of three be appointed to confer with Mr. D. Humbolt in regard to water works, which motion was carried. His Honor the Mayor appointed on that Committee Ald. Sneed Robinson and Boardman, on motion the Mayor was added to the Committee. On motion the Council adjourned. Fred Sleying Recorder
