CITY OF AUSTIN, TEXAS NOTICE OF SPECIAL MEETING TO THE MEMBERS OF THE CITY COUNCIL OF THE CITY OF AUSTIN Notice is hereby given that a Special Meeting of the City Council of the City of Austin will be held on the 29th day of August, 1969, at the Municipal Building, Eighth and Colorado, in Austin, Texas, at 3:10 P.M. for the purpose of naming of a City Manager. S/ Elsie Woosley City Clerk ATTEST: s/ Elsie Woosley City Clerk s/ Travis La Rue Mayor City of Austin, Texas CONSENT TO MEETING We, the undersigned members of the City Council, hereby accept service of the foregoing notice, waiving any and all irregularities in such service and such notice, and consent and agree that said City Council shall meet at the time an place therein named, and for the purpose therein stated. S/ Les Gage s/ R. E. Janes, Jr. S/ D. R. Price s/ Jay Johnson, Jr. s/ Stuart A. MacCorkle CITY OF AUSTIN. TEXAS MINUTES OF THE CITY COUNCIL CITY OF AUSTIN, TEXAS Special Meeting August 29, 1969 3:10 P. M. Council Chamber, City Hall The meeting was called to order with Mayor LaRue presiding. Roll Call: Present: Absent: Councilmen Gage, Janes, Johnson, MacCorkle, Price, Mayor LaRue Councilman Atkison MAYOR LA RUE announced this Special Meeting was called for the purpose of naming a City Manager. All Council members present signed the Notice of Special Meeting called by the Mayor. Councilman Price moved the Council name MR. NORMAN BARKER as City Manager, City of Austin. The motion, seconded by Councilman MacCorkle, carried by the following vote: Ayes: Noes: Councilmen Gage, Janes, Johnson, MacCorkle, Price, Mayor LaRue None Absent: Councilman Atkison Councilman Janes moved the Council adjourn. The motion, seconded by Councilman Johnson, carried by the following vote: Ayes: Noes: Councilmen Gage, Janes, Johnson, MacCorkle, Price, Mayor LaRue None Absent: Councilman Atkison ATTEST: At 3:15 P.M. the Council went back into Executive Session.
