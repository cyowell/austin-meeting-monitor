Austin Tackles AIDS, Growth, and New Regulations

Thursday, November 13, 1986 regular

Here's a summary of the Austin City Council meeting from November 13, 1986:

  • Public Health & Social Support:

    The Council initiated steps toward an AIDS discrimination ordinance, approved funding for AIDS education programs targeting at-risk groups, and allocated funds for a winter shelter and repairs for homeless individuals.

  • Major Development & Environmental Policy:

    Key decisions included granting consent for the large Senna Hills Municipal Utility District (MUD) with specific environmental conditions. The Council also voted to remove square footage impervious cover caps in certain residential zoning districts and held a first public hearing on annexing 2,510 acres near McKinney Falls State Park.

  • City Operations & Infrastructure:

    New comprehensive city-wide sign regulations were adopted. Significant investments were approved, including a $2.68 million steam turbine for a waste-to-energy plant and contracts for new fire station construction and renovation.

  • Streamlining & Tourism:

    Efforts to boost the city's tourism program advanced with plans for a new nonprofit organization, and zoning processes were streamlined by setting a 12:00 noon deadline for submitting petitions to oppose zoning changes.
