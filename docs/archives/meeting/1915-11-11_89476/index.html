Councilman Powell presented the following communication: Austin, Texas, November 10th 1915. To the Hon Mayor and City Council , City of Austin, Texas. Gentlemen : Some months ago we had Mr Bantelof the Engineering department of the Universityto help us in the work of making preliminary plans for the impro-vement of Little Shoal Creek from the alley north of 7th street to 19th street A considerable amount of work was put in on these plans and the grade lines of the bottem of the sewer were determined,the sizes of the sewer for the entire entire distancewere carefully figured out ,and we are now ready to take up the work of making detailed plans. In these investigations it was found that we could use all of the stone arch bridgesalong this creek except the 11th street arch which is entirely too small and will have to be removed.Now ,in improving this waterway,the bottoms of these bridges will have to be dug out,the walls underpinned and smoth cement bottoms will have to be put in.This character of work is difficult for a con-tractor to figure on and the main thing that he will consider in making his price on work like this will be to get them high enough,in which case the City will be required to pay the maximum price instead of a reasonable price ,and I recomm end that the City go to work and have these bridges put in shape for connecting up with the rest of the work when it is undertaken.If we do this work we should start it now in order that we may get out of the way of the contractor so there will be no confusion and delay caused by our getting in his way ,and I recommend that we start this work at once and that an appropriation from the Permanent Street fund for the amount of twenty five hundred(x,2,500) dollars be made to carry on this work throughout the balance of the year. Through all of these bridges along this drain there are a great many pipe lines which very seriously obstruct the flow of water by catching drift and debrisand stopping up the water way.These pipes belong to the Sewer department, the Water and Light department,and to the Gas Co,and I recommend that the Cou-oil at once order these pipes to be removed and placed either over or under the drain way.It will take sometime to do this,and even if no ore is ever done to the creek chanel,conditions will be very much improved. The bridges as proposed to be improved fit in with our plan for the entire workand will carry considerable more water than they are now carrying, thereby improving conditions very materially. Respectfully, M C Welborn. City Engineer.
