City Addresses Graves, Gas, and Wartime Livestock Rules

Thursday, May 17, 1945 regular
  • A family's request to manage their Oakwood Cemetery plot was addressed, allowing a new burial while resolving that existing unknown graves in the lot would remain undisturbed.
  • Extensive new gas main construction was approved for installation across several city streets by the Texas Public Service Company, detailing specific locations and safety requirements.
  • A public hearing addressed proposed zoning changes, notably rules for keeping livestock and poultry in residential areas. The city decided to temporarily suspend enforcement of these regulations (except for sanitary requirements) for the duration of World War II.
