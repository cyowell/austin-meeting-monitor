Austin's Development Debates & Hospital Care

Thursday, March 14, 1957 regular

  • Major Zoning Overhauls:

    Approved significant rezonings, transforming some residential areas into office, commercial, and industrial districts, notably along the Interregional Highway.

  • Flexible Development Standards:

    Discussed a developer's request for reduced street widths and cul-de-sac sizes in the Balcones Park subdivision due to challenging terrain and existing construction, with officials agreeing to an on-site inspection.

  • New Business & Infrastructure:

    Awarded a contract for paving Koenig Lane and greenlit plans for a new Gulf Oil drive-in gasoline station, specifying detailed construction, safety, and environmental conditions.

  • Hospital Access Intervention:

    The Mayor directly intervened after a citizen reported difficulties admitting his 14-year-old son to Brackenridge Hospital, instructing the City Manager to ensure the child's admission and later determine payment.
