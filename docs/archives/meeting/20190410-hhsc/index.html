Austin Healthcare Board Selects Zuniga

Wednesday, April 10, 2019 Health & Human Services Committee Regular Meeting
  • A key health committee nominated Julie Zuniga to serve on the Central Health Board of Managers.
  • The committee held a closed session to interview five highly qualified candidates for the board position.
  • Zuniga's nomination will now be forwarded to the Mayor and full City Council for their consideration and final approval.
