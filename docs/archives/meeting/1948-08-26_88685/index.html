Austin's Future: Zoning, Subdivisions, Fire Dept

Thursday, August 26, 1948 regular

  • Pecan Springs School Annexation Blocked:

    The City Council rejected a proposal to annex the Pecan Springs School District solely for educational purposes, clarifying that this is not current city policy.

  • Major Zoning Changes Approved:

    Following public hearings, several residential areas were re-zoned for commercial use, including a significant change to allow for a community center.

  • New Subdivision & Innovative Utility Funding:

    The Ramona Heights subdivision was approved, along with a contract detailing a unique public-private partnership for the construction and financing of its water and sewer infrastructure.

  • Essential City Services Expanded:

    Contracts were awarded for building two new fire stations and for a substantial purchase of cast iron water pipes to support the city's future water distribution needs.
