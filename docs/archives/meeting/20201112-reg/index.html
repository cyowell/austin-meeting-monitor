Austin: Fees, Housing, & Chemical Use Changes

Thursday, November 12, 2020 Austin City Council Regular Meeting

  • New Street Impact Fees Move Forward:

    Approved on first reading, these fees will require new development projects to contribute to Austin's transportation infrastructure. Discussions are ongoing to balance these costs with housing affordability concerns.

  • Major Affordable Housing Project Advanced:

    Plans for a 171-unit supportive housing development progressed, including the formation of a new non-profit to manage the project.

  • Herbicide Use Policy Shift:

    Austin Energy was directed to notify residents about chemical use for power line maintenance and offer the option to opt out, sparking a broader discussion on herbicide policy.

  • Development Site Postponed Amid Homelessness Concerns:

    A Chalmers Court development was delayed to gather more community input, reflecting ongoing debates about homelessness and its impact on neighborhood planning.
