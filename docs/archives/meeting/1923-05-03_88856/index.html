  • Extensive City Staffing Overhaul:

    The council spent a significant portion of the meeting appointing new personnel and confirming existing roles across numerous departments, including the City Marshal, police force, fire department, health officials, utility managers, and administrative staff.

  • Public Land and Events Considered:
