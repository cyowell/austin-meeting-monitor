Wednesday, June 27, 1951 special called
Transcript

  • Expanded Utilities for Bergstrom Air Force Base:

    The city approved a contract to provide expanded water and new sewerage services to the growing Bergstrom Air Force Base and its related housing developments.

  • New Utility Rates for the Base:

    The agreement ensured Bergstrom Air Force Base would pay the city's current, increased water rates and newly implemented sewer service fees, replacing an older contract that did not include sewer charges.

  • City Claims Land Ownership:

    Despite entering the service contract, city leaders formally asserted their position that the equitable title to the land occupied by Bergstrom Air Force Base rightfully belongs to the City of Austin.
