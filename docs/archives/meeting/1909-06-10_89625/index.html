Thursday, June 10, 1909 regular
Transcript

• Fatal Boxing Match Investigation: City Council held a 4-day public hearing examining a boxing match at a local gymnasium where contestant Victor Lyons died from injuries on June 5, 1909. Nearly 60 witnesses testified about whether police officers failed in their duty to stop the match.

• Police Officers Exonerated: After thorough investigation, City Marshal J.T. Laughlin and Sergeant J.D. Platt were fully exonerated of neglect of duty charges. The Council determined they had no reasonable cause to stop the match beforehand, as it appeared to be a routine sparring exhibition.

• Infrastructure & Staffing: Council approved multiple ordinances funding street improvements (paving West 6th Street), a $250 weed-cutting initiative, $400 for hospital repairs, and hired a new policeman while accepting another's resignation.

• Public Services: The Council addressed citizen petitions about water supply issues in southeast Austin, assessed tax disputes, and reviewed delinquent accounts for water and light services.
