Thursday, October 8, 2026 Regular Meeting

Austin Housing, Roads, Elections & Landmarks

  • Affordable Housing and Home Repairs Advanced:

    The Council approved up to $33 million in housing revenue bonds for the Elm Ridge Apartments on Airport Blvd for acquisition and rehabilitation. They also added $10 million to the G.O. Repair! program for home repairs, bringing its total to $32 million.

  • Key Rezonings, Including Debated South Austin Project:

    Historic landmark status was granted to the James Harvey and Pearl Caldwell House and the Doris Miller Auditorium. A rezoning for a mixed-use project at 1120-1122 S Capital of Texas Highway was approved with an amendment to prohibit certain uses, despite significant public opposition over traffic and safety concerns.

  • Infrastructure and Workforce Development:

    The Council authorized eminent domain for property needed for the Walnut Creek stream stabilization project and directed the City Manager to update construction training program requirements for major projects (over $10 million) to boost local workforce opportunities.

  • Future City Bond Planning Adopted:

    A resolution was approved to shift to a six-year bond cycle for future city financial planning, a decision that generated public discussion regarding its potential impact on project timelines.

  • November 2026 Election Details Finalized:

    Ordinances were passed to update and finalize election day and early voting details for the upcoming General Obligation Bond, City Council, and Special Charter Amendment elections.

  • Critical City Services and Safety Contracts:

    Various essential contracts were approved, including $2 million more for operational support to Rally Austin, $1.4 million for a new Rutland/Parkfield roundabout to improve traffic safety, and funds for public flood monitoring via the ATX Floods app.
