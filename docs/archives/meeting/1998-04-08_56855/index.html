Future Austin: Roads, Water, Bike Paths, Jobs

Wednesday, April 8, 1998

  • Transportation & Mobility:

    Approved final designs for Barton Springs Road and initiated a study for traffic flow improvements on Lamar Boulevard. Following a significant debate, a separate pedestrian and bicycle bridge was approved for the Lamar Boulevard area.

  • Water & Wastewater Infrastructure:

    Committed multi-million dollars to upgrade essential utility systems, including power distribution, water transmission mains, and a new material handling facility. The city also approved a wastewater service agreement with Rollingwood and extended water service to a church outside city limits.

  • Community & Public Services:

    Explored key issues through presentations on adult job training programs, significant state water policy changes, and the development of a combined emergency communication center. A public hearing was also scheduled for the adoption of Part 2 of the city's Bicycle Plan.
