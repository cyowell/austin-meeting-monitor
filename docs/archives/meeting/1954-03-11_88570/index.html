Austin Funds Growth, Debates Alcohol Sales

Thursday, March 11, 1954 regular
  • Austin secured $15 million in bonds to upgrade and expand the city's electric, water, and sewer systems.
  • The city moved forward with plans for a new Municipal Auditorium, earmarking $400,000 for preliminary work, and initiated land acquisition for the future Interregional Highway.
  • Contentious public hearings took place regarding zoning changes, particularly for businesses selling alcohol near schools, resulting in several denials after heated debate.
  • The council addressed hospital employee complaints about job security and approved modernizing tax records through microfilming, alongside correcting various property tax assessment errors.
