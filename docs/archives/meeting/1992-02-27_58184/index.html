Watershed Rules, Big Bonds, New Health Services

Thursday, February 27, 1992 regular

  • Environmental Protection & Drainage:

    Approved funding for erosion control projects and expanded comprehensive environmental protection rules across all city watersheds.

  • Major Utility Bond Refinancing:

    Authorized a substantial $690 million bond issuance to refinance combined utility system debt.

  • Enhanced Public Health Programs:

    Increased support for HIV/AIDS services, added staff for community development and housing initiatives, and approved plans for a new North Austin Health Clinic.

  • Future City Investments:

    Held a public hearing for a proposed 1992 bond election, laying groundwork for future major city projects.
