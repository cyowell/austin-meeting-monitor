Austin Streets, Sewers, and Surprising Tax Cuts

Thursday, December 25, 1913 regular

  • Major Infrastructure Projects:

    The city finalized payments for West 6th Street paving, awarded a new contract for the Upper East Avenue sewer, and approved plans and funding for a new storm sewer on Blanco Street, alongside purchasing modern sewage ejectors.

  • Financial Decisions & Tax Controversy:

    Funds were moved to cover an upcoming $10,000 sewer payment and bond interest. Notably, the city also canceled $29,150 in assessed taxes for a private investment company.

  • Public Services and Welfare:

    Contracts were approved for essential city supplies, hospital provisions, and services for paupers, including meals, drugs, and burials. A specific section of Oakwood Cemetery was officially designated for the interment of white paupers.
