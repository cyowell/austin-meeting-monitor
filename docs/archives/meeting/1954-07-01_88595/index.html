Austin Water Discount, Zoning Changes & Power Expansion

Thursday, July 1, 1954 regular

  • Land Use and Development:

    Approved a special permit for a home-based day nursery and greenlit a new drive-in gasoline station, complete with detailed construction and operational requirements. Several ordinances were also passed to change various zoning designations across the city, including conversions from residential to commercial districts.

  • Infrastructure Investment:

    Committed over $165,000 to modernize the power plant by awarding contracts for boiler combustion control and new boiler feed pumps. Additionally, the City initiated street paving and improvement projects, establishing a funding model where costs would be shared with abutting property owners.

  • Public Services and Traffic Flow:

    Provided a financial break to residents by approving an additional discount on water bills for July and August. To alleviate traffic congestion from ongoing highway construction, temporary one-way street designations were implemented on Bushy and San Marcos.
