Austin Police and Budget Decisions

Thursday, December 29, 1910 regular

• New Police Officer Hired: S.A. Moore was appointed and confirmed as Austin's newest policeman, replacing Guy Maddox who resigned.

• Criminal Fines Forgiven: The council remitted (cancelled) fines totaling over $200 owed by 19 people convicted in Recorder's Court, ranging from $1.05 to $33.55 per person.

• Damage Claim Filed: A citizen named B.W. Randolph petitioned for compensation after his team and wagon were damaged when frightened by blasting operations conducted by the Water & Light Department.

• Major Budget Appropriations: The council approved nearly $40,000 in spending for January and February 1911, covering salaries and accounts for all city departments including police, fire, sanitation, streets, water, and light services.

• Water Infrastructure Investment: Council authorized purchasing steel reinforcement materials from a local supplier for trenches needed by the Water Department.
