Austin Bond Exchange and Interest Payments 1906

Wednesday, October 10, 1906 special called

• Bond Refinancing Deal: The city council approved the exchange of $370,000 in water and light bonds for refunding bonds, allocating $800 to cover interest adjustments on the transaction.

• Interest Payment Appropriations: Council budgeted $250 to pay accrued interest on city bonds as part of ongoing financial obligations.

• Previous Ordinance Repealed: An earlier ordinance from September 26, 1906 that appropriated money for bond interest payments was repealed and any related checks were canceled.

• Legal Cases Dismissed: The city attorney was authorized to dismiss pending appeals in the Court of Civil Appeals under certain conditions.

The following Call of the Council was read Austin, Texas, October 10. 1906 To the members of the City Council of the City of Austin Gentlemen: There is hereby a special meeting of the City Council of the City of Austin Called to meet in the temporary Council Chamber of the City at 4 o'clock P.M. October 10th 1906, for the purpose of taking such action, passing such ordinances and resolutions as may be necessary or required to carry out the terms of an ordinance heretofore passed by the City Council, authorizing and directing the exchange of certain refunding bonds of the City of Austin for water and light bonds of said City, and to do all things that may be necessary in the matter.
