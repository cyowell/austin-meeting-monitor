Centennial Prep & City Upgrades

Thursday, April 23, 1936 regular

  • Preparing for the Texas Centennial:

    Discussions began for cleaning up Lake Austin Park and adding sanitary facilities for incoming automobile campers, with a $50 donation from the Junior Chamber of Commerce.

  • Investing in Public Health:

    An appropriation of $4,250 was approved to furnish the Nurses' Home at Brackenridge Hospital with new furniture, rugs, and draperies.

  • New Commercial Development:

    Magnolia Petroleum Company received approval to build a drive-in gasoline filling station and wash rack on South Congress Avenue, subject to detailed construction, drainage, and compliance conditions.

  • Infrastructure & Public-Private Paving:

    An agreement was reached with the International and Great Northern Railroad for them to fund paving a section of West 3rd Street, including parts of their property, with provisions to protect their right to lay future tracks.
