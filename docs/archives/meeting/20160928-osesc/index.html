Austin's Water, Parks, and Flood Futures

Wednesday, September 28, 2016 Open Space, Environment, and Sustainability Committee Regular Meeting

  • Water Quality Threat:

    Austin is actively opposing Dripping Springs' proposed plan to discharge nearly a million gallons of treated wastewater daily into Onion Creek, a vital recharge source for Barton Springs, citing concerns about significant water degradation and setting a concerning precedent for the region.

  • Park Concert Oversight:

    The Long Center's outdoor concert series prompted discussions on ensuring public parkland use balances community enjoyment with neighborhood impact. A city task force recommended reducing events at nearby Auditorium Shores and called for formalized guidelines for outdoor events and improved scheduling coordination for all venues in the area.

  • Flood Mitigation Study:

    A new study is underway to develop solutions for severe and frequent flooding in the Lower Shoal Creek area, which repeatedly impacts homes, businesses, and major transportation routes like Lamar Boulevard, even during moderate rain events.

  • Regional Conservation Vision:

    The Hill Country Alliance presented a long-term vision for a "Hill Country Endowment," proposing a broad regional collaboration to fund efforts protecting open spaces, water resources, and the unique character of the rapidly growing Central Texas Hill Country.
