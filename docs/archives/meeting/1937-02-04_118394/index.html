Austin: Traffic Safety and Infrastructure Boost

Thursday, February 4, 1937 regular

  • Traffic & Infrastructure Upgrades:

    New traffic regulations were enacted, including stop signs at various intersections, and the City Engineer was directed to identify streets for widening. Permits were also granted for extensive new gas main installations across the city.

  • Construction Project Safety:

    Several contractors received approval to temporarily use public sidewalks and streets for building alterations and construction, under strict conditions mandating safety barriers, pedestrian access, and property restoration.

  • Future Development & Zoning:

    A public hearing was scheduled to consider rezoning several residential lots on Shoal Creek Boulevard, signaling potential changes to urban development.

  • Fiscal Management & New Deal Support:

    The city appropriated funds for a New Deal-backed project for street marking and sign painting, and formally certified the cremation of over $240,000 in retired city bonds.
