Prop 1 Fails, Runoffs Set

Tuesday, November 18, 2014 Austin City Council Special Called Meeting
  • Voters rejected Proposition 1, a ballot measure for the city.
  • The city certified the November 4th election results, officially declaring Delia Garza (District 2), Ann Kitchen (District 5), and Kathie Tovo (District 9, due to opponent withdrawal) as winners of their council seats.
  • Runoff elections were ordered for December 16th to decide the Mayoral race and Council Districts 1, 3, 4, 6, 7, 8, and 10, as no candidate secured a majority vote.
