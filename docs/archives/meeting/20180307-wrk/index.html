  • Austin City Council is aligning future budget decisions with strategic outcomes focused on economic opportunity, affordability, mobility, and effective government.
  • Residents generally love living in Austin and rate city customer service exceptionally high, 25% above the national average, with overall satisfaction seeing an upward trend.

  • New data analytics tools, including dashboards and GIS mapping, will enable the council to drill down into resident feedback by neighborhood and demographics, informing more targeted policy and budget decisions.
