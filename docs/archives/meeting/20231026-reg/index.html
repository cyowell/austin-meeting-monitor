Austin Debates New Housing Density, Affordability

Thursday, October 26, 2023 Austin City Council Regular Meeting
  • Austin officials considered a proposal to allow up to three homes on all single-family residential lots citywide.
  • The "HOME Initiative" also seeks to remove limits on the number of unrelated adults living together in a home and ease regulations for accessory dwelling units and tiny homes.
  • Public and council members voiced significant concerns about the proposal's actual impact on housing affordability, potential for larger new homes, and strain on existing city infrastructure (water, traffic, fire safety).
  • This meeting was a public hearing only, with no votes taken. The Planning Commission is set to discuss and potentially vote on November 14th, and the City Council on December 7th.
