Hospital Safety Upgrades & New City Paving Plans

Thursday, October 21, 1915 regular

  • Public Safety:

    Approved a contract to install new fire escapes on both the old and new City Hospital buildings, enhancing patient safety.

  • Road Improvements:

    Officially accepted the completed paving of an alley between Congress and Brazos streets, authorizing final payment to the contractor.

  • Future Infrastructure:

    Authorized inviting bids for extensive new street paving projects across the city, requiring a substantial $5,000 deposit from interested contractors.
