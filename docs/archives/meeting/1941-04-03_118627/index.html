Austin Licenses Bikes, Buys Cemetery, Eases Taxes

Thursday, April 3, 1941 regular

Here's a summary of the Austin City Council's April 3, 1941 agenda:

  • New Bicycle Regulations:

    Approved an ordinance requiring all bicycles to be licensed, imposing a fee, and outlining penalties for violations to better regulate traffic and safety.

  • Major City Land Deals & Expansion:

    Agreed to purchase Austin Memorial Park (a cemetery) for $56,000, initiated proceedings to annex three additional tracts of land, and approved closing a public park and street sections in a subdivision.

  • Tax Penalty Postponement & Speed Limit Focus:

    Postponed the 5% penalty on 1940 property taxes until June 1, 1941 (6% interest still applies). The city also pledged cooperation with a campaign to enforce the new state 30 mph speed limit.
