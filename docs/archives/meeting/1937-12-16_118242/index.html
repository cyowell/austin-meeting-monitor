Austin: Gas Lines, New Zoning, Plane Damage

Thursday, December 16, 1937 regular
  • Reports detailed significant damage to two homes caused by a "falling aircraft," leading to property value reassessments.
  • Major infrastructure projects were approved, including extensive new natural gas lines across several streets and contracts for furnishing the new Municipal Building, a federal public works initiative.
  • The city made urban planning decisions, changing residential zoning near the City Hospital to commercial and establishing strict rules for contractors using public sidewalks and streets for construction.
  • Abandoned underground telephone conduits were acquired by the city, with plans to repurpose them for future municipal electric lines.
