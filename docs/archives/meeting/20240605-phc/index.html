Tackling Heat, Shelter & Health

Wednesday, June 5, 2024 Public Health Committee Regular Meeting

  • Summer Heat Plan:

    The city is activating and extending cooling center hours and increasing outreach to protect residents during extreme summer heat, citing a rise in heat-related illnesses.

  • Homeless Shelter Crossroads:

    The Marshaling Yard shelter will close by March 2025. Officials face significant challenges finding a replacement site but commit to rehousing all residents rather than returning them to the streets.

  • Community Health Worker Expansion:

    Discussions are ongoing to expand the vital Community Health Worker program, particularly focusing on mental health and outreach to unhoused populations, while pushing for increased city funding for these roles.

  • Black Men's Health Progress:

    The Black Men's Health Clinic highlights its success in addressing health disparities among Black and Brown men through culturally appropriate care and is exploring further integration with unhoused services.
