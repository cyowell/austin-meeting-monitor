Bus Service, Zoning, and Parking Meter Updates

Thursday, May 14, 1959 regular

  • Major Zoning Changes Approved:

    The city passed an ordinance to rezone several properties, changing districts for residential, office, and local retail use in various areas.

  • New Parking Meter Zone:

    A new parking meter zone was established on East 9th Street between Brazos and San Jacinto Streets.

  • Private Fueling Station Permit:

    A permit was granted to a local company to operate a private 2,000-gallon gasoline tank and pump for its own equipment at an industrial site.

  • Bus Service Discontinuation Debated:

    The council deferred action on a request from the Austin Transit Corp. to discontinue summer bus service to Ford Village.

  • Non-Profit Tax Contributions Questioned:

    The City Attorney was asked to investigate whether the Scottish Rite Dormitory, a non-profit, could be asked to contribute funds in lieu of taxes, as some other non-profit dorms reportedly do.
