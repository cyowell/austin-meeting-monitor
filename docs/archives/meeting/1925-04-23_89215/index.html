Austin Modernizes Laws, Adjusts Fines

Thursday, April 23, 1925 regular

  • Major Legal Overhaul:

    The city initiated a project to fully codify and revise all Austin ordinances, hiring a legal firm for $6,000 to simplify, update, and remove outdated laws.

  • City Pays for Damage:

    A $720 payment was approved to a resident whose property was damaged by fire from the city's incinerator plant, following an arbitration settlement.

  • Traffic Rule Update:

    A specific subsection of Austin's existing traffic ordinance was officially repealed.

  • Fines Waived:

    The council remitted fines for several individuals, including cases of traffic violations, speeding, and malicious mischief.
