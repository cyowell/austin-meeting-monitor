Austin Hires Assistant City Engineer

Thursday, April 20, 1905 special called

• Primary Action: City Council approved hiring an assistant to the City Engineer with a 11-2 vote, with only Aldermen Moore and Rabahl voting against the measure.

• Quick Process: The ordinance was introduced and passed on the same day under emergency suspension of normal rules, suggesting the position was considered urgent.

• Salary Set: The new position included a fixed salary (amount not specified in the transcript), and the assistant would support the existing City Engineer in their duties.

• Attendance: 12 of 14 aldermen were present for the special meeting; Alderman Von Rosenberg was out of town.

338 Called Meeting of the City-Council Austin, Texas, April 20, 1905 Hon F. O. Shelley, Mayor, Presiding Roll Called Present Aldermen Armstrong, Crooker, Duncan, Haynes, Miller, Moore, Patri, Powell, Rabahl, Shumate, Smith and Wilhelm 12 Absent Aldermen Thacker, Von Rosenberg 2 The following Call of the Council was read. Austin, Texas, April 20th 1905 Call of Council A Special Meeting of the City-Council of the City of Austin, Texas, is hereby Called to meet in the City Hall at 8 o’clock P.M. this 20th day of April A.D. 1905, for the purpose of employing an assistant to the City Engineer. F. O. Shelley Mayor L. W. Crooker Alderman 8th Ward Geo. D. Smith " 7th " Jno. Shumate " 6th " Austin, Texas, April 20th 1905 I hereby Certify that I have this day served the within Call for a Special Meeting of the City Council of the City of Austin, Tex, by reading same to the following named Aldermen, to-wit:F. L. Haynes, R. D. Jones, F. D. Armstrong, Joseph J. Thacker, Jno. Shumate, Louis Powell, A. L. Moore, H. Rabahl, H. S. Miller, H. Wilhelm, Henry Patri F. W. Powell, L. W. Crooker Alderman Von Rosenberg being out of the City-I left a Copy of the within Call at his residence J. I. Morris City Marshal. Alderman Shumate moved that City-Engineer Leonard be requested to address the Council on the matter mentioned in the Call. After remarks by Ordinance authorizing Alderman Leonard, Alderman Thacker seconded the the appointment of an Asst. to Engineer for motion. Carried. Alderman Miller introduced two months An ordinance employing an assistant Engineer to Assist the City Engineer and fixing the Salary of Said Assistant. The ordinance was read the first time and passed under suspension of the rules by the following Vote Yeas Aldermen Armstrong, Crooker, Duncan, Haynes, Thacker, Miller, Patri, Powell, Shumate, Smith and Wilhelm 11 Nays Aldermen Moore, Rabahl 2 On motion the Council adjourned. J. I. Morris City Clerk.
