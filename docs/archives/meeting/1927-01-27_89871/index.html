Utility Prices, Road Builds, Fair Debt Relief

Thursday, January 27, 1927 regular

  • New Electricity Rates:

    Comprehensive new rate schedules were adopted for residential, commercial, and industrial electricity services, set to take effect on February 1st.

  • Street Paving Contracts:

    Multiple bids were accepted for paving several city streets, with the Laurel Lane project specifically requiring payment from abutting property owners.

  • Fair's Debt Forgiven:

    The city cancelled an $800 debt owed by the Texas State Exposition for water, light, and power, acknowledging its financial difficulties.

  • Planning Commission Appointed:

    New members were confirmed for the City Planning Commission, an important body for guiding future urban development.
