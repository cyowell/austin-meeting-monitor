Housing Fund Reopens Down Payment Aid

Thursday, January 16, 1997
  • The Austin Housing Finance Corporation (AHFC) discussed a prior suspension of applications for its various funding programs.
  • Board members voted to lift this moratorium specifically for the Down Payment Assistance Program.
  • This decision immediately made over $800,000 available to help residents with down payments for home purchases.
