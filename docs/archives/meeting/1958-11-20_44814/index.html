Thursday, November 20, 1958 regular
Transcript

  • Long-Term Energy Secured:

    The city approved a major 23-year contract for natural gas to power its electric plants, outlining supply commitments, quality standards, and conditions for energy security.

  • City Expansion & Zoning Updates:

    New ordinances were passed to annex over 64 acres of land and amend zoning regulations, guiding development in recently added areas and specific commercial/residential properties.

  • Infrastructure Investments:

    Significant contracts were awarded for public works projects, including street paving with property assessments, new water and sewer mains, and equipment upgrades for the Holly Street Power Plant and Filter Plant #1.

  • Community & Property Issues:

    Residents requested a new family swimming pool and discussed concession stand operations at Barton Springs. The Council also heard numerous property tax appeals from citizens, often deciding to inspect the sites before making decisions.
