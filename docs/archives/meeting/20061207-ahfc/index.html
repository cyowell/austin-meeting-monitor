New Homes for Veterans & Austin Residents

Thursday, December 7, 2006 Austin Housing Finance Corporation (AHFC) Board of Directors AHFC Meeting
  • Approved a $100,000 loan to acquire two housing units for very low-income homeless military veterans, providing six transitional bedrooms.
  • Increased funding for the Guadalupe Neighborhood Development Corporation by $35,000 to develop seven new affordable housing units.
  • Consented to the transfer of ownership for the bond-financed Stassney Woods Apartments.
