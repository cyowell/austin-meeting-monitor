Austin's Urban Progress: Paving, Parks, Permits

Thursday, July 21, 1932 regular

  • Major Infrastructure Projects Approved:

    The city accepted newly completed paving on key sections of Congress Avenue and East Sixth Street, and awarded a contract for bookstacks for the Public Library.

  • New Park Land Acquired:

    An ordinance was passed to purchase land from the Public Free Schools for future parks and playgrounds, using $50,000 from a bond fund and promissory notes.

  • Strict Business Permits Issued:

    Permits were granted for a new commercial gasoline filling station and a private gasoline pump for a business, both with extensive conditions for construction, public access, and environmental protection.

  • Industrial Electricity Rates Adjusted:

    The council amended the "Rate F" industrial power service, detailing changes to electricity demand and energy charges for businesses.
