Pay Hikes, Park Land, and Paving Policy Push

Thursday, August 10, 1950 regular

Here's a summary of the August 10, 1950 Austin agenda:

  • Public Safety Pay & Benefits:

    Firefighters and police lobbied for substantial pay raises, overtime, and additional holidays, hinting at legal action if their demands, backed by an initiative petition, weren't met.

  • Controversial Zoning & Development:

    The city denied proposals for a bowling alley and a cafe selling beer in residential areas due to strong community opposition. However, they approved plans for a community mausoleum and moved to draft an ordinance allowing off-premise beer sales in designated commercial zones.

  • Infrastructure & Community Needs:

    Discussions included calls for more affordable street paving, a request from the school district for land in Pease Park for a new junior high, and demands for relocating the incinerator, new playgrounds, and dedicated facilities for Black residents.
