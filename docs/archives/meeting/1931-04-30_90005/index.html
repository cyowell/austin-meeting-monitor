Austin: Paving, Parks, and Local Hiring

Thursday, April 30, 1931 regular

  • Major Infrastructure Projects & Assessments:

    Approved contracts for storm sewer construction and accepted completed street paving projects. Property owners participated in public hearings regarding proposed street improvements across multiple areas, leading to the levying of assessments for these projects. Permits were also granted for new gas and telephone lines.

  • Significant Park Investments:

    Allocated over $42,000 from the Parks and Playgrounds Bond Fund for various enhancements, including improvements at Mexican Park and South Austin Park, and beautification projects on 15th Street and East Avenue, alongside relocating a bandstand.

  • New City-Wide Regulations Enacted:

    Passed comprehensive ordinances establishing a new Building Code, creating Fire Zones, and regulating signs, alongside approving a budget for the Building Inspector's office to enforce these new standards.

  • Addressing Unemployment with Local Preference:

    Created an unpaid "Investigator of Labor" position to prioritize Austin citizens for employment opportunities with the city and its contractors, responding to prevailing unemployment.
