Austin's Future: Transit, Wind Power, City Art

Thursday, March 9, 1995 regular

Here's a summary of the March 09, 1995 Austin agenda:

  • Adopted Major Transportation Blueprint:

    Approved the Austin Metropolitan Area Transportation Plan (AMATP), setting a long-term vision for regional transit and infrastructure.

  • Invested in Green Energy & Flood Control:

    Secured a 25-year contract for wind power and allocated funds for Waller Creek flood management and vital drainage project land acquisitions.

  • Enhanced City Spaces & Public Safety:

    Commissioned new public art for the Convention Center, advanced a graffiti ordinance, addressed noise concerns for the Aqua Festival, and approved airport environmental remediation.

  • Addressed Hospital Lease & Key Incident:

    Authorized legal services for the Brackenridge Hospital lease negotiations and directed an investigation into a specific incident on Cedar Street.
