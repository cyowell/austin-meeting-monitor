Wednesday, May 7, 1986 special called
Transcript
  • The meeting focused solely on a major rezoning effort for a large section of the Oak Hill area, shifting from temporary residential zoning.
  • Proposed changes would allow for a diverse range of development, including various residential types, commercial, office, industrial, agricultural, and even mobile home parks.
  • Specific property rezonings within Oak Hill included conditions for water quality protection, requiring filtration-sedimentation systems for some tracts.
  • All proposed zoning changes received initial approval, passing their first reading but not yet becoming final policy.
