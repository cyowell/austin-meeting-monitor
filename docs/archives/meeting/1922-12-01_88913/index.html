Austin: Gas Station Proposed, Garage Approved

Friday, December 1, 1922 regular
  • A proposal was submitted to install a new gasoline station at 29th and Guadalupe Streets, which was placed under review.
  • An application to build a galvanized iron shed at 200 Brazos Street was referred to the Safety Committee for consideration.
  • Following a special meeting, the application for an "iron garage" at 200 Brazos Street (likely the previously mentioned shed) received official approval.
