Elizabet Ney Museum, City Infrastructure & Lake

Thursday, July 24, 1941 regular

  • Elizabet Ney Museum Management:

    The city officially took over the management and maintenance of the historic Elizabet Ney Museum from the Texas Fine Arts Association, establishing an advisory board for its continued operation.

  • Infrastructure & Development Projects:

    Approved extensive new gas main construction across several streets and permitted a new drive-in gasoline filling station, complete with detailed regulations for construction and drainage.

  • Lake Use & Public Access:

    Reaffirmed current public access restrictions around the Tom Miller and Mansfield Dams. Separately, a private float (dock) was approved for Lake Austin, under strict conditions prohibiting commercial use or living quarters.
