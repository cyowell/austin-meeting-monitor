Austin's New Jobs, Energy & Equity Push

Thursday, April 7, 2011 Austin City Council Regular Meeting

  • Big Tech Investment:

    The city moved forward with an economic development deal for eBay (PayPal) aiming to create 1,000 high-paying software engineering jobs over a decade, projecting over $1 billion in net benefit for Austin, with incentives tied directly to job creation and salary benchmarks.

  • Weatherization Program Accountability:

    A federal grant-funded energy weatherization program for low-income homes faced sharp criticism from minority and women-owned contractors who alleged unfair work allocation, payment delays, and lack of transparency. The Council approved continued funding but directed staff to resolve these equity issues.

  • Support for Local Food Systems:

    New ordinance changes were passed to lower cost barriers for local food producers and expand educational cooking demonstrations at farmers' markets across the city by waiving certain temporary food permit fees.

  • Apartment Energy Efficiency Debated:

    A proposed ordinance on energy conservation audit disclosures for multi-family properties sparked concern from apartment associations, who requested delays to ensure that required energy upgrades remain economically feasible for property owners.
