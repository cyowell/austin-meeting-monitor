The Mayor announced this was a special meeting for the purpose of meeting with a committee from East Austin regarding a District Park; for considering the bids on Contract No. 57-C-36, and to discuss the plans for an underpass on Lamar Boulevard at Third Street. The City Manager and Director of Recreation presented aerial maps of three tracts of land available for a park in this area. Tract I contained approximately 40 acres and is located on East 12th Street extending south to Oak Springs Drive and bounded on the east by Springdale Road and Webberville Road. Tract II contained approximately 23 acres and is located on the south side of East 19th Street, south to East 12th Street from Airport Boulevard to Redwood Street. Tract III contained approximately 28 acres and is located south of Heflin Drive between Springdale Road and Webberville Road, being north of Simms School.
