Austin Housing Finance Boosts Local Projects

Thursday, April 11, 2002 Austin Housing Finance Corporation (AHFC)
  • Appointed new leadership to boards managing a low-income housing tax credit project and a 176-unit senior citizen housing facility.
  • Authorized negotiations for a $1 million loan guarantee to fund Heritage Village, a new 56-unit mixed-income subdivision built under S.M.A.R.T. Housing standards.
  • Approved a significant $9.63 million bond refinancing for the Riverchase Apartments, an existing multi-family housing complex.
