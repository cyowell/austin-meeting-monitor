Austin Funds Millions for Affordable Housing

Thursday, April 13, 2000 Austin Housing Finance Corporation (AHFC)
  • Approved a $500,000 loan to rehabilitate Village Green Apartments, preserving existing affordable housing.
  • Authorized a $100,000 loan to build four new affordable homes in the Guadalupe Neighborhood.
  • Issued over $17 million in revenue bonds to acquire and renovate four major apartment complexes across the city (Fairway Village, Santa Maria Village, Springdale Apartments, and South Congress Apartments) to boost the supply of affordable living options.
