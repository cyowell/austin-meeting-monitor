Resolution to get estimate of Cost of establishing permanent grades & levels Alderman Schuber offered the following resolution, which was adopted; Whereas, It is has become a matter of necessity that permanent grades and levels be established for the Streets Sidewalks, alleys and public ways of the City, So that all public improvements may Conform to a System; and Whereas, It is also necessary that a survey of the City be made for drainage and Sanitary purposes, and to provide for a System of Sewerage; and Whereas, It is necessary to employ an experienced. and Skilful engineer to advise and assist the City engineer in Such work; therefore, be it Resolved by the City Council of the City of Austin.
