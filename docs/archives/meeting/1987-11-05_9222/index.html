Austin's Airport Future Decided

Thursday, November 5, 1987 regular

  • New Airport Direction Approved:

    Voters decisively supported constructing a new airport near Manor, rejecting expansion of the existing Robert Mueller Municipal Airport, marking a pivotal decision for the city's future infrastructure.

  • Increased Utility Rate Transparency:

    Steps were initiated to enhance public oversight of electric utility rates by launching a search for both a Utility Hearing Examiner and a Consumer Advocate.

  • Economic Development & Infrastructure Push:

    The city began an Enterprise Zone Program to foster economic growth and advanced major highway improvements (US 183, US 290/SH 71) and wastewater system upgrades for growing areas.

  • Cultural Arts Facility Standoff:

    A contentious debate regarding the Mexican American Cultural Arts Facility saw a decision to reopen its proposal process reversed within the same meeting, indicating significant internal disagreement.
