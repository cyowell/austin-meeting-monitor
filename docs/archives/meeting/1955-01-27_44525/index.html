Austin's Mid-Century Debates: Pay, Parks, Planning

Thursday, January 27, 1955 regular

  • Governance & Compensation:

    Proposals were submitted to increase City Council pay (Mayor an extra $1,000, Councilmen $5,000 annually) and to have Austin voters elect Corporation Court judges.

  • Public Land & Lake Use:

    The city considered a request for a 20-acre public shooting range in a municipal park and addressed public concerns and a petition against annually lowering Lake Austin, citing impacts on fish spawning and recreation.

  • Future Growth & Infrastructure:

    A consultant was authorized to develop a comprehensive master plan for the city, and a decision was made to immediately acquire a new sanitary landfill to replace the existing site.

  • Community Services & Liability:

    Residents from East Austin raised concerns about traffic safety, police presence, and playground access. The city also discussed its legal immunity regarding claims for vehicle damages from a fire truck collision.
