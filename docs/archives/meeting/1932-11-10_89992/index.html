Here's a summary of the Austin City Council agenda from November 10, 1932:

  • Zoning Showdown in Enfield & Westfield:

    The Council heard passionate arguments for and against reclassifying upscale residential areas to allow apartment buildings. While some residents protested the change, a city board recommended approval, noting that existing private property deeds already permitted apartments in these zones.

  • New Public Library Furnished:

    A $6,125 contract was approved to equip Austin's new Public Library with all necessary furniture, including bookshelves, tables, chairs, and specialized items for various sections, preparing it for opening.

  • Austin Expands Park System:

    The City moved to acquire several key land tracts, totaling over $3,900, to expand parkland along Shoal Creek and secure another property, with landowners contributing public access rights-of-way for the Shoal Creek development.

  • Charity Carnival Receives Utility Discount:

    The Odd Fellows, a local charitable organization, were granted a reduced commercial rate for their week-long carnival's electricity bill, acknowledging its purpose was to raise funds for an orphanage.
