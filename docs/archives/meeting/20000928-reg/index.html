Thursday, September 28, 2000 Austin City Council Regular Meeting

Here's a summary of the Austin City Council's September 28, 2000 agenda:

  • Expanded Social Services & Public Health:

    Approved significant funding contracts for child care, emergency home repair, and tenant counseling programs. The council also adopted a new policy outlining the City's provision of public and primary health care.

  • Major Development & Green Space Investments:

    Advanced plans for a new Convention Center parking garage and utility plant, including land acquisition. Additionally, the council approved acquiring real property for greenways and destination parks near Spicewoods Springs Road.

  • Future Transit & Urban Planning:

    Initiated efforts to explore using airport revenues to fund a light rail connection to Austin-Bergstrom International Airport. The council also approved building variances in the Shoal Creek flood plain for a downtown project and updated the "Camping in Public Places" ordinance.

  • Key City Operations & Infrastructure:

    Authorized grants for Austin Police Department equipment and funded substantial water and wastewater utility projects, including new main construction, service replacements, and regional water agreements with neighboring communities.
