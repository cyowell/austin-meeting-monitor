Austin Funds Parks, Rejects Utilities, Hosts Lesotho

Monday, February 25, 1980 special called

  • Confirmed Bond Election Results:

    The city officially verified the February 23rd bond election, approving significant funding for park development, health and neighborhood centers, a new police and courts building, and railroad crossing safety improvements.

  • Rejected Key Infrastructure Funds:

    Voters significantly turned down major bond proposals for new hospital parking, police vehicle impoundment, airport improvements, and substantial extensions to the city's waterworks, sewer, and electric power systems.

  • Welcomed Sister City Delegation:

    Austin hosted His Excellency T.T. Thahane, Ambassador of Lesotho, and other representatives from Maseru, Austin's sister city in Southern Africa, to promote cultural understanding and explore investment opportunities.
