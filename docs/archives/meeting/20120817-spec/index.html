Austin's November Election & Bonds

Friday, August 17, 2012 Austin City Council Special Called Meeting
  • The entire special meeting was dedicated to planning for the upcoming November 2012 election.
  • A major focus was to formally approve the specific general obligation bonds that would be presented to voters, which are critical for funding future city projects.
  • Discussions also involved legal counsel and city staff to ensure all election and bond-related matters were handled appropriately and legally.
