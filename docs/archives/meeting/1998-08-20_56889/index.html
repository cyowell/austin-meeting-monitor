Protecting Water, Downtown Growth, Curfew

Thursday, August 20, 1998 regular

  • Major Land Acquisition for Water Protection:

    The city approved the purchase of over 1,700 acres of land to protect the water supply, following a recent bond election.

  • Juvenile Curfew Extended with Review:

    The existing juvenile curfew ordinance was continued, with a new requirement for annual review.

  • Downtown Development & Renewal:

    Land was acquired to support the Convention Center's expansion, and modifications to the Brackenridge Urban Renewal Plan were approved.

  • Future Planning & Public Input:

    Public hearings were held on the upcoming city budgets, Smart Growth initiatives, and critical neighborhood and housing issues.
