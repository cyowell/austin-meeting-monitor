Thursday, July 29, 2021 Austin Housing Finance Corporation (AHFC) Board of Directors AHFC Meeting
Official City Record Agenda Watch Video Transcript
  • Austin is launching a COVID-19 Mortgage Assistance Program to help homeowners affected by the pandemic.

  • $500,000

    in aid is available, aiming to support approximately 50-65 income-eligible households with up to six months of mortgage payments.
  • Applications open Monday, August 2nd, 2021, and will be processed on a first-come, first-served basis.
  • To apply, visit austintexas.gov/atxma. Eligibility requires households to be at or below 80% of the median family income and financially impacted by COVID-19.
