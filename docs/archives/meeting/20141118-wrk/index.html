Austin Tackles Floods, Properties, Pets

Tuesday, November 18, 2014 Austin City Council Work Session

  • Animal Shelter & Park Future

    Debated extending Austin Pets Alive!'s lease at the Town Lake site, weighing its no-kill mission against future park development and the city's animal shelter capacity needs.

  • Tackling Problem Properties

    Proposed broadening the "repeat offender" program to proactively address more minor code violations, aiming to improve neighborhood quality of life across the city.

  • Flood Control & Development

    Explored new interim development regulations to mitigate severe flooding in rapidly growing areas, while celebrating a federal funding win for the Onion Creek flood control project.

  • Land Use & State Politics

    Outlined plans to integrate expert guidance into ongoing land development code updates and strategized for a state legislative session expected to challenge local control on property taxes and transportation.
