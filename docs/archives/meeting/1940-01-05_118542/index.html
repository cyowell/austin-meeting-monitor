Debating Austin's New Bridge and Traffic Route

Friday, January 5, 1940 special called
  • A major public hearing addressed plans for a new Colorado River bridge and a boulevard along Shoal Creek, proposed to alleviate traffic congestion near the State University and prevent a state-planned bypass around Austin.
  • Numerous residents and property owners voiced strong opposition to the Shoal Creek boulevard, citing concerns about property damage, harm to Pease Park, increased traffic hazards, and suggesting alternative routes.
  • Proponents, including business leaders, argued this route was a necessary compromise to secure state funding for the vital new bridge and avoid a potentially damaging bypass that could hurt local commerce.
  • Many speakers, supported by the Mayor, emphasized preserving Shoal Creek Boulevard's residential and scenic character, with calls for a city charter amendment to prohibit commercial development along the route.
