Austin Action: Court, Safety, Fines, Settlements

Thursday, February 17, 2022 Austin City Council Regular Meeting

  • Downtown Court Location Delayed:

    A decision on the Downtown Austin Community Court's new home is postponed after extensive public input and debate over safety and neighborhood impact.

  • Prioritizing Public Safety Staffing:

    The City will develop a comprehensive plan to fill vacancies in Fire, EMS, and Police, addressing staffing shortages and aiming to improve response times.

  • Libraries Go Fine-Free:

    Austin Public Libraries will eliminate overdue fines and fees, a move intended to increase access to resources for all residents.

  • Marshal’s Office Plan Scrapped:

    A proposal to create a city Marshal’s Office is indefinitely postponed, with officials citing it wouldn't reduce the need for police officers as initially hoped.

  • Historic Protest Settlements:

    Over $10 million is approved in legal settlements for individuals injured during the May 2020 protests.
