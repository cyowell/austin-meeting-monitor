Shaping Austin: Budget, Taxes, Parks, Holly Funds

Monday, September 9, 2002 Austin City Council Regular Meeting

Here's a summary of key decisions from the September 9, 2002 meeting:

  • City Budget and Tax Rate Approved:

    The annual operating budget for the upcoming fiscal year was adopted, fixing the property tax rate at $0.4597 per $100 valuation and setting various city fees and charges.

  • Major Investments in Parks and Neighborhoods:

    Significant funds were allocated for community projects, including $1.5 million for Holly Power Plant mitigation efforts (such as the Metz Recreation Center expansion and a new police walking beat on East Cesar Chavez), and $4 million towards a new Colony Park Recreation Center.

  • Support for Community Programs:

    Funding was secured for vital programs, including restoring a youth employment specialist position, expanding energy conservation initiatives, and approving grants for animal welfare, economic development, and public health education.

  • Boost for Tourism:

    The Austin Convention and Visitors Bureau's $5.6 million marketing plan was approved, aimed at promoting tourism and strengthening the local economy.
