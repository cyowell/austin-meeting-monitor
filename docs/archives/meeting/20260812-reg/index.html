Wednesday, August 12, 2026 Regular Meeting

  • Approved the City Budget and Utility Rate Increases:

    The Council officially adopted the Fiscal Year 2026-2027 budget, setting the property tax rate and approving rate increases for city utilities like Austin Energy, Austin Water, Austin Resource Recovery (trash/recycling), and Watershed Protection (drainage). Many residents spoke during public hearings to oppose these utility rate hikes, citing affordability concerns.

  • Addressed Social Services Funding and Oversight:

    After significant public input regarding cuts, the budget was approved with amendments impacting social services. The Council also passed resolutions to develop a process for reviewing social service contract performance and to evaluate specific programs proposed for reductions or elimination in the budget.

  • Moved Towards Independent 911 Communications:

    In a notable decision, the Council approved a resolution to establish an Office of Joint Emergency Communications, with the clear directive to work towards creating an independent 911 department separate from the Austin Police Department. This aims to better match emergency responses to the nature of the crisis.

  • Increased Police Transparency and Accountability:

    The Council passed resolutions directing the City Manager to review key performance measures for the Austin Police Department. They also called for a summary list of police misconduct complaints (known as the "G-file") for Council review and an assessment of creating a publicly accessible online database for such allegations.

  • Supported Housing Programs and Microbusinesses:

    Over $90 million was approved for various housing programs to be managed by the Austin Housing Finance Corporation, aimed at supporting housing affordability. Additionally, a resolution passed to propose City Code changes to define "microbusinesses," reduce their permitting and licensing requirements, and develop a program to waive City fees for qualifying small enterprises.
