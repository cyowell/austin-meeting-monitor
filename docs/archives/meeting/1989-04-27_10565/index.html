Austin: New Airport, AIDS Fight, Green Rules

Thursday, April 27, 1989 regular

  • New Airport Planning

    Initial steps for property acquisition and relocation assistance services for Austin's new airport were authorized.

  • Public Health Priorities

    The establishment of an Austin-Travis County AIDS Commission was endorsed to address the ongoing health crisis.

  • Environmental & Land Use Regulations

    New ordinances were passed to regulate mobile incinerators, and the Land Development Code was amended regarding impervious cover limits in residential zones.

  • Social Policy Debate

    A proposed ordinance concerning city business ties to South Africa and Namibia, a topic of citizen concern, was postponed for further consideration.
