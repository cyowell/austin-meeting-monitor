Austin's Growth: Paving, Gas Stations, Zoning

Thursday, January 22, 1931 regular

  • Urban Development & Zoning Decisions:

    The Council addressed multiple requests for gasoline filling stations, denying one at 16th and Red River Streets after public protest and a lack of council support. Conversely, a permit for a station at Annie and South First Streets was approved, but with extensive safety, construction, and environmental conditions, including waste oil disposal requirements.

  • Infrastructure Funding & Public Assessments:

    Completed street paving projects on Rio Grande Street and West Fifth Street were accepted, triggering payments to contractors and assessments for abutting property owners. A public hearing confirmed assessments for 29th Street improvements, and cost estimates for future paving on East Avenue were approved, setting up further property assessments.

  • Utility Expansion & Regulation:

    Texas Public Service Company received permission to install new gas mains on West 42nd Street and Georgetown Road. The city specified construction guidelines, including minimum depths, and held the company liable for any damage to existing underground utilities.

  • City Financial Management & Regulatory Development:

    Funds were reallocated to support sanitary sewer projects, and money was appropriated for stenographic services, including the drafting of a new city building code, signaling ongoing administrative and regulatory efforts.
