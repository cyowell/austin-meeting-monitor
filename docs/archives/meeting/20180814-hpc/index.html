ATX Housing Vision: Motels, Goals, & Permits

Tuesday, August 14, 2018 Housing and Planning Committee Regular Meeting

  • Motel Resident Protection:

    City officials heard a plea to extend tenant relocation assistance to long-term motel residents, highlighting a policy gap impacting vulnerable populations facing displacement.

  • Ambitious Housing Plan:

    Discussions centered on implementing Austin's Strategic Housing Blueprint, outlining a 10-year goal to create 60,000 new affordable homes using new "opportunity" and "displacement risk" area maps.

  • Streamlining Development:

    The plan aims to improve the permitting process for affordable housing projects, with a new ombudsman position proposed to help navigate development roadblocks.

  • Strategic Growth Goals:

    Specific affordable housing production and preservation targets were set for each council district and major transit corridors, guided by factors like transit access and existing opportunity.
