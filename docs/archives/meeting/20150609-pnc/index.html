  • Accessory Dwelling Units (ADUs):

  • Downtown Construction Noise:

    Discussed new decibel limits for late-night concrete pouring, balancing resident noise complaints with construction industry concerns over operational feasibility and costs.

    Reviewed implementing "no-step" entrance and exterior path rules for new homes. Advocates pushed for timely implementation for disability access, while builders sought delays for clarity and to address cost impacts.
