Austin Deals: War, Zoning, Alcohol Ban

Thursday, September 10, 1942 regular

  • Supporting the War Effort:

    The city contributed to the WWII scrap drive by removing old streetcar rails for salvage and approved a contract to provide electricity to the new Del Valle Air Support Command Base.

  • Major Zoning Changes:

    Large tracts along Lamar Boulevard were rezoned from residential to commercial, signaling future business development in that area.

  • Unusual Land Sale:

    The city sold a piece of property with a unique and strict condition: no alcoholic beverages could ever be sold there, or the land would revert to the city.

  • Right-of-Way Declined:

    An offer for a "free" railroad right-of-way was turned down because it came with too many restrictive usage clauses.
