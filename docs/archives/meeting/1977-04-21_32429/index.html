Austin Secures Coal for Fayette Power Plant II

Thursday, April 21, 1977 special called

  • Major Coal Contract Approved:

    Austin and the LCRA jointly approved a 15-year contract with Atlantic-Richfield (ARCO) to supply coal for the Fayette Power Project Unit II, sourced from Wyoming.

  • Favorable Terms:

    The ARCO agreement was selected for its competitive pricing, low sulfur content ensuring environmental compliance, and a flexible delivery schedule.

  • Variable Pricing & Logistics:

    While initially priced at $6.15 per ton, the coal cost is subject to escalation based on economic conditions, labor changes, and taxes; transportation will rely on new dedicated rail cars.

  • Future Energy Exploration:

    Officials confirmed that despite the coal agreement, the exploration and potential utilization of lignite as a future power source would continue.
