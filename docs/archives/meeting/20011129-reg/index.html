Austin's Future: Growth, Environment, Power

Thursday, November 29, 2001 Austin City Council Regular Meeting

  • Major Growth & Neighborhood Planning:

    Approved the limited annexation of large tracts, including Avery Ranch and Wildhorse Ranch, for future development. The council also adopted comprehensive plans for established communities like Hyde Park and Rosewood.

  • Environmental & Utility Actions:

    Advanced critical flood control projects and acquired land for the Balcones Canyonlands Preserve. Significant bonds were approved for water and wastewater systems, and efforts were directed to encourage light-colored roofs to reduce urban heat.

  • Citizen Concerns & Key Policy Changes:

    Multiple citizens voiced strong calls for the closure of the Holly Street Power Plant. The council also adopted the 2000 International Energy Conservation Code and new rules for creating neighborhood planning sub-districts.
