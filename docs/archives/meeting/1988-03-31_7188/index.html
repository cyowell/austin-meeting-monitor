Austin's Big Moves: Utilities, Safety, Planning

Thursday, March 31, 1988 regular
  • Approved a $53 million Interim Electric Transmission Plan, reallocating funds from prior projects to modernize power delivery, and initiated land acquisition for Loop 360 electric lines.
  • Enacted new laws and adopted a city policy to regulate the safe transportation of hazardous materials, significantly boosting public safety.
  • Reinstated a Police Cadet Class by re-appropriating funds from the general budget, strengthening the city's police force.
  • Advanced key city planning efforts by moving forward the Interim Land Development Code and the Town Lake Comprehensive Plan after public hearings, and established an Endangered Species Task Force.
