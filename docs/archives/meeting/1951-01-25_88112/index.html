Austin Funds Parks, Libraries, Segregation

Thursday, January 25, 1951 regular

  • Segregated Recreation Services:

    The city council approved a budget specifically for "Colored Activities," providing distinct recreational programs and facilities, reflecting the era's racial segregation policies.

  • Expanded Public Leisure & Community Programs:

    Significant funding was allocated to a variety of community centers (including Pan-American and Northside), athletic programs, swimming pools, and special events, showcasing a commitment to public recreation.

  • Increased Support for Libraries & Culture:

    Budgets grew for both the Main and Branch Libraries, including resources for a "Bookmobile." The council also approved funding for the Austin Symphony Orchestra and historical museums like the Elizabet Ney and O'Henry.

  • City Infrastructure Investment:

    Overall city spending saw an increase, with dedicated capital outlay approved for improvements to recreation grounds, buildings, and equipment, indicating a period of growth and development.
