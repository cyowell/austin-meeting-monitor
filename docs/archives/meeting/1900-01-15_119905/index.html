Monday, January 15, 1900 regular
Transcript

• Water Infrastructure Investment: The city approved purchasing $3,800 worth of water and light bonds for its sinking fund to manage existing debt, with an additional $985 allocated for interest and broker fees.

• Newspaper Contract Awarded: The Tribune Publishing Company won the contract to handle all city printing and publications for 1900, including official council meeting minutes.

• Property Tax Appeals Process: Residents were granted the right to appeal property assessment changes made by the Board of Equalization, with a required 10-day public notice period in local newspapers.

• Hog Ordinance Under Review: Council moved forward with amending a previous ordinance that made keeping hogs within city limits a misdemeanor, sending the proposal to committee for further consideration.
