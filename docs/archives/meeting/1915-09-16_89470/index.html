Austin Paving, Flood Hero, Gas Pumps

Thursday, September 16, 1915 regular

  • Paving & Property Assessments:

    The city approved plans to pave an alley between Congress and Brazos Streets (7th to 8th Sts) with concrete. A public hearing is set for September 30th to discuss assessing property owners $1.70 per front foot for the cost.

  • Honoring a Flood Hero:

    Funds were allocated for the burial of Thos. Quinn, a firefighter who died while rescuing others during the severe flood of April 22, 1915.

  • Early Gasoline Station:

    A permit was issued for an underground gasoline tank and a curb-side pump on Congress Avenue (417-419), including specific regulations for its lighting and hose storage.

  • Jubilee Street Lights:

    Downtown merchants received permission to install festive colored lights on East Sixth Street for an upcoming "Jubilee occasion" on September 20th.
