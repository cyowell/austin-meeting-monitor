Thursday, July 24, 1913 regular
Transcript

  • New Street Paving Projects & Public Hearings:

    Approved plans to pave Red River, Trinity, and Neches streets. Property owners will be assessed for a portion of the cost, with a public hearing scheduled for August 7, 1913, where residents can discuss the proposed assessments and benefits.

  • Individual Property Tax Adjustments:

    The council corrected a 1912 property assessment for Jas H Raymond Jr and fully canceled a $2,000 credit assessment for Mrs. Lena Castleman, citing that her debts exceeded her receivables.

  • Temporary Sidewalk Use Permit:

    Granted Tom Louis a temporary and revocable permit to install and operate a wagon scale on a sidewalk along Neches Street.
