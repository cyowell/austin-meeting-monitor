Austin: Sewer Purchase, Street Plans, Tax Fix

Thursday, January 23, 1913 regular

  • Major Infrastructure Funding:

    Approved substantial fund transfers, including $30,000 for sanitary sewer projects and $12,500 for permanent street improvements.

  • Street Development Standards:

    Established the official permanent grades and elevations for 21st Street, an important step for future urban planning and construction.

  • Citizen Tax Relief:

    Corrected an individual tax issue by canceling an illegally assessed 1912 tax credit for Mrs. Alice L. Waldon.

  • Public Utility Acquisition:

    Received the results of a recent public election, confirming the city's decision to purchase the Austin Sewerage Company, moving towards public ownership of the city's sewer system.
