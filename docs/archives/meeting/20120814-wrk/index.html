Austin's Ballot Choices: Energy, Growth & Culture

Tuesday, August 14, 2012 Austin City Council Work Session

  • Utility Governance Postponed:

    Proposals for an independent board to oversee Austin Energy and for easier sale/lease of utility assets were withdrawn due to council and community concerns, delaying major changes to the city's power utility for years.

  • Massive Bond Package Debated:

    The council discussed a substantial $385-400 million bond package for the November ballot, earmarking funds for public safety facilities, parks, trails, arts and cultural centers, and other infrastructure projects.

  • Affordable Housing & Health Clinic Debated:

    Affordable housing received strong advocacy for increased funding within the bond. However, including funds for a specific health clinic raised legal questions from staff about state approval for city funding of "medical care," potentially risking the entire bond.

  • Controversial Bridge Funding Reduced:

    Amid public input about environmental impact and community engagement, initial funding for the Emmitt Shelton Bridge replacement design was reduced, with calls for further study and exploration of joint funding options.
