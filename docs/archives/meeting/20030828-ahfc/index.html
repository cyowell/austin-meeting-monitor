Austin Housing: Repairs & Mortgage Help

Thursday, August 28, 2003 Austin Housing Finance Corporation (AHFC)

  • $1 Million for Home Repairs:

    Approved a $1,000,000 contract with the Austin Area Urban League to fund an Emergency Home Repair Program, helping residents fix urgent housing issues.

  • Boosting Homeownership:

    Authorized an application for approximately $23,000,000 in bond authority to create a new Single Family Bond or Mortgage Credit Certificate Program, aimed at making homeownership more accessible.

  • Future Housing Budget:

    The Austin Housing Finance Corporation Board reviewed its proposed budget for the upcoming fiscal year, laying the groundwork for future affordable housing initiatives.
