Paws, Homes, & Austin's Expansion

Thursday, December 11, 1997 regular

  • Paws for Progress:

    The city adopted a plan to end the euthanasia of adoptable animals at the Town Lake Animal Center by 2002.

  • Community Investment:

    An additional $1.4 million was approved for low-income rental housing, bringing a project total to over $4.4 million, alongside a $6 million agreement for social services with Travis County.

  • Major City Expansion:

    Several significant annexations were finalized, adding thousands of acres to the city, including large areas near IH-35 South and the Village at Western Oaks, with initial steps taken for the expansive Circle C area.

  • Urban Development Decisions:

    Over $739,000 was allocated for downtown improvements, and a variance was granted to allow a new drugstore to be built within a 100-year floodplain.
