Austin Paving Battles & Airport Growth

Thursday, June 12, 1930 regular

  • Widespread Paving Assessments Approved:

    Despite numerous public protests citing financial hardship, the city approved assessments to fund extensive street paving projects on Guadalupe Street, East Avenue, and Red River Street.

  • Significant Airport Infrastructure Investment:

    $9,500 was allocated to extend essential water and light services to the Robert Mueller Airport, marking a key investment in city development.

  • New Business & Environmental Debates:

    A new gasoline filling station permit was granted under strict safety and operational conditions. Additionally, officials considered public petitions regarding a contested filling station proposal and the relocation of a city dump.

  • Utility Expansion:

    Permits were issued for the installation of new underground telegraph lines and gas mains, indicating ongoing growth in urban infrastructure.
