Monday, January 20, 1908 regular
Transcript

• Street Infrastructure Projects: Council approved nearly $1,400 in spending for street repairs, sidewalk grading at 8th and Colorado streets, extra labor and equipment, and negotiations to repair the Fredericksburg road with county assistance.

• Parks and Public Spaces: Passed resolutions to plant shade trees around Hemphill, Brush, and Wooldridge parks, and allocated $400 for cemetery improvements.

• Fire Department Roof Replacement: Accepted the lowest bid of $280 from contractor Gib Gay to replace the damaged tin roof on Protection Fire Hall.

• Governor Hogg Memorial: Purchased the historic desk used by former Governor James S. Hogg for $50 to display in the new City Hall Mayor's Office as a tribute to the state's first elected governor.

• Dam Franchise Rejected: The Water & Light Commission returned an ordinance granting the Consolidated Construction Company a franchise to build a dam across the Colorado River without approval.
