Austin Funds 14 New Infill Homes

Thursday, October 19, 1995
  • City officials approved an $825,000 contract for the construction of 14 new single-family homes.
  • These homes are part of the "Scattered Cooperative Infill Housing Program (SCIP) 3," aiming to integrate new housing into existing neighborhoods.
  • This decision represents a significant community investment to expand housing options and support infill development.

Present: Absent: MINUTES OF THE AUSTIN HOUSING FINANCE CORPORATION BOARD OF DIRECTOR'S MEETING October 19, 1995 Max Nofziger, Ronney Reynolds, Jackie Goodman, Brigid Shea, Gus Garcia, Eric Mitchell Bruce Todd The meeting was called to order at 3:32 p.m. = Item No. 1 (41) Item No. 2 (42) Approve the minutes of August 17, September 14, and September 28, 1995 meetings. Motion The Board, on Board Member Jackie Goodman's motion and Board Member Brigid Shea's second, approved Item No. 2 (42) with 4-0-0 vote.
