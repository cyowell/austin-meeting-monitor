Austin: Gas Tanks, Alley Sheds, Tin Roofs

Thursday, July 15, 1915 regular

  • Early Auto Infrastructure:

    Approved a permit for an underground gasoline tank and curb pump at 40th & Guadalupe, reflecting the growing presence of automobiles.

  • Urban Development Permits:

    Granted permits for new construction, including a material shack in an alley between the Littlefield and Driskill Hotel buildings, and a tin roof for a building on Congress Avenue.

  • Utility Account Adjustments:

    Authorized the Water, Light, and Power Department to settle accounts with the Consumers Fuel & Ice Co. by accepting approved company accounts against the City.

  • Public Fine Remission:

    Overturned a fine and costs previously assessed against Willie Wolter in the Recorder's Court.

312 .Regular meeting of the City Council : Austin,Texas ,July 15, 1915. The Council was called to order by Councilman Bartholomew, Mayor Pro Tem: Roll called : Present Councilmen Anthony, Bartholomew, Haynes & Powell: Absent Mayor Wooldridge 1 The minutes of July 1st & 8th ,and subsequent recessed meetings were read and approved: L J Williams Gasoline Tank Mr James Belger, who had been appointed a member of the Board of Equalization , was sworn in as such : Councilman Powell offered the following resolution : Be it resolved by the City Council of the City of Austin : That a permit be and is hereby granted to L J Williams of this City to install and maintain in front of his place of business at 40th & Guade lupe streets in this City a gasoline tank ,said tank to be placed under the sidewalk;and Be it further resolved : That said L J Williams is hereby authorized to install and maintain a gasoline pump at the curb line in front of his place of business at said 40th and Guade-lupe streets in this City,the work to be done under this permit must be appro- -ved in advance by the City Engineer of the City of Austin,Texas,; and Be it further and finally resolved : That this permit is temporary and revocable at any time in the pleasure of the City Council . The resolution was adopted by the following vote : Yeas Councilmen Anthony, Bartholomew, Haynes & Powell 4 Absent Mayor Wooldridge 1 Communication A J Zilker Councilman Powell ,to whom was referred the communication of A J Zilker ,in regard to his lot No 5 Block NO 60, in this City, presented a report of the City Engineer ,in the matter, which report was read and the matter referred to the City Engineer for further investigation and report. Willie Wolter fine remitted The Chair laid before the Council a petition from Willie Wolter, asking the remission of a fine assessed against him in the Recorders Court, which was read and on motion the fine & costs were remitted by the following vote: Yeas Councilmen Anthony, Bartholomew, Haynes & Powell 4 Nays none.
