Austin's Oak Hill Development Plans Advance

Wednesday, June 18, 1986 special called
  • A special public hearing was held to discuss the extensive Oak Hill Area Study, focusing on multiple land use and zoning cases.
  • Initial approvals (first readings) advanced for several specific properties within Oak Hill, allowing for diverse zoning changes including General Retail, Rural Residential, Limited Office, and Multi-Family uses.
  • A significant 15-acre portion of the broader Oak Hill map also received initial land use approval, though decisions on two individual zoning cases were postponed for later consideration.
