Austin: New Incinerator, Tax Decisions

Thursday, February 19, 1925 regular

  • City Finances:

    The 1924 tax rolls, valuing city property at over $38 million, received official approval.

  • Public Works:

    The city accepted its newly completed garbage incinerator plant and made a final payment of $13,105 to the contractor.

  • Tax Adjustments:

    A portion of the First Methodist Church property was exempted from taxes due to its use for religious worship.

  • Civic Recognition:

    A special tribute was paid to Frank M. Maddox, a respected former Alderman and Mayor, honoring his long service and leadership.
