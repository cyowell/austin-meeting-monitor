Austin's Growth Spurt: Land, Health, Tech

Thursday, October 24, 1996 regular

  • Major Land Acquisitions & Annexations:

    The city expanded its boundaries through full purpose annexations of over ten areas, and authorized an option to acquire 200 acres of the former State School property for future development.

  • Key Healthcare & Housing Investments:

    Approved a significant $3.075 million settlement related to the Brackenridge Hospital lease and allocated $300,000 for down payment assistance to 100 first-time homebuyers.

  • Modernizing City Infrastructure:

    Contracted for a $1.16 million automatic electric and water meter reading system, and set public hearings to establish rules for new telecommunications providers using city rights-of-way.

  • Community Safety & Planning:

    Implemented a new 20 mph school zone on Salina Street and continued public discussions on the design of the Lamar Bridge Project and overall downtown access.
