Austin Focuses on Flood Relief & Veterans

Thursday, November 7, 2013 Austin City Council Regular Meeting

  • Emergency Flood Response:

    The meeting was significantly streamlined to prioritize city efforts on recovering from recent widespread flooding, leading to the postponement of numerous agenda items. Emergency measures included waiving reconstruction permit fees for flood victims and ratifying a mayoral proclamation declaring a local state of disaster.

  • Honoring Veterans:

    A street in Southeast Austin leading to a new veterans clinic was officially renamed "Heroes Way." The city also established a Veterans Commission to address critical issues like employment, education, and support for women and homeless veterans, alongside formally proclaiming November 11 as Veterans Day.

  • Utility Bill Assistance Debated:

    Discussions touched on Austin Energy's payment arrangement policies for customers in need, with public input urging for temporary assistance and long-term policy adjustments, especially relevant given the recent flood impacts.

  • Park Parking Meter Proposal:

    A plan to install parking meters at the Butler Shores Park lot generated public debate, with concerns raised about the impact on park users and the financial rationale behind the proposed meters.
