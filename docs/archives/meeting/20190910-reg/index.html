Austin's Budget: Mental Health, Homes, Pay

Tuesday, September 10, 2019 Austin City Council Regular Meeting

Here's a summary of the Austin City Council meeting:

  • Enhanced Mental Health Response:

    Increased funding for mental health first responders, 911 call center reforms, and community health paramedics to provide a healthcare-focused approach.

  • Historic Homelessness & Housing Investment:

    Allocated a record $62.7 million for homelessness initiatives, including new "workforce first" encampment cleanup crews and substantial housing trust fund contributions.

  • City Employee Compensation:

    Approved a 2.5% cost-of-living adjustment for all city employees, with a commitment to competitive wages in future budgets. Discussions also covered fair pay for lifeguards and benefits for long-term temporary staff.

  • Boost for Arts & Preservation:

    Dedicated an additional $3.1 million each to cultural arts and historic preservation, funded by new hotel occupancy tax revenue.
