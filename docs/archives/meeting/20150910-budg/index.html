City Budget & Taxes: Police, Fire, Utilities, Housing

Thursday, September 10, 2015 Austin City Council Regular Meeting (Budget Reading)

  • Finalizing the City's Annual Budget & Taxes:

    The council will approve the operating and capital budgets for the upcoming fiscal year, set city fees, and ratify a property tax increase, with a public hearing scheduled on the proposed tax rate.

  • Boosting Public Safety Staffing:

    Key decisions will establish new positions and pay rates for Austin's Fire, Police, and Emergency Medical Services (EMS) departments.

  • Major Investments Across the City:

    The city plans to declare intent to finance significant projects, including transportation, park upgrades, affordable housing, library expansions, airport improvements, and upgrades to water and electric utility infrastructure.

  • Key Housing and Development Discussions:

    Separate meetings will address affordable housing initiatives via the Austin Housing Finance Corporation and ongoing development at Mueller.
