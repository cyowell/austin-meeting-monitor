  • New Minimum Traffic Fines Approved:

    The city council established a comprehensive schedule of minimum traffic fines, set to go into effect on July 15, 1955.

  • Varied Penalties for Drivers:

    Fines ranged from $1 for overtime parking (if paid promptly) to $5 for running a red light or stop sign, and up to $200 for a sixth offense of driving without an operator's license.
