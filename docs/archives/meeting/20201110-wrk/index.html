Austin COVID Surge Warning, Homeless Lodges

Tuesday, November 10, 2020 Austin City Council Work Session

  • COVID-19 Surge Alert Issued:

    Health officials warned of a severe surge in COVID-19 cases and hospitalizations, projecting Austin could reach Stage 4 alert level around Thanksgiving. This increase is attributed to reduced public vigilance and social gatherings.

  • Vaccine Planning Underway:

    Initial plans for COVID-19 vaccine distribution are being developed, targeting priority populations, with a potential first phase in December. The city continues offering free testing, PPE, and focused outreach to disproportionately affected communities.

  • Homeless Protective Lodges Closing:

    Despite the ongoing pandemic, the city plans to start closing some protective lodges for people experiencing homelessness in December due to expiring federal funds. The strategy emphasizes transitioning residents to permanent housing, influencing future hotel/motel conversions.
