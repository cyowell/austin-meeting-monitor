Austin's Future: Tech Growth, Healthcare, Environment

Thursday, March 17, 1994 regular

  • Green Light for Apple Campus Expansion:

    Austin approved a major development deal for the new Apple Computer campus, including unique terms on annexation, tax payments, and integrating Green Builder Program elements.

  • Future of Healthcare & Key Infrastructure:

    The city began a strategic review of Brackenridge Hospital and the wider healthcare system. Significant electric utility projects were advanced, like the Garfield Substation and an air quality agreement for the Holly Power Plant, plus funding for historic moonlight tower restoration.

  • Environmental & Development Debates:

    Public input included calls for a development moratorium due to salamander habitat concerns. A public hearing was also set to amend zoning for telecommunication towers.

  • Upcoming Elections & Community Initiatives:

    Austin ordered municipal elections for Mayor and three Council Members in May, along with proposed City Charter amendments. The Council also directed the formation of an Asian-American Employee Network.
