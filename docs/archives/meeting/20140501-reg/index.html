Thursday, May 1, 2014 Austin City Council Regular Meeting
Official City Record Agenda Watch Video Transcript

Here's a concise summary of the Austin City Council meeting:

  • Southwest Key Loan Forgiveness:

    Debated a request to forgive a $512,000 loan for nonprofit Southwest Key's expansion. Concerns were raised about the criteria for converting loans into grants and setting precedents for other city loans, leading to a postponement for more detailed documentation.

  • Seaholm Intake Redevelopment:

    Received an update on plans to transform the historic Seaholm Intake facility into a public destination through a public/private partnership, involving nonprofit organizations and a 20-year revocable license agreement.

  • Citywide Translation Services Policy:

    Briefed on a new policy aimed at improving language access for residents, proposing local translator certification, a list of prequalified vendors, an online translation database, and ensuring emergency notifications are available in multiple languages, starting with Spanish.

  • Austin Energy Cost Recovery:

    Discussed a new plan to recover costs for underground utility lines. The item was postponed due to concerns from stakeholders (including the Chamber of Commerce) about the proposed immediate, full cost recovery versus a phased implementation.

  • South Lamar Development Impacts:

    Agreed to use the rapidly developing South Lamar corridor as a study area to address mitigation strategies for infrastructure strain, traffic congestion, and other community impacts arising from dense urban growth.
