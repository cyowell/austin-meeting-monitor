Thursday, September 5, 1929 regular
Transcript

  • Infrastructure Growth:

    The city approved a major new sanitary sewer system, extending to Deep Eddy, and authorized significant new gas main construction across various streets.

  • Public Safety & Planning:

    East Ninth Street was closed to vehicular traffic to create a safe playground for school children, and a permit for a new gas station was denied at a busy intersection due to traffic hazards.

  • Regulatory Updates:

    New ordinances were passed to establish voting ward boundaries and regulate traffic flow on Colorado Street.

  • Future Safety Initiatives:

    The city began developing new ordinances to address overcrowding in theaters and prohibit smoking at gasoline filling stations, emphasizing public safety.
