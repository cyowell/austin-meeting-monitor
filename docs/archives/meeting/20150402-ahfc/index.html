Austin's Affordable Housing Funding Boost

Thursday, April 2, 2015 Austin Housing Finance Corporation (AHFC) Board of Directors AHFC Meeting
  • Approved plans to create or rehabilitate over 700 affordable housing units for families, individuals with disabilities, and homeless residents.
  • Debated and ultimately approved additional funding for three affordable housing projects due to significant construction cost overruns, which increased the city's previously committed contributions.
  • Discussed the nature of these "loans," noting they often feature 0% interest, deferred payments, and potential forgiveness, raising questions about them effectively being grants.
  • Authorized applying for state bonds to finance two more affordable housing initiatives, one for preserving existing units and another for new construction.
