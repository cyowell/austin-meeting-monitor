Thursday, September 6, 2018 Austin City Council Special Called Meeting
Official City Record Agenda Watch Video Transcript

  • Property Tax Relief Debated:

    The council discussed potentially increasing the property tax rate to fund city priorities, with a strong focus on boosting the senior homestead exemption to help older homeowners.

  • Mental Health Crisis Response:

    Members considered restoring funding for the Expanded Mobile Crisis Outreach Team (EMCOT) after losing state waivers, and proposed a $75,000 study to improve how the city responds to mental health incidents.

  • Increased Homelessness Funding:

    There was consensus to allocate an additional $1.9 million for homelessness services, with specific areas like rapid rehousing, outreach, eviction prevention, and support for the ARCH facility being prioritized.
