Austin: New Streets, Bonds, & Youth Justice

Thursday, May 2, 1912 regular

Here's a summary of the Austin City Council agenda from May 2, 1912:

  • Voters approved significant bond issues totaling over $750,000 for city-wide improvements

    , funding new sewers, street paving, school construction, a city hospital, and cemetery expansion.

  • Public hearings were scheduled for property owners regarding assessments for upcoming street paving projects

    on Colorado and West 7th Streets, which will utilize modern Bitulithic pavement.

  • Council remitted a $37.50 fine for a 17-year-old convicted of a misdemeanor

    , citing his youth and potential for reform.

Hon Mayor and City Council, City of Austin,Texas. Gentlemen : When the new grade was established for the Bituluthic pavement on Brazos street between Sixth & 7th streets, it left the present sidewalk in front of Washington Fire Hall entrance on an average of nine and a half inches above the gutter,making it difficult to back in the fire truck. In order that this may be remedied ,I would recommend that the present side -walk be cut out and put on new grade of the street ,and that a runway be b built on a gradual incline from the building out to the street, on a grade Entrance to Washington Fire Hall Remodled which will enable the fire truck to be backed in without difficulty. This w will necessitate extending the runway out into the street eight or ten feet,and in order that the surface of the street may be of uniform material I would recommend that it be made of Bitulithic material.On account of the doorway being level,and the street on a grade, it will be necesssary to break the grade of the sidewalk in two places and otherwise warp it so as to meet the two differing grades. While this will not present the best of appearance ,it is probably the best that can be done with this situation. The work will cost in the neighborhood of $ 70.00.
