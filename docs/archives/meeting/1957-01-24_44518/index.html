Austin's Commercial Shift & Infrastructure Push

Thursday, January 24, 1957 regular

  • Major Zoning Change:

    Residential lots in the Patterson Addition were rezoned for commercial use, a significant policy decision for future development.

  • New Gas Station Approved:

    A drive-in gasoline filling station was greenlit for East 12th Street and Chicon, subject to strict construction, drainage, and safety conditions.

  • Extensive Public Works Planned:

    The city is moving forward with several large paving projects, including Exposition Boulevard, Riverside Drive, Oltorf, Lake Austin Boulevard, and Koenig Lane, alongside new sewer extensions in South Austin.

  • Key Equipment & Supplies Secured:

    Contracts were awarded for two new motor graders essential for street maintenance and for the supply of lime used in city water filter plants.
