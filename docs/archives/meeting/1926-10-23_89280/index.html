Austin OKs Gas Stations, Updates Milk Rules

Saturday, October 23, 1926 special called

Here's a summary of the October 23, 1926 Austin City Council meeting:

  • Milk Safety Rules Under Review:

    The council is actively working to update its milk ordinance, instructing the City Attorney to prepare a redraft with new amendments for an upcoming vote, indicating a focus on public health standards.

  • Two New Gas Stations Approved:

    Permits were granted for two new gasoline filling stations—one on West 34th Street and another at Concordia and East Avenues—signaling the city's adaptation to the rise of automobiles.

  • Strict Safety & Traffic Conditions for Gas Stations:

    Both new stations must adhere to extensive regulations covering fire prevention (e.g., "No Smoking" signs, chemical extinguishers, approved equipment), safe electrical wiring, proper traffic flow to prevent sidewalk or street obstruction, and the city retained the right to revoke permits if conditions were not met.
