Austin's Power Play: Solar, Data Centers, Relief

Tuesday, October 7, 2025 Austin Energy Utility Oversight Committee Regular Meeting

  • New Solar Leasing Program:

    Austin Energy is fast-tracking a new residential solar leasing program to help homeowners get solar despite the upcoming end of federal tax credits, after a major federal grant was unexpectedly canceled.

  • Managing Big Energy Demands:

    The region faces a massive surge in electricity demand from large customers like data centers. While challenging the grid, these businesses could potentially help lower utility bills for all customers.

  • Record Customer Assistance:

    Austin Energy has successfully expanded financial assistance, now helping over 82,000 low-income households save $38 million annually, alongside record-breaking home weatherization efforts.
