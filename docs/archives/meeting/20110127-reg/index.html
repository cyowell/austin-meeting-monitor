Rethinking Austin's Traffic, Data, & Development

Thursday, January 27, 2011 Austin City Council Regular Meeting

  • Urban Revitalization Funds:

    Approved pursuing federal grants for Saltillo Plaza redevelopment, aiming to create hundreds of jobs and attract significant private investment in East Austin.

  • Regional Gang Database Expansion:

    Authorized new software for a shared gang intelligence database across local law enforcement, sparking public and council debate over civil liberties and privacy.

  • Major Traffic Intersection Upgrade:

    Greenlit advance funding for significant I-35 and 51st Street intersection improvements, with a council mandate for extensive public input on design.

  • Fire Department Leadership:

    Created an additional Battalion Chief role, prompted by an arbitration case, also highlighting a shift to assessment-based hiring for key leadership positions.

  • City Board Efficiency Review:

    Ordered a cost analysis of the city's volunteer boards and commissions, aiming for efficiency while acknowledging their essential role and expert contributions (e.g., "no-kill" animal policy).
