Austin Power Plant Gas Deal Heats Up

Monday, March 31, 1958 special called
  • Austin officials are in tense negotiations with their current gas providers, Southern Union and United Gas Pipe Line, to secure a long-term fuel contract for the city's power plant.
  • A major sticking point was the gas companies' inability to guarantee future prices, with one executive confessing a prior price estimate was drastically incorrect. They also highlighted the massive $150 million investment needed for future gas infrastructure and warned against switching to unproven suppliers.
  • To address concerns, Southern Union offered to cap its profit margin at 1.4 cents per thousand cubic feet, rather than a percentage. United proposed a 20-year supply contract with a flexible clause allowing Austin to review and potentially reject new gas prices every five years, giving the city options.
  • No final decision was reached, with an advisory committee tasked to continue deliberations the following day, underscoring the critical nature of securing a stable and affordable energy future for Austin.
