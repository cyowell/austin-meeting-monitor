Austin Addresses Affordability, Boosts Engagement

Thursday, January 29, 2015 Austin City Council Regular Meeting

  • Regional Affordability Committee Created:

    A new committee was launched to collaborate with regional entities (like school districts and the county) to tackle Austin's rising cost of living, addressing issues from housing to childcare.

  • Community Engagement Task Force Formed:

    A task force was established to enhance public participation, improve two-way communication between residents and the city, and ensure all community segments are heard.

  • Council Office Budget Flexibility Approved:

    The Mayor and Council members gained more control to reallocate funds within their office budgets, including portions of their salaries, to support their districts more effectively.

  • Calls for Transparency in City Contracts & Ethics:

    Discussions highlighted concerns over the transparency of large procurement contracts and affirmed the process for handling city employee ethics complaints within the civil service system.
