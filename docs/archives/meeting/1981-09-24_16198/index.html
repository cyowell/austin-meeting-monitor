Austin Budget: Fees, Raises, New Transit

Thursday, September 24, 1981 regular

Here's a summary of the Austin City Council meeting from September 24, 1981:

  • New City Budget Adopted with Widespread Fee Hikes:

    The 1981-82 operating budget was approved, introducing or increasing fees across many city services, including waste collection, permits, park facility rentals, and water/wastewater utility connections.

  • Major Infrastructure Investments and Utility Finance:

    Significant Capital Improvement Projects for water, wastewater, and electric utilities were funded to address critical needs. The Electric Utility Fund also transferred nearly $30 million to the city's General Fund.

  • Employee Compensation, Benefits & Elder Tax Relief:

    City employees received an 8% general wage increase, along with expanded life and retiree medical benefits. The Council also approved a $15,000 homestead exemption for elderly residents.

  • Planning for Future Transit and Energy:

    A citizen task force was created to explore the feasibility of a Metropolitan Transit Authority, and an Energy Management Plan was adopted in concept to guide future sustainability efforts.
