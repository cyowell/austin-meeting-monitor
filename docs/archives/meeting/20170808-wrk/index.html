Austin's Future: Manager, Pools, Homeless Aid

Tuesday, August 8, 2017 Austin City Council Work Session

  • Next City Manager Search:

    The task force presented a profile for Austin's next city manager and proposed remaining involved in candidate interviews, aiming to select a new leader by year-end.

  • Aquatics Master Plan Debate:

    A vote on the city's Aquatics Master Plan was postponed to allow a new task force to review proposals, gather more public input, and explore options for keeping neighborhood pools open.

  • Downtown Homelessness Response:

    An initiative was introduced to address safety concerns near the ARCH shelter, proposing measures like increased police presence and finding city facilities for a temporary emergency shelter.

  • Austin Energy Plan Public Input:

    A public hearing was held for the Austin Energy Generation Plan, inviting community feedback on the city's future energy strategy, with a final decision deferred to the following week.
