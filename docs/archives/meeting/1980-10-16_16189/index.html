Thursday, October 16, 1980 regular
Transcript

  • Nuclear Plant Delays and Costs:

    Austin's $236 million share of the South Texas Project (a nuclear power plant) faced construction delays and federal regulatory issues, highlighting ongoing challenges for the major power source.

  • Major Transportation Plan Updates:

    The city adopted a new Bikeway Plan and a revised Metropolitan Roadway Plan, scrapping the controversial Barton Skyway Bridge and an extension of Mesa Drive after extensive public input.

  • Key Energy Policy Decisions:

    A proposal for an "electric lifeline rate" (aimed at utility cost relief) failed to pass, while a task force was established to develop a comprehensive city-wide energy management plan.

  • Community Development & Growth Challenges:

    Federal funds for housing rehabilitation were frozen due to reconciliation issues with HUD, and a decision on new development fees (facing public concern over potential impacts on home prices) was postponed.
