Austin Housing: Senior Homes & New Leaders

Thursday, June 22, 2000 Austin Housing Finance Corporation (AHFC)
  • The Austin Housing Finance Corporation appointed new leadership, including Mayor Kirk Watson as its President.
  • City-owned land was approved for transfer, free of charge, to build affordable housing for seniors near Tillery and Oak Springs as part of the City's SMART Housing Program.
