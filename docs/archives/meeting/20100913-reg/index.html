Austin Budget Fight: Taxes, Water & Parks

Monday, September 13, 2010 Austin City Council Budget Adoption Reading

  • Public Outcry Against Tax & Water Hikes:

    Many residents spoke against proposed property tax and water rate increases, citing economic hardship, utility mismanagement, and inadequate city services like sewage and garbage collection.

  • "Trail of Lights" Cut to Fund Parks:

    A major budget decision eliminated funding for the popular "Trail of Lights" holiday event, reallocating over $374,000 to general city park maintenance and unmet needs.

  • Solid Waste Fees Frozen, Demolition Fees Raised:

    The monthly 60-gallon garbage cart fee was held at $10, avoiding an increase, while permit fees for demolishing older or historic buildings were significantly increased to cover specialized review costs.

  • Holly Power Plant Mitigation Funds Spark Debate:

    A contentious discussion arose over the allocation of funds meant for residents impacted by the former Holly Power Plant, with criticism over specific grants (including one to a church) versus direct home repair for seniors.
