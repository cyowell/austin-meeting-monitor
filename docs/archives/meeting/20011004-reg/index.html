Shaping Austin: Land, Airport, Local Services

Thursday, October 4, 2001 Austin City Council Regular Meeting

Here's a concise summary of the Austin City Council meeting on October 4, 2001:

  • Robert Mueller Airport's New Chapter:

    The City approved dismantling the former airport's terminal building, a key step toward the site's future redevelopment.

  • Austin's Expanding Footprint:

    Public hearings closed on multiple significant annexations across the city, preparing for new residential and commercial areas to join Austin's jurisdiction.

  • Shaping Neighborhoods & Governance:

    The Central East Austin Neighborhood Plan was advanced to guide future development, and a committee was directed to explore options for single-member districts in city elections.

  • Investing in Public Assets:

    The City greenlit major capital projects for water, wastewater, and electric utilities, including a large long-term coal supply. It also acquired significant land for new greenways and parks, and funded essential homeless services.
