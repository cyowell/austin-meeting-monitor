Austin: Electric Heat Price, Dog Catcher & City Truck

Thursday, May 31, 1917 regular
  • A new rate of three cents per kilowatt was set for electric current used for heating purposes, effective June 1917, impacting utility costs for residents.
  • Lon King was appointed as the city's Dog Catcher for the upcoming season, starting June 1st.
  • The Parks and Public Property Department was authorized to purchase a new Indiana Truck for $1,465.00 to support city operations.
  • A speeding fine of $19.80 against Thomas Garrett was remitted (canceled) by the City Council.
