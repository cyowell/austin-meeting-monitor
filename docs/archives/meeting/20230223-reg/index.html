Austin Tackles I-35, Housing, and Lawsuits

Thursday, February 23, 2023 Austin City Council Regular Meeting

  • Significant Legal Settlements:

    The city approved over $4.8 million in payouts for various lawsuits, including a 2017 officer-involved shooting death and several injury claims stemming from the 2020 protests.

  • I-35 Expansion Sparks Outcry:

    Numerous residents and advocacy groups strongly urged the city to reject TxDOT's I-35 expansion plans, citing concerns about increased traffic, pollution, and community division, pushing for alternatives like rerouting trucks to I-30 and full highway caps.

  • Student Housing Initiative Adopted:

    A new plan passed to explore and promote more affordable housing options for students, including leveraging co-ops and making existing city resources more accessible.

  • Arts Funding Program Scrutinized:

    Discussion and action on the "THRIVE" arts funding program were postponed after public comments highlighted concerns about inequities in the evaluation process and lack of diverse representation on the judging panel.

  • Affordable Housing Progress:

    The city approved a new affordable housing development as part of its ongoing strategy to address the local housing crisis.
