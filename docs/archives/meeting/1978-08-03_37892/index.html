Austin Debates Power Costs, New City Rules

Thursday, August 3, 1978 regular

    The Council authorized an independent cost study for the South Texas Nuclear Project and Fayette Power Project, following concerns about significant cost overruns and schedule delays, sparking intense debate on the city's energy future.

  • Key Public Hearings:

    Discussions included defining "inoperable vehicles" for zoning enforcement (postponed for clearer wording) and a notable debate over denying historic zoning for the American Legion Post #76 building at the owner's request, which the Council approved.

  • Modernizing Taxi Regulations:

    New rules were adopted to update taxi operations, including provisions for electronic taximeters, changes to performance bond requirements, and clearer standards for drivers and passenger limits.

  • Boosting Police Staffing:

    Immediate measures were approved to increase Austin Police Department personnel through over-hiring cadets and utilizing overtime for existing officers, aiming to combat rising crime rates and traffic fatalities.
