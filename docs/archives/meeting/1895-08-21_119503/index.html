Lucy, City Marshal. Austin, Texas, August 21, 1895. Mayor's Call. To the members of the Board of Aldermen of the city of to pass certain Austin, Texas appropriations Gentlemen- I hereby call a special meeting of the city Council of the City of Austin to assemble in the council chamber at the city hall on this day, August 21, 1895, at 5 o'clock p.m. for the purpose of taking such action as the city council may deem proper to take on ordinances now on second reading, entitled as follows: "An ordinance appropriating the sum of five thousand dollars for the purpose of paying expenses of the water and light system of the city of Austin." Respectfully Henry A Linn Acting Mayor --- Page break --- 229 $5000 W & L System An ordinance appropriating the sum of five thousand dollars for the purpose of paying expenses of the Water and Light System of the city of Austin was taken up on its second reading and read. The rules were on motion suspended and the ordinance was read a third time and on motion passed by the following vote: Yeas Aldermen Anderson, Ammann, Fischer, Glass, Glascock, Gaeth, Haynes, Lawless, Linn, Nitschke, Powell, Redd, Roberdeau, Schneider, Shelley, Tobin - 16. $2293.42 Norwich Belt Mfg Co Aldermen Gaeth, for the committee on finance, to whom was referred an ordinance entitled An ordinance appropriating the sum of $2293.48 for the purpose of paying Norwich Belt Manufacturing Company for belts, submitted a report thereon, recommending the passage of the same. The ordinance was read a second time and then a third, under suspension of the rules and passed by the following vote: Yeas Aldermen Anderson, Ammann, Fischer, Glass, Glascock, Gaeth, Haynes, Lawless, Linn, Nitschke, Powell, Redd, Roberdeau, Schneider, Shelley, Tobin - 16.
