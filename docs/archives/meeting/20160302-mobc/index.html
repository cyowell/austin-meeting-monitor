Austin's Mobility Makeover: Sidewalks & Funding

Wednesday, March 2, 2016 Mobility Committee Regular Meeting

  • Austin's Sidewalk Crisis & Action Plan

    Facing a critical deficit with nearly half of streets missing sidewalks and most existing ones in poor condition, Austin aims to spend $15M annually on repairs and $42M annually for six years to build new sidewalks near schools, bus stops, and parks.

  • Modernizing City-Wide Mobility

    A new "Strategic Mobility Plan" is underway to replace an outdated 1995 system. It will integrate all transportation modes, leverage technology, manage traffic, and shape future street design, incorporating extensive public input.

  • Billions Needed for Infrastructure

    With an estimated $1.5 billion required for all missing sidewalks alone, Austin is exploring new funding options like "Street Impact Fees" on new developments, along with existing bond capacity and Tax Increment Financing to meet the massive infrastructure needs.
