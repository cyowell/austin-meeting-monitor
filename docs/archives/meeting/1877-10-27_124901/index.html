Austin's 1877 Fire Department & Street Improvements

Saturday, October 27, 1877 special called

• Fire Department Investment: The city owned two steam fire engines valued at $13,000, two engine houses, and 2,000 feet of hose—representing a major municipal commitment to firefighting capability during the late 1800s.

• Budget Challenges: Austin faced significant uncollected taxes ($72,300 owed from previous years and current year) against a small cash treasury of just $3,038, indicating serious municipal financial strain.

• Street & Infrastructure Work: The city completed street improvements using prisoner labor and approved construction of a bridge over a gutter on Pecan Street near the Utah Railroad crossing to improve public access.

• Alcohol Licensing: Council approved five new liquor retailer licenses for selling beer, wine, and spirits in small quantities across different city wards.

• Vehicle Regulation: The city established its first vehicle licensing system, charging $1-$2.50 per week depending on the number of horses (1, 2, or 4) used for transporting passengers.
