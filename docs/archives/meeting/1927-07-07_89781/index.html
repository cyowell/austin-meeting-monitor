Public Paving Debate & Tax Adjustments

Thursday, July 7, 1927 regular

  • Duval Street Controversy:

    The city deferred a decision on paving Duval Street after hearing from property owners who opposed expensive options, favoring liquid asphalt, and other citizens who supported permanent paving while advocating for the removal of streetcar tracks in favor of a new bus line.

  • Widespread Tax Corrections:

    The city processed numerous individual resolutions to correct property and personal tax assessments, canceling taxes on non-existent improvements or items not owned, and refunding taxes on exempt household goods.

  • City Operations & Development:

    The city approved the purchase of a flow meter for the Filtration Plant, accepted land deeds to expand city streets, and granted a permit for a new gasoline pump and underground tank at a local business.
