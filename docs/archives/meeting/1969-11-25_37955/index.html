Austin: Charity Rejected, Major Roads, Lake Hearing

Tuesday, November 25, 1969 regular

  • Controversial Charity Permit Denied:

    The City denied a solicitation permit for Larry Jackson's Community United Front Association, a program offering free breakfast and health services, due to concerns over financial accountability and the applicant's past conduct.

  • Major Road Upgrades Approved:

    Austin officially joined the federal TOPICS program, clearing the way for significant funding for street widening, traffic signal modernization, and channelization projects on key city roads like 26th Street and Trinity Street.

  • Public Transit Franchise Extended:

    Austin Transit Corporation was granted a new five-year franchise to continue operating the city's motor bus system.

  • Town Lake Motor Debate Continues:

    A public hearing was scheduled for December 11, 1969, to discuss the ongoing debate regarding the use of gasoline outboard motors on Town Lake.
