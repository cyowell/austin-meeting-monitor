Austin Airports, Annexation & Legal Matters

Wednesday, October 30, 1996

  • Major Airport Updates:

    Reviewed progress and budget amendments for the new Austin-Bergstrom International Airport, including funding for noise mitigation near Del Valle schools and cargo infrastructure. Updates were also provided on the former Robert Mueller Municipal Airport's operations and redevelopment.

  • City Growth Strategy:

    Briefed on state laws governing annexation, a key process for city expansion and planning.

  • Confidential Legal Briefing:

    Discussed the status of ongoing litigation in an executive session, specifically regarding the case *John Joseph Sheffield v. City of Austin*.
