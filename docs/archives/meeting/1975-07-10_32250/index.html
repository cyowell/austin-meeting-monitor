Austin's EEO Landmark: Mall Growth, Property Disputes

Thursday, July 10, 1975 regular

  • Landmark Equal Employment Ordinance Passed:

    Austin adopted a pioneering ordinance prohibiting discrimination in employment based on race, color, religion, sex, sexual orientation, national origin, age, or physical handicap, covering private employers (15+ people) and city employment.

  • Northcross Mall Expansion Approved:

    The Council greenlit the vacation of streets to enable a major expansion of Northcross Mall, with plans for new traffic and drainage infrastructure addressing community concerns.

  • Eminent Domain for Municipal Annex Postponed:

    Proceedings to acquire private properties for a municipal expansion were delayed to allow further negotiation with long-time owners who expressed a strong desire to retain their family land.

  • Firefighters Request Enhanced Pension Contributions:

    The city's firefighters proposed a significant, phased increase in the city's contributions to their pension fund, citing the strenuous nature of their jobs and lack of Social Security coverage.

  • Fire Department Grooming Standards to be Reviewed:

    A committee was formed to update the Fire Department's hair and grooming standards to align with modern social norms while maintaining necessary safety requirements.
