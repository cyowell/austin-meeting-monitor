Austin Energy: Customer Support & Efficiency

Monday, February 27, 2017 Austin Energy Utility Oversight Committee Regular Meeting

  • Financial Support for Energy Bills:

    Discussing how Austin Energy manages customer payments, including options for payment arrangements, collections processes, and policies on service disconnections for overdue accounts.

  • Improving Customer Account Services:

    Reviewing overall customer account services and performance data to enhance customer collaboration and service delivery.

  • Energy Efficiency & Community Outreach:

    Hearing updates on energy-saving initiatives, such as a significant efficiency rebate for Dell Seton Medical Center, and community engagement through the Regional Science Festival.

THE AUSTIN ENERGY UTILITY OVERSIGHT COMMITTEE WILL MEET ON MONDAY, FEBRUARY 27, 2017 AT 9:30 AM, AUSTIN CITY HALL 301 W.
