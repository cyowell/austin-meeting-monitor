Hospital Real Estate, Contractor Lawsuit

Thursday, March 9, 1995 special called

  • Brackenridge Hospital Real Estate:

    Officials engaged in private discussions regarding potential real estate transactions involving Brackenridge Hospital.

  • Contractor Diversity Lawsuit:

    The council received legal advice on a federal appeals court lawsuit filed by Austin Black Contractors Assoc., Inc.

  • Flood Control & Drainage Land:

    Discussions were held on acquiring private land for both the Creek Bend Flood Control Project and the Tannehill Drainage Project.

  • City Code & Litigation:

    Other private discussions included legal advice on possible city code amendments and another pending litigation case.
