Austin's Future: Water, Energy, & Public Safety

Thursday, May 27, 2010 Austin City Council Regular Meeting

  • Key Decisions on Environment & Growth:

    Approved funding for the controversial Water Treatment Plant 4 despite environmental warnings and extended wastewater services into the sensitive Barton Springs watershed.

  • Protecting Vulnerable Residents:

    Enacted an ordinance safeguarding medically fragile citizens from electricity disconnections during hot weather.

  • Police Accountability & Efficiency:

    Authorized non-police phlebotomists for jail blood draws to improve safety and cost-effectiveness, with commitments to add facility cameras.

  • Investing in Green Energy:

    Secured a $10 million federal grant to launch a program financing energy efficiency and renewable projects for residents and businesses.
