ATX: Electric Vehicles Drive, Parks Thrive

Monday, June 2, 2025 Climate, Water, Environment, and Parks Committee Special Called Meeting

  • Driving Green:

    Austin is accelerating its electric vehicle (EV) fleet, aiming for over 700 vehicles and nearly 500 charging ports by 2030. This includes piloting electric police cars and refuse trucks, and has already saved $2.5 million in fuel and maintenance costs.

  • Greener Landscaping:

    A new study and pilot program are in progress to transition city lawn and landscape maintenance to electric equipment, aiming to reduce noise and emissions for a healthier community.

  • Community Powers Parks:

    The Austin Parks Foundation, a vital partner, leveraged 24,000 volunteers and significant funding last year to plant 14,000 trees and remove 250,000 pounds of trash, greatly enhancing city parks.

  • Iconic Park Upgrades:

    The beloved Zilker Eagle miniature train has welcomed over 100,000 riders in its first year, alongside other community-led improvements like new pocket parks and crucial shade installations across the city.
