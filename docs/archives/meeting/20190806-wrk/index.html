Austin Agenda: New Voting, Garden Fees, Zoning

Tuesday, August 6, 2019 Austin City Council Work Session

  • New Voting System Rollout:

    Travis County is implementing a new $9 million voting system with a verifiable paper trail for the upcoming November election, addressing concerns about security and auditability.

  • Zilker Botanical Garden Access Debate:

    Council continues discussing a proposed tripling of garden admission fees to support operations, sparking concerns about equitable access and a lack of previously promised free days.

  • Major Zoning Code Rewrite:

    The city's comprehensive Land Development Code and maps are set for release on October 4th, kicking off intensive public engagement, "office hours," and opportunities for neighborhoods to propose alternative plans.

  • New Homelessness Strategy Officer:

    Austin announced the hiring of a dedicated Homeless Strategy Officer and will prioritize initiatives focused on creating housing for unsheltered individuals, backed by significant budget allocations.
