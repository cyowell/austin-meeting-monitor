Paving Plans, Power Prices, Jitney Rules Set

Thursday, March 23, 1916 regular

  • New Street Paving & Utility Pricing:

    City leaders ordered major street paving projects for Rio Grande and West 19th streets, including a direct contract for Rio Grande. They also set a new electric rate of 4 cents per kilowatt for heating purposes.

  • Modern Transit Regulation (Jitneys):

    Significant amendments were approved for the "jitney" (early shared taxi) ordinance, including allowing vehicle substitutions during breakdowns and modifying route length and permit revocation rules.

  • Property Tax Adjustments & Flood Relief:

    Multiple property tax assessments were corrected or reduced due to errors or "misunderstandings." Additionally, a tax penalty was waived for a resident who suffered severe losses in the 1915 floods.

  • Community & Local Infrastructure:

    Funds were allocated for new sanitary sewer construction, as well as the purchase of property and equipment for the East Austin Hose Co No 4 fire station.

We will make the permits to expirewith the current year ,beginning with the date of their respective several issuances. 2. We woll modify the phraseoligy of the ordinance so that in the event of a Break-down or other misfortune to any car ,another car can be substituted under the same license until repairs are made . 3. We will change that provision of the ordinance which gives the City Council the right to revoke in its descretion any permit establishing a route,and substitute therefor the right only to revoke such permit as to routes for good and suffcicient causes. 4. We will change the length of the routes in any givrn direction,from terminus to terminus ,from thirty fivr (35) blocks as is the present requirement of the ordinance, to thirty (30) blocks where the routes start and end on this side of the Colorado river, and we will make the length of the routes 20 blocks where the routes begin and end , either on the south side of the Colorado river or cover a route partly on the south side of the Colorado river and partly on the north side of the Colorado river. The resolution was adopted by the following vote : Yeas Mayor Wooldridge, Councilmen Anthony, Bartholomew, Haynes & Powell 5 Nays none. Approved, A P Wooldridge, Mayor. The Council then adjourned. J.
