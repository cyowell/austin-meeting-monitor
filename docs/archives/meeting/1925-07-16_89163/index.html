Pease Park Land Swap, Garage, and Hearings

Thursday, July 16, 1925 regular

  • Pease Park Land Exchange:

    The city approved swapping a portion of Pease Park for private property, citing the public interest for the exchange.

  • Public Hearing Delayed:

    A hearing for property owners on West 32nd and 33rd Streets, concerning improvements and assessments, was postponed until the next meeting.

  • New Garage Proposed:

    An application to erect a garage and make improvements on Brazos Street was submitted and referred to the Safety Committee for review.
