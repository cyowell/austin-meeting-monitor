Austin: New Gas Mains, Liquor Laws, Zoning Fights

Thursday, August 14, 1941 regular

Here's a summary of the August 14, 1941 Austin City Council meeting:

  • New Gas Main Approved:

    The Council authorized the Texas Public Service Company to lay gas lines on East Avenue, detailing installation guidelines and company liability for potential damages.

  • Liquor Store Fees Set:

    A new ordinance was passed establishing an annual $125 license fee for "package store permits" (liquor stores).

  • Zoning Request Denied:

    The Board of Adjustment recommended against rezoning residential land for commercial use, citing sufficient existing commercial space and lack of public demand. The Council temporarily tabled the decision.

  • Tax Penalties Waived:

    The Council approved remitting specific penalties, a portion of interest, and court costs on over a decade of delinquent property taxes for one individual.
