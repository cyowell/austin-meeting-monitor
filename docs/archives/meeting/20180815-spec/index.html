ATX Soccer Stadium: Housing, Costs, & Control

Wednesday, August 15, 2018 Austin City Council Special Called Meeting

  • Affordable Housing Ensured:

    New terms require the MLS team to ensure affordable housing is built on the McKalla site, clarifying targets for families earning 60-80% of the median income.

  • Shared Control of Future Development:

    Any additional development on the stadium property will need mutual agreement between the city and the team, subject to final City Council approval.

  • Team to Cover Event-Related City Costs:

    The MLS team, not the city, will be responsible for off-site resources like police services for games and stadium events.

  • Transit Investment & Contract Enforcement:

    The deal includes a $3 million team contribution for Cap Metro transit improvements, alongside significant debate on legal enforceability of contract terms.
