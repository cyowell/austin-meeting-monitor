Austin: Nightclubs, Libraries, and Urban Development

Thursday, November 4, 1976 regular

  • Nightlife & Neighbors:

    Deferred a contentious zoning change for the "Rome Inn" nightclub in a residential area, granting a trial period to boost food sales and mitigate neighborhood parking and noise complaints.

  • New Library for South Austin:

    Approved an 1800 sq ft "special extension library" in South Austin, aimed at better serving ethnic and Spanish-speaking communities, despite mayoral concerns about long-term funding and budget management.

  • Major Northwest Development:

    Accepted plans for the 124-acre Great Hills P.U.D. #2, a significant development in an environmentally sensitive area, balancing housing and commercial needs with Lake Austin watershed protection.

  • Downtown Traffic & Parking:

    Established a task force to tackle critical downtown traffic and parking challenges, including issues like double parking, loading zones, and curb restrictions.

  • Future City Corridors:

    Initiated discussions on acquiring an abandoned railroad right-of-way for potential future public transit or parkland, seeking immediate funding solutions and county collaboration.
