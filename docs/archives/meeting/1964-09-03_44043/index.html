Sky-ride Debates, Safety Goals, and City Growth

Thursday, September 3, 1964 regular

Here's a summary of key decisions and discussions from the meeting:

  • Conditional Go-Ahead for Lake Austin Sky-ride:

    Plans for an aerial sky-ride across Lake Austin from Viking Marina to Lake Austin Inn received conditional approval. The project hinges on resolving significant issues like adequate parking, sanitation, and clear jurisdictional boundaries with West Lake Hills.

  • Traffic Safety Review Calls for Action:

    A critical report highlighted Austin's high traffic fatality rate and proposed eight key goals, including bolstering public safety education and better coordinating civic and government efforts to improve road safety.

  • Future of Southeast Austin Debated in Land-Use Shift:

    A public hearing explored changing 295 acres near I-35 from industrial to residential. The debate centered on preserving potential rail-served industrial sites versus meeting immediate housing needs, with the council deferring a decision for further review.

  • Austin Chosen for Civil Defense Shelter Pilot Program:

    The city was selected to lead a 100% federally funded project to develop a comprehensive community shelter plan, enhancing Civil Defense preparedness by identifying and allocating public shelter space.
