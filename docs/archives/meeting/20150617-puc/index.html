Fluoride in Water, Drainage Fees, ADU Costs

Wednesday, June 17, 2015 Public Utilities Committee Regular Meeting

  • Drainage Fee Overhaul Sparks Debate:

    A proposed change to how Austin calculates drainage fees, based on impervious cover, aims for equity but faces strong public opposition regarding significant cost increases for many single-family homeowners and renters.

  • Water Fluoridation Under Review:

    The city discussed continuing water fluoridation at optimal levels for public dental health, while advocates pressed for its discontinuation, citing health concerns and different practices in other developed nations.

  • ADU Development Hit by High Meter Costs:

    Citizens highlighted prohibitive new requirements for separate water meters for Accessory Dwelling Units (ADUs), noting costs exceeding $15,000 as a major barrier to building affordable housing.

  • Exemptions and Development Complexities:

    Concerns were raised over existing drainage fee exemptions for institutions and a lack of suitable methodology for planned developments (like Mueller) and charter schools, calling for more flexible and fair approaches.
