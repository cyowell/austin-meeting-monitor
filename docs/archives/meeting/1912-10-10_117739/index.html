Thursday, October 10, 1912 regular
Transcript

  • Major Infrastructure Boost:

    The city initiated several large-scale paving projects on Lavaca Street, 19th Street, University Avenue, and East 6th Street, detailing cost-sharing with railway companies and property owners. Additionally, $7,300 was appropriated for new concrete bridges across the city.

  • Tax Adjustments & Disputes:

    The Council addressed past financial errors, correcting a 1911 property assessment from $2000 to $500 due to an "obvious error" and resolving an individual's long-standing tax debt from 1898-1911 by adjusting the amount due to an erroneous prior tax sale.

  • Public Events & Advertising:

    Permits were granted for the Gentry Bros Dog and Pony Show to occupy East Avenue, a new "Krit Motor Car" electric sign on Congress Avenue, and temporary "mile-board" advertisements in South Austin.

  • Cemetery Expansion Halted:

    A previously ordered public election concerning the direction and locality of a city cemetery extension was revoked, following the withdrawal of a land offer for the expansion.
