Sobering Center Board Nominee Moves Forward

Wednesday, March 10, 2021 Health & Human Services Committee Regular Meeting
  • The Health and Human Services Committee recommended a candidate for a joint city-county appointment to the Sobering Center Local Government Corporation board.
  • This nominee will be presented to the full City Council on March 25th for final consideration and approval.
  • Discussions were held on streamlining the application process for future joint board appointments, aiming for easier candidate submissions.
