Thursday, August 5, 2021 Austin City Council Budget Work Session
Official City Record Agenda Watch Video Transcript

  • Property Tax Relief:

    Typical Austin homeowners are projected to save over $33 on their annual property tax bill, and seniors/disabled could save nearly $200, due to homestead exemptions and tax rate adjustments.

  • One-Time Funding Decisions:

    The city has an unexpected $23.3 million in one-time funds, sparking debate on how to use it for initiatives like employee stipends (including potential vaccination incentives), anti-displacement programs, and support for the live music sector.

  • Parks and Recreation Investment:

    Discussions focused on allocating funds for new park rangers, expanding after-school programs, and increasing maintenance for Austin's growing parkland.

  • Code Enforcement Scrutiny:

    Council members questioned a proposed increase in the Clean Community Fee and demanded better evening and weekend coverage from Code Compliance officers to address ongoing issues like short-term rental violations and substandard housing.
