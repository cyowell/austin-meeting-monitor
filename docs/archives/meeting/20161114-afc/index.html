New Flood Panel; Neighborhood Plan Overhaul

Monday, November 14, 2016 Audit and Finance Committee Regular Meeting
  • A city audit exposed significant problems with Austin's neighborhood planning, finding plans are largely outdated, ineffective in implementing recommendations, and misaligned with broader city goals like affordability and fair housing.
  • Volunteer "contact teams" responsible for advocating for these plans are often inaccessible, lack transparency, and have unrepresentative membership, including restrictive bylaws that limit renter participation.
  • This outdated and unequitable system creates disparities in how different areas influence land use decisions, with city staff citing resource limitations in their oversight.
  • In a key policy decision, a a new permanent committee was established under the Environmental Commission to specifically monitor and make recommendations on Austin's drainage infrastructure and flood mitigation efforts.
