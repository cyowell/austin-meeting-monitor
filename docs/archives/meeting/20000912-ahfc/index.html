Austin Boosts Housing Budget & Adds Jobs

Tuesday, September 12, 2000 Austin Housing Finance Corporation (AHFC)
  • The Austin Housing Finance Corporation (AHFC) approved its operating budget for the upcoming year, totaling over $16.4 million, dedicated to funding local housing programs.
  • An additional $591,247 was added to the budget, significantly expanding the city's investment in housing initiatives.
  • This budget increase also authorized the creation of five new staff positions to support the expanded housing programs.
  • The AHFC renewed its contract with the City of Austin, continuing its role in managing and operating various city housing programs.
