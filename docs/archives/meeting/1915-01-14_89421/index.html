Austin: Dam Payments, Fines, and Bridge Steel

Thursday, January 14, 1915 regular

  • Addressing Dam Impacts:

    Several landowners were compensated (over $2,900 approved, with more pending) for property submerged or destroyed by the lake formed by the new Colorado River dam.

  • Investing in City Infrastructure:

    The city approved a $10,000 investment for new machinery for the Water & Light Department and allocated funds for steel to build a reinforced concrete bridge over Shoal Creek.

  • Municipal Governance & Justice:

    The council referred an application for water diversion from the Colorado River for legal review, and made differing decisions on citizen fines, denying one request while remitting a fine for drunkenness.
