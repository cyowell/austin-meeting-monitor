Alderman Taylor offered the following majority report: Austin, Tex, July 2d 1894 To the Honorable City Council: Your special committee, to whom was referred the petition of the Austin and Northwestern Railroad Company for leave to use Third street --- Page break --- 737 east of its intersection with Congress avenue for depot purposes etc, etc, have had the same under consideration, and recommend that the said petition be granted upon the following terms: First. The cars of said company shall not be permitted to remain on the track or switch on Third street longer than delay be necessary to load and unload passengers, baggage and express matter on the departure and arrival of trains.
