Thursday, July 9, 1970 regular
Transcript

  • Urban Growth & Infrastructure:

    City officials approved extensive rezoning, shifting many residential areas to commercial, retail, and industrial uses. They also advanced the major Missouri-Pacific Boulevard (Loop 1) project through land acquisition and eminent domain, while also authorizing a comprehensive city-wide transportation study.

  • Barton Creek & Youth Recreation:

    A significant ordinance was passed prohibiting swimming, bathing, or wading in Barton Creek, except for the designated Barton Springs Pool area. The city also applied for federal funding to boost summer recreation programs for youth.

  • Public Utilities & Transit Scrutiny:

    Preparations were made for a public hearing on a proposed gas rate increase from Southern Union Gas, and the local bus company faced a hearing over a reported franchise violation and route changes, with a deadline also moved for bus operators to obtain permits.
