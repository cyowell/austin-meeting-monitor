Thursday, November 19, 2026 Regular Meeting

Austin Debates Boardwalk, Boosts Social Funds

  • Lady Bird Lake Boardwalk Moves Forward Amid Public Concern:

    City leaders approved a $25 million project for a new boardwalk segment near the Hyatt. The decision came despite public opposition raising questions about transparency, benefits to an adjacent luxury development, and impacts on existing lake businesses.

  • I-35 Expansion Progress and Land Acquisition:

    Multiple contracts were approved to relocate essential water, wastewater, and energy lines for the I-35 Capital Express Central project. Separately, the Council authorized the start of eminent domain to acquire private land for related energy infrastructure, facing recorded "no" votes and an abstention.

  • Increased Support for Homelessness & Eviction Services:

    Funding included a $1 million grant for a program helping families reentering the community and a $2 million renovation contract for the Housing Navigation Center. Additionally, funds were increased for housing vouchers and eviction representation services.

  • New Guidelines for Social Service Contracts:

    Policies were updated to enhance transparency and accountability for the city's social service contracts and grants, including adjustments to anti-lobbying requirements, aiming for a fairer and more efficient process.

  • Girls' School Expansion Upheld After Neighborhood Appeal:

    An appeal challenging the Planning Commission's approval for the Girls' School of Austin to increase its student capacity was denied, allowing the school's expansion to proceed despite neighborhood traffic and safety worries.

  • Historic Landmark Status for John Henry Faulk Building:

    The Council initiated steps to rezone the John Henry Faulk Building, now home to the Austin History Center, to add historic landmark combining district zoning, recognizing its architectural and historical significance.
