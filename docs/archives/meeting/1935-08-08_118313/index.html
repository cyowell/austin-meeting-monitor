Austin's Water, Taxi & Dog Debates

Thursday, August 8, 1935 regular
  • Sought federal government aid from the Public Works Administration (PWA) to finance emergency water system improvements.
  • Approved temporary permits for taxicab companies due to increased patronage and authorized new gas main installations across several city streets.
  • Heard public objections regarding a proposed dog ordinance and granted special permission for residents to construct decorative flagstone sidewalks instead of standard concrete.
