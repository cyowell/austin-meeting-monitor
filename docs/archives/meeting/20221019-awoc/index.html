Austin Tackles Flooding, Upgrades Waterways

Wednesday, October 19, 2022 Austin Water Oversight Committee Regular Meeting

  • New "Rain to River" Plan:

    Austin is updating its 20-year-old watershed protection strategy, incorporating climate justice and equity. The plan focuses on extensive community input to address flooding, erosion, and water pollution across the city.

  • Seeking State Flood Funds:

    The city is pushing for state support on major flood control projects, including a $12.6 million city-wide storm drain modeling study and critical flood defenses for essential facilities like the South Austin Regional Wastewater Treatment Plant.

  • Wastewater Plant Upgrades:

    Over $116 million in improvements are planned for the South Austin Regional Wastewater Treatment Plant, replacing aging infrastructure and enhancing flood protection to ensure reliable water services.
