Austin Housing Funds: Rental & Repairs

Thursday, January 23, 2014 Austin Housing Finance Corporation (AHFC) Board of Directors AHFC Meeting
  • The Austin Housing Finance Corporation approved a budget amendment allocating significant funds for affordable housing initiatives.
  • The approved funds were split, with 75% designated for rental housing developer assistance and 25% for homeowner repair programs, including accessibility improvements for renters.
  • This swift approval was crucial to allow staff to meet deadlines for securing housing tax credits for upcoming projects.
  • Despite the immediate allocation, the board committed to ongoing, in-depth policy discussions in the Audit and Finance committee regarding further priorities, such as permanent supportive housing and housing preservation, with continued community input.
