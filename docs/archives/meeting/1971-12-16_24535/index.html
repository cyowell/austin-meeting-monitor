Civic Center Site, Ambulance Shift, Paving Rules

Thursday, December 16, 1971 regular

  • Civic Center Site Sparks Heated Debate:

    Business owners strongly opposed a proposed 16-block downtown site for a new civic center, citing concerns over tax roll losses and business displacement, and demanded an immediate public vote.

  • Ambulance Service Overhaul Explored:

    A temporary ambulance service contract was approved, and the Mayor introduced a controversial proposal to integrate ambulance operations within the Police Department for future consideration.

  • Paving Fees Adjusted for Property Access:

    Residential property owners on Peyton Gin Road can avoid new road paving assessments by granting the city permanent access rights for future road widening.

  • New Environmental Quality Board Formed:

    The city established a Citizens' Board of Natural Resources and Environmental Quality, marking a step towards formal environmental policy and oversight.

  • Taxi Franchise Renewed Conditionally:

    The Airline Cab Company franchise was conditionally renewed despite failing to meet 24-hour service requirements, minimum gross income, and having a city employee as a partner.
