Parking, Lake Rules, & New Gas Stations

Friday, February 23, 1940 regular

  • Traffic & Parking:

    Expanded existing parking meter zones within the city.

  • Lake Austin Development:

    Approved a marine fueling station, established new regulations for piers and docks (including length limits), and permitted marine supply sales along the shore.

  • New Commercial & Infrastructure:

    Authorized the construction of a drive-in gasoline station and new gas pipelines in several neighborhoods.

  • Property Tax Adjustment:

    Reduced taxes and waived penalties for an estate due to land usage for the Austin Dam reconstruction project.
