Austin's Energy Contract & Budget Shifts

Thursday, February 9, 1989 regular

  • Finalized a major energy contract buyout:

    Approved a $3.5 million payment over five years to terminate the agreement with Valley View Energy Corporation.

  • Adjusted city finances and services:

    Reallocated funds to boost the Municipal Court budget and staff, reducing police department funds, and modified payments to Travis County for jail services.

  • Prepared for updated building and safety codes:

    Set public hearings for the adoption of new uniform building, fire, mechanical, and housing codes, along with related permit fees.

  • Addressed public concerns:

    Topics raised by citizens included naming the new Manor City Airport and regulating the sale of drug paraphernalia in local shops.
