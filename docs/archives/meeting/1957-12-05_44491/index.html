Old Austin: Road Fees, Taxi Drama, Zoning Fights

Thursday, December 5, 1957 regular

  • Taxi Company Seeks More Cabs, Faces Opposition:

    Roy's Taxi appealed a decision denying its request for 16 additional vehicles, facing scrutiny over operational capacity and public need, with other taxi companies voicing strong opposition.

  • Major Street Paving Program Sparks 7% Interest Debate:

    The city passed an ordinance for new street paving, but not without controversy. Property owners would be assessed for part of the cost, payable over three years with 7% interest, drawing objections from a council member and leading to a planned public meeting.

  • Humble Oil Funds Accelerated Storm Drainage Project:

    To speed up a storm sewer project on South Lamar Boulevard, Humble Oil & Refining Company provided an $11,000 advance to the city, to be repaid interest-free, to enhance drainage near its property.

  • Zoning Applications: Approvals, Denials, and Site Visits:

    Several requests to rezone properties for residential, retail, and office use were heard. While many passed, one general retail request was denied, and several others were postponed for council members to conduct personal site inspections.
