City Tackles Energy, Veterans, Youth Issues

Thursday, February 27, 1975 regular

  • Future Energy & Soaring Costs:

    Reviewed extensive long-term energy plans, including building new natural gas/fuel oil units, a joint coal power plant, and a nuclear facility, against a backdrop of dramatically rising fuel prices.

  • Veterans' Job Rights:

    Passed a resolution strongly supporting job preference for veterans, directly challenging a recent stance by the National League of Cities.

  • Community Health & Mobility:

    Advanced a proposed ordinance to curb glue and paint sniffing, and discussed plans for improving public transit services for disabled individuals and senior citizens.

  • Development & Public Land Use:

    Approved a substantial contract for a new $260,000 tennis center and scheduled public hearings for controversial apartment development near a school, along with a wastewater main project affecting Zilker Park.
