Discrimination, Utility Reform, and Civic Spending

Thursday, March 19, 1987 regular

  • Major Civil Rights Protection:

    Approved an ordinance to prohibit discrimination against disabled persons, following a public hearing.

  • Utility Rate & Economic Equity Debates:

    Continued public discussion on creating a new system for reviewing utility rates, and clarified requirements for minority and women-owned businesses in Civic Center contracts.

  • Community Support & Regulatory Actions:

    Funded a new shelter program for teens and parents, and approved the forfeiture of a taxi company's operating franchise.

  • Local Development & Infrastructure:

    Approved financing for St. David's Community Hospital facilities and advanced several utility infrastructure projects, including land acquisitions for electric circuits.
