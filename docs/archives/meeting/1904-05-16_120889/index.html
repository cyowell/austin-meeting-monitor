Austin Street Work and Bond Funding

Monday, May 16, 1904 regular

• Major Infrastructure Investment: City council approved approximately $3,000 in street repairs and improvements across all seven wards, including bridge repairs, sewer connections, and street work on Guadalupe Street and West 6th Street.

• City Finances: The Finance Committee purchased $17,000 in bonds for the city's sinking fund, paying $12,645.79 total—efforts to manage Austin's debt and future financial obligations.

• Sidewalk Ordinance Passed: After debate, the council approved a new sidewalk ordinance in a close vote (10-3), establishing standards for sidewalk construction throughout the city.

• Pound and Dog Ordinances: The council amended the pound ordinance (which regulated animal impoundment fees) and tabled discussion of repealing the dog ordinance for future consideration.

• Road Access for South Austin: Residents of South Austin secured council approval to open a new road through land owned by prominent citizen Geo. Brackenridge, improving connectivity to the developing southern area of the city.
