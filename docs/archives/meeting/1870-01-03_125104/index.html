Austin City Budget & Market House Plan 1876

Monday, January 3, 1870 regular

• $100 Approved for City Horse – The mayor received funding to purchase a horse and harness for official city use, recognizing the need for improved municipal transportation.

• Police and City Staff Payroll – The council approved approximately $244 in monthly wages for three police officers ($40 each) and various city workers, including staff for animal removal, prisoner care, and engine maintenance.
