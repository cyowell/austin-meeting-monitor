Future of Austin: Air Base, Clean Water, Jobs

Thursday, August 1, 1991 regular

Here's a summary of Austin's City Council meeting from August 1, 1991:

  • Bergstrom Air Base's Future:

    The Council made dual policy moves regarding Bergstrom Air Force Base's future, forming a conversion task force and passing resolutions supporting both its reuse as a civilian airport (with environmental considerations) and the continued presence of Air Force units.

  • Protecting Water Quality:

    Prioritized environmental protection by extending the critical Barton Creek Non-Degradation Ordinance and postponing a developer's special exception request for the Barton Springs Contributing Zone.

  • Community Investment:

    Allocated over $5.2 million in Community Development Block Grant funds for various activities and approved a $400,000 neighborhood loan for Don Limon's Bakery.

  • Key Infrastructure & Social Issues:

    Approved a vital telecommunications franchise agreement with Southwestern Bell and set a public hearing to address the Angel House and Day Laborers Program on East First Street.
