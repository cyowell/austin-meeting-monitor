Tuesday, April 5, 2022 Austin Energy Utility Oversight Committee Regular Meeting
Official City Record Agenda Watch Video Transcript

  • Electric Bill Adjustments Ahead:

    Austin Energy proposes its first base rate increase in nearly a decade due to financial losses, significant customer growth, and an outdated pricing structure. A transparent public review process is underway, with new rates expected by January 2023.

  • Fayette Coal Plant & Climate Goals:

    The utility is actively working to reduce carbon emissions from the Fayette coal plant through its "REACH" program, with an eventual goal of exit, aligning with public calls for cleaner energy.

  • Winter Storm Uri Readiness & Grid Upgrades:

    Austin Energy reported substantial progress on 70% of its post-Uri improvements, including expanding load-shed programs with large customers, boosting vegetation management, and upgrading substation equipment to enhance grid resilience.

  • Operational Hurdles & Workforce Transition:

    While facing supply chain delays and labor recruitment challenges, Austin Energy successfully retired two Decker Creek power units as planned, transitioning all affected employees to new roles without layoffs.
