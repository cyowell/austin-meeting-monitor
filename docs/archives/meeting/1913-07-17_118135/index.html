558 . Regular meeting of the City Council : Austin, Texas, July 17, 1913. Dog Muzzling Ordinance ✓ The Mayor laid before the Council An ordinance amending an ordinance entitled " An ordinance regulating the muzzling of Dogs , and prescribing a penalty for the violation thereof", The ordinance was read the third time. The Mayor laid before the Council the following resolution : Be it resolved by the City Council of the City of Austin : That the width of the center parking area of East Avenue from 2nd street south be and is hereby established as 80 feet between curb lines, Resolved , that the width of roadway on each side of center parks be and is hereby established as 30 feet between curb lines,and Resolved, that the parkways be rectangular in shape and that the corners of same be rounded to a fifteen foot radius ; and Park & East Avenue ✓ Resolved, that the parkway between 1st & 2nd streets.[strikethrough]to the north curb line of[/strikethrough] 1st street, from the south curb line of 2nd street to the north curb line of 1st street, measured on the West side of East Avenue ; that the parkway between 1st and Driskill streets extend from the south curb line of 1st street to the north curb line of Driskill street; that the parkway south from Driskill street extend from the south curb line of Driskill street a distance of approx-imately 165 feet ; the center of this parkway to be on the center line of Willow street as extended from the east,and that the south parkway be the same length as the north parkway and to start 30 feet from the parkway immediately north.
