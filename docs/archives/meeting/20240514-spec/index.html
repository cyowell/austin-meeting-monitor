Austin Finalizes May 4th Election Outcomes

Tuesday, May 14, 2024 Austin City Council Special Called Meeting

  • Special Election Results Certified:

    The city officially approved the outcomes of the May 4th special election.

  • Key Areas Disannexed:

    Propositions A, C, and F passed, leading to Lost Creek, Blue Goose Road, and River Place outparcels being removed from Austin's city limits.

  • Other Areas Remain in Austin:

    Propositions B, D, and E failed, meaning areas like Moreland Addition and Wild Horse Webb Tract will stay within the city.

  • Disannexed Lands Enter ETJ:

    Areas removed from the city will immediately become part of Austin's Extraterritorial Jurisdiction (ETJ).
