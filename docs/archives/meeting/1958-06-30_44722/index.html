Boosting City Employee Wages: $1 Minimum Pay?

Monday, June 30, 1958 special called
  • City officials began a detailed study of potential salary increases for municipal employees, evaluating how various pay raises would impact local taxes.
  • A key topic of discussion was a proposal to establish a minimum starting wage of $1.00 per hour for city staff.
  • A public hearing was scheduled for July 10th, inviting community members to provide input on the proposed wage adjustments.
