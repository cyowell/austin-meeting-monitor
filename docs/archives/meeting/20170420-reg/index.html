Austin's Agenda: Kids, Water, Schools, Transit

Thursday, April 20, 2017 Austin City Council Regular Meeting

  • Bowie High School Expansion & Land Use:

    City leaders discussed solutions for Bowie High School's severe overcrowding, including exploring land transactions and transferring impervious cover rights within the environmentally sensitive Barton Springs Zone to allow for expansion while improving water quality.

  • Water Meter Reading Contract:

    Debated a new multi-million dollar contract for water meter reading services, focusing on improving accuracy after previous billing issues and aligning with future plans to electrify water meters.

  • Green City Fleet & First Electric Van:

    Approved a $16 million contract for city vehicles, highlighting efforts towards sustainability and including the purchase of Austin's first large electric van for shuttle services.

  • Reducing Traffic with "Smart Trips":

    Authorized funding for the "Smart Trips" program to encourage less driving, ease congestion, and promote public transit, biking, and walking through targeted community outreach.

  • "Lemonade Day" Regulatory Relief:

    Supported annual waivers for "Lemonade Day" to allow children to operate stands without burdensome city permits and fees, sparking a broader discussion on simplifying regulations for small, casual businesses.
