Austin's Post-War Boom: Taxes, Parks, and Growth

Thursday, July 24, 1947 regular

Here are the key takeaways from the July 24, 1947 Austin City Council agenda:

  • Tax Ordinance Sparks Debate:

    Business leaders strongly protested a proposed property tax ordinance requiring inventory disclosures for assessment, citing fears over trade secrets. The council postponed action to allow for further study.

  • Key Infrastructure & Green Space Investment:

    The city committed funds for a new sewer system in the Govalle area, planned upgrades for Robert Mueller Municipal Airport and Zilker Springs, and authorized the purchase of 16 acres for a new park and playground.

  • Growth-Oriented Zoning & Development:

    Multiple commercial and industrial zoning changes were advanced, alongside approvals for new public and private gasoline stations, reflecting Austin's ongoing urban expansion.

  • Public Stockyard Established:

    A five-year lease was approved for a private operator to run a public stockyard on city-owned land, detailing financial and operational terms for this community service.
