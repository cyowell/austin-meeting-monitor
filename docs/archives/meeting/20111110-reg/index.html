Thursday, November 10, 2011 Austin City Council Regular Meeting
Official City Record Agenda Watch Video Transcript
  • Austin was recognized as the #1 US city among large populations for overall resident satisfaction with city services and customer service, with high marks for quality of life.
  • A controversial remediation project for the Loop 360 landfill near Barton Creek and Springs was approved despite public concerns about tree removal and drought conditions.
  • City staff assured that the landfill, containing lead and other contaminants, posed an environmental risk to the Barton Springs recharge zone, and committed to protecting heritage trees and extended watering for new saplings.
  • Key areas identified for continued focus and improvement by residents included streets, sidewalks, police services, and health and human services, with traffic flow noted as the biggest challenge.
