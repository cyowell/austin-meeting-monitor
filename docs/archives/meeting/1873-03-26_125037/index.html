Austin Funds General Sheridan's 1873 Visit

Wednesday, March 26, 1873 special called

• Major spending approved: The city council unanimously authorized $1,000 (a substantial sum at the time) to entertain General Philip Sheridan and Secretary of War William Belknap during their visit to Austin.

• Fast-tracked decision: Council suspended normal voting procedures to pass the entertainment budget immediately, indicating the visit was considered high priority and time-sensitive.

• Civic committee delegation: Rather than manage the funds directly, the council handed the money over to a citizen committee appointed by the Mayor to handle all arrangements and expenses for the distinguished guests.

• Unanimous support: All seven council members present voted in favor, with no opposition or debate recorded.

319 City Council Room Austin Tex March 26th 73 Called meeting The Council was called to order by His Honor J. B. Wheeler. Present: Ald. J. E. Sneed Wm. Brueggerhoff Ed. Christian Geo. Susemann J. H. Robinson J. W. Henning C. A. Scholz Absent: E. Bremond His Honor the Mayor announced that the meeting was called to make the necessary appropri- ation required for the entertainment of General Sheridan and Belknap during their stay in this City. Whereupon Ald. Sneed offered the following Resolution. Be it resolved by the City Council of the City of Austin. That the sum of One Thousand Dollars or as much thereof as may be necessary be and the same is hereby appropriated out of any money of the City not otherwise appropriated, to be used in the entertainment of Generals Belknap and Sheridan and the gentlemen comprising the party during their stay in this City. And that said sum be turned over to the Committee of arrangements of Citizens appointed by the Mayor to be expended for said purpose. And in case said sum be not in the City Treasury then the warrants of the City be issued for the amount herein appropriated On motion of Ald. Robinson the rules were suspended, the resolution passed its second and third reading, the yeas and nays being called it passed by the following vote. Yeas. Ald. Sneed Brueggerhoff " Christian Susemann " Robinson Scholz " Henning Nays none On motion the Council adjourned until its regular monthly meeting. Fred Steying Recorder
