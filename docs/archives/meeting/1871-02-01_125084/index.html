41 Call Meeting. of February, 1st 1859. at 7 Oclock P.M. City Council met pursuant to a special call of His Honor Leander Brown Mayor who was Roll called and the following names Alderman answered to their names, Alderman Brownell, Baker, Palan, Rawson, Democlice and Fox.
