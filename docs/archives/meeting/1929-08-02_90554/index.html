Paving, Sewers, and Property Deals

Friday, August 2, 1929 special called

  • New Property Acquisition:

    The City authorized the purchase of land located south of the City Hospital on Fourteenth Street.

  • Street Paving Completed:

    Approved and accepted major paving and improvement projects for both East 11th Street and East 6th Street, with costs partially covered by assessments on property owners.

  • Major Sewer Project Planned:

    Authorized the City Manager to seek bids for a significant $100,000 project to build new sanitary sewer lines in the Deep Eddy neighborhood and other city areas.

  • Guadalupe Street Hearing Postponed:

    A public hearing regarding proposed improvements for Guadalupe Street was continued to the next session.
