Austin's Spring '91: Big Airport Changes, New Recycling & Housing

Thursday, April 4, 1991 regular

  • Major Infrastructure & Airport Upgrades:

    Celebrated the 20-year completion of the Boggy Creek Project and approved significant funding for Robert Mueller Airport security enhancements and soundproofing for three nearby public schools.

  • Green Initiatives & Social Programs:

    Set a public hearing to establish city-wide drop-off recycling, launched a pilot day labor program for job opportunities, and authorized an urban homesteading initiative for affordable housing.

  • Equity & Policy Modernization:

    Scheduled a public hearing to review and potentially amend requirements for minority and women-owned businesses, and formally adopted "person-first" language for the Mayor's Committee for People with Disabilities.
