Thursday, November 14, 1985 regular
Transcript

  • Town Lake Development Sparks Debate:

    A large mixed-use project near Town Lake faced intense public debate over density and traffic, prompting a vote to negotiate public street access and delay final approval.

  • Major Infrastructure & Growth Investments:

    Over $60 million in utility bonds were authorized, alongside a $5.2 million expansion of the Walnut Creek Wastewater Treatment Plant, to fund city growth.

  • New Regulations for Firearm & Archery Ranges:

    Council began establishing rules for shooting ranges, including a proposal to prohibit automatic weapons, after hearing public input on safety concerns.

  • Key Development Controls & North Austin Projects:

    An existing development moratorium for Loop 360 was extended, while multiple large commercial projects in North Austin moved forward, addressing traffic impacts.
