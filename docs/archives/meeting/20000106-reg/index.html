Thursday, January 6, 2000 Austin City Council Regular Meeting

Here's a summary of the Austin City Council agenda from January 6, 2000:

  • Major land use decisions

    were made, including the annexation of the 1,630-acre Avery Ranch for planning and zoning, and adding specific environmental protections (like limiting building on steep slopes) to the Vista Ridge development. A large proposed settlement for development in southwest Travis and northern Hays counties was postponed.

  • Significant funding boosts

    were approved for community health, mental health, and substance abuse treatment programs, alongside renewing grants to support services for domestic violence victims.
  • The city committed to over $63 million in bond and cash reimbursements for various public projects and approved major purchases for public safety (police vehicles, fire trucks) and infrastructure (street improvements, convention center expansion).
  • A commercial rezoning request at 1101 Kramer Lane was notably denied, indicating a specific policy stance on local development, and a contract was approved to support the Texas Music Museum.
