Austin's Audit, Auto Fines & Iron Sheds

Thursday, April 30, 1914 regular

  • Financial Oversight:

    A new "Auditorium Account" was established to accurately track all income and expenses related to the city's auditorium building.

  • Traffic Fine Remissions:

    Two $19.80 fines were forgiven, including one for a non-resident who was unfamiliar with local automobile speed limits.

  • Fire & Police Force Updates:

    New rules were adopted for paid employees of the Colorado Hose Company No. 2, and a new patrolman was appointed.

  • Downtown Development:

    A revocable permit was granted to the General Manager of the Austin American newspaper to erect an all-iron shed behind their Congress Avenue office.

772 Regular meeting of the City Council : Austin, Texas, April 30, 1914 The Council was called to order by the Mayor : Roll called : Present Mayor Wooldridge, Councilmen Anthony, Bartholomew, Haynes & Powell 5 Absent none. The minutes of the last regular meeting were read and adopted: Councilman Anthony presented bids for painting roofs & Tower of Colorado Fire Hall as follows: Walter J Daly $ 105.00 Tony Wagner 72.05 C G Saladee 125.00. On motion of Councilman Anthony, the contract for the said work, was awarded to Tony Wagner , for $ 72.05, by the following vote: Yeas Mayor Wooldridge, Councilmen Anthony, Bartholomew, Haynes & Powell 5 Nays none. The Mayor laid before the Council the following resolution : Whereas, from time to time there are receipts of money from the rental of the auditorium Building in this City, situated upon the Public Park lying between Guadalupe and San Antonio streets , and expenditures of money be reason of the maintenance of the auditorium located on the above described property;and Whereas, at the present time there is no specificate account kept by either the Assessor & collector nor the City Treasurorof the receipts and expenditures of said auditorium Building. Now, therefore, Resolved by the City Council of the City of Austin; Texas: That both the Assessor & collector and the City Treasurer of the City of Austin, Texas, are authorized and instructed to open anappropriate account , entitled " Auditorium Account " , in which the receipts and expenditures of money by reason of the maintenance and conduct of said auditorium shall be correctly entered. The resolution was adopted by the following vote : Yeas Mayor Wooldridge, Councilmen Anthony, Bartholomew, Haynes & Powell 5 Nays none.
