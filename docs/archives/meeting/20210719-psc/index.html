Austin's EMS, Judges, Code Uniform Issues

Monday, July 19, 2021 Public Safety Committee Regular Meeting

  • EMS Winter Storm Crisis:

    Medics recounted harrowing experiences during Winter Storm Uri, detailing triple call volumes, dangerous conditions, power outages at stations, and a critical lack of breaks, food, and support, leaving them feeling "alone and helpless."

  • Urgent EMS Staffing & Breaks:

    Discussions included proposals to boost ambulance staffing for improved disaster readiness and to implement a policy for mandatory 45-minute breaks every 12 hours for paramedics.

  • Municipal Judge Appointments:

    The city outlined its process for selecting municipal court judges, involving a review panel. A key discussion point was the balance of police and community representation on this panel.

  • Code Uniforms & Public Perception:

    The Austin Code Department reviewed its uniforms, acknowledging concerns that inspectors are sometimes mistaken for law enforcement, leading to public fear. Efforts to build community trust through increased outreach and language services were highlighted.
