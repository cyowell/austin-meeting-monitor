Austin's Housing Future: LDC, Parking, & Schools

Tuesday, June 6, 2023 Austin City Council Work Session

Here's what Austin leaders discussed regarding the city's development future:

  • Major Code Overhaul Planned:

    City staff presented a draft schedule for addressing over 60 pending Land Development Code (LDC) amendments, aiming for a more comprehensive approach rather than ad hoc changes.

  • Focus on Housing Affordability:

    A key priority is accelerating LDC changes to increase housing supply and affordability, with discussions on amendments related to density bonuses, zoning, and eliminating citywide parking minimums.

  • Staffing & Process Challenges:

    The Planning Department highlighted severe staffing shortages and the complex, multi-departmental process required for code amendments, underscoring the need for more resources and potentially outside consultants.

  • Balancing Speed & Engagement:

    Council members debated the appropriate level of community engagement for various amendments, seeking to streamline the process while maintaining public trust and avoiding past controversies.

  • Expediting School Projects:

    A commitment was made to fast-track LDC amendments impacting the interlocal agreement with Austin ISD, crucial for new school construction to support the district's bond program.
