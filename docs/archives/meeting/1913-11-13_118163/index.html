Austin Streets, Sidewalks, & A Forgiven Fine

Thursday, November 13, 1913 regular

  • New Paved Streets and Sidewalks:

    Approved final payments for significant street paving projects on Colorado and 9th Streets, and established new sidewalk lines on Congress Avenue, specifically ensuring space for existing trees.

  • A Second Chance for a "Habitual Drunkard":

    Forgave a court fine for Andy McQuirter, citing a judge's belief it would aid his reform, and noting the original payment had already been distributed to his wife and children.

  • City Pays for Construction Damage:

    Authorized funds to compensate property owners on Red River and 11th Street for damages caused by the city's changes to street grades during public works.

  • Temporary Boost for Street Funds:

    Approved a short-term $5,000 transfer from the Sanitary Sewer Earnings fund to support general street improvements, with an agreement to return the money by early 1914.

650 . Regular meeting; of the City Council : Austin, Texas, November 13, 1913. . The Council was called to order by the Mayor : Roll called : Present Mayor Wooldridge, Councilmen Anthony, Bartholomew, Haynes & Powell 5 Absent none. The minutes of the last regular and subsequent recessed meetings were read and adopted. The Mayor laid before the Council the following reports from City Officers: Assessor & Collector, the Sexton, the Supt of Streets, Asst City Clerk, the Inspector Weights and Measures, the Treasurer, which were read and ordered filed. Councilman Powell offered the following resolution : A resolution finally accepting pavement and improvement of Colorado street from the south curb line of 8th street to the north property line of 9th street. Pavement C.F. 8th-9th accepted Whereas, the Supt of Streets and Public Improvements has filed his report with the City Council to the effect that the Texas Bitulithic Company has completed the work of paving and improving Colorado street from the south curb line of 8th street to the north property line of 9th street under contract with the City and that said work has been fully done in strict accordance with said contract and specifications adopted by the City ,therefore, Be it resolved by the City Council of the City of Austin : That the said work of improvement be finally accepted by the City and that the final estimate due by the City to the said Company be paid. The resolution was adopted by the following vote : Yeas Mayor Wooldridge, Councilmen Anthony, Bartholomew, Haynes & Powell 5 Nays none. Councilman Anthony offered the following resolution : Be it resolved by the City Council of the City of Austin : Andy McQuirter Fine remitted ✓ That the fine and costs imposed upon Andy McQuirter ,in cause No 7963, in the Corporation Court of the City of Austin, Texas, be and the same are hereby remitted, and the Clerk of the Corporation Court declared to be intitled to take credit on his accounts accordingly. The reasons moving the Council to remit the fine and costs in this case are that it believes, upon information of the Judge of the Corporation Court ,that such action will help to reform said Andy McQuirter,heretofore almost an habitual drunkard. It is also here stated ,as a part of the moving cause to the Council for its action in this matter , that the fine and costs in this cause' have already been paid by the defendant McQuirter into the hands of the Clerk of the Corporation Court , and have by him from time, to time, been turned over to the Wife and Children,.of said Defendant.
