Sunday, November 8, 2015 Austin City Council Special Called Meeting
Official City Record Agenda Watch Video Transcript

  • Emergency Flood Response & Buyouts Demanded:

    An unusual Sunday meeting saw residents and advocates passionately call for immediate, expedited home buyouts in flood-stricken areas like Onion and Williamson Creeks, citing repeated devastation, lives lost, and inadequate city response to past floods.

  • Equity Concerns in Buyout Process:

    Public testimony highlighted significant inequities, with lower-income residents and communities of color facing disproportionate impacts and challenges with property valuations, insurance deductions, and bureaucratic hurdles in the buyout program.

  • Development's Role in Flooding:

    Speakers criticized the city's past development approvals in floodplains and upstream, insufficient drainage, and annexation policies, arguing these factors exacerbate flood risks and demand a reevaluation of long-term mitigation strategies.
