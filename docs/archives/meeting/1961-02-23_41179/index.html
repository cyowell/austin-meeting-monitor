Austin's Energy, Parks, and Road Upgrades

Thursday, February 23, 1961 regular

Here's a summary of key decisions and discussions from Austin City Council's February 23, 1961 agenda:

  • Major Power Plant Investments:

    Awarded over $2 million in contracts for a new condenser and steam generator at the Holly Street Power Plant, enhancing the city's electricity generation capabilities.

  • Parks and Recreation Expansion:

    Responded to a public petition for a new district park in South Austin, considered a private donation to construct a "Hike and Bike Trail" along Shoal Creek, and approved repairs for Disch Field ball park.

  • Infrastructure & Traffic Improvements:

    Authorized the use of eminent domain to acquire land for widening South First Street, approved plans to channelize West 12th Street, and directed the installation of 300 additional parking meters.

  • Urban Development & Regulations:

    Approved new commercial and private gasoline stations, annexed additional territory into the city limits, and began discussions on implementing fees for special events on Town Lake.

  • City Operations & Personnel:

    Increased the Chief of Police's salary and extended a land lease agreement for the local American Red Cross headquarters.
