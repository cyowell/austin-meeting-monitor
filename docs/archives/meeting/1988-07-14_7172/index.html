Austin's Green Shift & Sign Fee Halt

Thursday, July 14, 1988 regular

  • Environmental Policy Shift:

    The city decided to abandon its large Resource Recovery Plant project, while simultaneously reinstating its energy loan program (with a focus on low-income applicants) and adopting a comprehensive Water Management Plan to emphasize conservation.

  • Sign Permit Fees Suspended:

    Following public discussion, operating permit fees for signs were temporarily halted, with refunds authorized for past payments and a task force created to explore new enforcement methods.

  • Key Infrastructure & Development Plans:

    The council supported flood control proposals for Shoal Creek, amended the paving assessment policy (including protections for homestead properties), and initiated the process for land acquisition for a new Civic Center project.

  • Debate on Construction Wages:

    A public hearing was scheduled to discuss potentially adopting a lower, market-aligned prevailing wage rate for city construction projects, aiming to reduce costs and boost local building activity.
