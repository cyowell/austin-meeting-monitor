Austin: Rats, Gas Lines, & Show Permits

Thursday, September 13, 1934 regular

  • Public Health Campaign:

    Launched an initiative to exterminate rats, with city officials exploring federal relief workers for assistance.

  • Infrastructure Development:

    Approved the expansion of natural gas lines on McDonald Avenue and a new commercial driveway on East 6th Street.

  • Community Debates:

    Deferred decisions on a controversial request to annex school district territory and a permit for the Harley Sadler Shows carnival.
