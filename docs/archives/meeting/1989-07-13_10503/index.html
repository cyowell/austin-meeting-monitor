Austin's Future: Development, Environment, Equity

Thursday, July 13, 1989 regular

  • Approved major infrastructure projects

    , including a $3.3 million contract for new airport land acquisition services and significant water and wastewater system upgrades for the downtown area.

  • Addressed key urban development and environmental policies

    , denying an appeal for development in the Bull Creek watershed, allowing development rights transfers in Barton Creek, and revising city-wide parking requirements.

  • Voted to appeal a major lawsuit

    against Houston Lighting and Power, and set a public hearing to consider allowing commercial blood plasma donation centers in certain business districts.

  • Prioritized social equity and community services

    , greenlighting interlocal agreements with Travis County for senior lunch and home care programs, and requesting all census materials be available in both English and Spanish.
