Paving Plans, Permits, and People's Fines

Thursday, November 6, 1913 regular

  • Major Infrastructure Projects:

    Advanced significant paving initiatives for Guadalupe and 19th Streets, establishing public hearings for property owner assessments. A $1,500 concrete culvert was approved, alongside new sidewalks for West 10th Street and finalized permanent grades for Red River Street.

  • Public Permits & City Spaces:

    Issued permits for the "Gentry Show" circus, a temporary church building in a city park, and religious banners spanning downtown streets.

  • Individual Fines, Claims & Relief:

    Granted leniency by remitting an intoxication fine for an elderly citizen and reducing speeding fines for young, first-time motorcycle offenders. The council also settled a $100 damage claim for Mrs. Emma Gage and canceled an old $1,000 assessment.
