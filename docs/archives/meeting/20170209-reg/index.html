Official City Record Agenda Watch Video Transcript

Here's a summary of the 2017-02-09 Austin City Council agenda:

  • City Leader Search:

    The Council debated and voted to hire a search firm for the new City Manager, with extensive discussion on candidate experience, diversity, and engagement with Austin's unique governmental dynamics.

  • Affordable Housing Debates:

    Multiple low-income housing projects were approved, but discussions highlighted concerns about the definition of "affordable" and the appropriate inclusion of market-rate units in subsidized developments.

  • Major Project Overruns:

    Council members raised alarms over the Waller Creek project, noting its costs had swelled to six times the original voter-approved budget, prompting questions about procurement and project timelines.

  • City Facilities Plan:

    A significant discussion revolved around a proposed property purchase to consolidate various city departments, touching on financial implications, co-location strategies, and the future use of the One Texas Center.

  • State Legislative Priorities:

    The Council finalized its legislative agenda for the state, emphasizing support for revitalizing the Austin State Hospital for behavioral healthcare and opposing state-imposed revenue caps on local governments.
