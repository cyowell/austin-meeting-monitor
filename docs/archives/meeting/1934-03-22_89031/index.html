Austin's Water Discounts, New Streets, Safety

Thursday, March 22, 1934 regular

Here's a look at some key decisions made by Austin leaders on March 22, 1934:

  • Summer Water Bill Relief:

    Residents received a boost as the city approved a 10-25% reduction on domestic water bills for the summer months, aiming to encourage city beautification and create employment.

  • Growing Infrastructure:

    City officials accepted a new section of Gaston Avenue, expanding the city's road network, and permitted Southwestern Bell Telephone Company to install new pole lines across several streets.

  • Prioritizing Public Safety:

    Strict safety measures were put in place for a downtown construction project, requiring guard rails, a designated public walkway, and a $5,000 surety bond for temporary sidewalk use.

  • Addressing Local Concerns:

    The council acknowledged a request from Guadalupe Street merchants for relief from parking restrictions, deferring the matter for further discussion and study with the University of Texas Board of Regents.
