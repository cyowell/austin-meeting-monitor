Austin Progress: Neighborhood Plans, Safety & Airport Growth

Thursday, December 13, 2001 Austin City Council Regular Meeting

  • Neighborhoods Get New Plans:

    City Council approved comprehensive neighborhood plans for Central East Austin and Holly, guiding future development. They also advanced a Conservation District in Hyde Park to preserve its unique character.

  • Boosting Public Safety & Green Spaces:

    Key actions included updating emergency management procedures (reflecting post-9/11 concerns), securing grants for victim support services, and acquiring over 230 acres for new parks and open spaces to protect water quality.

  • Community & Economic Development:

    Initiatives were launched to support at-risk youth with a truancy court pilot, establish a task force to expand Austin's music industry, and approve a new management agreement for airport hangars.
