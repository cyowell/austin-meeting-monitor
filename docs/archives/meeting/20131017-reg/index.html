Rainey St, Dillo Dirt, & Cemetery Rules

Thursday, October 17, 2013 Austin City Council Regular Meeting

  • Rainey Street Development Policy:

    The council approved changes to the Rainey Street density bonus program, allowing developers flexibility to provide affordable housing either on-site or through fee-in-lieu payments for projects seeking increased density.

  • Hornsby Bend Biosolids Contract:

    A multi-million dollar contract for biosolids management at Hornsby Bend was approved, despite public debate and concerns raised regarding its cost, environmental benefits (composting vs. land application), and procurement process transparency.

  • Cemetery Decoration Rules:

    The council heard emotional public testimony from citizens regarding proposed stricter rules on grave decorations, and subsequently approved a measure that allows waivers for placing items at gravesites.

  • Urban Farm Ordinance:

    A new ordinance was approved to establish "Urban Farm" as a classification in the City Code, setting specific development regulations and standards for urban farming operations within Austin.
