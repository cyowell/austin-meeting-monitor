Thursday, November 21, 1974 regular
Transcript

  • Debated public official ethics and transparency,

    considering a new Code of Ethics and Financial Disclosure for city officials and board members. While proponents pushed for transparency, opponents voiced concerns about privacy and discouraging public service. The council postponed a vote for further public discussion.

  • Strengthened historic preservation efforts

    by passing an ordinance that temporarily protects buildings from demolition or alteration while they are being considered for historic landmark status.

  • Addressed the future of Emergency Medical Services (EMS),

    hearing proposals for significant upgrades to the ambulance system, including options for private operation or a new city department. Staff were directed to propose a plan for rapid implementation.

  • Moved to curb outdoor advertising on new roadways

    like MoPac and Loop 360. Following public support, the City Attorney was instructed to draft an ordinance to prohibit billboards and regulate other signs, aiming to protect scenic views.
