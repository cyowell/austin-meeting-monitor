Austin Homeowners See Tax Relief, Big Budget Talks

Wednesday, July 30, 2025 Audit and Finance Committee Special Called Meeting

  • Homeowner Tax Relief:

    Updated property tax rolls mean typical Austin homeowners will see a smaller property tax bill increase for FY2026, now $8.68/month (5.3%) instead of the initially projected $12.90/month.

  • Total Bill Impacts Reduced:

    Including all city rates and fees, the average homeowner's total monthly bill increase is now $18.13 (a 4% rise), also lower than previous estimates.

  • Senior Facilities & Police Spending:

    A resident advocated for investing in a long-awaited senior facility at Gus Garcia Recreation Center, proposing temporary portables, and called for a moratorium on police budget increases, questioning resource allocation.

  • Upcoming Tax Rate Decisions:

    The full body will vote tomorrow to set a "maximum tax rate," which allows for broader discussion on a potential Tax Rate Election (TRE) to fund additional services.

  • TRE Scenarios Ahead:

    The committee plans a dedicated discussion next Tuesday, August 5th, to analyze various Tax Rate Election scenarios and their potential impacts on funding and taxpayer costs.
