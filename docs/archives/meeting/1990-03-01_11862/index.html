Thursday, March 1, 1990 regular
Transcript

  • Key Infrastructure & Development Moves Forward:

    Over $2.5 million was approved for wastewater system upgrades and water utility relocations. The city also advanced land acquisitions for the US 183/290 highway project and set a public hearing for new water and wastewater impact fees.

  • City Ethics and Lobbying Rules Strengthened:

    The council adopted amendments to the city's ethics and lobbying codes and tasked a subcommittee with further revisions to enhance transparency.

  • Community & Arts Funding Approved:

    $200,000 was allocated to health and human services for a past shortfall, $28,000 went to cultural event promotion, and a state grant was secured for a library Job Information Center.

  • New Ranching & Farming Land Use Policy:

    A resolution was passed to regulate land clearing activities for ranching and farming within city limits.
