Austin's Big Moves: Airport, Growth, Greenbelt

Thursday, December 19, 1991 regular

  • New Airport Scrapped, Current Airport Upgraded:

    The city voted to abandon the controversial $698 million Manor Airport project, ending plans for a new facility. Simultaneously, engineering was approved for significant improvements to Robert Mueller Municipal Airport.

  • Major City Expansion & Development:

    Multiple large areas, including Walnut Creek and parts of the Barton Creek Greenbelt, were approved for full annexation, significantly expanding the city's boundaries and laying groundwork for future growth.

  • Community & Environmental Investments:

    Over $5 million was allocated to dozens of social service agencies and a $1.2 million grant for infill housing projects. Funds were also approved for the Balcones Canyonlands Conservation Plan and new watershed protection rules.

  • Key Infrastructure & Economic Outreach:

    A $5.7 million natural gas pipeline for the Decker Power Plant was approved, alongside land acquisitions for the US 183/290 highway expansion. Contracts were also awarded to Hispanic and African-American Chambers of Commerce to boost tourism.
