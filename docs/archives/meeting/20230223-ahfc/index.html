ATX Housing Boost: Vouchers & Projects

Thursday, February 23, 2023 Austin Housing Finance Corporation (AHFC) Board of Directors Regular Meeting

  • Major Housing Boost:

    Approved contracts to provide 160 new local housing vouchers, significantly expanding Austin's permanent supportive housing options.

  • Karen Point at Cameron (District 4):

    Authorized development of 150 units, including 100 housing vouchers, through a partnership with Masino Group and Caritas of Austin.

  • The Roz Parkside (District 3):

    Approved plans for 100-110 units with 60 housing vouchers, developed in collaboration with Sagebrook USDA and New Hope Housing.
  • These initiatives aim to provide crucial affordable housing and support services for vulnerable residents in the city.
