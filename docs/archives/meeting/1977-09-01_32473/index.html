  • Consumer Protection Debate Rages:

    A controversial "Truth in Selling Ordinance," requiring zoning disclosure for homebuyers, faced repeal efforts amid public accusations of real estate industry influence on council members, leading to a scheduled public hearing.

  • Budget Battles for Key Services:

    Citizens vocally advocated for funding to keep Hancock Fire Station #12 and the Rosewood-Zaragosa Library open, maintain the Hancock Golf Course, and increase property tax exemptions for seniors during a lively budget hearing.
