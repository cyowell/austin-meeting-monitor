Austin: Water Expansion, Nightlife Controversy, City Growth

Thursday, January 26, 1967 regular

  • Major Water Infrastructure Project:

    A nearly $6 million contract was approved for Water Treatment Plant No. 3, poised to significantly expand Austin's water treatment capacity with the aid of $1.5 million in federal funding.

  • Social Morality and Public Standards:

    The Council received a letter from a local church commending police for addressing "indecent exposure" in nightclubs and explicitly opposing "topless" and "strip-tease" performances as degrading to community morals.

  • Strategic Urban Planning & Growth:

    Discussions advanced on establishing city-wide goals, land acquisition for future airport expansion, and exploring the relocation of the Civil Defense office for better efficiency.

  • Local Development & Community Amenities:

    The Council approved specific shoreline improvements on Lake Austin, new clubhouses for Disch Field, and continued to address citizen concerns, including power plant noise, traffic safety, and park maintenance.
