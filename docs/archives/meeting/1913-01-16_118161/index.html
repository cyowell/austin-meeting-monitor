Thursday, January 16, 1913 regular
Transcript

  • Austin's critical dam rebuilding project advanced:

    The city authorized a surveyor to map land to be flooded by the new dam and extended the contractors' timeline due to flood delays, acknowledging over $58,000 spent on the project.

  • Major infrastructure upgrades approved:

    The council appropriated $4,500 to purchase land for new City Stables and established permanent street grades for a section of Lavacca Street.

  • First petroleum storage permit issued:

    A permit was granted to the Pierce-Fordyce Oil Association for an underground 300-gallon gasoline tank and pump on West 6th Street, requiring strict engineering and fire marshal oversight.

  • Cemetery plot modified for monument:

    A crosswalk in Oakwood Cemetery was vacated for Dr. and Mrs. Maxwell to erect a monument, provided they construct a new public cement walkway.

The Council was called to order by the Mayor with all members present: The Mayor laid before the Council the following resolution : Be it resolved by the City Council of the City of Austin : That permit be and is hereby granted the Pierce-Fordyce Oil Association to install and maintain in the alley in the rear of No 203 West 6th street a gasoline tank ,for the purpose of storing gasoline.Said tank is to be undergroundBowser tank of 300 gallons capacity. Pierce Fordyce Oil Co. Permit It must not be placed less than two feet under ground,and installed under the supervision jointly of the City Engineer and Fire Marshal of this City. Authority is also granted to said Pierce-Fordyce Oil Association to instal a gasoline pump ,to be used in connection with said Gasoline tank,within the premises occupied by said Pierce-Fordyce Oil Association,at 203 West 6th street. The Mayor laid before the Council the following resolution : Be it resolved by the City Council of the City of Austin : That the east and west cross walkbetween lots 110 and 120 in Oakwood Cemetery ,which lots are owned by T O Maxwell and F A Maxwell ,of this City, is hereby vacated and authorized by the Council to be closed. Vacation Cemetary Lot Be it further resolved: That the owners of said lots be and they are hereby authorized to erect a monument in or about the center of said lots after said crosswalk is closed;provided, however,that if said cross walk is closed and said monument erected ,a cement walk must be constructed upon said crosswalk ,so that the public may conveniently cross the premises herein above described if they so desire. The resolution was adopted by the following vote : Yeas Mayor Wooldridge, Councilmen Bartholomew,Hart, Haynes & Powell 5 Nays none. Approved, A P Wooldridge, Mayor. The Council then adjourned . Geo.
