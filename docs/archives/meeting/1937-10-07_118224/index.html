New Gas Stations, Food Safety & Utility Upgrades

Thursday, October 7, 1937 regular
  • University students called for enhanced food safety, requesting stricter inspections of eateries near campus, which the Mayor agreed the City would fully support.
  • Two new drive-in gasoline filling stations were approved for construction at key intersections, signaling the city's expansion and growing infrastructure for motorists.
  • Significant utility upgrades were greenlit, allowing Southwestern Bell to install underground telephone conduits and Texas Public Service Company to lay new gas mains across various city streets.
  • A property owner sought to rezone land on West Sixth Street from residential to commercial, with the request referred for further review, indicating ongoing shifts in urban development.
