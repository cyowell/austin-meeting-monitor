Austin: Paving Streets, Suspending Fines

Thursday, August 6, 1914 regular

Here's a summary of the August 06, 1914 Austin City Council agenda:

  • Street Upgrades Approved:

    The council allocated $900 for essential extra work, including retaining walls and excavation, to complete the paving of alleys near Congress Avenue between 7th and 10th streets.

  • New Sidewalk Standards Proposed:

    An ordinance was introduced to establish uniform specifications for concrete sidewalks, curbing, and gutters, indicating a move towards standardized city infrastructure.

  • Citizen Damages Paid:

    A $50 payment was approved for Mrs. Matilda Miller to compensate her for damages to her property.

  • Vagrancy Fines Suspended:

    The fines for at least seven individuals—Bessie Gray, Mary Jackson, Laura Johnson, Hattie Brooks, Molley Graham, Annie Francis, and Debora Roberts—who were charged with vagrancy were suspended indefinitely, pending further council review.
