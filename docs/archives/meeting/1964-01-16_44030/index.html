Austin Tackles Rights, Traffic, Conventions

Thursday, January 16, 1964 regular

  • Addresses Human Relations:

    A "Citizens Committee" was established to study community human relations needs and group relationships, tasked with reporting recommendations to the city.

  • Boosts Convention Business:

    A new policy was approved to waive concession fees for large conventions using city facilities during July and August, immediately benefiting a 14,000-person Jehovah's Witnesses convention.

  • Major Traffic & Infrastructure Overhaul:

    The newly completed Montopolis Interchange facility's elliptical outer roadway was named "Levander Loop," and extensive one-way street patterns and revised speed limits were enacted across several East Austin roadways.

  • Expands City Footprint & Development:

    The city advanced the annexation of multiple school properties and large land tracts, alongside approving significant retail and apartment developments.
