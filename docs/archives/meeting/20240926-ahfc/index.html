Austin Fuels New Affordable Housing & Vouchers

Thursday, September 26, 2024 Austin Housing Finance Corporation (AHFC) Board of Directors Regular Meeting
  • Approved over $64.8 million in bonds for the "Sage at Franklin Park" affordable housing development.
  • Extended a $6 million contract for the local housing voucher program, helping residents afford rent.
  • Authorized a $674,000 loan to acquire a fourplex property on Riverstone Drive for housing.
  • Greenlit a new housing assistance contract for local vouchers at "Bungalows at Century Park."
