Austin's Bergstrom Base Redevelopment Begins

Monday, November 30, 1992 special called
  • An Aviation Sub-Committee met to discuss the future non-aviation reuse of Bergstrom Air Force Base.
  • Consultants presented initial concepts, market analysis, and potential land-use ideas for the redevelopment of the Bergstrom site.
  • The committee approved sending the preliminary goals and objectives for the Bergstrom reuse plan to the full City Council for a final vote.
