New Bridge, Gas Pump Rules, and City Leadership

Thursday, July 6, 1916 regular

  • Infrastructure Investment:

    Approved significant funds for public works, including $10,000 for a new reinforced arch bridge over Waller Creek on East 19th Street and allocations for sewer line construction on West 12th Street and Rio Grande Street.

  • Early Gas Station Regulation:

    Granted a permit for Thomson Motor Car Co. to install a 280-gallon underground gasoline tank and a pump on East 5th Street, establishing specific rules for placement and hose storage.

  • Leadership Change:

    Accepted the resignation of the Mayor Pro Tem and elected a new individual to the position.
