Austin Housing Code: Density, Affordability, Fees

Tuesday, December 10, 2019 Austin City Council Special Called Meeting
  • Council continued debating Austin's new Land Development Code, focusing on key housing affordability policies.
  • Extensive discussion covered preserving existing Vertical Mixed-Use (VMU) affordability standards (e.g., 10% affordable units at 60% Median Family Income) and establishing annual reviews to calibrate all affordability programs for maximum impact.
  • A heated debate took place regarding "missing middle housing" (duplexes, triplexes, etc.), weighing whether increased density should require on-site affordable units or if developer fees are a more effective approach for citywide affordability.
  • A proposal to implement monitoring fees for developers using affordable housing bonuses was tabled, due to concerns it could create new barriers to constructing affordable homes.
