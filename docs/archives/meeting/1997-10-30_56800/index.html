Austin's Future: Land, Tech, & Community Needs

Thursday, October 30, 1997 regular

  • Major Annexation Debates:

    Public hearings were held to consider significant city expansions, including the Four Points area, IH-35 South, Carson Creek, and Thompson Lane.

  • Community Health & Safety:

    New measures included approving a 20 mph school zone near T.A. Brown Elementary. The Council also reallocated funds for HIV services and a mental health housing grant, while a citizen raised concerns about sewage spreading on golf courses within drinking water watersheds.

  • Future Tech & Development:

    A new Commission on Information Management and Technology was established, signaling a focus on digital governance. Additionally, zoning was advanced for a new H.E.B. grocery store and historic preservation for the Wroe-Bustin House.

  • Affordable Housing Focus:

    A key proposal concerning the development of 48 additional low-income rental housing units was postponed for further discussion.
