Austin Budget: Pay Hike, Tech Warning, Fee Fight

Wednesday, September 14, 2016 Austin City Council Regular Meeting (Budget Reading)

  • City Employees Get Pay Bump:

    Council voted to spend $127,500 to accelerate a merit pay increase for city workers.

  • Urgent Data Center Risks:

    Staff warned of "huge risks" to core city systems due to the aging 22-year-old data center, highlighting the critical need for its multi-year relocation and cautioning against further budget delays.

  • Utility Fee Hikes Questioned:

    Council members debated freezing planned increases for water, waste, drainage, and transportation utility fees, challenging departments to maintain current rates.

  • Funds Reallocated & Social Priorities:

    $130,961 was moved from the Second Street TIF fund to the general budget. Discussions also covered allocating funds for I-35 underpass restrooms, and boosting support for health services, senior tax exemptions, and minority contractor programs.
