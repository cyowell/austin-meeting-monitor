Austin's New Fire Chief, Street Name, Sidewalks

Thursday, February 24, 1916 regular

  • Professionalizing Fire Services:

    The city initiated a transition from a volunteer fire department to a paid one, appointing its first salaried Fire Chief.

  • Honoring a Public Servant:

    A newly opened street was officially named "Powell Street" to recognize P.W. Powell, a long-serving city official.

  • Shaping Urban Landscape:

    New sewer lines were approved for construction, and a petition to narrow sidewalks for a housing development was granted to create wider roadways.

  • Managing City Funds:

    Several appropriations were made to cover interest charges on recent general fund overdrafts.
