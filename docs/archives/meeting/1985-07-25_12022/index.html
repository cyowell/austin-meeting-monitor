Austin's Future: Parks, Economy, and Homelessness

Thursday, July 25, 1985 regular

  • Economic Development Commission Formed

    Following a public hearing, an 11-member commission was created to advise on city growth and business initiatives.

  • New Parkland Dedication Policy

    Council passed an ordinance requiring developers to dedicate land for parks or pay fees, after extensive debate on its impact on affordable housing.

  • Homeless Shelter Site Proposed

    A task force recommended a specific downtown location for a homeless shelter, initiating a City Manager review and a future public hearing.

  • Brackenridge Hospital Layoffs

    Discussions addressed significant workforce reductions at Brackenridge Hospital due to financial strains, prompting a mayoral proposal for increased Council oversight on future layoffs.

  • Key Budget Allocations & Services

    Over $1.6 million was allocated to the Medical Assistance Program, and commercial garbage rates were updated for specific downtown commercial districts.
