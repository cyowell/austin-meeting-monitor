Austin's 1905 Street Repairs and Infrastructure Spending

Monday, January 16, 1905 regular

• Major infrastructure investments approved: Council appropriated nearly $3,000 for various city improvements including $750 for cemetery improvements, $500 for South Congress Avenue repairs, and $300 for gravel on the Speedway.

• Fire protection expanded: Council authorized $6,000 to build a new North Austin Fire Company hall (with funds not available until May 1905), though one alderman voted against it.

• Street and sanitation focus: Multiple spending bills passed for street labor, ditch cleaning on San Antonio Street, and repairs to Frost Street, reflecting the council's priority on city maintenance.

• Charter amendment proposed: City leaders moved to amend Austin's charter to give the city council more power to determine street improvements, sidewalk construction, and assessment authority—essentially expanding municipal control over infrastructure projects.

• Routine business: Council approved monthly salaries for aldermen ($140), city officers ($2,800), and sanitary workers ($325), along with standard financial reports and committee recommendations.
