Austin's Future: Homeless Aid, Airport, Green Rules

Thursday, April 6, 2000 Austin City Council Regular Meeting

  • Support for Homeless Services:

    The city committed over $4 million in funding and fee waivers to construct a new Salvation Army homeless center and clinic.

  • Major Urban Redevelopment:

    Plans advanced for the significant redevelopment of the former Robert Mueller Municipal Airport site, and a large "Traditional Neighborhood Development" in northeast Austin received city incentives.

  • Environmental Policy Shifts:

    New land development rules were adopted, including updates to impervious cover calculations, construction on slopes, and provisions allowing some redevelopment to supersede parts of the Save Our Springs Initiative.

  • Public Infrastructure Upgrades:

    Major funding was approved for a regional emergency communications center, a new district cooling plant for city facilities, and continued improvements to Barton Springs Pool.
