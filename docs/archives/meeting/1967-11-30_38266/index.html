Austin Growth: New Zones, Annexations, Tech

Thursday, November 30, 1967 regular

  • Significant Land Use Changes Approved:

    The city moved to rezone numerous properties, often converting residential areas to allow for denser housing, commercial developments, or even light industrial use, with some decisions including specific development conditions.

  • City Boundaries Expand:

    Several annexations were advanced, including a large 3,881-acre territory slated for a future public hearing, indicating ongoing city expansion efforts.

  • Key Infrastructure & Modernization:

    Approvals were granted for a new sewer line installation, a grant application for airport runway improvements, and a lease agreement for updated data processing equipment for city operations.
