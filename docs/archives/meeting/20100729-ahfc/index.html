Oltorf Housing Loan Nixed, New Housing Leaders

Thursday, July 29, 2010 Austin Housing Finance Corporation (AHFC) Board of Directors AHFC Meeting

  • Housing Finance Leadership Updates:

    New officers were appointed to the Austin Housing Finance Corporation.

  • Affordable Housing Board Appointments:

    Directors were appointed to the boards of several non-profit subsidiaries that manage local affordable housing projects (e.g., Arbors at Creekside, Villas on Sixth).

  • $1.17M Housing Loan Revoked:

    A previously approved $1.17 million loan for a proposed multi-family development on East Oltorf Street was rescinded.
