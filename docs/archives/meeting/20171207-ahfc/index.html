Austin funds key affordable housing projects

Thursday, December 7, 2017 Austin Housing Finance Corporation (AHFC) Board of Directors AHFC Meeting
  • Approved financing for two significant affordable housing initiatives: the Govalle Affordability Housing Project and the Housing First Oak Springs Project.
  • Advanced the multi-year Govalle affordable housing development in the "Think East" area, including securing funds for necessary street improvements.
  • Authorized multi-family housing revenue bonds to support the Housing First Oak Springs Project, located at 3001 Oak Springs Drive.
