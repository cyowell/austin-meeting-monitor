Austin: Water Quality, Paramount Theatre, Gas Bills

Thursday, June 13, 1991 regular

Here's a concise look at key decisions from the Austin City Council meeting on June 13, 1991:

  • Environmental Protection:

    The Council began amending water quality regulations for urban watersheds, introducing new rules for development and a formula for payments in lieu of structural stormwater controls.

  • Cultural Investment:

    $2 million was approved for the Paramount Theatre project, with conditions including the acquisition of the State Theatre and a 25% matching fund requirement.

  • Utility Rate Changes:

    Southern Union Gas Company received approval for rate increases, adjusting customer charges for both residential and small commercial accounts.

  • Jurisdictional & Land Decisions:

    Over 2,100 acres of Austin's extraterritorial jurisdiction were released to the City of Pflugerville, and land was approved for sale to Travis County for a new medical examiner facility.

  • Major Lawsuit Settlement:

    The city approved a $230,000 settlement for a wrongful death lawsuit.
