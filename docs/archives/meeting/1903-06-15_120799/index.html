Austin's 1903 Infrastructure & Building Permits

Monday, June 15, 1903 regular

• Building Permits & Safety: Council reviewed applications for building permits within fire limits, including for the Lone Star Ice Company and Beasley & Harris, requiring Fire Commission approval for public safety.

• Streetwork & Drainage: Approved $225 for opening 5th Street to Bowie Street and $150 for cleaning drainage ditches and pipes throughout the city to improve infrastructure.

• Bond Financial Management: Finance Committee completed a sophisticated bond exchange and purchase totaling $1,725 in interest, with special committee authorized to destroy old bonds and coupons for record-keeping.

• Public Refunds & Fees: Multiple citizens and businesses (pound fees, pawn fees) successfully petitioned to have various municipal charges refunded; Low Shan Brewing Co. had its tax assessment corrected.

• Police Matters: Council confirmed J.L. Brodie as a new policeman following the death of Officer W.A. Bates; approved $250 appropriation for dog catchers' salaries.
