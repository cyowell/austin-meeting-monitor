Austin Adjusts Property Taxes, Drive-in Values

Thursday, November 4, 1954 special called

  • Resolved numerous property tax appeals

    from residents and organizations throughout the city.

  • Significantly reduced the assessed land value for the Delwood Drive-in theater

    , after an extensive debate comparing its assessment to other local drive-ins.

  • Modified the "obsolescence factor"

    used in property tax calculations, leading to a lower assessed value for the Texas Federation of Women's Clubs property.

  • Acknowledged a systemic inequity in property assessments

    for lots facing side streets versus commercial streets, with a commitment to address and correct these discrepancies in the following year.
