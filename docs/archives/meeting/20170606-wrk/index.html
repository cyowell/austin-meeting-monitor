ATX Zoning & Park Events Spark Debate

Tuesday, June 6, 2017 Austin City Council Work Session

  • CodeNEXT Zoning Rewrite:

    Intense debate continues over the tight timeline and complexity of Austin's massive zoning overhaul, with calls for more public input time and clearer explanations of how proposed changes will affect neighborhood character, housing affordability, and existing plans.

  • Park Event Limits & Vending:

    Discussions focused on implementing new limits for event days at popular parks like Zilker, Festival Beach, and Auditorium Shores, alongside concerns about event parking solutions, fair event classification, and strengthening enforcement against illegal vending.

  • Fire Department Merger Oversight:

    A new proposal was presented to add Council representatives to the coordinating committee for the Emergency Services District 4 (ESD4) merger, aiming to ensure direct taxpayer oversight and address future annexation concerns.
