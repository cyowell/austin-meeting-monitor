Tuesday, November 10, 2015 Austin City Council Work Session
Official City Record Agenda Watch Video Transcript
  • City leaders denied annexation for Old Lampasas Trail, siding with 100% of property owners who opposed joining the city due to concerns about environmental protections, existing services, and local control.
  • Initiated efforts to develop new policies aimed at protecting tenants from displacement caused by development, signaling a renewed focus on affordable housing challenges.
  • Council members pushed for greater transparency and review of various city contracts, including an amendment for federal healthcare enrollment services and a contract related to a Pakistan embassy.
  • Most other annexation items were approved or moved forward, and one zoning request was postponed.
