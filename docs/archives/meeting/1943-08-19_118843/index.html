War Bonds, Navy Drills, & Austin Property Zoning

Thursday, August 19, 1943 regular

  • Supporting the War Effort:

    A daily street closure was approved for the University of Texas Navy V-12 unit's morning calisthenics, and a proclamation for an upcoming War Bond Rally was set.

  • Zoning Debates:

    Discussions were held on land use, including a proposal to revert a property near Congress Avenue and 15th Street from commercial to residential, and the deferral of a large residential-to-commercial rezoning request in the Highlands Subdivision.

  • City Planning Appointments:

    Two new members were appointed to fill vacancies on the City Plan Commission.
