Thursday, July 14, 1994 regular
Transcript

  • Holly Power Plant Future:

    Faced with strong citizen demands for immediate closure due to health and safety, the council set a goal to reduce the plant's operations and expedite acquiring replacement energy.

  • Urban Development & Livability:

    Key policy decisions aimed to fund a Central City Entertainment Center, plan for sidewalk cafes to enhance urban areas, and establish a citizen group to update the city's Master Plan.

  • Public Health & Safety Initiatives:

    The city secured significant grant funding for critical HIV/AIDS and tuberculosis elimination programs, and approved a mutual aid agreement for emergency management with Travis County.

  • Community & Operational Debates:

    Public hearings were held on extending the city's nighttime curfew, while the council debated and approved a large cellular phone contract for city employees and moved to rename a street.
