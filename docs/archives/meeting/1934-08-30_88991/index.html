Austin Expands Sewers with Federal Aid

Thursday, August 30, 1934 regular

  • Secured Federal Funding for Sewer Expansion:

    The city approved a $175,000 federal loan and grant from the Public Works Administration to extend sanitary sewer systems into unserved residential areas.

  • Approved New Utility Infrastructure:

    Permits were issued for Southwestern Bell to install new telephone pole lines and for Texas Public Service Company to lay gas mains, supporting city growth.

  • Allocated Funds for City Operations:

    Monies were appropriated from various funds for purposes such as securing easements for the sewer disposal plant and supplementing city claims accounts.

  • Granted Taxicab Permits:

    Two new taxicab permits were approved, notably one with probationary status due to the applicant's prior minor traffic violations.

  • Began Discussions on Land Development:

    New ordinances concerning the approval of a subdivision plat and the vacation of streets in another area were introduced for future consideration.
