Austin's City Vision: Roads, Zoning, & Concerts

Thursday, February 26, 1948 regular

  • Roads and City Services:

    Funds were allocated for grading and graveling roads and a park's parking area. The city also cancelled an order for street sweepers to purchase a more versatile truck-mounted street flusher.

  • Major Zoning Changes:

    Approved significant rezonings, including shifting some residential areas to industrial and commercial designations to allow for development like a tourist court expansion and a floral shop.

  • Public Space & Commercial Use:

    Permits were granted for a lumber company to build a sidewalk loading platform and for a demolition project to temporarily occupy sidewalk space, both with strict safety and liability requirements.

  • Traffic & Community Life:

    Updated traffic ordinances to refine signal light, stop sign, and parking regulations. The city also funded a public concert by the Austin Symphony Orchestra.
