Austin Housing Boost: $13M for Homes

Thursday, April 7, 2005 Austin Housing Finance Corporation (AHFC) Board of Directors AHFC Meeting

  • Major Affordable Housing Investment:

    Over $13.6 million was approved to fund critical housing and community development programs for Austin residents.

  • Diverse Resident Support:

    Funds are allocated to assist with rental costs, help first-time homebuyers, and provide essential home repairs, including accessibility upgrades.

  • Addressing Key Safety and Quality Needs:

    Specific initiatives include removing hazardous lead paint from homes and supporting various neighborhood improvement projects.

  • Benefiting Thousands of Households:

    These programs are designed to provide direct housing assistance and improvements to hundreds, potentially thousands, of local families.
