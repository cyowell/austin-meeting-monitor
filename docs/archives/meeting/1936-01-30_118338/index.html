Thursday, January 30, 1936 regular

  • Boosted Commercial Development:

  • Expanded City Utilities:

A trial was had and the evidence showed to the court that defendant was guilty; defendant paid his fine, and seemed to be satisfied. This fine was paid into the City Treasury.
