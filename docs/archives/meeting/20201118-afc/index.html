Wednesday, November 18, 2020 Audit and Finance Committee Regular Meeting
Official City Record Agenda Watch Video Transcript
  • An audit revealed the city could save money and improve compliance by centralizing software license management, creating a citywide inventory, and better tracking usage. The CIO's office is already developing a system for this.
  • Approved minor administrative updates to the bylaws for the Parks and Recreation Board (renaming a committee) and the Mayor's Committee for People with Disabilities.
  • Received an overview of the City Auditor's role in promoting transparency, accountability, and continuous improvement, highlighting how residents can access audit reports and recommendations online.
