City Funds, Congress Ave Garage, New Officer

Thursday, January 5, 1922 regular

  • Emergency Funds Allocated:

    The city approved $72,500 in temporary emergency appropriations for key departments, including streets, police, fire, hospital, and water/light/power, pending the finalization of the annual budget.

  • Congress Avenue Garage Review:
