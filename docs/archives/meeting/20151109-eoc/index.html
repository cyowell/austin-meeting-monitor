  • Coyote Management Debate:

    A representative from Lyft highlighted the significant economic opportunities provided by ride-sharing services, particularly for underserved communities, and urged supportive regulations.

    Extensive discussion was held on a "Ban the Box" ordinance, aimed at removing criminal history questions from initial job applications to aid ex-offenders. Businesses voiced concerns about new regulations, record-keeping burdens, and legal liabilities.
