Thursday, July 28, 1932 regular
Transcript

  • New Drug Store Parking on Brazos:

    Approved a permit allowing the Austin Labor Temple Association to set back the curb on Brazos Street, creating more parking for a new drug store. This approval came with strict conditions on construction, materials, and liability.

  • Shoal Creek Park Plans Unveiled:

    Preliminary plans for developing the Shoal Creek project were presented by the Park Board and a landscape architect, along with a request for budget revisions (action deferred).

  • Property Tax Reassessment:

    Authorized a significant reduction in property tax assessments for a specific property (Cora Weaver Collier's) from 1921-1931, acknowledging an over-assessment and dilapidated conditions.

  • Hyde Park Playground Water Upgrade:

    Approved $150 to improve the water system at Hyde Park Playground, addressing issues with undersized pipes and connecting to a new water main.
