Bonds, Bias, & Busy Streets

Thursday, July 26, 1951 regular

  • Secured Major Funding:

    Austin accepted a bid for $2.8 million in General Obligation Bonds, providing significant capital for city projects and ongoing development.

  • Confronted Racial Inequity:

    Public dialogues revealed challenges of racial segregation, including unequal access to main library resources for "colored people" and a controversial taxi franchise transfer that shifted cabs from a "colored quota."

  • Approved Sweeping Traffic & Infrastructure Plans:

    Extensive upgrades were greenlit, encompassing new traffic signals, stop signs, speed limits, and bus routes, along with purchasing pipes for a critical water main to Bergstrom Air Force Base.

  • Set Annual Tax Rates & Debated Zoning:

    The municipal and school tax rates for 1951 were finalized after discussions on property valuation, while several zoning change requests for commercial and residential areas were considered or decided.
