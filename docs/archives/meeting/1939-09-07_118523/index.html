Austin's Transit Transformation & New Builds

Thursday, September 7, 1939 regular

  • Transit Revolution:

    The city council moved forward with renewing the Austin Street Railway Company's franchise, setting the stage for a city-wide shift from streetcars to a modern bus service and addressing public demand to remove old tracks.

  • New Fire Station Planned:

    Architects were authorized to begin designing a new fire station to be built at the corner of East Second and Chicon Streets.

  • Zoning Changes Approved:

    Despite a recommendation against "spot zoning" from an advisory board, the council approved commercial zoning reclassifications for properties on East Tenth Street and near the Grant Chapel A.M.E. Church, expanding commercial districts.

  • Utility Infrastructure Boost:

    Key permits were granted for significant utility expansions across the city, including extensive new gas mains, telephone pole lines, and an important sewer line crossing under railroad tracks.
