Austin Plans Huge Infrastructure Vote

Monday, December 3, 1979 special called

  • Bond Election Scheduled:

    Austin leaders voted to hold a special bond election on February 23, 1980, aiming to fund essential infrastructure and new public facilities.

  • Major Funding Priorities:

    The proposed bond package earmarked over $77 million for water and wastewater upgrades, aimed to double parkland acquisition across the city, and included $19 million for a new civic/convention center, alongside $4.1 million for railroad crossing safety improvements.

  • Contentious New Needs:

    Significant debate occurred over including funds for a new hospital parking garage and airport enhancements, with questions raised about the timing of the election (less than a year after the last one) and whether revenue bonds could be used instead of general obligation bonds for some projects.
