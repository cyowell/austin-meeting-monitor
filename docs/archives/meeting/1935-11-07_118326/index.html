Thursday, November 7, 1935 regular
Transcript

  • New Drive-In Gas Stations Approved:

    Two new drive-in gasoline filling stations, complete with grease racks, were approved for construction on Navasota Street and at Guadalupe and West 28th Streets, with strict conditions for drainage, construction, and waste disposal to prevent spills onto city sidewalks.

  • Arson Reward Paid:

    A $300 reward was distributed among four individuals for their assistance in a 1935 arson case, leading to the arrest and conviction of those responsible. The City Attorney was also directed to amend the existing arson reward resolution.

  • Major Utility Expansions:

    Permits were issued for Southwestern Bell Telephone to install underground conduits on Barton Springs and Fredericksburg Roads, and for Texas Public Service Company to lay new gas mains across Fredericksburg Road, West Fourteenth Street, and Rosedale Avenue.

  • Nurses' Home Funding:

    Over $7,200 was appropriated from the General Fund to advance payments for the construction of the Nurses' Home.
