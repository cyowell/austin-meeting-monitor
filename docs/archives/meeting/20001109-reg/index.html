Roads Through Parks & Huge New Center

Thursday, November 9, 2000 Austin City Council Regular Meeting

  • Major Urban Developments:

    Authorized a nearly $39 million contract to build the Town Lake Park Community Events Center and Parking Facility. Additionally, plans for the Downtown Public Improvement District advanced with approval of a $1.4 million budget for downtown revitalization.

  • Parks and Land Use Decisions:

    Approved a controversial agreement to route a section of Barton Springs Road through the dedicated parkland of Umlauf Sculpture Gardens. They also moved to settle a significant lawsuit concerning the "Sand Beach Reserve," impacting future land use.

  • Community and Green Initiatives:

    Extended a drainage fee exemption for religious organizations providing housing for the homeless. The council also backed over $800,000 for childcare programs and approved more than $145,000 in energy efficiency rebates for large properties.

  • Zoning Dispute Resolution:

    Denied an appeal against a conditional use permit for the Hyde Park Baptist Church Quarries Multi-Use Facility, while imposing conditions for emergency access and continued engagement with neighboring residents.
