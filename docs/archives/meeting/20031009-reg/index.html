Annexation, Smoking & Sign Regulations

Thursday, October 9, 2003 Austin City Council Regular Meeting

  • Public Health & Environment:

    Received recommendations from the Smoking Ordinance Task Force on public health policy. Approved a significant federal grant for a "Steps to a Healthier U.S." program and authorized the acquisition of land for new parks and to protect open spaces, including in the sensitive Barton Springs area.

  • City Growth & Development:

    Set public hearings for the full annexation of multiple large tracts of land, signaling future city expansion. The council also approved engineering for the extension of the James D. Pfluger Bicycle and Pedestrian Bridge and various other infrastructure improvements.

  • Community Rules & Events:

    Held a public hearing and approved updates to the City's sign regulations, a topic that generated citizen comments. Fees were also waived for several community events, including the Veteran's Day Parade and a Mothers Against Drunk Driving (MADD) charity run.
