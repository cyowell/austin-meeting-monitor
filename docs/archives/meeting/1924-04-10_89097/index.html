Paving, Train Sheds & Tax Relief in Austin

Thursday, April 10, 1924 regular
  • A major contract was awarded for resurfacing 75,000 square yards of paved streets, and authorization was given for the Houston & Texas Central Railroad to construct a new train shed downtown.
  • Multiple errors in 1923 property tax valuations were corrected, leading to reductions for individuals, a coffee company, and a seminary, with overpayments refunded.
  • A permit was granted for a new cement bathroom construction at a residence, reflecting local building oversight.
  • The city extended an invitation to the Sons of Hermann fraternal organization to hold their next convention in Austin, and resolved a court case allowing existing sidewalks to remain.
