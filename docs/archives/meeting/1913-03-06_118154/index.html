Austin Paves Streets, Adjusts Taxes, Settles Suit

Thursday, March 6, 1913 regular

Here's a summary of the Austin City Council agenda:

  • Major Street Upgrades & Paving:

    The Council approved significant infrastructure projects, including improvements for West 6th, East 5th, and San Jacinto streets, finalizing the paving of 19th Street, and funding the paving of Lavaca Street, notably a section near the Governor's Mansion.

  • New Tax Assessment Policy:

    A city-wide policy was adopted to standardize the valuation of corporate securities, notes, and banking capital at 80% of their face value for tax purposes, aiming for fair and equal taxation.

  • Preparing for City Growth & Public Order:

    A "Cemetery Purchase Fund" was established with over $6,800 specifically for acquiring additional land, indicating plans for future city expansion. An ordinance to prohibit "fowls" (like chickens) from running at large in a designated district was also introduced.

  • Resolving a Property Lawsuit:

    To settle a lawsuit with Mrs. Erin Rutherford, the city agreed to extensive property improvements including building a retaining wall, curbing, steps, and sidewalks around her property, in addition to covering court costs and a small cash payment.
