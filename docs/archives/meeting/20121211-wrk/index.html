Tuesday, December 11, 2012 Austin City Council Work Session
  • A city official challenged health department regulations making it difficult for a community group to provide free water on popular running trails, pushing for more practical solutions.
  • Plans for a $5 million multipurpose theater, designed to revitalize an urban neighborhood, were delayed due to disagreements over whether its entertainment uses complied with existing zoning laws.
