Austin Debates Park Limits, Immigrant Aid, City Mgr

Thursday, June 8, 2017 Austin City Council Regular Meeting

  • Immigrant Legal Aid Debate:

    The Council discussed a grant for immigrant legal defense, specifically questioning whether to use an existing contractor or open the process to competitive bids for services critical in light of state law SB4.

  • City Manager Search Plan:

    Concerns were raised regarding the proposed timeline for public input meetings in the search for Austin's next city manager, aiming to balance community engagement with attracting a strong pool of candidates.

  • Parkland Event Restrictions:

    A proposed ordinance to limit the number of event days in major parks, such as Zilker and Auditorium Shores, was discussed, with supporters emphasizing the need to preserve public access and green spaces.
