Austin Preps for Sales Tax Election

Wednesday, September 13, 1967 special called
  • Focused entirely on administrative preparations for an upcoming special election.
  • Appointed election judges and clerks for over 40 voting precincts across the city.
  • The special election concerned a city-wide vote on a proposed sales tax.
  • The sales tax election was scheduled to take place just over two weeks later, on September 30, 1967.
