Taxi Crackdown, Big Power Plant, Zoning Reform

Thursday, December 16, 1948 regular

  • Zoning Overhaul & Development Decisions:

    The Council began a public hearing on significant amendments to zoning, including reclassifying commercial districts for alcohol sales and allowing veterinary hospitals with special permits. They also weighed a large industrial zoning request for the Jefferson Chemical Company, with advisors suggesting commercial use to protect residential areas, and denied another commercial rezoning due to neighborhood opposition and traffic concerns. The city also moved to annex 141 acres for future growth.

  • Infrastructure Investment:

    A major contract worth $560,113 was approved for two new 200,000 lbs/hr steam generating units, a substantial investment in the city's power capacity.

  • Social Issues & Employee Welfare:

    The City ordered stricter enforcement against uninsured "Negro bootleg taxicabs." Additionally, all city employees earning under $350 per month were granted a $10 monthly salary increase, effective January 1, 1949.
