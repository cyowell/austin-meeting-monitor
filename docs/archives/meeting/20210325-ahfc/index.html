Austin Greenlights Senior Affordable Housing Project

Thursday, March 25, 2021 Austin Housing Finance Corporation (AHFC) Board of Directors AHFC Meeting

  • Senior Housing Project Advances:

    Final financing was approved for "City Heights," a 179-unit affordable senior housing development on Nuckols Crossing, following extensive debate.

  • Safety Concerns Addressed:

    The project moved forward despite community concerns about traffic safety and infrastructure on the existing "substandard" Nuckols Crossing road.

  • Major Road Improvements Promised:

    To alleviate safety concerns, the city committed to significant upgrades including road widening, new turn lanes, sidewalks, and an all-way stop at a key intersection.

  • Additional Senior Housing Funding:

    Initial funding was also approved for a separate $9 million senior housing development by the Guadalupe Neighborhood Development Corporation.
