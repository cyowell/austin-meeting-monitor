Austin Eyes Growth: Civil Rights, Bonds, New Parks

Thursday, February 16, 1956 regular

  • Civil Rights Debate:

    Activists from the NAACP urged the Council to clarify policy on bus seating rights and police conduct regarding segregation.

  • Major Infrastructure Investment:

    Plans were presented for $28 million in bonds to fund critical city improvements, including streets, sewers, water, electricity, fire stations, and airport expansion, without increasing tax or utility rates.

  • Youth Baseball Fields Approved:

    The Council approved Junior Baseball, Inc.'s proposal to build new Pony and Colt League fields at Northwest Park, with the organization providing construction and the city supplying water and electricity.

  • Rapid City Expansion Concerns:

    Zoning changes and annexation of 67 acres were finalized, while the Mayor also addressed public safety issues arising from abandoned wells in the quickly growing city.
