Austin Tax Battle: Home Values & Schools

Tuesday, December 15, 1959 special called
  • The special meeting was dedicated entirely to hearing numerous public appeals from property owners disputing their tax valuations, asserting their assessments were unfairly high.
  • Residents newly annexed into the school district strongly protested their property valuations, citing unaffordable taxes and water rates. The Mayor acknowledged the school district's significant debt and investment in new facilities within the area.
  • Notable appeals included a fraternity seeking tax exemption for an educational building and homeowners challenging valuations due to environmental factors like drainage issues, utility easements, or unfavorable neighborhood views.
  • The Council largely deferred decisions, opting to thoroughly study the presented cases, personally inspect many of the disputed properties, and consult legal counsel on complex exemption requests.
