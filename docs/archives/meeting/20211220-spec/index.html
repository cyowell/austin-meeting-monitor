Austin Waterfront: TIRZ Plan & Housing Focus

Monday, December 20, 2021 Austin City Council Special Called Meeting

  • South Central Waterfront Development Advances:

    Council approved the initial steps for a Tax Increment Reinvestment Zone (TIRZ) in the South Central Waterfront, setting the stage for future development discussions.

  • Affordable Housing Mandate Added:

    The preliminary plan was amended to explicitly include affordable housing as a priority for the TIRZ, with further detailed planning expected to align with city policy.

  • Initial Tax Capture Set at 0% for Now:

    The TIRZ's tax increment capture rate was temporarily set to zero, allowing for more detailed financial planning, public input, and clarification on public benefits in early 2022 before final funding decisions.

  • City's Preservation Officer Honored:

    The meeting began with a special proclamation recognizing Steve Sadowsky, Austin's retiring Historic Preservation Officer, for his two decades of service, declaring December 20th "Steve Sadowsky Day."
