Title: Austin Budget Work Session on Social Services

  • Held a Budget Work Session:

    The Council convened a work session to hear public input on the proposed budget, specifically focusing on social services, community health, and homelessness and housing initiatives. This meeting was for discussion and testimony, not for voting on budget items.

  • Heard Widespread Opposition to Social Service Cuts:

    Numerous residents and non-profit leaders spoke passionately against proposed reductions to social service contracts. They highlighted the critical work of organizations providing support for domestic violence survivors (like SAFE Alliance), mental health services (Integral Care, Austin Child Guidance Center), immigrant legal aid, violence prevention (At Peace CVI), and family stabilization grants.

  • Debated Police Budget vs. Social Spending:

    A recurring theme in public comment was the contrast between proposed cuts to social services and a potential $25 million increase for the Austin Police Department. Many speakers urged the Council to reallocate funds, keep the police budget flat, and prioritize "upstream" investments in community care to prevent crises.

  • Criticism of Homeless Encampment Sweeps:

    Community members, including those experiencing homelessness, voiced strong opposition to the City's ongoing encampment sweeps. They described the sweeps as ineffective, costly, and harmful, advocating for increased investment in housing services, dignified shelters, and direct assistance instead.

  • Concerns Raised Over Audit and Utility Rates:

    Speakers also raised concerns about the validity and impact of a recent audit of non-profit contracts, arguing it should not lead to indiscriminate funding cuts. Additionally, appeals were made to prevent an increase in Austin Energy rates and to ensure a more thorough review process for any such changes.

  • No Decisions Made:

    The Council did not take any votes, nor did they approve any ordinances, contracts, or policies at this meeting. They did, however, outline a detailed schedule for upcoming budget work sessions, a public hearing to set the maximum tax rate on July 30th, and final budget approval meetings scheduled for August 12-14.
