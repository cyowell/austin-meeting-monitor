Austin Focus: Environment, Utility Costs, City Growth

Thursday, August 20, 1987 regular

  • Environmental Protection Stands Strong:

    Waivers from the Comprehensive Watersheds Ordinance were denied for Fairway Estates and Gill Ranch, prioritizing environmental protection over certain developments.

  • Key Utility Decisions:

    The city approved a rate increase for Southern Union Gas Company and moved forward with a 20-year public utility franchise for Lone Star Gas.

  • Major Infrastructure and Social Investment:

    Over $19 million was allocated for the Walnut Creek Wastewater Treatment Plant expansion, and plans for enlarging the Garner House juvenile facility were made a top priority.

  • Speed Zones Adjusted:

    Several speed limits across the city, including on IH-35, Jones Road, and Pleasant Valley Road, were formally modified.
