Austin: Baseball Dreams, Daylight, New Safety Rules

Thursday, April 25, 1940 regular

  • Major League Baseball Field Planned

    Officials approved negotiations to acquire land at the Austin Dam for a potential baseball field, proposed for training major league teams.

  • Daylight Saving Time Debate

    The council discussed a petition for Daylight Saving Time and opted to poll citizens at an upcoming election to gauge broader public opinion.

  • New Building Safety Codes

    An ordinance passed to enhance building safety, requiring extended stairways in taller buildings and additional standpipes/scuttles in multi-story structures.

  • New Gas Station Approved

    A permit was granted for a drive-in gasoline station, with specific conditions regarding drainage and its location near a public high school.
