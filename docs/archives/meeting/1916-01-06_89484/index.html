Thursday, January 6, 1916 regular
Transcript

  • City Land Purchase:

    Acquired a lot for $250 to be used for future street, bridge, and storm sewer development.

  • Fines Remitted:

    Forgave fines and costs for two individuals caught "unlawfully riding a train," citing "justifiable reasons."

  • Red River Street Upgrades:

    Set precise new elevation grades for Red River Street between 6th and 16th streets, repealing previous standards.

  • Early Gas Station Rules:

    Issued a permit for an underground gasoline tank and curb-side pump on Guadalupe Street, establishing new safety and operational requirements, including mandatory evening lighting.
