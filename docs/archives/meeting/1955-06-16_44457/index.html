Austin's Expanding Footprint: Roads, River, Retail

Thursday, June 16, 1955 regular

  • Launched a new "voluntary" paving program

    , committing city funds for engineering and intersection paving while requiring property owners to petition for street improvements, backed by a $100,000 budget allocation.

  • Initiated ambitious riverfront and recreation plans

    , including developing boat docks under bridges, cleaning the riverbed, and making sand beaches usable, with dedicated funds for these projects.

  • Approved city expansion and extensive land use changes

    , annexing 45.4 acres and allowing numerous zoning shifts from residential to commercial or retail, actively shaping Austin's urban development.

  • Addressed essential city services and business needs

    , such as awarding a significant milk and cream contract for city hospitals and permitting private gasoline plants for local companies.
