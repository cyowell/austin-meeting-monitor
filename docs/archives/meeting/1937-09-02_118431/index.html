Austin's PWA Projects, New Bus Routes, & Zoning

Thursday, September 2, 1937 regular

  • Major Public Works Projects Advance:

    Architects were nominated for significant Public Works Administration (PWA) projects, including a new Central Fire Station and improvements to City Hall.

  • Infrastructure & Transit Upgrades:

    The city approved new gas main installations on West 38th Street and a revised route for the Oil Mill Bus Line to enhance neighborhood service.

  • Zoning and Development Approvals:

    Several zoning changes were discussed and approved (including commercial rezoning for a portion of West Lynn Street), alongside permits for private construction like a unique flagstone sidewalk.

  • City Property Sale & Building Standards:

    Council authorized the sale of a city lot and directed a new building at West 8th and Colorado Streets to align with existing architectural lines.
