Riverchase Apartments Refinance Stalled

Thursday, January 17, 2002 Austin Housing Finance Corporation (AHFC)
  • The Austin Housing Finance Corporation (AHFC) Board considered a proposal to refinance existing bonds for the Riverchase Apartments.
  • The plan involved issuing up to $9 million in new bonds to replace debt originally from 1985.
  • However, the Board ultimately took no action on the proposed Riverchase Apartments bond refinancing.
  • A separate special meeting for the AHFC Board was also postponed.
