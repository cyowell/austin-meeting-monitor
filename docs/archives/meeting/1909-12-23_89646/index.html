Thursday, December 23, 1909 regular
Transcript

• Street Improvements Approved: Council unanimously passed $150 for West 5th Street paving and $50 for engineering work, plus $225 for street department payroll.

• Damage Claim Settled: Council approved a $209.90 payment to L. Littman for damages with unanimous support.

• Tax Valuation Cuts Rejected: Mayor Wooldridge's proposal to reduce property tax valuations by 20% failed after Councilman Bartholomew's amendment to increase it to 25% also lost, leaving valuations unchanged initially.

• Major Property Revaluations: Council later voted to revise numerous property valuations downward across Austin neighborhoods, including significant reductions for prominent landowners like Lewis Hancock and Joseph Nalle, with voting splits indicating disagreement over fair market values.
