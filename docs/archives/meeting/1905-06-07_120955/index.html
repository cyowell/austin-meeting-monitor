Austin Police Officer Faces Discipline for Domestic Violence

Wednesday, June 7, 1905 special called

• Police Officer Suspended: Officer J.A. Sholok was suspended for 10 days after striking his wife during a domestic dispute. The Police Committee found mitigating circumstances, noting the wife was the aggressor, but still disciplined him for "insubordination unbecoming an officer."

• Water & Light Plant Discussion: Council met with a Business League committee to discuss the struggling Water & Light Plant and consider providing financial assistance to keep it operational.

• Dog Catcher Charges Unresolved: A separate report on charges against Dog Catcher F.E. Shovick was called for but not addressed in this meeting's transcript.

• Budget Vote Fails: Council rejected a proposal to transfer funds from the City Hall Fund to the General Revenue Fund, with 8 aldermen voting against it and only 6 in favor.

Mayor and City Council of the City of Austin --- Page break --- 372 Gentlemen: Your Police Committee to whom was referred the charge against Officer J.A. Sholok, beg leave to report as follows. That on Oct 8th inst., Said Officer was arrested and a charge filed against him for disturbing the peace by wilfully assaulting and striking his wife, and that his Honor the Mayor very promptly and properly suspended him from duty, pending investigation by your Committee. Your Committee in open Session heard before them Several credible witnesses, Neighbors of the accused, and all the evidence showed that the domestic relations of the accused were very unpleasant and that in this case there were mitigating circumstances in favor of the accused, the wife being the aggressor and refusing to appear before your Committee. While the evidence does not show that he did wilfully assault or strike his wife, it does show, and he admits that on strong provocation he did strike or slap her in defending himself from attack, instead of unconditionally surrendering as a dutiful husband should have done. Therefore we have to recommend that he be reprimanded to wit he suspended for an act of insubordination, unbecoming an officer and husband, and that he be suspended from duty for ten days from Oct 9th inst., with the understanding that on the filing of another like Complaint he will be discharged. Respectfully Submitted F.T. Powell St. Haynes J.S.
