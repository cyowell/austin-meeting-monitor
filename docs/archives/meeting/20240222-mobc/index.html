Austin Transit Expansion & City Mobility

Thursday, February 22, 2024 Mobility Committee Regular Meeting
  • Public feedback is currently open for the proposed Austin Light Rail route, detailing its 10-mile path through central and south Austin, including specific design options for stations and how it will integrate with other modes of transport.
  • Project Connect's wider mobility efforts are making progress, with new MetroRapid transit stations opening (like Macalla) and ongoing work to improve walking and biking access across the city to support transit use.
  • Discussions also covered planning for future light rail extensions, maximizing private parking for transit users, and leveraging urban development to support transit-oriented communities.
  • Committee members raised immediate public concerns regarding tricky bike lane intersections, parking in bike lanes, and the need for adaptive street design for safer, more efficient traffic flow.
