Austin Funds Parks, Power Plant; Tax Relief Debated

Thursday, March 23, 1933 regular

  • Major Power Plant Investment:

    Over $130,000 was approved for new equipment, including a steam turbine and surface condenser, to upgrade the city's Electric Light and Power Plant.

  • Significant Park and Recreation Spending:

    Several projects received funding, including a new playground, road development in Shoal Creek Park, temporary dams on Blunn Creek, and notably, the construction of a swimming pool in "Mexican Park."

  • Land Use Reorganization:

    The City authorized a land exchange to facilitate improvements on municipal property located south of the Colorado River and west of Barton Creek.

  • Debate on Tax Relief:

    A proposal to waive penalties on city taxes for 1932 and 1933 and allow quarterly payments for 1934 failed to pass, indicating a desire for further study on taxpayer relief.
