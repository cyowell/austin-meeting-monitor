Austin Utility Management, Energy, Budget Hearing

Wednesday, August 13, 1997

  • Curb Cut Funding Concerns:

    Several citizens spoke to express concerns and discuss funding for curb cuts, highlighting accessibility issues in the city.

  • Major Electric Utility Contract:

    Approved a nearly $6 million contract with Metzler & Associates for accelerated management services for the Electric Utility, with an emphasis on stakeholder and public inclusion.

  • Energy Efficiency & Low-Income Aid:

    Authorized a rebate for energy-efficient apartment installations and a $350,000 agreement with Travis County for weatherization services benefiting economically disadvantaged customers.

  • Upcoming Public Budget Review:

    Scheduled a public hearing for September 8, 1997, to discuss the city's proposed 1997-98 operating and capital budgets, as well as the Electric Utility's business plan.
