Austin Policy: Homeless Aid, Solar Power, Growth

Thursday, February 15, 1996 regular

  • Support for Homeless Services:

    The Council approved an ordinance exempting churches that provide assistance to homeless individuals from drainage utility fees, addressing a prominent community concern.

  • Investment in Solar Energy:

    Over $500,000 in federal funds were accepted to install rooftop solar power systems on local homes and businesses, advancing green energy initiatives.

  • Growth and Infrastructure Decisions:

    Key land-use decisions included advancing a telecommunications franchise for Time Warner and setting a public hearing to review a contested residential treatment facility, alongside adjusting Austin's corporate boundary with Sunset Valley.
