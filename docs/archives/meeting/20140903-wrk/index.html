Austin Budget Battle: Homes, Health, Utilities

Wednesday, September 3, 2014 Austin City Council Budget Work Session

  • Flood Recovery Funding:

    Public appeals pressed for the final $78 million to buy out high-risk homes in flood plains following the 2013 Halloween floods, emphasizing the need for safer communities.

  • Economic Equity & Affordability:

    Concerns were raised over income inequality, advocating for utility rates based on ability to pay, curbing corporate tax incentives, and increasing support for low-income housing and tenant aid.

  • Revamping Energy Efficiency:

    Austin Energy's low-income weatherization program faced criticism for unspent funds and costly, low-impact measures like HVAC replacement. The utility pledged to shift to more cost-effective home improvements.

  • Investing in Community & Youth Services:

    Calls were made for more funding for critical areas like year-round swimming, after-school programs (due to recent state/federal cuts), and cultural or mentoring initiatives for at-risk youth, alongside better sidewalk accessibility.
