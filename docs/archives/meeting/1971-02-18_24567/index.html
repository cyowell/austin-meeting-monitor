Major Shifts: Nursing, Vendors, & City Growth

Thursday, February 18, 1971 regular

  • Future of Nursing Education Debated:

    A major policy shift began to transfer the Brackenridge Hospital School of Nursing from a city-run diploma program to a 2-year Associate Degree managed by Southwest Texas State University, sparking intense debate over nurse quality and cost.

  • Infrastructure & City Expansion:

    Substantial investments were approved for critical sewer lines and power station upgrades, while the city also annexed new land and rezoned properties for continued urban growth.

  • Citizen Concerns & Sidewalk Vendors:

    Residents voiced pressing issues like severe flooding and speeding in new subdivisions. Separately, sidewalk vendors rallied against potential bans, seeking dialogue on their role in the local economy.

  • Community Programs & Daily Life:

    The city approved funding for arts and social initiatives via the Model Cities Plan and addressed everyday challenges such as taxi fare increases and university area parking solutions.
