Austin Street Paving and Building Permits Approved

Thursday, November 18, 1909 regular

• Street Infrastructure Investment: The council approved a $1,257.40 appropriation to pave East 6th Street, representing a significant infrastructure investment for the growing city.

• Building Permits Granted: Two local residents, S.L. Costley and Geo Balagia, received permission to construct buildings within Austin's fire district after approval from the Fire Commissioners.

• Tax Assessment Corrections: The council cancelled erroneous 1908 tax assessments against nine insurance and financial companies, instructing city officials to adjust their records accordingly.

• Minor Budget Adjustments: The council approved a $50 engineering expenditure and transferred $20 in street damages paid by Ringling Bros. circus to the proper city fund.
