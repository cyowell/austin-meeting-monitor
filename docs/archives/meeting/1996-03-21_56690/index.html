Thursday, March 21, 1996 regular
Transcript

  • Electric Utility Strategy Shift:

    The city voted to halt a Request for Information (RFI) exploring future electric utility service options, indicating a decision to maintain existing public control.

  • Major Urban Revitalization Initiatives:

    Significant funding and an interlocal agreement were approved to kickstart redevelopment of the East 11th and 12th Street corridor. Additional Community Development Block Grant funds were reprogrammed for housing, youth support, and child care services.

  • Planning for Growth and Mobility:

    The 1995 Bicycle Plan, Part 1, was approved to enhance city cycling infrastructure. A comprehensive study was also commissioned to examine the broad impacts of Austin's metropolitan growth on traffic, air quality, water resources, and economic indicators.

  • Community and Environmental Action:

    Amendments were adopted to the Minority-Owned and Women-Owned Business Enterprises (MBE/WBE) procurement program. Additionally, an oak wilt suppression program was initiated, and new exemptions were approved to support local community gardens.
