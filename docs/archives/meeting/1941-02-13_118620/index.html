Austin: Gas Stations, Utilities, & Scout Ideas

Thursday, February 13, 1941 regular

  • New Urban Development:

    Approved plans for a drive-in gas station and advanced an ordinance to regulate local stockyards, including leasing the municipal yard under specific safety and labor conditions.

  • Expanding City Infrastructure:

    Granted permits for Southwestern Bell to install new telephone pole lines and for Texas Public Service to lay extensive gas mains across several Austin streets.

  • Addressing Public Concerns:

    Referred a request for an anti-ticket scalping ordinance to the legal department and held a public hearing for major street improvements with no public opposition.

  • Youth Civic Engagement:

    Welcomed a Boy Scout "City Council" who offered recommendations on bicycle licensing and addressing unsightly neighborhood shacks.
