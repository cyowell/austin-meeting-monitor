Austin: New Police Chief, SOS Protections, Parking

Thursday, August 20, 1992 regular

  • Confirmed Elizabeth M. Watson as the new Police Chief

    , marking a significant leadership appointment for the city.

  • Advanced the "SOS Ordinance,"

    codifying and amending this critical environmental protection law, particularly for the Barton Springs Zone.

  • Decriminalized parking offenses

    , establishing a new Parking Adjudication Program to change how parking tickets are handled.

  • Adopted a framework for downtown revitalization

    based on the "A Call to Action" report and approved a tax abatement to support Don Limon's Bakery and Restaurant.
