CodeNEXT Expansion, Bull Creek Deal, Housing Needs

Thursday, April 30, 2015 Planning and Neighborhoods Committee Regular Meeting

Here's a summary of key actions and discussions:

  • CodeNEXT Advisory Group Expands Representation:

    New members will be added to the CodeNEXT advisory group to ensure broader geographic, renter, environmental, and small business perspectives are included in the city's major land development code rewrite.

  • Environmental Cleanup & Public Access on Bull Creek:

    An agreement was approved to remove an auto salvage yard from a critical Bull Creek site, leading to environmental remediation and a public access easement, in exchange for transferable development credits.

  • Affordable Housing & ADU Discussion:

    Public input emphasized the need for more affordable housing solutions, including "two houses on one lot" (accessory dwelling units/ADUs), and stronger enforcement of affordable housing incentives.

  • Robert Mueller Development Oversight:

    Citizens requested detailed council briefings and a "midpoint review" for the ongoing Mueller development, with a focus on comprehensive housing and transportation planning.
