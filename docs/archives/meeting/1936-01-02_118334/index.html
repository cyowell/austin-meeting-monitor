New Gas Lines, School Bonds, Street Closures

Thursday, January 2, 1936 regular

Here's a look at some key decisions and discussions from a recent meeting:

  • Infrastructure Expansion:

    Approval was given to the Texas Public Service Company to lay new gas lines on several streets, including Woodrow, East 34th, West 41st, Speedway, and East 1st Street, expanding the city's utility network.

  • Education Investment:

    A significant $350,000 bond issue was approved to fund school construction and improvements, with bids for purchasing these bonds to be advertised soon.

  • Public Land Change:

    A portion of Cole Street and an adjacent alley in the Christian and Fellman Addition were permanently closed and vacated at the request of the Texas Wesleyan Academy.

  • Upcoming Budget Review:

    A public hearing was scheduled for the following evening to review and discuss the city's proposed budget for the year 1936.
