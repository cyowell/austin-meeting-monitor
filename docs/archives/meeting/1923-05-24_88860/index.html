Austin: Church Taxes, Traffic Rules, Special Police

Thursday, May 24, 1923 regular

  • Financial Corrections and Relief:

    Affirmed Highland Presbyterian Church's tax-exempt status since 1899, corrected several property and business tax assessment errors, and remitted specific fines and impound fees for citizens.

  • New Traffic Regulations:

    Enacted an ordinance prohibiting vehicles from standing on a section of East 7th Street to improve urban traffic flow.

  • Public Safety and Infrastructure:

    Appointed five special police officers and authorized a hotel company to fill an old city fire cistern on East 7th Street.
