Austin Election Results Certified

Monday, April 8, 1957 special called
  • This special meeting convened solely to officially count and certify the results of the Austin city election held on April 6.
  • The final vote tally confirmed the election of five individuals: Mrs. Stuart (Emma) Long, Ben White, Tom Miller, Lester E. Palmer, and Wesley Pearson.
  • These individuals were formally declared the new leaders for Austin, authorized to begin their two-year terms of service on May 15, 1957.
