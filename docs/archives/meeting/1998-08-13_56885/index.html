Austin's Future: Airport, Housing, and Growth

Thursday, August 13, 1998 regular

  • Airport Retail Deals Approved:

    Multi-million dollar contracts were secured for new newsstands and specialty retail shops at the Austin-Bergstrom International Airport, projected to generate over $4 million in city revenue over five years.

  • Affordable Housing Investment:

    The Austin Housing Finance Corporation authorized up to $15 million in bonds to acquire and rehabilitate a 294-unit "reasonably priced" rental housing complex, the Rutland I and II Apartments.

  • Smart Growth Incentives:

    A development agreement was approved for Lamar-Sixth Austin, Inc., providing over $2.1 million in fee waivers and infrastructure participation for a project aligning with the city's Smart Growth Matrix.

  • Environmental Ordinance Settlement:

    A settlement was reached in a major lawsuit (W.W. Deerfield v. City of Austin) concerning land use regulations and the "Save Our Springs" (SOS) Ordinance, including a case-specific amendment to the ordinance.

  • Parkland Development Variance:

    The council approved a variance for the Parks and Recreation Department to construct a new parking lot for Red Bud Isle, despite its location within a 25-year floodplain.
