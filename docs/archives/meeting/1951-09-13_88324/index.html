Austin: Fire Safety, Bus Bench Ads, Road Plans

Thursday, September 13, 1951 regular

  • Fire Safety Regulations Under Review:

    A public hearing on a new fire protective devices ordinance led the Council to form a seven-member committee to redraft the rules and explore creating a Fire Commission, addressing public concerns about costs and outdated requirements.

  • Bus Stop Advertising Benches Proposed:

    A private company presented a plan to install over 200 advertising-supported benches at bus stops, proposing to pay the city a percentage of gross income, though consideration was postponed.

  • Major Health Infrastructure Funding:

    The city confirmed receiving substantial federal grants, including $750,000 for Brackenridge Hospital and $100,000 for the Austin Public Health Center.

  • Traffic and Parking Updates:

    New ordinances were passed to amend and modernize 1937 traffic regulations, affecting parking meter zones, stop signs, and two-hour parking locations.

  • Key Infrastructure Development:

    The Council moved forward with acquiring land for a new water plant and began preliminary planning for right-of-way purchases for an upcoming interregional highway.
