Hyde Park Preservation & Job Bill Funds

Sunday, June 19, 1983

  • Hyde Park Development Freeze:

    A 90-day moratorium was enacted on demolitions and structure move-offs in the Hyde Park neighborhood. This temporary halt allows for a review of preservation ordinances, consideration of historic zoning, and assessment of local infrastructure capacity.

  • Million-Dollar Job & Housing Boost:

    The council approved the allocation of $1.378 million from federal emergency jobs funds. This money will support initiatives including high-tech and other job training programs, housing authority projects, Planned Parenthood, and housing rehabilitation.

  • Downtown Blighted Area Hearing:

    A public hearing was scheduled to consider officially designating a central downtown area as "blighted," a step that typically enables specific urban renewal and redevelopment projects.
