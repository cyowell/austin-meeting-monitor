Friday, December 11, 2015 Public Safety Committee Regular Meeting
Official City Record Agenda Watch Video Transcript
  • The Public Safety Committee met to consider candidates for municipal court judge positions.
  • The committee recommended George Thomas for a full-time associate judge role and Belinda Herrera for a full-time judge specializing in juvenile cases.
  • Ryan Turner, John Yeager, and Patrick Mcnellis were recommended to serve as substitute judges.
  • These recommendations will now be forwarded to the full City Council for final approval, with an emphasis on quickly appointing the substitute judges.
