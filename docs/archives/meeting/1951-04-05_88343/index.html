Austin: Public Safety, Parks & Big Projects

Thursday, April 5, 1951 regular

  • Public Safety & Police Concerns:

    A lively discussion unfolded about rising crime rates, police staffing shortages, and the condition of the city jail. Concerns were also raised about the ethics of a private promoter handling a police fundraising ball, with fears of coercive ticket sales.

  • East Austin Development & Services:

    Community members from East Austin pressed for numerous improvements, including a new fire station location, exploration of a golf course, cleaning of alleys, and the installation of sanitary facilities, lighting, and a concrete slab at Govalle Park.

  • Major Infrastructure & Utility Expansion:

    The city authorized a $200,000 purchase of a water control district to expand its water system. Engineering was approved for new bridges over the Colorado River and Shoal Creek, and a contract was awarded for a new tennis court at Metz Playground.
