Shaping the City: Gas, Benefits, and Zoning

Thursday, December 22, 1955 regular

  • Secured Future Energy Supply:

    Extended the natural gas contract for Austin's municipal power plant through 1965, with new pricing terms to power the city.

  • Enhanced Employee Benefits:

    Established a Social Security Trust Fund and governing board specifically for Police and Fire Department employees.

  • Guiding Urban Development:

    Denied multiple requests for new commercial zoning that could impact residential areas, while approving permits for two new drive-in gasoline stations.

  • Investing in Infrastructure:

    Approved major contracts for widespread street paving and significant upgrades to the city's sewage treatment plant.
