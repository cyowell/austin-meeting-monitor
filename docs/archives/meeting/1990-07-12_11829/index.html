New Police, Downtown Ban, Convention Center & Ethics

Thursday, July 12, 1990 regular

  • Public Safety & Downtown Conduct:

    The city approved adding 11 sworn positions to the Police Department and enacted a ban on glass containers in downtown public areas. A decision regarding a downtown public alcohol consumption ban was postponed.

  • Urban Development & Civic Ethics:

    New design guidelines and interim development controls were adopted for the Convention Center District, including approval of a multi-million dollar insurance program for the facility. Additionally, an ordinance was passed prohibiting lobbyists or their employees from serving on city boards and commissions.

  • Environmental & Long-Term Planning:

    A trust fund was established for the future decommissioning of the South Texas Nuclear Project. Ongoing discussions about the controversial TDS landfill led to orders for staff to re-examine its contracts and environmental ordinance compliance.

  • Infrastructure & Housing Initiatives:

    Speed limits were adjusted on a section of RM 620, and the Austin Housing Finance Corporation was designated to lead urban homesteading programs across the city.
