Thursday, April 19, 1984 regular
Transcript

  • Nuclear Power Plant Funding & Risk:

    Authorized the intent to issue $605 million in utility system revenue bonds, largely for the South Texas Project (a nuclear power plant), amidst public protest. Additionally, a risk assessment study was approved for the project, scrutinizing its regulatory future, operating license prospects, and decommissioning costs.

  • Community Development & Housing Needs:

    Held an extensive public hearing on the 1984-85 Community Development Block Grant (CDBG) program, where numerous citizens advocated for increased funding for low-income housing, neighborhood repairs, and various social service agencies.

  • Controversial Large-Scale Development:

    Postponed a major zoning decision for the "Mayfield-Wilkerson" project following significant neighborhood opposition regarding increased traffic, congestion, and potential environmental impact on Bull Creek. The proposal included specific conditions on density, building design, and restricted commercial uses.

  • Environmental & Energy Policy:

    Adopted an "Environmental Program" as part of city planning, formally renamed and refocused the "Energy Advisory Commission" to emphasize efficient energy use, alternative technologies, and conservation, and passed temporary amendments to septic tank regulations.
