Austin's Airport, Alcohol Rules, & Joint Services

Thursday, July 19, 1990 regular

  • Airport Future & Military Presence:

    Decided to work toward relocating the municipal airport to Bergstrom Air Force Base, aiming to retain U.S. Air Force reserve units.

  • Downtown Public Alcohol Ban:

    Approved on its first reading a new ordinance prohibiting alcohol consumption on public property in the downtown area.

  • City-County Service Consolidation:

    Took significant steps to advance interlocal contracts and committees for consolidating services with Travis County, including parks, vehicle maintenance, and information systems.

  • Parks & Environmental Investment:

    Approved over $1 million in state grants for developing Mary Moore Searight Park and Veloway Park, and allocated funds for Environmental and Conservation Services personnel.
