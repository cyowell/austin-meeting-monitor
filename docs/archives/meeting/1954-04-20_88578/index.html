Austin's $4.9 Million Public Works Boost

Tuesday, April 20, 1954 special called
  • Secured nearly $5 million in funding to significantly upgrade Austin's public infrastructure.
  • Approved major investments, including $2 million for public schools and $1.15 million for hospital improvements.
  • Additional funds will modernize Austin's streets, expand the municipal airport, build a new auditorium, and enhance parks, sewers, and fire stations.
  • The city finalized the sale of these bonds to Bankers Trust Company, securing the necessary financing at a favorable interest rate.
