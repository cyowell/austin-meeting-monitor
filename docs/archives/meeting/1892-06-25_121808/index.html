Austin Approves Underground Sewer System

Saturday, June 25, 1892 special called

• Major Infrastructure Project Approved: The City Council voted to grant the Lewis Mercer Construction Company permission to build a modern underground sanitary sewer system throughout Austin's streets and alleys.

• Unanimous Support (Nearly): The ordinance passed overwhelmingly with only two aldermen voting against it, signaling strong council support for this public infrastructure improvement.

• Fast-Tracked Approval: The council suspended normal rules to rush the sewer project through all three required readings in a single special meeting, indicating urgency about getting the work underway.
