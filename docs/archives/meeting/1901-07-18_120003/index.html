Austin School Bond Interest Payment Debate

Thursday, July 18, 1901 special called

• School Bond Interest Funding: Council met to appropriate $1,125 to pay interest due on $45,000 in high school bonds, with an amendment reducing both figures to $120 and $25,000 respectively.

• Amendment Dispute: Alderman Shumate proposed significant cuts to the original appropriation amounts, though the council failed to suspend rules for final passage with only a 2-to-1 vote in favor.

• Attendance Issues: Multiple aldermen were absent; three were excused while the city marshal was directed to bring in others, indicating enforcement of council attendance requirements.
