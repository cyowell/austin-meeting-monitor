Austin's 1906 Infrastructure & Government Updates

Monday, June 18, 1906 regular

• Infrastructure Investments: Council approved funding for a bridge across Waller Creek ($750) and authorized underground conduits for the Western Telegraph and Telephone Company, modernizing the city's utilities.

• Cemetery Expansion: After initial rejection, Council approved purchasing 200 cemetery lots from the Austin City Cemetery Association for $5,000 to accommodate the growing city's burial needs.

• New City Physician: Following Dr. Joe Gilbert's resignation, Dr. R.S. Graves and then Dr. J.M. Loving competed in back-to-back elections, with Dr. Loving ultimately selected as the new city physician.

• Building Inspection & Modernization Debate: A resolution proposed major renovations to the deteriorating City Hall, including adding a third story and modernizing the structure with new utilities and facilities, though the vote to move forward failed.

• Public Utilities Proposal: Council referred a proposition from American District Telegraph Company to establish a new fire alarm system for the city to the Fire Committee for further review.
