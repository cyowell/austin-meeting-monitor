Big Projects: Highways, Lake, Golf Course Future

Thursday, April 5, 1962 regular

Here are the key takeaways from the April 5, 1962 Austin City Council meeting:

  • New Outer Loop Highway:

    Plans advanced for a major multi-lane highway, the "outer belt loop," to circle the west side of Austin, with right-of-way costs shared with local partners.

  • Town Lake Beautification:

    Discussions moved forward on naming and beautifying the lakefront between Congress and Lamar bridges, including engaging a landscape architect for the Auditorium area.

  • Golf Course vs. Airport Runways:

    Development of a new golf course was delayed amidst concerns it might hinder future airport runway expansions, prompting a legal review to clarify land use.

  • Rapid Development & Services:

    The council approved substantial sanitary sewer upgrades and numerous rezonings for commercial and office use, reflecting the city's growth. They also addressed a critical hospital bed shortage and debated implementing a future garbage collection fee.
