Austin Roads Convention & City Infrastructure Updates

Monday, December 4, 1899 regular

• Roads & Streets Convention Funding: The council approved $150 in funding for a Good Roads and Street Convention scheduled for December 11-12, 1899, reflecting Austin's interest in improving transportation infrastructure.

• Street Drainage & Bridge Work Completed: The Street Committee reported that petitions for draining West 10th Street and building a bridge on 1st Street (Onion Street) had both been successfully completed.

• Public Scale Permission Granted: A.S. Noit received approval to install a public scale on Lot 7 Block 2, fronting west on 2nd Street for commercial use.

• Board of Equalization Amendment: Council passed an ordinance amending the powers and duties of the Board of Equalization to allow them additional time to complete their work.

• Water & Light Commission Nominations: The mayor nominated two members to the Water & Light Commission, though the council voted to refer the nominations to committee for further review rather than confirm them immediately.
