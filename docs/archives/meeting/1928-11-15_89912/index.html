Austin's Early Auto Era & Big Bonds

Thursday, November 15, 1928 regular

  • New Gas Stations & Safety Rules:

    Multiple drive-in gasoline stations and auto service centers received permits, but with strict safety and operational conditions, from specific building materials to waste oil disposal and fire prevention.

  • City Bond Market Flop:

    All initial bids for a major $750,000 city bond issue were rejected, prompting officials to seek new proposals for financing city projects.

  • Expanding Utilities:

    Approved construction for new telephone pole lines and several significant gas mains across various streets, signaling Austin's ongoing infrastructure growth.

  • Parks & Policy:

    Considered purchasing land for new city parks, including the "Eastwoods" area, while also denying a charity's request for a sewer fee waiver to avoid setting a financial precedent.
