Austin: New Signs, Employee Pay, Street Names

Thursday, August 12, 1915 regular

  • Urban Modernization & Infrastructure:

    The city considered renaming Bell Street, granted multiple permits for new electric light signs on Congress Avenue, and approved a temporary permit for a fuel oil tank installation.

  • Financial Accountability for Employees:

    A new policy was enacted to withhold paychecks from city employees who are delinquent on debts owed to the city until their accounts are settled.

  • Support for City Services & Events:

    Funds were transferred to fuel the City Hospital, and permission was granted for advertising streamers for an upcoming Chautauqua event.
