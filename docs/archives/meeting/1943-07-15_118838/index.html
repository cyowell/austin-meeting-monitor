City Plans Highways, Lake Docks, Forgives Taxes

Thursday, July 15, 1943 regular

  • Post-War Highway Development:

    The City committed to a major infrastructure plan with the Texas Highway Commission, outlining responsibilities for new highway construction, including immediate right-of-way acquisition, with much of the work slated for the post-war period.

  • Lake Austin Dock Regulations:

    Approved the construction of a private boat dock on Lake Austin, but imposed strict conditions against commercial activities (beyond marine supplies) and mandated safety features like proper lighting.

  • Tax Relief & Urban Planning:

    Provided financial relief by remitting penalties and partial interest on several overdue city tax accounts, and scheduled a public hearing for a proposed commercial zoning change.

  • Local Permits Issued:

    Granted multiple taxicab operator and driver permits, notably including one for Mrs. Walter Les Carter, identified as "colored," and also issued various private boat licenses.
