Austin: Water Discounts, Tax Deadline Extended

Thursday, March 30, 1944 regular

  • Temporary Water Rate Reduction:

    Approved lower domestic water rates from April 1st to October 31st, 1944, to encourage "Victory Gardens" and beautification efforts.

  • Tax Payment Extension:

    Extended the deadline for city tax payments without interest to April 12th, following a request from the University of Texas due to state employee salary delays.

  • Utility Expansion:

    Authorized the Texas Public Service Company to install new gas mains along San Jacinto Boulevard, East 12th Street, and East 14th Street.

  • Tax Penalty Remission:

    Approved the remission of penalties and half of the accrued interest for delinquent property taxes in several specific cases.
