Austin's Big School Tax, New Sewers, & Carnival

Thursday, November 18, 1915 regular

Here's a look at some key decisions and events from the November 18, 1915 Austin City Council meeting:

  • School Tax Increase Approved:

    The City Clerk reported the successful outcome of a public election, showing citizens voted in favor of increasing the city's school tax.

  • Major Infrastructure Investments:

    Substantial funds were allocated for building several new sewer lines across the city, and new specifications for various street paving materials were adopted, setting the stage for future road improvements.

  • Carnival Comes to Austin:

    A permit was issued for the W. H. Rice Carnival Shows to operate for a week, with temporary street closures and a required deposit to ensure proper cleanup.

  • Compensation for Dam Impacts:

    Funds were approved to compensate a landowner for property affected by overflow from the city's dam.
