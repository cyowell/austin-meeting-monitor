Thursday, September 26, 1912 regular
Transcript

  • Major Street Paving Approved:

    The city secured a significant paving contract for key streets, including Lavacca, 19th, University, and East 6th. The agreement with Texas Bitulithic Company outlined detailed costs and multi-year payment plans.

  • Fines and Animal Pound Fees Remitted:

    In a series of notable decisions, the council pardoned fines for several residents and waived impound fees for animals, including a sheep (with a humorous nod to the "cost of high living") and two mules.

  • Paving Assessment Disputes:

    A legal process was initiated to appoint commissioners to determine paving assessments for 4th Street, as some property owners had not agreed to their share of the improvement costs.

  • Early Gasoline Storage Permit:

    Signaling the automotive era's emergence, a temporary permit was issued for a steel gasoline storage tank to be placed under a sidewalk on West 25th Street.
