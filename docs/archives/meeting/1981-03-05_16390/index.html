Austin's Greenbelt, Power Rates & Airport Future

Thursday, March 5, 1981 regular

  • Electric Rate Revamp Blocked:

    A proposal to eliminate "declining block" electricity rates, intended to promote conservation and slightly shift billing, failed to pass after a public hearing and significant debate among council members.

  • Greenbelt Expanded, Energy Explored:

    The city acquired 10 acres of land to expand the Barton Creek Greenbelt and approved funding for a study into conservation and renewable energy sources.

  • Accessibility Motion Fails:

    A motion to appropriate funds for a deaf interpreter at City Council meetings did not pass.

  • Airport Car Rental Debate Set:

    A public hearing was scheduled to discuss potentially adding a fifth car rental operator at the Municipal Airport, exploring options for service expansion.
