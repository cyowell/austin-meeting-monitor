Fluoride, Zoning, & Urban Progress

Thursday, August 19, 1971 regular

  • Fluoridation Decision to Voters

    After extensive public debate over adding fluoride to the city's water for public health, the Council decided to put the controversial issue to a city-wide referendum, pledging to abide by the voters' decision.

  • Major Urban & Infrastructure Investments

    Approved over $1 million in Model Cities funds for new sidewalks, streets, drainage, park improvements, and a mobile health van, alongside acquiring land for the Mo-Pac Expressway project.

  • Contentious Land Use Debates

    The Council navigated several public zoning hearings, approving new commercial and multi-family developments, sometimes against Planning Commission recommendations, and denying a citizen-backed effort to prevent higher-density housing.

  • Environmental Protection Push

    A State Senator advocated for establishing a new City department focused on environmental matters, including water quality and air control, emphasizing available state and federal support.
