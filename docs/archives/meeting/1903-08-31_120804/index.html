Austin Street Improvements & Fire Station Funding 1903

Monday, August 31, 1903 special called

• West 12th Street Paving Project: Council approved $550 to repair and improve West 12th Street paving between Rio Grande and Blanco Streets, plus funding for extra street labor and teams.

• Fire Company Buildings: Two ordinances passed allocating $1,500 to purchase property for North Austin Fire Company and $2,500 to build a new fire hall for South Austin Fire Company.

• Property Tax Redemption: Residents who lost property in tax foreclosures could now reclaim their land within six months under new conditions, offering a second chance to Austin property owners.

• Procedural Issues: Multiple special council meetings were deemed illegal because proper notification procedures weren't followed, highlighting the importance of following charter requirements.

• Municipal Finances: Council approved $540 for the Board of Equalization, $191 refund to Travis County for hospital costs, and transferred funds between city accounts to manage bond interest payments.
