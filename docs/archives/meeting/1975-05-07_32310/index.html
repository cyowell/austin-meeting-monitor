Blueprint for Austin: Citizen Vision for Growth

Wednesday, May 7, 1975 special called

  • Citizen-Led Future Vision:

    The city formally received the "AUSTIN TOMORROW Goals Report," a comprehensive blueprint for the city's future developed over two years by more than 3,500 citizens.

  • Key Development Priorities:

    The report outlined 10 critical areas for Austin's future, including public transportation, expanded green spaces, controlled urban growth, environmental protection, affordable housing, and equitable social services.

  • Commitment to Implementation:

    City leaders, both current and incoming, enthusiastically accepted the report, pledging to integrate these citizen-led goals into future policy decisions and establish an ongoing committee for their implementation.
