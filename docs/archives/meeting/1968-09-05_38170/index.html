Austin: Growing Pains and Future Plans

Thursday, September 5, 1968 regular
  • Austin celebrated reaching a quarter-million residents, marking significant city growth. The Chamber of Commerce's mission shifted to focus more on overall economic development and research, rather than primarily tourism.
  • The City authorized major land acquisitions for critical infrastructure projects, including extensions for MoPac Boulevard and the Crosstown Expressway. New traffic signals were approved for several I-35 intersections.
  • A key policy debate emerged regarding commercial zoning, with officials discussing whether to prevent "strip development" along major roads. Several members expressed concern that such restrictions could hinder small businesses, leading to the decision being postponed for further study.
  • The city's Aviation Director reported that airport passenger traffic was dramatically exceeding 10-year forecasts, highlighting the unexpected pace of growth and future needs for expansion.
