Austin Expands Parks, Debates Gas Stations

Thursday, October 10, 1929 regular
  • The city approved significant land acquisitions for public spaces, investing over $11,000 from bond funds, including a large 8.78-acre tract near Pease Park.
  • A permit for a new gasoline filling station was denied following citizen protests and an incomplete zoning review; other gas station applications were deferred for later consideration.
  • City leaders authorized a substantial purchase of concrete pipe to move forward with upgrades to the sewer system.
  • Public concerns were actively addressed, with citizens protesting a proposed dry cleaning establishment and successfully opposing a gas station permit.
