Thursday, May 11, 1933 regular
Transcript

  • Key Infrastructure & Public Works Projects:

    The City Council approved contracts for grading and gravelling Shoal Creek Boulevard, alongside allocating funds for various Depression-era Reconstruction Finance Corporation (RFC) cooperative projects like street, park (Zilker Tract), and airport improvements.

  • Urban Growth & Public Services:

    Major policy decisions included officially renaming numerous city streets to resolve numbering confusion, approving permits for a new drive-in gasoline station, and ensuring free admission to Zaragosa and Palm public swimming pools.

  • Community Welfare & Legacy:

    The council acknowledged A.J. Zilker's significant land donations (including Barton Springs property) which established a substantial trust fund for public school programs, and considered a request for a police matron at Barton Springs to assist youth.
