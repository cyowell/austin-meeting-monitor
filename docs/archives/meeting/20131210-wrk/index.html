Tuesday, December 10, 2013 Austin City Council Work Session

  • Google Fiber Site Selection:

    A crucial decision on "Project Connect," Austin's long-term transportation plan, was scheduled for a public vote, with discussions focusing on setting it up for a potential 2014 election.

  • Improving Disaster Response:

    Following recent floods, the council discussed creating clearer guidelines for elected officials during emergencies, aiming to support crisis efforts effectively without interfering with staff.

  • City Hall Office Shake-Up:

    Council members debated the relocation of the City Auditor and Economic Development Department within City Hall, scrutinizing renovation costs and the efficiency implications for key city functions.
