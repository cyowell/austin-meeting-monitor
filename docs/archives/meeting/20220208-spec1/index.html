Austin's Omicron Dip: Masks, Shots, SXSW Hopes

Tuesday, February 8, 2022 Austin City Council Special Called Meeting - 09:00 AM Joint Meeting with Travis County Commissioners Court

  • Austin's COVID-19 Update: Omicron Cases Declining, Nearing Stage 4.

    Health officials report a significant drop in Omicron cases and hospitalizations, with the area approaching Stage 4 guidelines, urging continued caution.

  • Masks and Boosters Are Key Defenses.

    New data confirms masks significantly reduce COVID-19 risk (N95s by 85%, cloth by 56%), but only 31% of fully vaccinated residents have received a booster shot, emphasizing the need for up-to-date vaccinations.

  • Vaccine Access Expanded, Incentives Offered.

    Mobile clinics continue to provide vaccines in underserved neighborhoods, and financial incentives are available to support residents getting their shots without income loss.

  • Positive Outlook for SXSW, Future Boosters Discussed.

    Mayor Adler expressed optimism for a safe South by Southwest due to improving numbers and protocols; discussions are ongoing about potential future booster shots and vaccines for children under 5.
