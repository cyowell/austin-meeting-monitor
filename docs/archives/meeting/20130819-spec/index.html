Austin: Budget Cuts, Parks, & Housing Priorities

Monday, August 19, 2013 Austin City Council Special Called Meeting

  • Property Tax Rate Debate:

    The Mayor proposed significant across-the-board budget cuts (excluding public safety) to prevent a property tax *rate* increase, aiming for 50.29 cents per $100 valuation, which would save $7.3 million.

  • Parks System Maintenance Crisis:

    The Parks Department is grappling with a $1.4 billion infrastructure need, prioritizing maintenance for aging facilities (like pools averaging 44 years old) over new projects. Staff are exploring public-private partnerships and increased user fees to help fund operations.

  • Affordable Housing & Repair Needs:

    Council discussed adjusting housing trust fund calculations and a larger housing bond program. A notable gap was identified for housing repair funds, with only $400,000 in the proposed budget against a $3 million community recommendation.

  • New Community Programs & Fee Adjustments:

    Updates included the upcoming launch of the "Roving Leaders" mobile youth program for teens and continued funding for senior meals. The city is also reviewing facility rental fees with plans for increases to better cover service costs.
