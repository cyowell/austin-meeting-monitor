Austin's Segregated Housing & Water Future

Thursday, March 31, 1938 regular

  • Segregated Public Housing Initiatives:

    Austin officials began the process for new public housing projects, requesting zoning changes for designated "negro," "white," and "mexican" housing units. A public hearing was scheduled to review these proposals.

  • Major Water Infrastructure Upgrade:

    Approved a $60,000 appropriation for significant expansion of the city's water Filtration Plant, aiming to increase capacity from 12.5 million to 18 million gallons per day to meet anticipated growth in demand.

  • Community Center Revitalization:

    Allocated $1,500 for extensive repairs and construction at the Rosewood Community Center, including roof work, new plaster, plumbing, flooring, painting, and a new bandstand.

  • Public Health Planning:

    Agreed to a joint meeting with County Commissioners to discuss plans for establishing a new city-county tuberculosis sanatorium.
