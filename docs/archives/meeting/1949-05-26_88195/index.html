Thursday, May 26, 1949 regular
Transcript

  • Public Health Initiatives:

    The city allocated $5,000 for a new DDT fog machine to combat diseases like polio and extended a dog quarantine due to ongoing rabies concerns.

  • Future Green Space Debated:

    Citizens, with a petition of 560 signatures, urged the city to dedicate the Country Club as a public park and playground; the council agreed to study the proposal.

  • Land Use & Setbacks:

    A second public hearing was held regarding controversial 25-foot building setbacks along major thoroughfares, with the council deferring action to inspect affected properties.

  • Urban Development & Infrastructure:

    The council advanced numerous zoning requests, approved the installation of new gas and water mains, and permitted a new filling station with a specific setback condition.
