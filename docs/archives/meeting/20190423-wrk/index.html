Tuesday, April 23, 2019 Austin City Council Work Session
Official City Record Agenda Watch Video Transcript
  • City leaders debated policies to ensure all city contractors pay a living wage and include wage escalation in long-term agreements, balancing permanent and contract staff.
  • A three-year, interest-free city loan was proposed for Workforce Solutions Capital Area to fund facilities, freeing up money for job retraining and development programs. Staff will also explore ways to leverage federal funding and facility sharing.
  • Council members pressed for an urgent update on the police department's persistent DNA test kit backlog and plans to expedite processing before upcoming contract votes.
  • Discussions advanced on rewriting the Land Development Code, agreeing to use "Draft 3" as a baseline for revisions focused on simplification and community feedback. The goal is completion by year-end, emphasizing thoroughness over speed and transparent public engagement.
  • Key policy areas for the new code include re-evaluating Neighborhood Conservation Districts (NCCDs) and Conditional Overlays (COs), streamlining use restrictions (e.g., banning certain uses like self-storage on corridors), integrating advanced water quality and conservation goals, and fostering age-friendly communities and creative spaces through new zoning provisions.
