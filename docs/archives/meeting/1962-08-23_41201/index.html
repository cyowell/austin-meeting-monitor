Thursday, August 23, 1962 regular
Transcript

  • Highway & Bridge Expansion:

    Plans advanced to widen Highway 183 and construct a new Montopolis Bridge, authorizing eminent domain for land. Contracts were also secured for a major sanitary sewer project and a new Trinity Street bridge.

  • Shopping Center Blocked:

    Faced with strong public opposition regarding traffic and property values, leaders rejected a controversial rezoning proposal for a new neighborhood shopping center near Mohawk Road.

  • Boosted City Services:

    Funds were approved to expand the Twin-Oaks branch library and to match a 32% increase in firemen's pension payments. A public hearing was set for the upcoming city budget.

  • Growing City Boundaries:

    Several annexations proceeded to bring more land into Austin's city limits, alongside various other commercial and residential zoning adjustments.
