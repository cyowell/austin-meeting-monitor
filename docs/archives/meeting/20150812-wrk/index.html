Austin Budget: Tax Rate, EMS Overtime, Festival Costs

Wednesday, August 12, 2015 Austin City Council Budget Work Session

  • No Property Tax Rate Increase:

    Mayor Adler is prioritizing a budget that avoids any increase in the city's property tax rate.

  • EMS Staffing & Wellness Overhaul:

    Austin EMS is tackling severe staff overtime, stress, and a paramedic shortage with a proposed multi-year plan costing $6.5 million to reduce work weeks and expand mental health and wellness programs.

  • Festival Cost Recovery & Community Health:

    The city is looking to recover more public safety costs from major festivals. Meanwhile, the highly effective Community Health Paramedic program, which drastically reduces repeat 911 calls, faces uncertain future funding.

  • Fire Department Growth & Development:

    The Austin Fire Department is expanding to meet city growth, hiring new cadets, planning a new Onion Creek fire station, and managing an increasing volume of development permits.
