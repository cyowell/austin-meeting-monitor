Austin Funds Operations, Adjusts Fees & Pay

Tuesday, September 26, 1967 special called

  • Budget & Taxes Advanced:

    The city's annual budget for the upcoming fiscal year and its property tax rates were moved closer to final approval, passing their second reading.

  • City Employee Pay Raises Approved:

    Significant salary increases for police officers, firefighters, and other city employees were formally passed into law.

  • City Service Fees Adjusted:

    Several ordinances were passed to increase fees across various city services, including recreation, building permits, electrical, plumbing, heating/AC permits, and garbage collection.
