Austin's Park Gift and Market Regulations

Monday, May 24, 1875 regular

• Major Land Donation: Former Governor E.M. Pease gifted 23 acres of land to Austin for use as a public park, located between Ninon Avenue and Shoal Creek—the council accepted the generous donation

• Market Regulations: The city banned street vegetable vendors from selling during official market hours to protect merchants who paid for market stalls, with violators facing misdemeanor charges

• Street Auction Ban: Auctioneers were prohibited from selling animals, vehicles, or merchandise on city streets (except state-mandated sales) due to noise complaints and pedestrian obstruction at Congress Avenue and Pecan Street

• Fire Department Funding: The council approved a $100 monthly salary for a full-time engineer at the Colorado Steam Fire Engine Company No. 2

• Liquor License Issues: Four applicants seeking retail liquor licenses were sent back to reapply after failing to meet city ordinance requirements

The following Resolution was unanimously passed, to wit: Whereas, From and after the first day of May 1875: provisions will be made for the erection of a vegetable Market House on market Hill. by the renting of stands or stalls for the shelter and benefit of the gardeners and public, and --- Page break --- 551 Whereas, It being the custom of some market gardners to sell their produce in the corporate limits during market hours which will result to the detriment and injury to those paying for stall rights; therefore be it, Resolved by the city Council of the city of Austin, that no person engaged in the sale of vegetables from any vehicle shall be allowed to sell or dispose of the same in the corporate limits during market hours, which shall be until 7 a.m. from the 20th of March to the 20th of September, and until 8 a.m. from the 20th of September to the 20th of March. Any violation of this resolution shall be considered a misdemeanor and punished as in other cases of misdemeanor. The following resolution was introduced and read, towit: Whereas, There being repeated complaints from pedestrians and citizens doing business in the neighborhood of the crossing of Congress Avenue and Pecan Street, arising from the excessive noise and shouting from auctioneers and the daily blocking of the side-walks by a crowd of people attending the sale of stock by said auctioneers on said streets, thereby impeding and preventing the free passage of said side-walks; therefore be it. Resolved by the city Council of the city of Austin, that from and after the passage of this resolution, no animal, vehicle or any article of merchandise shall be permitted to be hired, or sold at auction in the streets of this city, except sales in pursuance of the Laws of the State and a violation of this resolution shall be a misdemeanor and punished as in other cases of misdemeanors. The following resolution was introduced and read, towit: Be it resolved by the city Council of the city of Austin, That Colorado Steam Fire Engine Company No 2, is hereby authorized to elect an engineer to take charge of their engine, who shall be a competent and skillful engineer and whose duty it shall be to be at all times on duty at the engine house of said Company; and who shall hold his office, subject to the regulations of the Fire Department until the her annual election in said Company.
