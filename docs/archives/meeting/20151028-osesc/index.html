Wednesday, October 28, 2015 Open Space, Environment, and Sustainability Committee Regular Meeting

  • New federal ozone standards pose a risk to Austin's air quality compliance, with officials warning of significant financial consequences if the city falls into non-attainment status.
  • Discussions highlighted a critical lack of accessible neighborhood parks and a historical decline in funding for parkland acquisition, prompting calls for new strategies like using city-owned land and school sites.
