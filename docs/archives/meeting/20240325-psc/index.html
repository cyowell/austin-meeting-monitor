Monday, March 25, 2024 Public Safety Committee Special Called Meeting
Official City Record Agenda Watch Video Transcript

  • Austin Police Academy Reforms Under Scrutiny:

    Concerns persist over slow progress on training reforms, leadership turnover, and delays in implementing a new operations manual and crucial internal audits, despite new civilian staff hires.

  • Fentanyl Driving Overdose Surge:

    Travis County leads Texas in opioid overdose deaths per capita, largely due to accidental fentanyl exposures in other street drugs like cocaine and meth, leading to a rise from 30 to 100 overdoses per month.

  • EMS Expands Lifesaving Opioid Response:

    Austin-Travis County EMS reports significant success with widespread Narcan distribution (78% of overdoses now get Narcan before EMS arrival) and a 93% success rate for its "buprenorphine bridge" program, linking users to long-term treatment.

  • Accountability & Transparency Demanded:

    Council and public speakers urged APD to provide clearer timelines for finalizing reform processes, establishing oversight committees, and ensuring data-driven accountability for police training.
