Affordable Homes, Lake Protection, & Energy Savings

Tuesday, June 25, 2013 Austin City Council Work Session

  • New Affordable Housing Tool:

    Discussed implementing a new state law to create "Homestead Preservation Districts." These districts would use tax funding to help low-income homeowners and renters combat rising property taxes and prevent displacement, with a focus on areas like East Riverside.

  • Protecting Lake Austin:

    Addressed a resolution to safeguard environmental protections (like setbacks and vegetation buffers) in Lake Austin zoning, which are currently lost when properties are rezoned. Council also debated improving the variance approval process.

  • Apartment Energy & Water Savings:

    Reviewed a program providing significant rebates to apartment owners for efficiency upgrades (e.g., duct sealing, water devices), leading to considerable utility bill savings for tenants. The city will monitor if landlords pass savings on or raise rents.

  • Senior Meal Funding Challenge:

    Explored options to continue vital senior meal programs in parks facilities. Council expressed concern about potentially reallocating funds from other critical social services, prompting a search for alternative budget sources.
