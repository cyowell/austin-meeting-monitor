Brackenridge Hospital: Control, Costs, and Care

Monday, March 27, 1978 special called
  • The public hearing focused on the future management of Brackenridge Hospital, debating whether the City should maintain direct control or transition to an independent hospital district or board.
  • Advocates strongly urged the City to expand access to healthcare for low-income residents, proposing initiatives like sliding payment scales, neighborhood clinics, and integrated preventive health services.
  • Calls were made for significant administrative reforms at the hospital, including improved efficiency, cost containment, fair pay scales, and consolidating all City health services under one unified administration.
  • The Council indicated it would make a final decision on Brackenridge Hospital's management and operations in early April, following extensive public input on the complex issues.
