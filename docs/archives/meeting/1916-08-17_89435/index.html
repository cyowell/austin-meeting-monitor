Thursday, August 17, 1916 regular
Transcript
  • A fire department driver was suspended for reckless driving that damaged a fire truck and a building, injuring passengers; other firefighters were summoned for failing to intervene.
  • Permits were granted for installing new gasoline tanks and curbside pumps on Congress Avenue, and for a large oil storage facility, with specific safety and lighting regulations.
  • A major 11th Street paving project was approved, and a public hearing was scheduled for property owners to discuss how the costs would be assessed.
  • The city approved providing free water and light services for visiting U.S. Army and National Guard troops in September, and decided to sell condemned mules unfit for city work.

Lawrence Hanley Reckless Driving The Mayor laid before the Council the following resolution: Whereas, in the judgement of the City Council ,Lawrence Hanley , the then driver of North Austin Hose Company No 5,was guilty of reckless driving in driving the car of said North Austin Hose Co No 5 to a reported fire at 1505 Colorado street on the night of Friday August 18th 1916, in that by reason of said reckless driving the greatly injured the car he was driving and destroyed the store building situated at the corner of 19th and --- Page break --- 590 Guadalupe streets in this City, and more or less injured all of the persons upon said car which the said Lawrence Hanley was driving. Now, therefore, Be it resolved by the City Council of the City of Austin, Texas : That in punishment for said reckless driving which in the judgement of the City Council constitutes a breach of the necessary discipline to be enforced in said Austin Fire Department, the said Lawrence Hanley is hereby suspended from occupation and pay from the 20th day of August 1916, to the 1st day of January 1917, and Be it further resolved by the City Council of the City of Austin, Texas: That James Quinlan and John Luckey be and they are hereby summonsed to appear before the City Council at its meeting to be held on Thursday, the 24th day of August, 2916, to receive a public reprimand for failure to remonstrate with the said Lawrence Hanley for the reckless gate at which he was driving said North Austin Hose Co No 5 car.
