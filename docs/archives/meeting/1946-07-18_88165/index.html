Austin Growth: Lake, Zoning & New Era

Thursday, July 18, 1946 regular

  • Addressing Post-War Housing:

    Committees were active in managing the housing shortage and rent control, highlighting challenges for a growing population.

  • Major Development & Zoning Battles:

    The city debated re-zoning over 1,900 acres for commercial and industrial use, including a controversial plan for a new drive-in theater.

  • Lake Austin's Health & Recreation:

    Petitions sought a temporary lowering of Lake Austin to control moss and improve navigability for public enjoyment.

  • Expanding City Infrastructure:

    Major projects for new sanitary sewers and gas lines were approved to support urban expansion and new housing construction.
