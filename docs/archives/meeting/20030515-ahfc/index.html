Thursday, May 15, 2003 Austin Housing Finance Corporation (AHFC)

  • Housing Development Investment:

    The Austin Housing Finance Corporation (AHFC) approved a commitment of up to $1 million to support the "Pedernales Urban Digs" mixed-use condominium development on East Sixth Street.

  • Securing Residential Units:

    This funding is designed to guarantee the purchase of up to ten completed residential units within the development, aimed at increasing local housing options.

  • Community Housing Initiative:

    The project falls under the AHFC's Acquisition and Development Program, with financing contingent on funds generated by the S.M.A.R.T. Housing capital improvement initiatives.
