Austin's Energy Supply Decision

Wednesday, February 17, 1960 special called

  • Major Gas Supply Offer:

    A company, Texas Central Gas, proposed a 20-year contract to provide natural gas to Austin's electric generating plants at an initial rate of 21 cents per thousand cubic feet, claiming it was the best and lowest bid available.

  • Financial and Reserve Concerns:

    City officials expressed significant doubts about the gas company's financial stability, its ability to guarantee sufficient gas reserves, and the adequacy of its proposed $500,000 performance bond for such a large contract.

  • Competitive Bidding Environment:

    This offer came as the City had already issued a letter of intent to another company, Intra-Tex Gas, at higher prices, prompting discussions about allowing other firms to submit bids.

  • Independent Review Ordered:

    To resolve the uncertainties, the City Council voted to hire independent consultants (DeGolyer and MacNaughton) to thoroughly evaluate the proposed gas reserves and the financial strength of the companies involved.
