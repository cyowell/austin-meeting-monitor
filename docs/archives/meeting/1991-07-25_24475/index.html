Thursday, July 25, 1991 regular
Transcript

  • Environmental Protection for Barton Creek:

    Extended temporary rules safeguarding Barton Creek and its contributing watersheds, while also setting a timeline for developing comprehensive new environmental regulations.

  • Strict Rules for New Development:

    Approved a major multi-family zoning change with extensive conditions on density, building materials, parking, and privacy, reflecting careful urban planning.

  • Airport Funding Boost:

    Authorized a new Passenger Facility Charge at Robert Mueller Municipal Airport, projected to generate $6 million annually for airport improvements.

  • Planning for Bergstrom's Future:

    Established a task force to begin planning for the conversion and future use of the Bergstrom Air Force Base property.
