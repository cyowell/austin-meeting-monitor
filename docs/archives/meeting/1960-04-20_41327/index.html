Austin Growth, New Zoning, & Golf Course Lease

Wednesday, April 20, 1960 regular
  • The city approved annexations of nearly 20 acres of land and vacated multiple utility and drainage easements to facilitate new developments, notably in the North Loop Plaza area.
  • Contracts totaling over $13,900 were authorized for essential infrastructure projects, including new pipe culverts and water main construction along Riverside Drive.
  • Several zoning changes were debated in public hearings, with some granted, including one for a beauty parlor with specific conditions, and another for commercial use despite not being recommended by the Planning Commission. An application for a 16-22 unit apartment complex was withdrawn due to public opposition.
  • A new one-year lease was authorized for the Pitch and Putt Golf Course at a rate of $200 per month.
