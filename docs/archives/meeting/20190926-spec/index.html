Austin's Code Vision: Water, Flooding, Housing

Thursday, September 26, 2019 Austin City Council Special Called Meeting

  • Major Land Code Changes Advance:

    Austin is moving forward with a significant rewrite of its Land Development Code, with a full proposal and new maps slated for public release on October 4th, followed by extensive public engagement and Council hearings through December.

  • Prioritizing Clean Water & Flood Safety:

    The proposed code aims to improve water quality and reduce flood risk citywide. This includes requiring modern stormwater management for redeveloping properties (even older ones), expanding green infrastructure, and increasing triggers for water quality controls.

  • Boosting Diverse Housing Options:

    Streamlined permitting processes are proposed to facilitate "missing middle" housing (like duplexes and townhomes) on existing residential lots. This approach seeks to encourage density and reduce sprawl while maintaining overall citywide impervious cover levels.

  • Key Debates Emerge:

    Significant discussions are expected around proposed amendments to the "Save Our Springs" ordinance and changes to impervious cover limits in certain "transition zones," which will require broad community and Council consensus to pass.

  • Rebuilding Your Home Clarified:

    Staff confirmed that homeowners whose single-family homes are destroyed or voluntarily demolished in transition zones will generally be allowed to rebuild them, potentially even larger, dispelling concerns about being forced to construct multi-unit dwellings.
