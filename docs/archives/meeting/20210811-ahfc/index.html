Wednesday, August 11, 2021 Austin Housing Finance Corporation (AHFC) Board of Directors AHFC Meeting
Official City Record Agenda Watch Video Transcript
  • The Austin Housing Finance Corporation (AHFC) unanimously approved its capital budget, operating budget, and housing assistance fund budget for the upcoming fiscal year.
  • A resident raised concerns about the lack of investment in homeownership opportunities within current housing development programs, specifically noting that projects like St. Johns 19 acres excluded ownership.
  • The public comment also highlighted a perceived overemphasis on permanent supportive housing in some developments, questioning the balance of housing strategies.
  • Citizens were encouraged to report potential fraud, waste, or abuse related to pandemic oversight efforts.
