Austin's Growth, New Traffic Rules, Airport Plans

Thursday, September 28, 1995 regular

Here's a concise summary of the September 28, 1995 Austin City Council agenda:

  • State Law Alters Development Rules:

    The council noted new state legislation (SB 1396) now limits the city's ability to deny or condition development projects based solely on potential traffic impacts, a significant shift in local planning control.

  • Future Growth and Airport Expansion:

    Public hearings were held on the full annexation of several large areas across the city, outlining future growth. Additionally, land acquisition via eminent domain was approved for the new Austin-Bergstrom International Airport.

  • Community and Environmental Initiatives:

    The city advanced programs for weatherization and energy efficiency rebates benefiting low-income residents, while also passing a resolution supporting a strong Safe Drinking Water Act.

  • Key Zoning Decisions and Legal Settlements:

    Multiple rezoning requests were approved, often with specific conditional overlays to guide development. The council also authorized settlements for several lawsuits against the city.
