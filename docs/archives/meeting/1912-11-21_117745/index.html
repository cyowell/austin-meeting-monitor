Austin Progress: Lights, Hospital, Citizen Claims

Thursday, November 21, 1912 regular

  • Infrastructure Upgrades:

    Authorized a new street lighting system for Lavaca, 19th Streets, and University Avenue. Also, fixed street centerlines and sidewalk widths for several other key areas.

  • New Hospital Building Progress:

    Increased the local architect's compensation for the upcoming City Hospital project and officially appointed Leslie N. Iredell to oversee its construction.

  • Citizen Claims and Financial Matters:

    Approved payments for a horse injury ($10) and a significant legal judgment (over $800) against the city, while also rejecting another claim and cancelling an erroneous property assessment.
