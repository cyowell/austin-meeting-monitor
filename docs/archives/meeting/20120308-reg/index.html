Austin's Future: Districts, Art, & Police Structure

Thursday, March 8, 2012 Austin City Council Regular Meeting

  • City Governance Overhaul:

    Debated a shift to geographic-based districts (10-1 or 10-2-1) for city representation, with proposals for an independent redistricting commission and significant campaign finance transparency reforms.

  • Creative Economy Boost:

    A briefing highlighted the booming local creative sector (music, film, tech) as a key economic driver, discussing growth challenges like mobility, downtown development, and balancing sound ordinances.

  • Police Community Focus:

    Approved reorganizing police leadership to create regional "bureau chiefs" under an additional Assistant Chief, aiming for enhanced community policing and more direct neighborhood engagement.
