Austin Approves Carnival and Water Project

Monday, October 13, 1924 special called

  • Carnival & Parades:

    Approved permits for the Jack Schafer Carnival to operate on East Avenue and for Travis Post No. 76 to hold an Armistice Day Parade on Congress Avenue.

  • Infrastructure Investment:

    Awarded a significant contract, valued at over $7,000, to Kirkwood & Wharton for new water main construction.

  • Citizen Concerns:

    Addressed petitions regarding street improvements in East Austin and deferred a decision on extending a downtown parking ordinance to a future meeting.
