Austin: Urban Renewal, Lake, Zoning & Growth

Wednesday, December 23, 1959 regular

  • Major Urban Renewal Effort Launched:

    The city officially adopted the Urban Renewal Law of Texas, establishing an agency to tackle "slum or blighted areas" through rehabilitation and redevelopment.

  • Significant City Expansion:

    Multiple ordinances passed for the annexation of over 113 acres of land, expanding Austin's boundary limits. Plans for extensive street paving projects were also approved, with costs shared by the city and abutting property owners.

  • Zoning Decisions and Public Input:

    Several zoning change requests were reviewed, with one deferred due to a "drainage problem" and another outright denied. The city also decided to inspect a citizen's request for a special driveway and parking setup.

  • Seasonal Lake Management:

    A resolution was approved to lower the city lake for a three-week period starting January 31st, allowing residents to perform maintenance or construction like building docks with proper permits.

  • Democratic Process in Action:

    The City Clerk certified that a petition concerning land near I-35 and Red River Street failed to meet the required number of qualified voter signatures.
