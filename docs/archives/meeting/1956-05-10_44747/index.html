Austin: Zoning, River Access & New City Bonds

Thursday, May 10, 1956 regular

  • Major Infrastructure and Funding:

    The city approved $320,000 in bonds for fire stations and hospital improvements, alongside a $124,000 contract for water meters. Funds were also allocated to advance the Justin Lane widening and paving project, and a commitment was made to resolve sewer issues in Manor Hills.

  • Key Zoning Changes:

    Several zoning requests were granted, transitioning properties from residential to commercial or office use across the city, including a substantial general retail tract on Burnet Road. Notably, a commercial zoning upgrade request on North Lamar Boulevard was denied.

  • Community and Business Permits:

    The city responded to a public request by designating specific Colorado River locations for baptismal services, with plans for future facilities. Permits were also issued for the construction of a new drive-in gasoline filling station and a private fuel plant for a local linen service.
