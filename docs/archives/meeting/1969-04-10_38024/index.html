Austin Expands: Sidewalks, Airport, Urban Plans

Thursday, April 10, 1969 regular

  • Massive Land Annexation:

    Austin significantly expanded its boundaries by annexing hundreds of acres across Travis County, incorporating new territories into the city limits.

  • New Sidewalk Mandate:

    A new ordinance was introduced to require the installation of sidewalks under specific conditions, affecting future construction and urban design.

  • Airport Growth & Land Acquisition:

    The city authorized using eminent domain to acquire land needed for the expansion of Robert Mueller Municipal Airport.

  • Major Urban Planning Re-evaluation:

    An extension was sought for the Community Renewal Program, reflecting the need to update long-term urban development strategies due to Austin's rapid growth and new federal initiatives.

  • Zilker Park's Temporary New Name:

    For a special "Speaker Gus Mutscher Day," Zilker Park was ceremonially designated "Gus Mutscher Park" for April 23rd.
