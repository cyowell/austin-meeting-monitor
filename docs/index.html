Meeting Highlights

Here are the key items from the Austin City Council agenda: * **Expanding Housing Options and Reforming Parking Rules:** Council will consider significant policy changes to allow for more housing types like townhomes and mixed-use developments ("missing middle housing"), reduce required off-street parking in transit areas, and explore separating parking fees from housing costs. They will also consider selling city land to develop affordable housing. * **Major Land Use and Parkland Decisions:** Public hearings include amending city sign and billboard regulations, a proposal to permanently use a portion of Auditorium Shores parkland for a reclaimed water line, and several disannexations of properties from the city limits as mandated by state law. * **Strategic Plans and City Infrastructure:** Council will vote on the Austin Homeless Strategies and Operations 2025-2027 Strategic Plan and update the city's overall transportation plan. They will also approve numerous contracts for essential city services and infrastructure projects, including a new facility for Austin Resource Recovery's reuse program.
